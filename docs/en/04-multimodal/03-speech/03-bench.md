# 4.3.3 Bench

- [Refining the evaluation of speech synthesis: A summary of the Blizzard Challenge 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csl.2024.101747): Summarizes Blizzard Challenge 2023 speech-synthesis evaluation, including listening-test design and multidimensional judgments such as naturalness, intelligibility, and speaker similarity.
- [Hallucinations in Neural Automatic Speech Recognition: Identifying Errors and Hallucinatory Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01572): Defines ASR hallucinations as fluent but semantically unrelated transcriptions, shows WER can miss them, and proposes perturbation plus semantic-fluency diagnostics.
- [MuTox: Universal MUltilingual Audio-based TOXicity Dataset and Zero-shot Detector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05060): Introduces a multilingual audio-toxicity dataset and zero-shot detector.
- [MLAAD: The Multi-Language Audio Anti-Spoofing Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09512): Provides a multilingual audio anti-spoofing dataset for evaluating synthetic-speech detection across languages.
- [ICASSP 2024 Speech Signal Improvement Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14444): Defines a shared challenge for evaluating speech signal improvement methods.
- [SpeechBERTScore: Reference-Aware Automatic Evaluation of Speech Generation Leveraging NLP Evaluation Metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16812): Adapts BERTScore-style dense speech features and discrete-token metrics to compare generated speech with a reference and correlate with human speech-generation judgments.
- [Singing Voice Data Scaling-up: An Introduction to ACE-Opencpop and ACE-KiSing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17619): Builds ACE-Opencpop and ACE-KiSing with synthesizer-assisted augmentation plus manual tuning, then tests them as large-scale multi-singer singing-voice-synthesis resources.
- [Objective and subjective evaluation of speech enhancement methods in the UDASE task of the 7th CHiME challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01413): Evaluates CHiME-7 UDASE speech-enhancement submissions on real noisy CHiME-5 domestic recordings, contrasting intrusive metrics, nonintrusive metrics, and listener ratings.
- [SHIELD: an evaluation benchmark for face spoofing and forgery detection with multimodal large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04178): Tests MLLMs on face anti-spoofing and forgery detection with true/false and multiple-choice questions over RGB, infrared, depth, and manipulated face evidence.
- [AIR-Bench: Benchmarking Large Audio-Language Models via Generative Comprehension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07729): Evaluates large audio-language models with instruction-style audio comprehension tasks that require open-ended generation rather than only ASR or fixed-label audio classification.
- [Codec-SUPERB: An In-Depth Analysis of Sound Codec Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13071): Benchmarks neural sound codecs across downstream applications and signal-level metrics to test whether codec tokens preserve content, speaker, paralinguistic, and audio information.
- [SpeechColab Leaderboard: An Open-Source Platform for Automatic Speech Recognition Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08196) ([leaderboard](https://github.com/SpeechColab/Leaderboard)): Provides an open ASR evaluation platform comparing open and commercial systems while auditing scoring choices such as punctuation, casing, synonyms, and modified token error rate.
- [ZAEBUC-Spoken: A Multilingual Multidialectal Arabic-English Speech Corpus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18182): Releases a multilingual, multidialectal Arabic-English speech corpus.
- [Real Acoustic Fields: An Audio-Visual Room Acoustics Dataset and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18821): Captures real room impulse responses with multiview images and 6DoF emitter-listener poses for novel-view acoustic synthesis and room-acoustics generation evaluation.
- [The VoicePrivacy 2024 Challenge Evaluation Plan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02677) ([results](https://www.voiceprivacychallenge.org/vp2024/)): Defines voice anonymization tracks that hide speaker identity while preserving linguistic content and emotion, with shared data, scripts, baselines, and anonymized-audio submissions.
- [Cross-Domain Audio Deepfake Detection: Dataset and Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04904): Provides a cross-domain dataset and analysis for audio deepfake detection.
- [Audio Dialogues: Dialogues dataset for audio and music understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07616): Creates 163.8k multi-turn audio and music dialogue samples plus multi-audio QA pairs, generated from existing captions to evaluate interactive audio understanding.
- [A Large-Scale Evaluation of Speech Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09385): Establishes SUPERB-style evaluation with frozen speech foundation models and lightweight task heads, then analyzes cross-task generalization, leaderboard reproducibility, and weighted-sum scoring.
- [MAD Speech: Measures of Acoustic Diversity of Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10419): Defines acoustic-diversity metrics for generated speech across voice, gender, emotion, accent, and background-noise facets using specialized embeddings and aggregation functions.
- [An RFP dataset for Real, Fake, and Partially fake audio detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17721): Introduces an RFP detection set with real, noisy, voice-converted, TTS, and partially fake audio, showing partial fakes produce much higher EER than fully fake clips.
- [Benchmarking Representations for Speech, Music, and Acoustic Events](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00934): Presents ARCH, a 12-dataset benchmark for audio representation learning across speech, music, and acoustic-event classification domains.
- [Mixat: A Data Set of Bilingual Emirati-English Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02578): Releases 15 hours of Emirati-English code-mixed podcast speech and evaluates Arabic and multilingual ASR systems on dialectal and code-switching recognition.
- [The Codecfake Dataset and Countermeasures for the Universally Detection of Deepfake Audio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04880): Builds a 1M-plus English and Chinese Codecfake set for ALM-based audio deepfake detection and evaluates CSAM with EER across codec-generated test conditions.
- [SVDD Challenge 2024: A Singing Voice Deepfake Detection Challenge Evaluation Plan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05244): Defines the first singing-voice deepfake-detection challenge, separating lab-controlled and in-the-wild bona fide and fake singing recordings.
- [Evaluating Text-to-Speech Synthesis from a Large Discrete Token-based Speech Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09768): Evaluates TTS from a large discrete-token speech language model for naturalness, intelligibility, non-speech noise, and hallucinated or unintelligible speech artifacts.
- [Enabling ASR for Low-Resource Languages: A Comprehensive Dataset Creation Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01446): Proposes an audiobook-to-ASR pipeline that segments hours-long audio into 4-15 second training clips for low-resource and regional language recognition.
- [CtrSVDD: A Benchmark Dataset and Baseline Analysis for Controlled Singing Voice Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02438): Provides 47.64 hours of bona fide and 260.34 hours of fake singing vocals across 14 generation methods and 164 singer identities for controlled SVDD baselines.
- [Harder or Different? Understanding Generalization of Audio Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03512): Decomposes audio deepfake detector failures into sample hardness and generator-domain difference, showing out-of-domain gaps are driven mainly by distribution difference.
- [URGENT Challenge: Universality, Robustness, and Generalizability For Speech Enhancement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04660): Defines a universal speech-enhancement challenge spanning denoising, dereverberation, bandwidth extension, and declipping with robustness and generalization metrics.
- [The Database and Benchmark For the Source Speaker Tracing Challenge 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04951): Provides a database and benchmark for tracing the source speaker of generated or transformed speech.
- [Do Prompts Really Prompt? Exploring the Prompt Understanding Capability of Whisper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05806): Evaluates whether Whisper understands prompts in speech-processing settings.
- [EARS: An Anechoic Fullband Speech Dataset Benchmarked for Speech Enhancement and Dereverberation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06185): Releases and benchmarks an anechoic fullband speech dataset for enhancement and dereverberation.
- [AudioMarkBench: Benchmarking Robustness of Audio Watermarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06979): Evaluates the robustness of audio watermarking methods under transformations and attacks.
- [EmoBox: Multilingual Multi-corpus Speech Emotion Recognition Toolkit and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07162): Standardizes intra-corpus and cross-corpus speech emotion recognition over multiple languages and corpora with reusable splits and toolkit baselines.
- [The Interspeech 2024 Challenge on Speech Processing Using Discrete Units](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07725): Evaluates discrete speech units on multilingual ASR, TTS, and singing voice synthesis to test whether tokenized audio preserves task-relevant information.
- [LibriTTS-P: A Corpus with Speaking Style and Speaker Identity Prompts for Text-to-Speech and Style Captioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07969): Extends LibriTTS-R with utterance-level speaking-style prompts and speaker-characteristic prompts built from manual and synthetic annotations for controllable TTS evaluation.
- [FakeSound: Deepfake General Audio Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08052): Introduces deepfake general-audio detection with manipulated-region localization and an automated pipeline for creating realistic fake non-speech and speech audio.
- [DCASE 2024 Task 4: Sound Event Detection with Heterogeneous Data and Missing Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08056) ([results](https://dcase.community/challenge2024/task-sound-event-detection-with-heterogeneous-training-dataset-and-potentially-missing-labels-results)): Defines sound-event detection with heterogeneous data and missing labels.
- [ML-SUPERB 2.0: Benchmarking Multilingual Speech Models Across Modeling Constraints, Languages, and Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08641) ([leaderboard](https://multilingual.superbbenchmark.org/leaderboard)): Benchmarks multilingual speech models across modeling constraints, languages, and datasets.
- [The Second DISPLACE Challenge : DIarization of SPeaker and LAnguage in Conversational Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09494): Defines a challenge for speaker and language diarization in conversational environments.
- [On the Evaluation of Speech Foundation Models for Spoken Language Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10083): Uses SLUE spoken-language-understanding tasks to compare speech foundation models and integration strategies on natural-speech classification and sequence-generation problems.
- [MINT: a Multi-modal Image and Narrative Text Dubbing Dataset for Foley Audio Content Planning and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10591): Pairs images and narrative text with Foley-audio planning targets so models can evaluate cross-modal scene matching for audio dubbing and generation.
- [SingMOS: An extensive Open-Source Singing Voice Dataset for MOS Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10911): Releases MOS-annotated singing-voice samples to train and test automatic quality predictors for singing synthesis and voice-conversion outputs.
- [MusicScore: A Dataset for Music Score Modeling and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11462): Collects a large-scale music-score dataset from IMSLP for score modeling and generation, extending evaluation beyond optical music recognition alone.
- [GigaSpeech 2: An Evolving, Large-Scale and Multi-domain ASR Corpus for Low-Resource Languages with Automated Crawling, Transcription and Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11546): Releases an evolving large-scale multilingual ASR corpus with automated crawling, transcription, and refinement.
- [SD-Eval: A Benchmark Dataset for Spoken Dialogue Understanding Beyond Words](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13340): Tests spoken-dialogue response appropriateness using content, paralinguistic, and environmental speech cues rather than transcript-only understanding.
- [DASB - Discrete Audio and Speech Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14294): Benchmarks discrete audio tokens on discriminative and generative tasks across speech, general audio, and music to test phonetic, speaker, and paralinguistic preservation.
- [The Music Maestro or The Musically Challenged, A Massive Music Evaluation Benchmark for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15885): Provides ZIQI-Eval with more than 14,000 music questions across 10 categories and 56 subcategories for LLM music understanding and generation reasoning.
- [AudioBench: A Universal Benchmark for Audio Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16020) ([leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/AudioLLMs/AudioBench-Leaderboard-Extend)): Evaluates AudioLLMs on 8 tasks and 26 datasets spanning speech understanding, audio-scene understanding, and voice or paralinguistic understanding.
- [MSR-86K: An Evolving, Multilingual Corpus with 86,300 Hours of Transcribed Audio for Speech Recognition Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18301): Provides 86,300 hours of evolving multilingual transcribed audio for ASR, emphasizing large-scale corpus growth rather than a fixed small test set.
- [FMSG-JLESS Submission for DCASE 2024 Task4 on Sound Event Detection with Heterogeneous Training Dataset and Potentially Missing Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00291): Reports a DCASE 2024 Task 4 system for heterogeneous, missing-label sound-event detection using domain-generalized audio transformer and convolutional features.
- [AudioTime: A Temporally-aligned Audio-text Benchmark Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02857): Adds temporally aligned text annotations for audio events so text-to-audio systems can be tested on timestamp control rather than only clip-level captions.
- [Probing the Feasibility of Multilingual Speaker Anonymization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02937): Evaluates multilingual speaker anonymization feasibility.
- [YourMT3+: Multi-Instrument Music Transcription with Enhanced Transformer Architectures and Cross-Dataset STEM Augmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04822): Evaluates multi-instrument music transcription with enhanced Transformer architectures and cross-dataset STEM augmentation for richer music-audio transcription benchmarks.
- [A Benchmark for Multi-Speaker Anonymization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05608): Defines evaluation for anonymizing multiple speakers in the same recording, testing privacy protection and downstream speaker or ASR utility together.
- [VoxBlink2: A 100K+ Speaker Recognition Corpus and the Open-Set Speaker-Identification Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11510): Releases a 100K-plus speaker corpus and open-set speaker-identification benchmark for large-scale recognition with unknown speakers.
- [TTSDS - Text-to-Speech Distribution Score](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12707): Proposes Text-to-Speech Distribution Score to compare synthetic and real speech distributions across prosody, speaker identity, intelligibility, and other quality factors.
- [MSceneSpeech: A Multi-Scene Speech Dataset For Expressive Speech Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14006): Provides a high-quality Mandarin expressive TTS dataset recorded across daily-life scenarios, multiple speakers, and varied prosodic styles.
- [Overview of Speaker Modeling and Its Applications: From the Lens of Deep Speaker Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15188): Organizes speaker-representation evaluation axes across recognition, diarization, synthesis, and target-speaker extraction rather than adding a single new dataset.
- [The CHiME-8 DASR Challenge for Generalizable and Array Agnostic Distant Automatic Speech Recognition and Diarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16447): Defines joint distant ASR and diarization over arbitrary speaker counts, heterogeneous microphone arrays, and diverse acoustic environments.
- [Audio Entailment: Assessing Deductive Reasoning for Audio Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18062): Introduces audio entailment to test whether audio-language models can make deductive judgments from audio evidence, beyond captioning or retrieval.
- [VoxSim: A perceptual voice similarity dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18505): Provides about 41,000 VoxCeleb utterance pairs with perceptual voice-similarity ratings for training and validating speaker-similarity metrics in speech synthesis.
- [MMTrail: A Multimodal Trailer Video Dataset with Language and Music Descriptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20962): Adds trailer videos with aligned language and music descriptions to evaluate audio-visual correlation and music-language grounding in multimodal models.
- [Can LLMs "Reason" in Music? An Evaluation of LLMs' Capability of Music Understanding and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21531): Tests LLMs on symbolic-music understanding and conditioned generation tasks that require multi-step reasoning for editable and interactive music creation.
- [MuChoMusic: Evaluating Music Understanding in Multimodal Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01337): Benchmarks music understanding for audio-language models through language queries over music inputs, targeting interpretation beyond generic audio captioning.
- [Contextual Cross-Modal Attention for Audio-Visual Deepfake Detection and Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01532): Evaluates audio-visual deepfake detection and localization under modality gaps, using cross-modal attention to align manipulated visual and audio cues.
- [The DeepSpeak Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05366): Releases identity-oriented deepfake video and audio material for high-stakes scenarios such as impostor hiring, addressing outdated and low-quality forensic training data.
- [Lyrics Transcription for Humans: A Readability-Aware Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06370): Evaluates automatic lyrics transcription with punctuation, formatting, song structure, and vocal-role readability instead of word sequence accuracy alone.
- [Spoken Stereoset: on Evaluating Social Bias Toward Speaker in Speech Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07665): Converts bias evaluation into spoken inputs to test how speaker cues affect speech large language model stereotype and anti-stereotype judgments.
- [ASVspoof 5: Crowdsourced Speech Data, Deepfakes, and Adversarial Attacks at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08739): Benchmarks speech anti-spoofing with crowdsourced speech, deepfakes, and adversarial attacks at scale.
- [FLEURS-ASL: Including American Sign Language in Massively Multilingual Multitask Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13585): Extends FLORES and FLEURS with American Sign Language video translated by certified Deaf interpreters, supporting translation between ASL and text or speech-language benchmarks.
- [SpeechCraft: A Fine-Grained Expressive Speech Dataset with Natural Language Description](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13608): Builds a fine-grained expressive-speech dataset with natural-language style descriptions produced by an automatic annotation system for speech-style comprehension and control.
- [A Preliminary Case Study on Long-Form In-the-Wild Audio Spoofing Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14066): Stress-tests AASIST on long, multi-speaker, acoustically complex spoofing scenarios instead of short single-speaker waveform clips.
- [SONICS: Synthetic Or Not - Identifying Counterfeit Songs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14080): Frames song authenticity detection as a counterfeit-song benchmark, separating synthetic or manipulated singing and music from bona fide recordings.
- [SVDD 2024: The Inaugural Singing Voice Deepfake Detection Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16132) ([results](https://www.music-ir.org/mirex/wiki/2024:Singing_Voice_Deepfake_Detection_Results)): Documents the inaugural singing-voice deepfake-detection challenge.
- [AASIST3: KAN-Enhanced AASIST Speech Deepfake Detection using SSL Features and Additional Regularization for the ASVspoof 2024 Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17352): Reports an ASVspoof 2024 countermeasure system combining AASIST3, SSL features, KAN layers, and regularization for synthetic speech and voice-conversion attacks.
- [LibriheavyMix: A 20,000-Hour Dataset for Single-Channel Reverberant Multi-Talker Speech Separation, ASR and Speaker Diarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00819): Creates 20,000 hours of single-channel reverberant multi-talker mixtures for speech separation, ASR, and speaker diarization in meeting-like conditions.
- [IndicVoices-R: Unlocking a Massive Multilingual Multi-speaker Speech Corpus for Scaling Indian TTS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05356): Converts noisy multilingual Indian ASR data into a large multi-speaker TTS corpus through denoising, diarization, and quality filtering.
- [VoiceWukong: Benchmarking Deepfake Voice Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06348): Benchmarks deepfake-voice detectors using voices generated by 19 commercial and 15 open-source tools plus 38 real-world manipulation variants.
- [The Voicemos Challenge 2024: Beyond Speech Quality Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07001): Defines three VoiceMOS 2024 tracks for high-quality synthetic speech naturalness, singing or voice-conversion ratings across languages, and semi-supervised noisy or enhanced speech quality.
- [Salmon: A Suite for Acoustic Language Model Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07437): Tests acoustic language models on awareness of background noise, emotion, speaker identity, and room impulse response beyond spoken-content recognition.
- [OpenACE: An Open Benchmark for Evaluating Audio Coding Performance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08374): Provides an open full-band audio and speech coding benchmark with variable content types and reproducible quality tests for Opus, EVS, LC3, and LC3-plus.
- [LLM-Powered Grapheme-to-Phoneme Conversion: Benchmark and Case Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08554): Evaluates LLMs on grapheme-to-phoneme conversion with polyphones and context-dependent pronunciations, including prompting and post-processing without extra labeled data.
- [The Text-to-speech in the Wild (TITW) Database](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08711): Builds TITW-Hard and TITW-Easy from VoxCeleb1 through automated transcription, segmentation, enhancement, and DNSMOS filtering for noisy in-the-wild TTS training and testing.
- [DFADD: The Diffusion and Flow-Matching Based Audio Deepfake Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08731): Collects diffusion- and flow-matching-based TTS deepfakes to test whether anti-spoofing systems handle Voicebox-style and Seed-TTS-style generators.
- [The T05 System for the voicemos challenge 2024: Transfer Learning from Deep Image Classifier to Naturalness MOS Prediction of High-Quality Synthetic Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09305): Describes a VoiceMOS 2024 Track 1 MOS predictor that fuses SSL speech features with spectrogram-derived image features for high-quality synthetic-speech naturalness.
- [A Study on Zero-shot Non-intrusive Speech Assessment using Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09914): Compares GPT-4o audio analysis and GPT-Whisper prompting for zero-shot speech quality and intelligibility assessment against human ratings and ASR character error rate.
- [FakeMusicCaps: A Dataset for Detection and Attribution of Synthetic Music Generated via Text-to-Music Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10684): Provides detection and attribution data for synthetic music generated by text-to-music models.
- [Simulating Native Speaker Shadowing for Nonnative Speech Assessment with Latent Speech Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11742): Simulates native-speaker shadowing with voice conversion and latent speech representations to score L2 speech intelligibility beyond WER.
- [ASR Benchmarking: Need for a More Representative Conversational Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12042): Builds a multilingual TalkBank-derived conversational ASR dataset and shows strong WER drops under pauses, interruptions, disfluencies, and accent variation.
- [Codec-Superb @ SLT 2024: A Lightweight Benchmark For Neural Audio Codec Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14085): Defines a lightweight Codec-SUPERB challenge for comparing neural audio codecs on speech tasks while preserving content, speaker, and paralinguistic information at low bitrates.
- [LlamaPartialSpoof: An LLM-Driven Fake Speech Dataset Simulating Disinformation Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14743): Creates a 130-hour LLM-driven fake-speech dataset with full and partial voice-cloned edits to expose countermeasure weaknesses under attacker-motivated disinformation scenarios.
- [OmniBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15272) ([code](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/OmniBench), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/m-a-p/OmniBench)): evaluates tri-modal visual, acoustic, and textual understanding for omni-language models. The core idea is to test whether models can recognize, interpret, and reason over multiple simultaneous modalities rather than treating multimodal evaluation as image-text only.
- [ESPnet-Codec: Comprehensive Training and Evaluation of Neural Codecs For Audio, Music, and Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15897): Provides ESPnet-based codec recipes for training and evaluating neural codecs across audio, music, and speech downstream settings under consistent conditions.
- [Enabling Auditory Large Language Models for Automatic Speech Quality Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16644): Fine-tunes auditory LLMs to predict MOS, speaker similarity, and A/B preferences, while generating natural-language quality descriptions such as noisiness and distortion.
- [Did You Hear That? Introducing AADG: A Framework for Generating Benchmark Data in Audio Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03904): Uses LLMs as world models to synthesize broader real-world audio anomaly detection and localization scenarios beyond industrial machine sounds.
- [Efficiently Identifying Low-Quality Language Subsets in Multilingual Datasets: A Case Study on a Large-Scale Multilingual Audio Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04292): Proposes a Preference Proportion Test that flags low-quality language subsets in multilingual transcribed audio with about 20 annotated samples per subset.
- [Bahasa Harmony: A Comprehensive Dataset for Bahasa Text-to-Speech Synthesis with Discrete Codec Modeling of EnGen-TTS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06608): Releases about 55 hours and 52K Bahasa TTS recordings with EnGen-TTS baselines and MOS evaluation for Indonesian synthetic speech.
- [SCOREQ: Speech Quality Assessment with Contrastive Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06675): Uses triplet-loss contrastive regression for no-reference MOS prediction, benchmarking domain generalization across speech-quality datasets.
- [MLPerf Power: Benchmarking the Energy Efficiency of Machine Learning Systems from μWatts to MWatts for Sustainable AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12032): Defines MLPerf Power rules for measuring energy efficiency from tiny devices to datacenters, including inference workloads rather than a speech-only accuracy benchmark.
- [Sound Check: Auditing Audio Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13114): Audits audio datasets to expose quality, provenance, and evaluation risks for speech and audio models.
- [Can Large Audio-Language Models Truly Hear? Tackling Hallucinations with Multi-Task Assessment and Stepwise Audio Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16130): Evaluates audio hallucinations with object existence, temporal order, and object attribute tasks plus stepwise audio reasoning prompts.
- [VoiceBench: Benchmarking LLM-Based Voice Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17196): Tests LLM-based voice assistants on real-time speech interaction scenarios with speaker, environment, and content variation beyond clean ASR or text knowledge tasks.
- [Challenge on Sound Scene Synthesis: Evaluating Text-to-Audio Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17589): Defines the DCASE 2024 Sound Scene Synthesis challenge with structured prompts, Fréchet Audio Distance, and perceptual ratings for text-to-audio controllability.
- [MMAU: A Massive Multi-Task Audio Understanding and Reasoning Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.19168) ([leaderboard](https://sakshi113.github.io/mmau_homepage/)): Provides 10K audio clips with human QA across speech, environmental sound, and music, requiring 27 perception and reasoning skills.
- [MOS-Bench: Benchmarking Generalization Abilities of Subjective Speech Quality Assessment Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03715): Collects 8 training sets and 17 test sets to measure out-of-domain generalization of subjective speech quality assessment and MOS prediction models.
- [Performance Evaluation of SLAM-ASR: The Good, the Bad, the Ugly, and the Way Forward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03866): Stress-tests SLAM-ASR connector architectures under domain shifts and speech perturbations to characterize when speech-encoder-to-LLM ASR remains robust.
- [ParaLBench: A Large-Scale Benchmark for Computational Paralinguistics Over Acoustic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09349): Standardizes 13 paralinguistic tasks from 10 datasets across 14 acoustic foundation models, covering emotion, health, age, gender, and temporal characteristics.
- [Rethinking MUSHRA: Addressing Modern Challenges in Text-to-Speech Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12719): Diagnoses MUSHRA reference bias and rater ambiguity for modern TTS, then releases MANGO with 246K human ratings across Hindi and Tamil.
- [AudioSetCaps: An Enriched Audio-Caption Dataset Using Automated Generation Pipeline With Large Audio and Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18953): Generates 1.9M AudioSet caption pairs and 4.1M additional synthetic pairs using audio-language extraction, LLM captioning, and CLAP-based refinement.
- [Circumventing shortcuts in audio-visual deepfake detection datasets with unsupervised learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00175): Shows leading-silence artifacts let detectors separate fake and real samples in common audio-video deepfake datasets, then evaluates unsupervised shortcut removal.
- [The Codec Language Model-Based Zero-Shot Spontaneous Style TTS System for CoVoC Challenge 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01100): Reports a CoVoC 2024 spontaneous-style zero-shot TTS system using a LLaMA-based codec language model, delay pattern, and classifier-free guidance.
- [AV-Odyssey Bench: Can Your Multimodal LLMs Really Understand Audio-Visual Information?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02611): Evaluates MLLMs on audio-visual understanding after DeafTest failures, including loudness, pitch, and multimodal reasoning across thousands of clips.
- [ASR-EC Benchmark: Evaluating Large Language Models on Chinese ASR Error Correction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03075): Evaluates large language models on Chinese automatic-speech-recognition error correction.
- [Benchmarking Open-ended Audio Dialogue Understanding for Large Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05167): Introduces ADU-Bench with 20K-plus open-ended audio dialogues across 3 scenarios, 12 skills, 9 languages, and ambiguity from intonation, pauses, and homophones.
- [2M-BELEBELE: Highly Multilingual Speech and American Sign Language Comprehension Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08274): Extends comprehension benchmarking to highly multilingual speech and American Sign Language.
