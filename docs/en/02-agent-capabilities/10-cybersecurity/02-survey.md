# 2.10.2 Survey

- [When LLMs meet cybersecurity: a systematic literature review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03644): Maps offensive, defensive, educational, and operational cybersecurity uses and gaps.
- [Large Language Models for Cyber Security: A Systematic Literature Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04760): Organizes vulnerability detection, penetration testing, malware analysis, response, and automation.
- [Harnessing the power of language models in cybersecurity](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jjimei.2024.100315): Reviews how language models support threat detection, vulnerability analysis, incident response, and security automation workflows.
- [Forewarned is Forearmed: A Survey on Large Language Model-based Agents in Autonomous Cyberattacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12786): Focuses on reconnaissance, exploitation, persistence, tools, and mitigation for offensive agents.
- [From Firewalls to Frontiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11398): Argues that AI red teaming evolves from cyber red teaming, importing threat modeling, adversarial methodology, and domain-specific security practice.
- [A Survey on Agentic Security: Applications, Threats and Defenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06445): Connects security-agent applications with threats and defensive designs.
- [Enhancing Software Vulnerability Analysis via Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/DSC67331.2025.00092): Systematically reviews how LLMs support vulnerability analysis, covering methods, evaluation settings, and research gaps.
- [LLM-Powered Security Test Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/MC.2025.3625694): Reviews how LLMs support security-test oracles, vulnerability probing, and adversarial input generation for software-security assessment.
- [Review of generative AI for multimodal cybersecurity threat simulation](https://doi.org/10.30574/wjarr.2025.27.1.2532): Reviews LLMs, GANs, and multimodal transformers for synthetic cyber-threat simulation across text, audio, image, and video channels.
- [A Survey of Agentic AI and Cybersecurity: Challenges, Opportunities and Use-case Prototypes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05293): Reviews use cases, prototypes, opportunities, and risk boundaries for autonomous security agents.
- [AI-Driven Security Alert Screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08316): Surveys AI-driven security alert screening and alert-fatigue mitigation in security operations centers.
- [LLMs for Agentic NetOps and AIOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12729): Surveys LLMs for agentic NetOps and AIOps, covering architectures, evaluation, and safety.
- [Harnessing LLMs for Cyber-Resilience](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3682720): Systematically reviews LLM use in cyber-resilience, threat mitigation, and future cybersecurity trajectories.
- [Large language models for software vulnerability detection](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10207-025-00992-7): Surveys LLM-based vulnerability detection models, methods, datasets, metrics, and researcher guidance for software security.
- [GAI-Driven Offensive Cybersecurity](https://doi.org/10.5220/0013378700003899): Positions generative AI as an offensive-security and penetration-testing enabler, connecting attack automation with proactive defense considerations.
- [SoK: Automated TTP Extraction from CTI Reports - Are We There Yet?](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/buechel): Systematizes TTP extraction methods and evaluates NLP, embedding, and generative approaches in a unified CTI setting.
