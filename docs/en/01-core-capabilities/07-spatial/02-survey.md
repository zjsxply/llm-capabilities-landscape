# 1.7.2 Survey

- [How to Enable LLM with 3D Capacity? A Survey of Spatial Reasoning in LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05786): Surveys acquisition and evaluation of 3D spatial reasoning capacity in language models.
- [A Survey of Large Language Model-Powered Spatial Intelligence Across Scales: Advances in Embodied Agents, Smart Cities, and Earth Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09848): Extends spatial intelligence from object relations to embodied, urban, and geoscience scales.
- [A Review of 3D Object Detection with Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18738): Surveys 3D object detection with VLMs, covering point-cloud, voxel, CLIP-style, and language-grounded approaches for spatial perception.
- [Large Language Models for Computer-Aided Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137): Treats CAD as a spatial design-automation setting, covering text-to-CAD, constraint reasoning, code generation, and design evaluation.
- [Reconstructing 4D Spatial Intelligence: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21045): Reviews dynamic geometry and spatiotemporal understanding beyond static spatial QA.
- [Multimodal Spatial Reasoning in the Large Model Era: A Survey and Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25760): Organizes multimodal spatial tasks, methods, and benchmark families.
- [Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models: A Survey of Tasks, Benchmarks and Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15722): Provides a focused guide to spatial-reasoning tasks and methods for MLLMs.
- [3D asset generation: a survey of evolution towards autoregressive and agent-driven paradigms](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-025-50381-5): Surveys 3D asset generation, including autoregressive and agent-driven paradigms relevant to spatial model construction.
