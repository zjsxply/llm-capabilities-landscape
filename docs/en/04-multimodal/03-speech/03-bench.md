# 4.3.3 Bench

- [OmniBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15272) ([code](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/OmniBench), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/m-a-p/OmniBench)): evaluates tri-modal visual, acoustic, and textual understanding for omni-language models. The core idea is to test whether models can recognize, interpret, and reason over multiple simultaneous modalities rather than treating multimodal evaluation as image-text only.
- [MusicEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10811): Evaluates text-to-music generation with expert-rated music samples. Core idea: use expert judgments to support automatic evaluation of generated music quality and prompt alignment, filling an audio-generation benchmark gap adjacent to TTA-Bench.
- [Jailbreak-AudioBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13772): Evaluates jailbreak threats for large audio-language models. Core idea: test whether harmful requests can bypass safety through spoken or audio-channel variation, extending safety evaluation beyond text-only jailbreak prompts.
- [Talking Turns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01174): Benchmarks audio foundation models on turn-taking dynamics. Core idea: use a turn-taking event judge and user-study protocol to test whether spoken dialogue systems avoid excessive overlap or silence and manage conversational floor changes.
- [Full-Duplex-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04721): Evaluates turn-taking in full-duplex spoken dialogue models. Core idea: test whether speech systems can listen and speak concurrently, interrupt appropriately, and manage conversational floor changes rather than operating as turn-isolated assistants.
- [S2S-Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05085): Evaluates paralinguistic instruction following in speech-to-speech models. Core idea: test whether spoken dialogue systems can follow instructions about tone, prosody, emotion, and other non-lexical cues rather than only preserving transcript-level content.
- [QualiSpeech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20290) (dataset: [tsinghua-ee/QualiSpeech](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsinghua-ee/QualiSpeech)): Evaluates low-level speech quality understanding by auditory LLMs. Core idea: replace scalar audio scores with natural-language quality descriptions and reasoning across 11 noise and distortion aspects.
- [FinAudio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20990): Benchmarks audio-language models on financial audio tasks such as conference-call and CEO-speech understanding. Core idea: test whether audio LLMs can handle domain speech, sentiment, and event reasoning rather than only clean general audio QA.
- [OmniAudio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14906): Evaluates 360-degree-video-to-spatial-audio generation through Sphere360/Sphere360-Bench. Core idea: pair panoramic video with first-order ambisonic spatial audio so audio-generation systems are judged on spatial grounding, temporal alignment, and immersive audio quality.
- [Vox-Profile](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14648): Evaluates speech foundation models on diverse speaker and speech-trait characterization. Core idea: diagnose whether audio-language models can infer speaker attributes, vocal conditions, and speech traits instead of reducing speech input to words.
- [AudioTrust](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16211): Benchmarks multifaceted trustworthiness of audio large language models. Core idea: evaluate safety, robustness, privacy, and reliability dimensions for audio-language systems rather than treating audio capability as only recognition accuracy or perceptual quality.
- [JALMBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17568): Benchmarks jailbreak vulnerabilities in large audio language models. Core idea: provide a unified evaluation framework with large-scale text and audio samples, multiple attack methods, mainstream LALMs, and defenses so audio jailbreak robustness can be compared systematically.
- [SpokenNativQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19163): Evaluates multilingual spoken question answering with about 33,000 naturally spoken, culturally aligned questions. Core idea: measure speech-input robustness, accents, and low-resource spoken-language behavior instead of assuming text-only multilingual QA.
- [EmergentTTS-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23009): Evaluates TTS models on complex prosody, expressiveness, and linguistic challenges. Core idea: use model-as-a-judge protocols to measure speech-generation behavior beyond ordinary intelligibility or naturalness scores.
- [SOVA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02457): Benchmarks speech conversation ability for LLM-based voice assistants. Core idea: evaluate general knowledge, speech recognition and understanding, semantic response quality, and acoustic generation quality in one speech-assistant protocol.
- [MMSU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04779): evaluates spoken-language understanding and reasoning across many tasks. Core idea: test whether omni or audio-language models can reason over speech input rather than only transcribed text.
- [InstructTTSEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16381): Benchmarks complex natural-language instruction following in text-to-speech systems. Core idea: evaluate whether speech generators can satisfy style, prosody, speaker, and content instructions rather than only producing intelligible audio.
- [WildSpeech-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21875): Benchmarks audio LLMs in natural speech conversations. Core idea: use in-the-wild conversational speech to test robustness to spontaneous speaking style, interaction context, and natural acoustic variation beyond clean scripted audio.
- [MULTIVOX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10859): Evaluates voice assistants for multimodal interactions. Core idea: test whether spoken assistants can coordinate voice, visual or contextual evidence in user-facing interactive tasks rather than only answer isolated audio questions.
- [WearVox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11824) ([open-source code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/wearvox)): Evaluates context awareness in wearable voice assistants. Core idea: combine speech, vision, and user context to evaluate assistant ability in mobile life scenarios, complementing real-assistant forms beyond desktop/web workflows.
- [TELEVAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18061): Evaluates spoken language models in realistic Chinese interactive scenarios. Core idea: score both reliable content fulfillment and appropriate interaction strategies so spoken agents are not judged only by task-answer accuracy.
- [SpeechIQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19361): Evaluates voice-understanding models through a speech-agentic intelligence quotient across remembering, understanding, and application levels. Core idea: move beyond word error rate by comparing cascaded and end-to-end voice agents on interpretation, downstream QA, annotation-error detection, and hallucination signals.
- [C3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22968): Evaluates bilingual spoken dialogue models in complex conversations. Core idea: use multi-turn spoken interactions to test whether models preserve context, handle conversation challenges, and respond appropriately across languages.
- [MECAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23511): Evaluates fine-grained audio understanding in audio-language models. Core idea: construct tasks with multi-expert analysis so benchmarks can distinguish detailed, human-like audio comprehension from generic captions or coarse answers.
- [SpeechRole](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02013): Evaluates speech role-playing agents. Core idea: pair a large speech-to-speech role-playing corpus with SpeechRole-Eval so agents are tested on interaction ability, speech expressiveness, and role-playing fidelity rather than text persona consistency alone.
- [SpeechR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02018): Evaluates speech reasoning in large audio-language models. Core idea: test whether models can infer, compare, and reason from spoken audio evidence rather than only transcribe or classify surface speech content.
- [Omni-SafetyBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07173): Evaluates safety of audio-visual large language models under joint modality inputs. Core idea: use parallel modality variations and cross-modal safety-consistency metrics so omni-modal safety is not reduced to text-only or image-only refusal behavior.
- [MSU-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08155): Evaluates spoken-language understanding in conversational multi-talker scenarios. Core idea: organize speaker-centric tasks from static and dynamic speaker attributes to multi-speaker background and interaction understanding, exposing failures hidden by single-speaker audio benchmarks.
- [MMAU-Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13992): Evaluates holistic audio general intelligence across speech, non-speech sounds, and music. Core idea: raise the difficulty and coverage of audio-language evaluation so models are tested on broad auditory understanding rather than isolated speech or music subtasks.
- [MTalk-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18240): Evaluates speech-to-speech models in multi-turn dialogue with arena-style and rubric protocols. Core idea: judge spoken interaction over dialogue trajectories so evaluation covers coherence, turn management, and response quality beyond single-turn speech tasks.
- [AHELM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21376): Provides a holistic evaluation of audio-language models. Core idea: organize audio-centric tasks and metrics into a unified benchmark so speech, sound, and music understanding can be compared under one protocol.
- [AudioMOS Challenge 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01336): Evaluates automatic prediction of audio mean-opinion scores. Core idea: use a shared challenge protocol to compare whether systems can match human quality judgments for generated or processed audio.
- [TTA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02398): Evaluates text-to-audio generation across functional performance, reliability, and social responsibility. Core idea: combine diverse prompts, objective metrics, and large-scale human annotations in one protocol.
- [VoxRole](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03940): Evaluates speech-based role-playing agents. Core idea: move role-playing evaluation beyond text by testing persona consistency, prosody, emotion, and spoken interaction quality in character-driven dialogue.
- [VStyle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09716): Evaluates voice style adaptation from spoken instructions. Core idea: test whether speech systems can interpret vocal style requirements and adapt generated speech beyond transcript-level correctness.
- [MMedFD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19817): Evaluates multi-turn full-duplex automatic speech recognition in real healthcare settings. Core idea: stress streaming medical conversations where overlapping speech, turns, and domain terminology make recognition harder than clean single-speaker ASR.
- [CMDAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22461): Evaluates Chinese multi-scene dynamic audio reasoning. Core idea: use diverse audio scenes and reasoning questions to test whether models understand events, context, and temporal changes in Chinese audio settings.
- [XGC-AVQuiz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23251): Evaluates audio-visual content understanding in real and AI-generated videos. Core idea: cover temporal alignment and cross-modal reasoning across many tasks so models are tested on how audio and visual evidence jointly determine the answer.
- [PodEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00485): Evaluates podcast-like multimodal audio generation. Core idea: pair a real-world podcast dataset with text, speech, and audio evaluation dimensions so long-form open-ended audio generation is judged beyond single-reference answers or ad hoc human ratings.
- [SingMOS-Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01812): Evaluates singing quality assessment. Core idea: provide a benchmark for judging generated or processed singing audio so vocal quality can be compared beyond generic audio scores.
- [Full-Duplex-Bench-v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07838): A multi-turn evaluation framework for full-duplex dialogue systems with an automated examiner. Core idea: extend full-duplex speech evaluation from turn-taking snapshots to longer interaction trajectories that test interruption, overlap handling, and examiner-driven conversational control.
- [MRSAudio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10396): Evaluates spatial audio understanding and generation with multimodal recorded data. Core idea: provide synchronized binaural and ambisonic audio, egocentric and exocentric video, motion trajectories, transcripts, phoneme boundaries, lyrics, scores, and prompts across life, speech, music, and singing scenarios.
- [LISTEN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10444): Evaluates whether audio language models rely on lexical cues or acoustic emotion cues. Core idea: use controlled cue alignment and conflict settings to test whether models genuinely listen to paralinguistic evidence rather than only transcribing speech content.
- [VCB Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11098): Evaluates audio-grounded large language model conversational agents. Core idea: move spoken-dialogue evaluation beyond transcripts by checking whether agents ground multi-turn responses in acoustic evidence.
- [SpeechLLM-as-Judges / SpeechEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14664): evaluates synthetic speech quality with structured, explanation-based SpeechLLM judging. Core idea: use multilingual speech clips and annotated quality dimensions so speech generation can be compared beyond scalar preference scores.
- [VocalBench-DF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15406): Evaluates speech-LLM robustness to disfluent speech. Core idea: use a multi-dimensional disfluency taxonomy, including speech-impairment cases, to test whether spoken-language models remain reliable beyond clean audio inputs.
- [EchoMind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22758): Evaluates empathetic speech language models through interrelated multi-level tasks. Core idea: link spoken-content understanding, vocal-cue perception, emotional reasoning, and empathetic response generation instead of scoring each ability in isolation.
- [MULTI-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00850): Evaluates emotional intelligence in multi-turn spoken dialogue models. Core idea: use interactive conversations to test whether spoken agents understand affective cues, maintain emotional context, and respond appropriately across turns.
- [SpeechJudge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07931): Evaluates speech naturalness judgment with large-scale human preference pairs. Core idea: compare speech and audio LLM judges against human naturalness and intelligibility preferences so synthetic-speech evaluation is not reduced to transcript accuracy or generic audio quality.
- [SACRED-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10222): Evaluates speech-audio compositional attacks against multimodal LLMs. Core idea: combine harmful and benign speech, non-speech audio, and multi-speaker dialogue so audio safety testing covers black-box attacks hidden in complex auditory scenes.
- [MTR-DuplexBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10262): Evaluates full-duplex speech language models in multi-round conversations. Core idea: segment continuous overlapping dialogues into turn-level assessments and test conversational, contextual, and interaction-quality dimensions beyond single-turn speech QA.
- [HPSU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23178): Benchmarks human-level perception in real-world spoken speech understanding. Core idea: evaluate whether speech models capture perceptual cues in natural spoken audio beyond transcript-level semantics.
- [Mandarin Speech Prosody Benchmark](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2025-1873): Evaluates whether AI systems understand Mandarin speech prosody. Core idea: use a dedicated framework and benchmark showcase so prosodic understanding is tested beyond transcript-level Mandarin speech recognition.
- [ICASSP 2026 HumDial Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05564): Evaluates human-like spoken dialogue systems across emotional intelligence and full-duplex interaction tracks. Core idea: use authentic human conversations and shared challenge protocols to test whether spoken agents can combine affective understanding with real-time turn-taking.
- [RSA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10384): Benchmarks audio large models in real-world acoustic scenarios. Core idea: stress auditory understanding under realistic environmental sounds and acoustic conditions rather than clean speech-only inputs.
- [AQUA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12248): Evaluates audio question answering with unanswerable cases. Core idea: test whether audio-language models know when the acoustic evidence does not support an answer rather than forcing a hallucinated response.
- [VoxPrivacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19956): Evaluates interactional privacy in speech language models. Core idea: test whether spoken assistants handle privacy-sensitive conversational situations appropriately, complementing audio benchmarks that focus mainly on recognition, reasoning, or generation quality.
- [LALM-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04796): Benchmarks large audio-language models for safety evaluation in multi-turn spoken dialogues. Core idea: test whether audio-language judges can detect unsafe behavior across spoken interaction trajectories.
- [Aegis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07379): Red-teams governance, integrity, and security risks in AI voice agents. Core idea: model realistic voice-agent deployment pipelines and test adversarial scenarios such as privacy leakage, privilege escalation, and resource abuse in banking, IT support, and logistics settings.
- [AdvBench-Omni](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10161): Evaluates omni-modal safety under cross-modality semantic conflict. Core idea: decouple harmful semantics across modalities so safety evaluation can reveal whether refusal behavior survives when text, image, audio, or video evidence disagree.
- [Interspeech 2026 Audio Reasoning Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14224): Evaluates reasoning-process quality for audio reasoning models and agents. Core idea: use a shared challenge protocol to judge not only final audio-reasoning answers but also the quality of intermediate reasoning behavior.
- [Human or Machine?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24080): Proposes a Turing-test-style evaluation for speech-to-speech interaction. Core idea: compare human and machine spoken interactions directly so naturalness, responsiveness, and conversational human-likeness can be assessed beyond transcript accuracy.
- [PolyBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05128): Evaluates compositional reasoning in polyphonic audio. Core idea: test whether audio-language models can disentangle and reason over overlapping sound events rather than treating audio as a single coarse scene label.
- [PARSA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14456): evaluates Persian audio-language models. Core idea: cover speech understanding, paralinguistic analysis, cultural audio understanding, poetry, traditional music, and code-switching so Persian audio capability is not reduced to translated text evaluation.
- [DEAF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18048): Evaluates acoustic faithfulness in audio language models. Core idea: diagnose whether model outputs are grounded in the actual audio signal, extending hallucination-style evaluation from text and vision into auditory evidence.
- [SID-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24144): Evaluates semantic-aware interruption detection in spoken dialogue systems. Core idea: use real human dialogues and an Average Penalty Time metric to balance false interruptions against late responses in full-duplex interaction.
- [Full-Duplex-Bench-v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04847): Evaluates tool use by full-duplex voice agents under real-world disfluency. Core idea: pair human audio with chained API-call scenarios and score accuracy, latency, and turn-taking so spoken tool agents are tested beyond clean transcript function calling.
- [AudioSafetyBench / AudioGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08867): Evaluates audio-system safety across audio-native harmful events, risky speaker attributes, impersonation, voice-content combinations, and non-speech sounds. Core idea: make audio safety policy-grounded and measurable instead of reducing voice-agent safety to unsafe text spoken aloud.
- [Jamendo-MT-QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09721): A benchmark for multi-track comparative music question answering, extending audio/music evaluation from generation quality to structured listening and comparison.
- [HumDial-EIBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11594): Evaluates emotional intelligence in human-recorded multi-turn audio dialogue. Core idea: use spoken interaction rather than transcript-only text to test whether audio language models recognize and respond to affective cues across turns.
- [NVBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16211): Evaluates speech synthesis with non-verbal vocalizations. Core idea: test whether speech-generation systems handle laughs, sighs, breaths, and other non-verbal vocal events instead of scoring only ordinary spoken content.
- [VIBE / Voice-Induced Bias Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17248): Evaluates open-ended bias in large audio-language models through real-world speech. Core idea: test whether vocal cues and spoken delivery change model judgments or responses, extending bias evaluation beyond text-only prompts.
- [MINT-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17958): Evaluates multilingual instruction following in text-to-speech systems. Core idea: test whether speech generation follows content, style, language, and control instructions across languages rather than only producing intelligible audio.
- [SpeechParaling-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20842): evaluates paralinguistic-aware speech generation. Core idea: make non-verbal speech factors such as prosody, emotion, and speaker-style control measurable instead of judging generated speech only by generic quality scores.
- [SongBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25937): A fine-grained, multi-aspect benchmark for song quality assessment, complementing music-generation evaluation with more detailed quality dimensions.
- [VoxDialogue](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b20adb0a2bd79dc0ec89e8a07417c0e3ccd1297): Evaluates whether spoken dialogue systems understand information beyond word transcripts. Core idea: test multi-turn spoken dialogue understanding with acoustic and paralinguistic cues so audio-language models are judged on speaker state and interaction evidence rather than transcript semantics alone.
- [MedMosaic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00969): evaluates diverse medical-audio understanding. Core idea: use a large-scale medical audio benchmark to test whether audio-language models can handle clinically relevant acoustic evidence rather than only general speech or music.
- [Massive Sound Embedding Benchmark (MSEB)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04556): Benchmarks LLMs and audio models on sound-embedding tasks. Core idea: evaluate whether learned audio representations transfer across sound understanding settings, adding an embedding-quality view beyond speech QA or audio generation.
- [Expressive Appropriateness of Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09413): Evaluates whether generated speech is expressively appropriate in rich contexts. Core idea: judge prosody, affect, and delivery against contextual expectations rather than scoring speech quality only with generic naturalness or intelligibility metrics.
- [Fine-Grained Multi-Dimensional Speech Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12036): evaluates speech understanding across fine-grained dimensions. Core idea: pair a data pipeline, benchmark tasks, and model comparisons so audio-language models are tested beyond transcription-like understanding.
