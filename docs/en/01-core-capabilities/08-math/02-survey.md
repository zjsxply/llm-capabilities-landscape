# 1.8.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Deep Learning for Theorem Proving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09939): Covers formal systems, proof search, datasets, and theorem-proving evaluation.
- [A Survey of Mathematical Reasoning in the Era of Multimodal Large Language Model: Benchmark, Method and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11936): Reviews diagram-, chart-, and image-grounded mathematical reasoning.
- [A Survey on Mathematical Reasoning and Optimization with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17726): Surveys mathematical reasoning, optimization, benchmarks, and tool-augmented methods.
- [Autoformalization in the Era of Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23486): Explains the bridge from natural-language mathematics to checker-verifiable formal proof.
- [A Survey on Large Language Models for Mathematical Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08446): A recent overview of LLM mathematical reasoning methods, datasets, and open problems.
- [Solving Mathematical Problems using Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.3724/2096-7004.di.2025.0064): Organizes LLM math solving as a pipeline of prompting, decomposition, verification, tool use, and answer synthesis.
- [Machine-Assisted Proof](https://doi.org/10.1090/noti3041): Reviews machine-assisted proof as a mathematical reasoning workflow spanning formal tools and AI assistance.
