# 2.4 Deep Research

> Parent section: 2. Basic Agents


## 2.4.1 Leaderboard

- [Deep Research Bench Leaderboard](https://drb.futuresearch.ai/): FutureSearch's official continuous leaderboard covering both DRB and BTF-2; useful for tracking end-to-end web research capability under reproducible frozen corpora and long-trajectory auditing.
- [DeepResearch Bench Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/muset-ai/DeepResearch-Bench-Leaderboard): The official Hugging Face leaderboard for DeepResearch Bench; useful for comparing deep research agents by `RACE` report quality and `FACT` citation credibility.
- [LiveResearchBench Leaderboard](https://livedeepresearch.github.io/): The LiveResearchBench project page includes a real-time deep research leaderboard; useful for observing agent differences under dynamic webpages, user-centered tasks, and citation-link evaluation.
- [Deep Research Arena](https://www.deepresearcharena.com/): A public deep research battle and leaderboard site; useful for tracking ongoing performance of productized deep research systems, open-source harnesses, and commercial agents.
- [Deep Research SOTA on BrowseComp-ZH](https://www.wizwand.com/sota/deep-research-on-browsecomp-zh-score): A third-party SOTA aggregation page; useful for tracking public results of deep-search / deep-research agents such as WebThinker, BrowseMaster, InfoSeeker, and ReSum along BrowseComp-ZH.

## 2.4.2 Bench

- [ResearchArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10291): Evaluates academic research and survey generation; core idea: turn "retrieve literature -> compare methods -> write a structured survey" into a scoreable research benchmark.
- [ResearchRubrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02077) ([code](https://github.com/scaleapi/researchrubrics)): Evaluates the quality of research-oriented long-form outputs; core idea: use rubrics to make dimensions such as coverage, evidence, citations, reproducibility, structure, and writing quality explicit and comparable.
- [BrowseComp-ZH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19314) ([code](https://github.com/PALIN2018/BrowseComp-ZH); [Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/PALIN2018/BrowseComp-ZH); [Deep Research SOTA on BrowseComp-ZH](https://www.wizwand.com/sota/deep-research-on-browsecomp-zh-score)): Evaluates difficult web browsing and multi-hop retrieval reasoning in the Chinese internet environment; core idea: explicitly benchmark platform fragmentation, cross-page retrieval, and information synthesis in the Chinese web ecosystem, and it is often used as a SOTA comparison for deep-search/deep-research agents.
- [Deep Research Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06287) ([Leaderboard](https://drb.futuresearch.ai/)): Evaluates reproducible web-research agents; core idea: use 89 multi-step web research tasks and a frozen `RetroSearch` web corpus to avoid live-web drift while auditing tool use, forgetting, and hallucination in long trajectories.
- [DeepResearch Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763) ([homepage](https://deepresearch-bench.github.io/); [code](https://github.com/Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench)): Evaluates deep research agents' long-chain retrieval, evidence organization, and report-generation capability across 22 domains; core idea: combine `RACE` and `FACT` protocols to measure report quality and citation credibility respectively.
- [Characterizing Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04183): Evaluates broad conceptual exploration in deep research; core idea: distinguish deep research from ordinary long-form QA in the formal definition, emphasizing high fan-out, cross-concept retrieval, and reasoning-intensive information exploration.
- [BrowseComp-VL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05748): A supplementary sub-benchmark for multimodal deep research; core idea: include visual web evidence in deep research trajectory synthesis and evaluation.
- [BrowseComp-Plus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06600) ([code](https://github.com/texttron/BrowseComp-Plus)): Evaluates deep research agents over a fixed corpus. Core idea: derive a controllable document collection, human-verified supporting documents, and hard negatives from BrowseComp so researchers can separate retrieval quality, citation accuracy, and agent context engineering.
- [ReportBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15804) ([code](https://github.com/ByteDance-BandAI/ReportBench)): Evaluates the quality of academic survey reports generated by deep research agents; core idea: reverse-engineer tasks from expert surveys and separately check cited-paper relevance, citation support, and correctness of uncited facts.
- [DeepScholar-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20033) ([code](https://github.com/guestrin-lab/deepscholar)): Evaluates generative academic research synthesis; core idea: extract tasks from recent high-quality arXiv papers, requiring systems to retrieve, synthesize, and cite related work while automatically measuring knowledge synthesis, retrieval quality, and verifiability.
- [DeepTRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04499): Evaluates evidence-tracing reliability in deep research and generative search systems; core idea: decompose answers, sources, and citations into statement-level support matrices to audit whether conclusions are actually supported by cited evidence.
- [DRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00172) ([code](https://github.com/ServiceNow/drbench), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ServiceNow/drbench)): Evaluates enterprise deep research. Core idea: require agents to retrieve evidence across public webpages, private files, emails, chats, and productivity tools, then generate cited reports; explicitly distinct from Dr. Bench.
- [Dr. Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02190): Evaluates deep research agents across dimensions from short answers to full reports; core idea: cover task decomposition, cross-source retrieval, multi-stage reasoning, information integration, and structured output simultaneously, avoiding final-answer-only scoring.
- [LiveResearchBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14240) ([homepage](https://livedeepresearch.github.io/); [code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/LiveResearchBench)): Evaluates user-centered deep research in real dynamic web environments; core idea: use live, user-centric, multi-faceted tasks and `DeepEval` multi-protocol evaluation to explicitly test real-time retrieval, synthesis analysis, and citation-link quality.
- [Deep Research Arena](https://www.deepresearcharena.com/): What it evaluates: public deep research battles and leaderboard-style comparisons. Core idea: compare productized deep research systems, open-source harnesses, and commercial agents in a continuous arena entry point, complementing paper benchmarks where coverage of deployed systems is limited.
- [DeepWideSearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20168): Evaluates the simultaneous satisfaction of "deep multi-hop reasoning" and "wide-area information collection" in agentic information seeking; core idea: require agents to reason over multi-hop retrieval paths among many candidate pieces of information, exposing failure modes such as insufficient reflection, insufficient retrieval, and context overflow.
- [DEER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17776): Evaluates expert-level report generation by deep research agents; core idea: use expert report tasks to jointly evaluate report quality, domain expertise, and report-wide claim verification, reducing bias from scoring only style or local citations.
- [DeepSynth-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03540): Evaluates post-retrieval information consolidation in deep survey writing. Core idea: provide oracle contexts and checklist-style objective scoring so synthesis quality can be separated from retrieval quality.
- [Over-Searching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05503): Evaluates the opposite failure mode of premature stopping in search-augmented agents. Core idea: measure unnecessary search, noisy evidence uptake, and the cost-quality tradeoff through search-efficiency metrics.
- [IDRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06676): Evaluates interactive deep research agents; core idea: unfold the research process into multi-turn retrieval, plan updates, and evidence synthesis, emphasizing process quality rather than only the final draft.
- [Video Deep Research Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06943): What it evaluates: video deep research on the open web. Core idea: require agents to watch, retrieve, and reason over video evidence, evaluating the closed loop of long-video understanding, web search, and cross-modal evidence integration.
- [DeepResearch Bench II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08536) ([code](https://github.com/imlrz/DeepResearch-Bench-II)): What it evaluates: rubric diagnosis for deep research reports. Core idea: decompose expert reports into verifiable items and directly inspect information retrieval, analysis, and presentation.
- [DeepResearchEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09688): Evaluates automated task construction and agentic evaluation for deep research. Core idea: generate persona-driven tasks and actively fact-check reports even when citations are missing, weak, or unreliable.
- [DR-Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10504): Evaluates deep research agents through dynamic trend-grounded tasks. Core idea: build information trees from recent topics and adaptively escalate task difficulty, distinct from the public Deep Research Arena battle website.
- [MMDeepResearch-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12346): Evaluates multimodal deep research agents. Core idea: require image-text evidence collection, long-form report synthesis, citation alignment, and visual-integrity checks in one benchmark.
- [TaxoBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12369): Evaluates whether deep research agents can retrieve and organize a field. Core idea: compare retrieved papers and taxonomy trees against expert taxonomies rather than only judging report prose.
- [Mr Dre](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13217): Evaluates multi-turn report revision for deep research agents. Core idea: measure whether agents can incorporate user feedback while preserving citation support and avoiding regression in already-correct sections.
- [DeepSearchQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20975): Evaluates difficult multi-step information-seeking for deep research agents. Core idea: use many-field exhaustive-answer tasks to test whether agents search broadly enough, manage long evidence chains, and avoid stopping after a partial answer.
- [ScholarGym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21654): What it evaluates: the information-gathering stage of deep research. Core idea: separately benchmark the "find materials before researching" front half, directly testing whether agents can locate, filter, and organize high-value sources.
- [Deep Research Hallucination Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22984): What it evaluates: hallucination across complete deep research trajectories. Core idea: audit not only factual errors in the final report, but also how hallucinations arise and propagate along search, reading, notes, and citation chains.
- [Wiki Live Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01590): What it evaluates: expert-level Wikipedia-entry-style deep research. Core idea: use dynamic encyclopedia-entry needs to force broad retrieval, evidence synthesis, and citable writing, reducing static QA leakage.
- [Vision-DeepResearch Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02185) ([code](https://github.com/Osilly/Vision-DeepResearch)): What it evaluates: deep research capability with mixed visual and text search. Core idea: place visual web evidence, image retrieval, and text evidence into the same deep research task to diagnose whether multimodal search truly improves report quality.
- [When Is Enough Not Enough?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07549): What it evaluates: premature stopping and false sense of completion in search agents. Core idea: construct tasks where the answer appears to have been found but key evidence is still missing, evaluating when agents continue searching and when they stop.
- [DRACO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11685): Evaluates deep research accuracy, completeness, and objectivity on cross-domain user-style research questions. Core idea: grade final reports with expert-written rubrics that separately score factuality, breadth and depth, citation support, and presentation.
- [DREAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18940): Evaluates deep research with agentic metrics. Core idea: make the evaluator itself tool-using and adaptive so temporal validity, factual correctness, and task-specific evidence can be checked beyond static rubrics.
- [DEEPSYNTH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21143) ([code](https://github.com/agentdeepsynthesis/deepsynth-bench)): Evaluates deep cross-source information synthesis; core idea: use 120 real time-consuming tasks across 7 domains, requiring agents to collect official data sources, form hypotheses, conduct structured reasoning, and produce verifiable insights.
- [TRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21230): What it evaluates: trajectory-level comprehensive evaluation of deep research agents. Core idea: use trajectory-aware metrics to jointly assess correctness, evidence quality, efficiency, and reasoning robustness, avoiding the high-score illusion caused by final scores alone.
- [DeepConsult](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21658): Evaluates long-chain research and report generation for "consulting-style questions"; core idea: constrain open-ended research into scoreable long-form output and process norms.
- [Super Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00582): What it evaluates: extremely broad and deep research on complex questions. Core idea: isolate problems requiring large-scale evidence collection, long-term planning, and heterogeneous-source synthesis, covering the intersection of Deep Research and Wide Search.
- [DeepFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05912): Evaluates factuality in deep research reports. Core idea: co-evolve benchmark items and auditing agents so claim-level labels remain revisable and evidence-backed rather than fixed once.
- [MyScholarQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16120): Evaluates personalized deep research with real users. Core idea: test whether agents infer individual research preferences and information needs instead of optimizing only task-generic report quality.
- [TRQA (Total Recall QA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18516): Evaluates fact retrieval and synthesis by deep research systems under long evidence chains; core idea: make "whether retrieval is complete and citations are accurate" explicit through high-recall requirements.
- [MiroEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28407): What it evaluates: process and results of deep research systems. Core idea: evaluate final reports, fact verification, and process auditing together.
- [Reference Hallucination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03173): Evaluates fabricated, invalid, and stale references in commercial LLMs and deep research agents. Core idea: separate broken links from invented citations and provide tooling for large-scale URL-validity audits.
- [Towards Knowledgeable Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07720): What it evaluates: frameworks and evaluation for knowledge-enhanced deep research. Core idea: put external knowledge organization, retrieval, and report generation into one protocol, distinguishing "can search" from "can form knowledge structures".
- [PaperScope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11307): Evaluates agentic deep research across massive scientific papers. Core idea: use a knowledge graph of more than 2,000 AI papers and multi-modal evidence from text, tables, and figures to test retrieval, reasoning, summarization, and problem solving across coherent multi-paper sets.
- [DR3-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14683) ([code](https://github.com/NJU-LINK/DR3-Eval)): What it evaluates: realistic, multimodal, reproducible deep research evaluation. Core idea: use user files, a static sandbox, and fine-grained rubrics.
- [Cited but Not Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06635): Evaluates source attribution in deep research reports. Core idea: parse Markdown citations reproducibly, then score link validity, relevance, and factual support at report scale.
- [ViDR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13034): What it evaluates: alignment between multimodal deep research reports and visual evidence. Core idea: require report conclusions to point back to specific visual sources, reducing multimodal hallucination where a page is cited but visual evidence does not support the claim.
## 2.4.3 Agent Harness

- [STORM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14207) ([code](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm); [project page](http://storm.genie.stanford.edu)): A retrieval-grounded long-form writing harness that asks multi-perspective questions, builds an outline, and drafts cited reports; it is an important precursor to modern deep-research agents.
- [Co-STORM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15232) ([code](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm); [project page](http://storm.genie.stanford.edu)): A collaborative exploratory-research harness with agent conversations, user steering, a dynamic mind map, and cited report output.
- [Agent Laboratory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04227) ([code](https://github.com/SamuelSchmidgall/AgentLaboratory)): An end-to-end research assistant workflow that starts from a human research idea and covers literature review, experimentation, and report writing.
- [DeepResearcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03160) ([code](https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/DeepResearcher); trains a deep research agent with end-to-end reinforcement learning in a real web-search environment, explicitly including multi-agent browsing, cross-validation, and self-reflection)
- [WebThinker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21776) ([code](https://github.com/RUC-NLPIR/WebThinker); interleaves "reasoning-search-writing" into a single long-chain `think-search-draft` workflow)
- [WebDancer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22648) ([code](https://github.com/Alibaba-NLP/DeepResearch/tree/main/WebAgent/WebDancer); a training-led but systemically clear information-retrieval web agent)
- [OWL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23885) ([code](https://github.com/camel-ai/owl); explicitly decomposes deep research into a role-based `retrieval -> verification -> writing` pipeline)
- [Deep Cognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15759) (code: no stable public repository found; a transparent, interruptible multi-agent deep research system; core idea: expose fine-grained human steering and collaboration points during research instead of treating deep research as a sealed input-output process)
- [WebWatcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05748) ([code](https://github.com/Alibaba-NLP/DeepResearch/tree/main/WebAgent/WebWatcher); a vision-language agent for multimodal deep research that also proposes BrowseComp-VL)
- [WebWeaver](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13312) ([code](https://github.com/Alibaba-NLP/DeepResearch/tree/main/WebAgent/WebWeaver); a `planner + writer` dual-agent system that manages long-report context with dynamic outlines and an evidence memory bank)
- [Flash-Searcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25301) ([code](https://github.com/OPPO-PersonalAI/Flash-Searcher); turns a serial pipeline into DAG-based parallel execution and schedules dynamically by dependency)
- [FlowSearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08521) ([code](https://github.com/InternScience/InternAgent); flow-orchestration-driven integrated search and writing)
- [BrowserAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10666) ([code](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/BrowserAgent); connects real browser action spaces to the agent and uses explicit memory to support long-horizon web tasks)
- DRBA / DRBench Baseline Agent ([code](https://github.com/ServiceNow/drbench); no independent paper; the enterprise deep research baseline agent for DRBench, built around retrieving evidence from enterprise private materials, public webpages, messages, and productivity tools, then organizing evidence and generating cited reports)
- [Enterprise Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17797) ([code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/enterprise-deep-research); a multi-agent deep research system implementation for enterprise research scenarios)
- [Tongyi Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24701) ([code](https://github.com/Alibaba-NLP/DeepResearch); an integrated open-source implementation of an end-to-end deep research agent)
- [Dingtalk DeepResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24760) (code: not public; a deep research agent for enterprise office scenarios, emphasizing workflow embedding and collaborative-office integration)
- [IterResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07327) ([code](https://github.com/Chen-GX/IterResearch); a long-horizon deep research agent; core idea: use multi-round interaction scaling and state reconstruction to avoid noise pollution and context suffocation from a single ever-growing context)
- [RhinoInsight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18743) (code: not public; a research agent oriented toward enterprise intelligence insights)
- [Step-DeepResearch Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20491) ([code](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/StepDeepResearch); an end-to-end training route and evaluation supplement for open-ended deep research, including checklist-style judging)
- [Self-Manager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17879) (code: not public; delegates multi-agent thread scheduling itself to an agent)
- [Yunque DeepResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19578) ([code](https://github.com/Tencent-BAC/YunqueAgent); Tencent's end-to-end deep research agent)
- FutureSearch ReAct Agent ([code](https://github.com/futuresearch/futuresearch-python); no independent arXiv paper; FutureSearch's open-source web research agent implementation cited in the BTF-2 paper, including tools, time management, and a ReAct-style system prompt, suitable for reproducible experimental deep research / forecasting harnesses)
- [FS-Researcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01566) ([code](https://github.com/Ignoramus0817/FS-Researcher); a staged retrieval and drafting research agent)
- [IntentRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03468) (code: no stable public repository found; trains proactive intent-clarification behavior before open-ended deep research, adding a user-interaction gate before expensive long-horizon search)
- [AgentCPM-Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06540) ([code](https://github.com/OpenBMB/AgentCPM/tree/main/AgentCPM-Report); a Chinese long-report generation specialized agent)
- [Table-as-Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06724) (code: no stable public repository found; tabularizes long-horizon information search; core idea: have the agent maintain fields to fill, evidence cells, and gaps, unifying deep search, wide search, and deep-wide search)
- [W&D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07359) (code: no stable public repository found; Wide-and-Deep parallel tool-calling harness; core idea: horizontally call search/reading tools in parallel within a single agent step to improve source coverage and validation redundancy for BrowseComp-style tasks)
- [DualGraph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13830) (code: no stable public repository found; separates knowledge exploration from outline planning through two co-evolving graphs, reducing the tendency to mix source discovery and report-structure decisions)
- [Search More, Think Less (SMTL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22675) ([code](https://github.com/OPPO-PersonalAI/SMTL); a long-horizon deep research/search agent; core idea: prioritize retrieval breadth and evidence coverage, then synthesize with lighter reasoning to reduce long-chain cost)
- [MiroFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22808) ([code](https://github.com/MiroMindAI/MiroFlow); flowchart-style multi-stage research orchestration)
- [Hyper-Search (MM-DeepResearch)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01050) ([code](https://github.com/HJYao00/MM-DeepResearch); a multimodal evidence-integration deep research agent; the link has previously been unstable)
- [SynPlanResearch-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07853) ([code](https://github.com/HansiZeng/syn-plan-research); trains tool exploration with synthetic plans; core idea: first generate an executable research plan, then constrain retrieval, reading, and writing trajectories with the plan)
- [MiroThinker-1.7 & H1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15726) ([code](https://github.com/MiroMindAI/MiroThinker); an open-source deep research agent; core idea: interaction scaling plus verification-style reasoning)
- [OpenResearcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20278) ([code](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/OpenResearcher); trains an open deep research agent with a 15M-document offline corpus, `search/open/find` primitives, and synthetic long trajectories, emphasizing a reproducible offline search-writing loop)
- [Marco DeepResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28376) (code: not confirmed public; a verification-centric deep research agent whose core idea is adding explicit verification to QA synthesis, trajectory construction, and test-time scaling so long-horizon search errors are caught before they propagate into the final report)
- [Trustworthy Report Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05952) (code: no stable public repository found; a deep research agent with progressive confidence estimation and calibration; core idea: continuously maintain evidence confidence during report generation to reduce unstable citations and overconfident conclusions)
- [DataSTORM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06474) (code: not confirmed public; a structured-data deep research agent whose core idea is to combine exploratory data analysis, web evidence, thesis generation, and data storytelling so agents can research large databases rather than only unstructured webpages)
- [Deep-Reporter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10741) (code: no stable public repository found; a grounded multimodal long-form generation harness; core idea: combine multimodal search and filtering, checklist-guided incremental synthesis, and recurrent context management)
- [ARIS / Auto-Research-In-Sleep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03042) ([code](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep); [intro](https://wanshuiyin.github.io/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/ARIS_INTRO.html)): A skill-based autonomous research harness with cross-model adversarial review, persistent research wiki, literature and experiment workflows, citation/claim audits, and Codex/OpenClaw/Claude Code portability.
- [LongSeeker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05191) (code: not public; a long-horizon search/deep research agent; core idea: use Context-ReAct to dynamically compress, roll back, excerpt, and delete working memory, validating long-chain retrieval gains on BrowseComp and BrowseComp-ZH)
- [AgentDisCo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11732) (code: no stable public repository found; a disentangled collaborative deep-research architecture; core idea: separate critic-driven outline/query refinement from generator-driven retrieval and outline revision, then use meta-optimization to discover reusable research-agent design strategies)
- [Argus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16217): An evidence-assembly deep research agent; core idea: Searcher collects evidence while Navigator maintains an evidence graph and schedules parallel searches.
- Onyx ([code](https://github.com/onyx-dot-app/onyx); no arXiv paper; an open-source enterprise AI platform with built-in Deep Research, multi-step search, RAG, MCP, and Code Interpreter)
- LangChain Open Deep Research ([code](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research); no arXiv paper; LangChain's official open-source deep research system implementation)
- AI-Q NVIDIA Research Assistant ([code](https://github.com/NVIDIA-AI-Blueprints/aiq); no arXiv paper; a fixed research loop of "plan -> parallel retrieval -> writing -> reflective supplementary retrieval -> human intervention")
- DeerFlow ([code](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow); no arXiv paper; abstracts deep research into a reusable super-agent harness and emphasizes decoupling task branches)
- Spring AI Alibaba DeepResearch ([code](https://github.com/spring-ai-alibaba/deepresearch); no arXiv paper; a Spring ecosystem integrated version of deep research)
- CellCog ([public SDK](https://github.com/CellCog/cellcog_python); no arXiv paper; a hosted multi-agent platform and Python SDK supporting agent teams and research-cog)

- [Deep-Research-skills](https://github.com/Weizhena/Deep-Research-skills) (a multi-skill deep research suite: outline, field expansion, parallel research, and final report)
- [deep-research](https://github.com/wshuyi/deep-research/tree/main/skills/deep-research) (a Chinese procedural deep research skill emphasizing fact layering and retention of intermediate artifacts)
- [ARIS research skills](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/tree/main/skills) (paper: [ARIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03042); examples on skills.sh: [research-pipeline](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/research-pipeline), [research-lit](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/research-lit), [citation-audit](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/citation-audit)) cover literature search, multi-source retrieval, research-wiki memory, novelty checks, cross-model review, and citation/claim audits, so they are closer to an executable research harness than a single report prompt.
- [parallel-deep-research](https://skills.sh/parallel-web/parallel-agent-skills/parallel-deep-research) is suitable for deep research workflows where source discovery and reading can be parallelized before final synthesis.
- [firecrawl-deep-research](https://skills.sh/firecrawl/firecrawl-workflows/firecrawl-deep-research) is suitable for retrieval-to-report workflows built on provider-backed crawling and content extraction.
- [doublecheck](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot/tree/main/skills/doublecheck) is useful for claim extraction, source search, and support/contradiction checking, complementing citation-reliability benchmarks such as Reference Hallucination Detection and Cited but Not Verified.
- [search-layer](https://github.com/blessonism/openclaw-search-skills/tree/main/search-layer) (more of a low-level retrieval layer for deep research: multi-source parallel search, deduplication, ranking, and citation-chain tracking)
- [last30days-skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill) and [last30days-skill-cn](https://github.com/Jesseovo/last30days-skill-cn) are recent-source discovery skills for researching the last month across social platforms, news, communities, and the web; they are useful when deep research needs freshness and cross-platform trend coverage before longer synthesis.
- [deep-research-pro](https://clawhub.ai/parags/deep-research-pro) (a commercial-style long-report template)
- [deepresearchwork](https://clawhub.ai/jiacode/deepresearchwork) (a research template with explicit task decomposition)
- [in-depth-research](https://clawhub.ai/ivangdavila/in-depth-research) (a long-report template based on deep follow-up questioning)
