# 2.5.2 Survey

- [Survey of User Interface Design and Interaction Techniques in Generative AI Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22370): Maps recent work and open issues for computer use gui capability coverage.
- [Foundations and Recent Trends in Multimodal Mobile Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02006): Surveys recent methods, risks, and open problems for Computer Use GUI.
- [GUI Agents with Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04890): GUI Agents with Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey surveys computer use gui methods, taxonomy, evidence, or practice.
- [Fine-tuning Large Language Models with Limited Data: A Survey and Practical Guide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09539): Surveys GUI, mobile, screen, or web-navigation agent capability, adding a recent map of methods, benchmarks, risks, or open problems.
- [GUI Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13501): Reviews perception, grounding, planning, action spaces, benchmarks, and failures across interfaces.
- [Vision-Based Multimodal Interfaces: A Survey and Taxonomy for Enhanced Context-Aware System Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13443): Taxonomizes vision-based multimodal interfaces for context-aware system design.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Agents for Computer Use: Foundations, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16150): Surveys environments, interaction loops, memory, safety, and evaluation for computer-use agents.
- [Towards Computer-Using Personal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15515): Surveys, reviews, or taxonomizes computer-use GUI agents methods, risks, datasets, or evaluation practices.
- [Towards Trustworthy GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23434): Uses trustworthiness as the organizing lens for GUI agents, spanning perception, grounding, planning, action safety, and evaluation.
- [A Survey on (M)LLM-Based GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13865): Maps UI grounding, action prediction, model capabilities, and system challenges.
- [A Survey on GUI Agents with Foundation Models Enhanced by Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20464): Surveys how reinforcement learning enhances foundation-model GUI agents.
- [A Survey on the Safety and Security Threats of Computer-Using Agents: JARVIS or Ultron?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10924): Reviews risks from GUI action, cross-application execution, privacy exposure, and tool misuse.
- [OS Agents: A Survey on MLLM-based Agents for General Computing Devices Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04482): Focuses on general computing-device operation, observation, action, and evaluation.
- [Next Generation XR Systems—Large Language Models Meet Augmented and Virtual Reality](https://doi.org/10.1109/mcg.2025.3548554): Surveys computer use and GUI interaction methods, evaluation gaps, or deployment issues.
- [LLM Integration in Extended Reality: A Comprehensive Review of Current Trends, Challenges, and Future Perspectives](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714224): Surveys computer use and GUI interaction methods, evaluation gaps, or deployment issues.
