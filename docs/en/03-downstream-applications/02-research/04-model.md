# 3.2.4 Model

- [Spark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20090): Combines retrieval-augmented scientific idea generation with Judge, a reviewer model trained on 600K OpenReview reviews, to provide a task-specific scientific-review signal.
- [REMOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11718): Uses reasoning LLMs and multi-objective reinforcement learning to generate peer reviews aligned with multiple review-quality dimensions.
- [SciGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08032): Adapts Qwen3 for scientific literature understanding through domain distillation, sparse MoE attention for long documents, and ontology-aware knowledge adaptation.
- [AI-Noether](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23004): Introduces an abductive-inference system that proposes axiom-system corrections to align AI-derived scientific laws with canonical theory.
- [NAIPv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25179): Trains a debiased pairwise paper-quality estimator from domain-year comparison groups and reviewer-tendency signals.
- [Scientific novelty via knowledge recombination and propagation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2025.129794): Uses graph structure and LLM analysis to score scientific novelty through knowledge recombination and propagation.
- [Transformer-Based Novelty Scoring](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISMSIT67332.2025.11267836): Uses a transformer pipeline to score research-idea novelty, fitting the model-side layer of AI-assisted ideation and review.
- [A unified multimodal understanding and generation model for cross-disciplinary scientific research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01363): Introduces a unified multimodal model aimed at scientific understanding and generation across disciplines.
- [Reward Modeling for Scientific Writing Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11374): Trains reward models for scientific-writing evaluation, targeting expert-domain requirements beyond generic writing quality.
- [EchoReview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00733): Learns automated peer review signals from citation echoes, addressing scarcity and single-source bias in review supervision.
- [TableTale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22908): Models the narrative relationship between tables and surrounding text in scientific papers.
- [AI Can Learn Scientific Taste](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14473): Formalizes scientific taste as preference modeling and trains Scientific Judge from community feedback, then trains Scientific Thinker with that judge as a reward model.
- [LLMs learn scientific taste from institutional traces across the social sciences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16659): Fine-tunes LLM evaluators on field-specific publication outcomes to rank research pitches in low-verifiability social-science domains.
- [Cicq](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-026-05594-1): Combines citation impact and content-quality signals for automated literature evaluation, complementing scientific-taste and paper-quality models.
- [ScilinkBERT](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-025-07838-y): Adapts a BERT-style language model to scientific texts with citation context for literature understanding.
