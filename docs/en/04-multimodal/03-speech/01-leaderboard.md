# 4.3.1 Leaderboard

- [AudioBench Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/AudioLLMs/AudioBench-Leaderboard-Extend): A public leaderboard for audio-language model capability evaluation.
- [Open ASR Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard): A public recognition-focused leaderboard for speech models.
- [Open Universal Arabic ASR Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/elmresearchcenter/open_universal_arabic_asr_leaderboard) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13788)): A continuous Arabic ASR leaderboard reporting WER and CER across multidialect test sets, with robustness, speaker adaptation, inference efficiency, and memory-use comparisons.
- [HELM Audio](https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/audio/latest/): Public HELM audio results for broad audio-model comparison.
- [SpeechColab Leaderboard](https://github.com/SpeechColab/Leaderboard): An open-source ASR benchmarking platform with public ranking tables and reproducible evaluation scripts.
- [SUPERB Leaderboard](https://superbbenchmark.github.io/): The official leaderboard for universal speech representation evaluation across the SUPERB task suite.
- [Dynamic-SUPERB Leaderboard](https://dynamicsuperb-leaderboard.static.hf.space/index.html): A public leaderboard for Dynamic-SUPERB instruction-following speech tasks.
- [ML-SUPERB 2.0 Leaderboard](https://multilingual.superbbenchmark.org/leaderboard): The official multilingual ASR and language-identification challenge leaderboard.
- [VoicePrivacy 2024 Results](https://www.voiceprivacychallenge.org/vp2024/): Official results and system-description page for the VoicePrivacy 2024 Challenge.
- [DCASE 2024 Task 4 Results](https://dcase.community/challenge2024/task-sound-event-detection-with-heterogeneous-training-dataset-and-potentially-missing-labels-results): Official team and system rankings for heterogeneous-data sound-event detection.
- [DCASE 2025 Task 4 Results](https://dcase.community/challenge2025/task-spatial-semantic-segmentation-of-sound-scenes-results): Official rankings for spatial semantic segmentation of sound scenes.
- [URGENT 2025 Leaderboard](https://urgent-challenge.github.io/urgent2025/leaderboard/): Official leaderboard for the Interspeech 2025 URGENT speech-enhancement challenge.
- [SVDD 2024 Results](https://www.music-ir.org/mirex/wiki/2024:Singing_Voice_Deepfake_Detection_Results): MIREX-hosted official results for the Singing Voice Deepfake Detection Challenge.
- [MMAU Leaderboard](https://sakshi113.github.io/mmau_homepage/): Public leaderboard for massive multi-task audio understanding and reasoning across sound, music, and speech.
