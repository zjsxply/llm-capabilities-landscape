# 2.11.4 Model

- [RT-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15818): A field-defining vision-language-action model line that transfers web-scale vision-language knowledge to robotic control by representing robot actions as model outputs.
- [Octo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12213): An open-source generalist robot policy trained across robot embodiments and tasks, providing a pre-OpenVLA open-policy anchor.
- [OpenVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246): Trains a 7B open-source VLA on 970K real-world robot demonstrations and releases checkpoints, fine-tuning notebooks, and training code.
- [pi0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24164): A vision-language-action flow model for general robot control, making flow matching a central VLA action-generation design.
- [FAST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09747): Proposes efficient action tokenization for VLA models, improving how continuous robot actions are represented and generated by language-model backbones.
- [GeoManip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09783): Uses LLM-derived geometric constraints and scene parsing as a training-free interface for language-conditioned robot manipulation.
- [DexVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05855): Adds a diffusion action expert to a vision-language model for general robot control and uses embodiment-aware training to improve long-horizon manipulation.
- [Video2Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09886): Reconstructs manipulation tasks from internet videos, uses LLM-generated rewards to train policies in simulation, and transfers the resulting skills to robots.
- [Humanoid-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14795): Aligns language, egocentric vision, and whole-body motion control so humanoid robots can interact with objects and explore environments under natural-language goals.
- [DemoGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16932): Turns a single demonstration into spatially augmented synthetic demonstrations, improving data-efficient visuomotor policy learning across manipulation configurations.
- [Hi Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19417): Introduces Open-Ended Instruction Following with Hierarchical Vision-Language-Action Models, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [ROCKET-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02505): Steers visuomotor policies through cross-view goal alignment for embodied action control.
- [SICNav-Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08858): Couples diffusion-based crowd-trajectory prediction with bilevel model-predictive control for safe, interaction-aware robot navigation.
- [HybridVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10631): Integrates autoregressive and diffusion action prediction in one VLA model, using collaborative training and action ensembling for robot manipulation.
- [GR00T N1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14734): An open foundation model for generalist humanoid robots, extending VLA-style training to humanoid manipulation and whole-body action settings.
- [Sim-and-Real Co-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24361): Studies how to mix simulation and real data for vision-based robotic manipulation across robot-arm and humanoid settings.
- [Unified World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02792): Introduces Coupling Video and Action Diffusion for Pretraining on Large Robotic Datasets, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [MAPLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06084): Encodes dexterous robotic-manipulation priors learned from egocentric videos for embodied policy learning.
- [Two by Two](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06961): Learns generalizable robot assembly across daily object pairs, connecting geometric alignment and functional relations in manipulation policies.
- [pi0.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16054): Extends VLA models toward open-world generalization for practical real-world robot tasks outside narrow lab settings.
- [SkillMimic-V2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02094): Uses trajectory stitching and transition-field augmentation to train robust interaction policies from sparse, noisy demonstrations.
- [GraspVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03233): Introduces Grasping Foundation Model Pre-trained on Billion-scale Synthetic Action Data, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [UniDiffGrasp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06832): Integrates VLM reasoning with part-level diffusion to support open-vocabulary constrained dual-arm grasping.
- [EgoDex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11709): Learns dexterous manipulation from large-scale egocentric video, turning scalable human video into robot policy supervision.
- [FLARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15659): Adds implicit world modeling to robot policies by aligning current representations with future latent observations.
- [SwarmDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15679): Uses a diffusion transformer to generate risk-aware swarm-robot trajectories in cluttered environments.
- [ManipLVM-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16517): Uses reinforcement learning with affordance perception and trajectory-matching rewards to improve embodied manipulation reasoning and physical action plausibility.
- [3D Equivariant Visuomotor Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16969): Uses spherical projection to bring 3D equivariant diffusion-policy learning to eye-in-hand RGB robot manipulation.
- [Interactive Post-Training for VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17016): Studies post-training VLA policies through interaction, directly targeting embodied policy improvement.
- [VLA-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18719): Introduces Towards Masterful and General Robotic Manipulation with Scalable Reinforcement Learning, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [MaskedManipulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19086): Distills a generative full-body manipulation controller from large-scale motion tracking into high-level objective-conditioned control.
- [What Can RL Bring to VLA Generalization?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19789): Compares reinforcement learning with supervised fine-tuning for VLA generalization under visual, semantic, and execution shifts, and proposes a PPO-based recipe.
- [EquAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21351): Adds SE(3)-equivariant structure to language-conditioned 3D transformer policies for open-loop robotic manipulation.
- [CoDA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21437): Optimizes coordinated diffusion noise for whole-body manipulation of articulated objects, linking human-motion generation with robotics-style object interaction.
- [Reinforced Reasoning for Embodied Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22050): Trains visual-language models with supervised fine-tuning and GRPO rewards to improve multi-step planning from observations, language goals, and environment feedback.
- [ForceVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22159): Introduces Enhancing VLA Models with a Force-aware MoE for Contact-rich Manipulation, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [RoboTransfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23171): Uses geometry-consistent video diffusion to transfer robotic visual policies across domains and reduce sim-to-real gaps.
- [SmolVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01844): Builds an affordable and efficient VLA model, making model size, deployment cost, and training accessibility part of the embodied-agent model line.
- [Rodrigues Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02618): Adds kinematic inductive bias for learning robot actions through a neural generalization of Rodrigues-style forward kinematics.
- [What Do Latent Action Models Actually Learn?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15691): Analyzes latent action models, clarifying what action abstractions they capture for embodied and video-conditioned policy learning.
- [Dex1B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17198): Studies dexterous manipulation learning at billion-demonstration scale, expanding the data-scaling line for embodied policies.
- [WorldVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21539): Introduces Towards Autoregressive Action World Model, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [EgoVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12440): Introduces Learning Vision-Language-Action Models from Egocentric Human Videos, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [AnyPos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12768): Learns task-agnostic action representations for bimanual manipulation, separating embodiment dynamics from task-specific demonstrations.
- [ThinkAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16815): Introduces Vision-Language-Action Reasoning via Reinforced Visual Latent Planning, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [MolmoAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07917): Introduces Action Reasoning Models that connect perception, mid-level spatial plans, and low-level robot actions through an open VLA-style architecture and dataset.
- [BeyondMimic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08241): Uses guided diffusion to move humanoid control from motion tracking toward versatile skill composition for unseen downstream tasks.
- [SimGenHOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14120): Combines generative modeling with reinforcement learning to produce physically executable whole-body humanoid-object interactions.
- [Discrete-Guided Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20095): Integrates discrete multi-agent path finding with constrained diffusion generation for scalable collision-free multi-robot motion planning.
- [EMMA for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04443): Scales mobile-manipulation policies by co-training on egocentric human mobile-manipulation data and robot data.
- [SimpleVLA-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09674): Introduces Scaling VLA Training via Reinforcement Learning, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [KungfuBot2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16638): Trains a unified whole-body controller with mixture-of-experts specialization for diverse and dynamic humanoid motion skills.
- [MotionTrans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17759): Transfers motion-level knowledge from human VR data into robotic manipulation policies.
- [EMMA Generative Visual Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22407): Augments real-world VLA manipulation policies through generative visual transfer across object appearances.
- [Compose Your Policies!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01068): Improves diffusion-based and flow-based robot policies through test-time distribution-level composition.
- [X-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10274): Introduces Soft-Prompted Transformer as Scalable Cross-Embodiment Vision-Language-Action Model, a model-side contribution for Embodied Vla.
- [PhysHSI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11072): Builds a deployable humanoid-scene interaction system that combines simulation training, scene perception, and real-world execution.
- [VLA-0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13054): Shows that representing actions directly as text can produce strong VLA performance without modifying the VLM vocabulary or adding specialized action heads.
- [RM-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15189): Uses role-model reinforcement learning to improve precise robot manipulation where small action errors matter.
- [MoS-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16617): Learns a basis skill space from Open X-Embodiment data and adapts VLA control to new manipulation tasks from one demonstration without gradient updates.
- [Shared-Autonomy Arm-Hand VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00139): Combines VR teleoperation with autonomous hand VLA policies to collect data for end-to-end dexterous arm-hand control.
- [RobustVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01331): Applies robustness-aware reinforcement post-training to VLA models for reliability under visual and environment variation.
- [High-Consistency Embodied World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12882): Uses multi-view trajectory videos to improve embodied world-model consistency, targeting action-conditioned physical scene prediction.
- [Audio World Models for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08405): Learns robot manipulation policies from audio world models.
- [ISS Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15020): Adds implicit scene supervision to a 3D visuomotor diffusion policy so actions stay consistent with scene geometry over time.
- [Dream-VL and Dream-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22615): Builds open diffusion-language vision and VLA models, using a diffusion language-model backbone for visual planning and robotic action generation.
- [Closing the Reality Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02778): Uses tactile simulation, torque sensing, and reinforcement learning for zero-shot sim-to-real dexterous force-based grasping and manipulation.
- [Fast-ThinkAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09708): Improves VLA reasoning efficiency with verbalizable latent planning before control execution.
- [ForeDiffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12925): Uses future-view construction to condition diffusion policies for robot manipulation.
- [DextER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16046): Uses language-conditioned embodied reasoning to generate dexterous grasps, strengthening VLA-style manipulation where grasp choice depends on object function and task intent.
- [Thinker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21199): Introduces a vision-language foundation model for embodied intelligence.
- [mjlab](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22074): Provides a lightweight GPU-accelerated robot-learning framework, lowering the cost of large-scale policy training and simulation loops.
- [Task-Centric Latent Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22714): Uses VLM task descriptions to separate controllable task-relevant motion from distractors when training latent action models.
- [GSR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01693): Learns structured reasoning for embodied manipulation, turning manipulation policy learning into an explicit reasoning and action-grounding problem.
- [HumanX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02473): Turns human videos into agile humanoid interaction skills through a data-generation pipeline and reward-free real-world policy learning.
- [RDT2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03310): Scales UMI demonstration data for a VLA robotic foundation model, targeting zero-shot generalization across robot embodiments.
- [InterPrior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06035): Scales generative humanoid-object control through imitation pretraining and reinforcement-learning post-training, targeting compositional loco-manipulation skills.
- [DreamDojo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06949): Trains a generalist robot world model from large-scale human videos so robot agents can simulate action outcomes before acting.
- [TwinRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09023): Uses digital-twin-driven reinforcement learning for real-world robotic manipulation through simulated-to-real feedback.
- [HAIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11758): Uses a dynamics-aware world model to control agile humanoid-object interaction with underactuated objects and occluded state.
- [Learning to Manipulate Anything](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11885): Studies data scaling laws for bounding-box-guided robot policies, clarifying how visual localization supervision scales toward open-ended manipulation.
- [Robot-DIFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11934): Distills diffusion features for geometrically consistent visuomotor control, using generative visual representations as policy-side spatial signals.
- [VLAW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12063): Co-trains a VLA policy and world model through iterative improvement, linking action learning with predictive environment modeling.
- [GigaBrain-0.5M*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12099): Trains a VLA through world-model-based reinforcement learning, connecting action learning with learned environment dynamics.
- [LongNav-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12351): Trains VLA navigation with horizon-adaptive multi-turn reinforcement learning over continuous embodied interaction.
- [Dual-Granularity Contrastive Reward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12636): Uses dual-granularity contrastive rewards and generated episodic guidance for embodied reinforcement learning.
- [Imitating What Works](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13197): Filters human-video demonstrations through simulation before modular policy learning, making video imitation more execution-grounded for robot control.
- [Perceptive Humanoid Parkour](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15827): Chains dynamic humanoid skills through motion matching and perception-driven selection, targeting long-horizon parkour-style control in complex scenes.
- [EgoScale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16710): Scales dexterous manipulation by transferring diverse egocentric human data into high-degree-of-freedom robotic control.
- [RoboCurate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18742): Uses action-verified neural trajectories to curate diverse robot-learning data, focusing model improvement on demonstrations that survive execution checks.
- [Compositional Planning with Jumpy World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19634): Uses jumpy world models for compositional planning, targeting longer-horizon embodied decisions through learned state-transition abstraction.
- [Recursive Belief VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20659): Introduces recursive belief modeling for VLA policies, making uncertainty over observations and actions part of the policy representation.
- [IG-RFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20715): Applies interaction-guided reinforcement learning to VLA models for long-horizon robotic manipulation, emphasizing feedback-driven post-training.
- [HALO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21157): Unifies vision, language, and action modeling for embodied multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning.
- [EgoAVFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22461): Learns robot policies with active vision from human egocentric videos through 3D flow.
- [FAVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23648): Adds force-adaptive fast-slow control to VLA models for contact-rich manipulation, connecting language-conditioned policies with tactile force dynamics.
- [OmniXtreme](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23843): Scales high-dynamic humanoid control by addressing multi-motion optimization and real-world executability constraints.
- [EgoMoD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00167): Predicts maps of dynamics from egocentric robot observations, giving embodied navigation policies a model of motion beyond the visible local scene.
- [Embedding Morphology into Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00182): Encodes robot morphology inside transformer policies to improve cross-robot transfer, treating embodiment differences as model inputs rather than fixed assumptions.
- [HoMMI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03243): Learns whole-body mobile manipulation from human demonstrations, extending embodied policy learning beyond arm-only manipulation.
- [MEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03596): Combines video-based short-horizon memory and text-based long-horizon memory so VLA policies can execute multi-stage real-world tasks.
- [TeamHOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07988): Learns a decentralized Transformer policy for cooperative humanoid-object interactions across variable team sizes.
- [VP-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22003): Introduces visual prompting as an interface for VLA models, improving spatial grounding and robustness in action generation.
- [ThinkJEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22281): Couples latent JEPA-style world modeling with vision-language reasoning to predict trajectories and actions in embodied settings.
- [Heracles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27756): Combines precise motion tracking with generative recovery behavior for more general and perturbation-tolerant humanoid control.
- [Multi-View Video Diffusion Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03181): Builds a 3D spatio-temporal-aware video action model, linking multi-view video generation with action-conditioned embodied policy learning.
- [SnapFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05656): Compresses multi-step flow-matching VLA denoising into one-step action generation through progressive self-distillation.
- [Train-Small Deploy-Large](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06598): Uses diffusion-based multi-robot planning to transfer from small training settings to larger deployment teams.
- [HY-Embodied-0.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07430): Builds embodied foundation models with a mixture-of-transformers design, latent tokens, self-evolving post-training, and on-policy distillation.
- [Switch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14834): Learns hierarchical skill switching for humanoid robots, using a skill graph and transition policy to move safely between agile behaviors.
- [Cross-Geometry Skill Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15455): Transfers learned robotic policies across object geometry through part decomposition; classify as embodied-model work, not reusable agent skills.
- [VLA Foundry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19728): Provides a unified framework for training VLA models, covering model, data, training, and evaluation components.
- [DeVI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20841): Uses text-conditioned synthetic videos as imitation signals for physics-based dexterous human-object control.
- [Latent Bridge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02739): Predicts feature deltas for efficient dual-system VLA inference, reducing expensive backbone calls while preserving action quality.
- [Dynamic Full-body Motion Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11369): Blends pretrained modular controllers for full-body motion with object interaction, fitting embodied action-generation models.
- [Think Twice, Act Once](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12620): Uses a trained verifier to select among sampled embodied actions at test time without changing the base policy.
- [CreFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14274): Uses corrective reflow for sparse-reward embodied video diffusion reinforcement learning.
- [InCLET](https://ifaamas.csc.liv.ac.uk/Proceedings/aamas2025/pdfs/p2134.pdf): Uses LLM in-context trajectory imagination to form task representations for language-conditioned reinforcement-learning agents in embodied control environments.
