- [A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432): A foundational review of architectures, planning, memory, tools, multi-agent interaction, and evaluation.

- [Comprehensive Exploration of Synthetic Data Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02524): Surveys 417 synthetic-data generation models from the past decade, framing the field around data scarcity, privacy constraints, model selection, and application tradeoffs.
- [Unlocking Efficiency in Large Language Model Inference: A Comprehensive Survey of Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07851): Systematizes speculative decoding as a draft-and-parallel-verify inference family, covering algorithm variants, acceleration settings, and deployment limits for autoregressive LLM serving.

- [A Comprehensive Survey of Compression Algorithms for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15347): Taxonomizes language-model compression around pruning, quantization, knowledge distillation, low-rank methods, and other algorithms, with accuracy, carbon, and maintenance cost as evaluation pressures.

- [Position: Graph Foundation Models Are Already Here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02216): Frames graph foundation models as a shift from task-specific graph neural networks toward pretraining on broad graph corpora, emphasizing transfer, domain diversity, and graph-specific scaling challenges.
- [Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for Pre-Trained Vision Models: A Survey and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02242): Reviews and benchmarks PEFT for pretrained vision models, comparing adapter, prompt, low-rank, and related methods under the storage and compute limits of full finetuning.

- [Knowledge Graphs Meet Multi-Modal Learning: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05391): Reviews more than 300 papers through two axes: KG-driven multimodal learning, where knowledge graphs support multimodal tasks, and multimodal knowledge graphs as the modeled artifact.

- [Towards Uncovering How Large Language Model Works: An Explainability Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10688): Uses explainability to map how LLMs store knowledge, reason, and fail, connecting mechanistic opacity to hallucination, toxicity, and misalignment risks.
