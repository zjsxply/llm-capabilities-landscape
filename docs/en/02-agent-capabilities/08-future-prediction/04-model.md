# 2.8.4 Model

- [InterMat: Accelerating Band Offset Prediction in Semiconductor Interfaces with DFT and Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02021): Combines DFT surface and interface calculations with graph neural networks to predict semiconductor band edges and band offsets across large heterojunction candidate spaces.
- [DiffDA: a diffusion model for weather-scale data assimilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05932): Adapts GraphCast into a denoising diffusion data-assimilation model that fuses sparse observations with forecasts to produce global atmospheric initial conditions at 0.25 degree resolution.
- [Long-Term Typhoon Trajectory Prediction: A Physics-Conditioned Approach Without Reanalysis Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15726): Adds a model entry for future, centered on physics-conditioned long-term typhoon trajectory prediction.
- [TrackGPT - A generative pre-trained transformer for cross-domain entity trajectory forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00066): Uses a GPT-style sequence model for domain-agnostic trajectory forecasting from minimal location and time features, reporting maritime and air-domain forecasts against deep-learning baselines.
- [AutoTimes: Autoregressive Time Series Forecasters via Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02370): Repurposes decoder-only LLMs as autoregressive time-series forecasters by projecting series into token-embedding space, enabling arbitrary-horizon generation and in-context forecasting with timestamp prompts.
- [XiHe: A Data-Driven Model for Global Ocean Eddy-Resolving Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02995): Introduces a 1/12 degree global ocean forecasting model with a hierarchical transformer, land-ocean masking, and ocean-specific blocks for local circulation and teleconnection patterns.
- [Approaching Human-Level Forecasting with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18563): Builds a retrieval-augmented language-model forecasting system that searches for evidence, generates forecasts, and aggregates predictions on questions from competitive forecasting platforms.
- [The pitfalls of next-token prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06963): Shows that teacher-forced next-token training can fail on simple planning-style future-token tasks and proposes a teacherless multi-token objective as a mitigation.
- [Is Mamba Effective for Time Series Forecasting?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11144): Introduces model-side work on is Mamba Effective for Time Series Forecasting?, fitting the repository's training, architecture, post-training, verifier, or synthetic-data coverage.
- [TFB: Towards Comprehensive and Fair Benchmarking of Time Series Forecasting Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.20150): Introduces model-side work on tFB: Towards Comprehensive and Fair Benchmarking of Time Series Forecasting Methods, fitting the repository's training, architecture, post-training, verifier, or synthetic-data coverage.
- [Towards explainable traffic flow prediction with large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02937): Converts multimodal traffic observations into language instructions and fine-tunes an LLM to produce both traffic-flow forecasts and natural-language explanations.
- [A Unified Replay-Based Continuous Learning Framework for Spatio-Temporal Prediction on Streaming Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14999): Uses replay buffers, spatio-temporal mixup, and STSimSiam representation preservation to reduce catastrophic forgetting in streaming spatio-temporal predictors.
- [TKAN: Temporal Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07344): Combines recurrent Kolmogorov-Arnold layers with LSTM-style memory management for efficient multi-step time-series forecasting.
- [Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) for Time Series Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08790): Applies spline-parameterized Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks to satellite-traffic forecasting, emphasizing adaptive activations and fewer learnable parameters than MLP baselines.
- [TimeCMA: Towards LLM-Empowered Multivariate Time Series Forecasting via Cross-Modality Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01638): Aligns time-series data with language-model representations for multivariate forecasting.
- [A Temporal Kolmogorov-Arnold Transformer for Time Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02486): Builds a TKAN-based encoder-decoder transformer that combines Kolmogorov-Arnold representations with self-attention for interpretable multivariate forecasting.
- [Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Time Series: Bridging Predictive Power and Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02496): Proposes T-KAN for concept-drift detection and symbolic-regression explanations, plus MT-KAN for multivariate forecasting.
- [C-Mamba: Channel Correlation Enhanced State Space Models for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05316): Extends Mamba with data-dependent channel-correlation modeling so state-space forecasters can capture cross-channel dependencies in multivariate series.
- [Text2TimeSeries: Enhancing Financial Forecasting through Time Series Prediction Updates with Event-Driven Insights from Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03689): Updates numerical financial forecasts using LLM-derived event insights, linking textual market events to real-valued time-series prediction changes.
- [Leveraging Large Language Models for Integrated Satellite-Aerial-Terrestrial Networks: Recent Advances and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04581): Surveys how LLMs can support predictive network management in integrated satellite, aerial, and terrestrial systems, including resource allocation, routing, and security decisions.
- [ViTime: Foundation Model for Time Series Forecasting Powered by Vision Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07311): Recasts time series into an image-based metric space and trains a vision-powered foundation model that supports both point and probabilistic forecasting.
- [Adaptive Prediction Ensemble: Improving Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Motion Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09475): Combines deep trajectory predictors with rule-based experts through a learned routing function to improve zero-shot OOD motion forecasting on Waymo and Argoverse data.
- [OpenEP: Open-Ended Future Event Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06578): Defines open-ended future event prediction and constructs OpenEPBench, where models generate flexible outcomes across event location, time, development, impact, and response questions.
- [LIMP: Large Language Model Enhanced Intent-aware Mobility Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12832): Uses an Analyze-Abstract-Infer LLM workflow and distillation to infer mobility intentions, then integrates those intent representations into scalable spatio-temporal mobility prediction.
- [Elucidating the Design Choice of Probability Paths in Flow Matching for Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03229): Shows that latent flow-matching forecasters are sensitive to probability-path design and proposes a path model that improves convergence and few-step probabilistic inference.
- [VEDIT: Latent Prediction Architecture For Procedural Video Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03478): Predicts future procedural-video steps in frozen vision-encoder latent space, avoiding pixel-level pretraining while supporting forecasting and planning.
- [Learning Pattern-Specific Experts for Time Series Forecasting Under Patch-level Distribution Shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09836): Introduces TFPS, which clusters patch-level patterns with dual time-frequency encoders and routes each patch to pattern-specific forecasting experts.
- [Moirai-MoE: Empowering Time Series Foundation Models with Sparse Mixture of Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10469): Replaces coarse frequency-level specialization in time-series foundation models with sparse mixture-of-experts routing over heterogeneous and non-stationary patterns.
- [Can AI weather models predict out-of-distribution gray swan tropical cyclones?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14932): Stress-tests FourCastNet variants trained with or without major tropical cyclones to measure whether AI weather models extrapolate to unseen Category 5 storms.
- [A Mamba Foundation Model for Time Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02941): Builds TSMamba, a linear-complexity time-series foundation model using forward and backward Mamba encoders plus two-stage transfer from pretrained Mamba LLMs.
- [MarketGPT: Developing a Pre-trained transformer (GPT) for Modeling Financial Time Series](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16585): Trains a GPT-style order-generation engine for limit-order-book simulation, producing long order-message streams that reproduce market stylized facts.
- [How Much Can Time-related Features Enhance Time Series Forecasting?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01557): Adds TimeSter and TimeLinear, lightweight modules that explicitly encode calendar and timestamp features to improve long-term forecasting under limited context.
- [SpecFuse: Ensembling Large Language Models via Next-Segment Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07380): Presents SpecEM, a training-free LLM ensemble that drafts and verifies segment-level continuations while dynamically reweighting models by online performance.
- [ChatTime: A Unified Multimodal Time Series Foundation Model Bridging Numerical and Textual Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11376): Treats time series as a foreign language so one multimodal foundation model can handle numerical and textual inputs and outputs for zero-shot forecasting.
- [DINO-Foresight: Looking into the Future with DINO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11673): Trains a masked feature transformer to forecast DINO-style vision-foundation-model features, letting downstream heads analyze future frames without pixel-space prediction.
- [AIFS-CRPS: ensemble forecasting using a model trained with a loss function based on the continuous ranked probability score](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15832): Trains ECMWF's stochastic AIFS variant with an almost-fair CRPS loss so it can generate exchangeable weather-ensemble members for medium-range and subseasonal forecasts.
- [LLMScenario: Large Language Model Driven Scenario Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/tsmc.2024.3392930): Uses LLM-driven scenario generation for systems and decision modeling, making future scenario construction the model-side contribution rather than a passive benchmark.
- [TimePFN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16294): Uses synthetic multivariate time-series data and a transformer architecture for few-shot forecasting.
- [From Perceptions to Decisions: Wildfire Evacuation Decision Prediction with Behavioral Theory-informed LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17701): Introduces FLARE, which combines behavioral-theory-guided chain-of-thought reasoning with memory-based reinforcement learning for wildfire evacuation decision prediction.
- [TimesBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21245): Builds a BERT-style foundation model for time-series understanding, extending future-prediction coverage beyond numeric forecasting heads.
- [TS-RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation based Time Series Foundation Models are Stronger Zero-Shot Forecaster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07649): Uses retrieval-augmented generation with time-series foundation models for zero-shot forecasting, adding an evidence-retrieval route to forecasting-model adaptation.
