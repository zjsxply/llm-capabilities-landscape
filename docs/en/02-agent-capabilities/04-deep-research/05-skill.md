# 2.4.5 Skill

- [assafelovic/gpt-researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher) (end-to-end retrieval-to-draft framework)
- [meta-research](https://github.com/AmberLJC/meta-research) (a scientific research workflow skill for "literature review -> hypothesis tree -> judgment gate -> experiment -> reflection")
- [deep-research](https://skills.sh/bytedance/deer-flow/deep-research) (DeerFlow official deep research skill)
- [github-deep-research](https://skills.sh/bytedance/deer-flow/github-deep-research) (DeerFlow official repository deep research skill; GitHub API + web retrieval + structured report generation)
- [deep-research](https://github.com/daymade/claude-code-skills/tree/main/deep-research) (emphasizes evidence tables, format contracts, and merging multiple drafts)
- [deep-research-openclaw-agent](https://clawhub.ai/milleniumgenai/deep-research-openclaw-agent) (an installation/wiring skill for a structured deep-research subagent)
- [deep-research](https://skills.sh/shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps/deep-research) (a procedural deep research template emphasizing multi-source synthesis and citation tracking)
- [academic-researcher](https://skills.sh/shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps/academic-researcher) (an academic writing and literature-review template, oriented toward structured output)
- [tooluniverse-literature-deep-research](https://skills.sh/mims-harvard/tooluniverse/tooluniverse-literature-deep-research) (literature-oriented, mainly biomedical)
- [literature-review](https://skills.sh/davila7/claude-code-templates/literature-review) (a systematic-review workflow template covering deduplication, screening, citation validation, and output scripts)
- [web-research](https://skills.sh/langchain-ai/deepagents/web-research) (LangChain official web research subskill)
- [websearch-deep](https://skills.sh/thomasholknielsen/claude-code-config/websearch-deep) (a template that drafts directly after web retrieval)
- [deep-research](https://skills.sh/199-biotechnologies/claude-deep-research-skill/deep-research) (a stepwise research template with dense checkpoints)
- [deep-research-agent](https://skills.sh/qodex-ai/ai-agent-skills/deep-research-agent) (mainly process prompts and sample code, with insufficient experimental execution and evaluation capability)
- [academic-deep-research](https://skills.sh/kesslerio/academic-deep-research-clawhub-skill/academic-deep-research) (an academic-literature-oriented drafting template)
- [deep-research](https://github.com/feiskyer/claude-code-settings/tree/main/skills/deep-research) (a multi-agent orchestration deep research skill, oriented toward multi-process research and aggregation)
- [Deep-Research-skills](https://github.com/Weizhena/Deep-Research-skills) (a multi-skill deep research suite: outline, field expansion, parallel research, and final report)
- [deep-research](https://github.com/wshuyi/deep-research/tree/main/skills/deep-research) (a Chinese procedural deep research skill emphasizing fact layering and retention of intermediate artifacts)
- [ARIS research skills](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/tree/main/skills) (paper: [ARIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03042); examples on skills.sh: [research-pipeline](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/research-pipeline), [research-lit](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/research-lit), [citation-audit](https://skills.sh/wanshuiyin/auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/citation-audit)) cover literature search, multi-source retrieval, research-wiki memory, novelty checks, cross-model review, and citation/claim audits, so they are closer to an executable research harness than a single report prompt.
- [parallel-deep-research](https://skills.sh/parallel-web/parallel-agent-skills/parallel-deep-research) is suitable for deep research workflows where source discovery and reading can be parallelized before final synthesis.
- [firecrawl-deep-research](https://skills.sh/firecrawl/firecrawl-workflows/firecrawl-deep-research) is suitable for retrieval-to-report workflows built on provider-backed crawling and content extraction.
- [doublecheck](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot/tree/main/skills/doublecheck) is useful for claim extraction, source search, and support/contradiction checking, complementing citation-reliability benchmarks such as Reference Hallucination Detection and Cited but Not Verified.
- [search-layer](https://github.com/blessonism/openclaw-search-skills/tree/main/search-layer) (more of a low-level retrieval layer for deep research: multi-source parallel search, deduplication, ranking, and citation-chain tracking)
- [last30days-skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill) and [last30days-skill-cn](https://github.com/Jesseovo/last30days-skill-cn) are recent-source discovery skills for researching the last month across social platforms, news, communities, and the web; they are useful when deep research needs freshness and cross-platform trend coverage before longer synthesis.
- [deep-research-pro](https://clawhub.ai/parags/deep-research-pro) (a commercial-style long-report template)
- [deepresearchwork](https://clawhub.ai/jiacode/deepresearchwork) (a research template with explicit task decomposition)
- [in-depth-research](https://clawhub.ai/ivangdavila/in-depth-research) (a long-report template based on deep follow-up questioning)
