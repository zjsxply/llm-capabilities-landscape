# 1.4 Hallucination

> Parent chapter: 1. Basic Capabilities

## 1.4.1 Leaderboard

- [Hallucinations Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hallucinations-leaderboard/leaderboard): an early open hallucination leaderboard attempt. Its value is aggregating signals such as TruthfulQA, HaluEval, and FEVER into one comparison entry point.
- [LLM-AggreFact Leaderboard](https://llm-aggrefact.github.io/): a leaderboard for grounded factuality verifiers. Its value is unifying multi-source factual-consistency data into comparable evaluation sets and connecting to runnable detectors such as MiniCheck.
- [Vectara Hallucination Evaluation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/vectara/Hallucination-evaluation-leaderboard): a public hallucination leaderboard for summarization/RAG scenarios. Its value is continuously comparing whether answers are supported by input facts and providing leads on automatic evaluators such as HHEM and FaithJudge.
- [FACTS entries on Google DeepMind Eval Suite](https://deepmind.google/research/evals/): public entry points for FACTS factuality evaluation. Its value is organizing grounded, search, parametric, and multimodal factuality settings into comparable submissions and review tracks.

## 1.4.2 Survey

- [Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03629): A foundational taxonomy of hallucination definitions, causes, detection, datasets, and mitigation.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Hallucination Mitigation Techniques in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01313): Organizes retrieval, verification, decoding, prompting, and training-side mitigation.
- [Factuality of Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02420): Frames hallucination within broader factuality evaluation and improvement methods.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Hallucination in Large Language, Image, Video and Audio Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09589): Maps hallucination across language, image, video, and audio foundation models.
- [A Survey of Multimodal Hallucination Evaluation and Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19024): Focuses on benchmark and detector design for multimodal hallucinations.

## 1.4.3 Bench

- [FEVER](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05355): evaluates evidence-based fact checking and claim verification. The core idea is to organize claims, Wikipedia evidence sentences, and supports/refutes/not-enough-info labels into a retrievable and decidable factuality task; it is also one of the early signals aggregated by Hallucinations Leaderboard.
- [TruthfulQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07958) (dataset: [truthfulqa/truthful_qa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/truthfulqa/truthful_qa)): evaluates whether models answer "truthfully rather than sycophantically" on common misconception questions. The core idea is to design questions where the most common wrong answer is tempting, exposing hallucination and fabrication tendencies.
- [HaluEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11747): evaluates LLMs' ability to recognize and generate hallucinations. The core idea is to construct large-scale hallucinated samples through sampling and filtering, then combine human annotation to check whether models can detect content that conflicts with sources or factual knowledge.
- [FActScore](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251) ([open-source code](https://github.com/shmsw25/FActScore)): evaluates the atomic factual correctness of long-form answers. The core idea is `atomic fact decomposition -> item-level judgment -> aggregate scoring`, separating "verifiability" from subjective evaluation.
- [FELM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00741): evaluates factuality evaluators themselves. The core idea is to annotate LLM outputs with fine-grained, segment-level factual labels and cover many error types including world knowledge, math, and reasoning, rather than only encyclopedic facts.
- [RAGTruth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00396) ([open-source code](https://github.com/ParticleMedia/RAGTruth)): evaluates word-level hallucination in RAG scenarios. The core idea is to collect nearly 18,000 RAG-generated answers with human annotations, distinguish unsupported and contradictory claims, and specifically support RAG hallucination detection and mitigation.
- [LongFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18802) ([open-source code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/long-form-factuality)): evaluates factuality and evidence support in long-form generation. The core idea is to split answers into verifiable atomic claims and use a search-augmented judgment protocol to measure overall factuality; the paper also includes settings such as LongFact-Objects/Concepts.
- [LLM-AggreFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10774) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Liyan06/MiniCheck)): evaluates grounded factuality verifiers. The core idea is to aggregate multi-source factual-consistency data and compare detectors under one framework for judging whether answers are supported by reference documents.
- [FACTS Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03200): evaluates whether long-form answers are sufficiently supported by given evidence. The core idea is to compare models' grounded factuality with a public leaderboard and a protocol calibrated by human preference, later expanding to a broader FACTS factuality leaderboard.
- [HLE-Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (dataset: [lmms-lab/HLE-Verified](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmms-lab/HLE-Verified)): a manually reviewed verifiable subset of difficult knowledge QA. The core idea is to improve "decidability" through question filtering and verification, making it more suitable for reliability/factuality stress tests.
- [HalluVerse25](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07833): evaluates fine-grained multilingual hallucination detection. The core idea is to annotate entity-, relation-, and sentence-level hallucinations in English, Arabic, and Turkish, avoiding an English-only factual-QA view.
- [HalluLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17550) ([open-source code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/HalluLens)): provides unified evaluation of intrinsic/extrinsic hallucination. The core idea is to first clarify the boundary between hallucination and factuality, then reduce data leakage and saturation with dynamically generated extrinsic tests.
- [HalluMix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00506) (dataset: [quotientai/HalluMix](https://huggingface.co/datasets/quotientai/HalluMix)): evaluates hallucination detection across real multi-domain, short- and long-context settings. The core idea is to cover multidocument and full-sentence outputs in RAG scenarios and check whether detectors are robust to task and context-length variation.
- [MultiHal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14101): evaluates knowledge-graph-supported multilingual multi-hop hallucination. The core idea is to construct high-quality KG paths from open knowledge graphs so generation evaluation can jointly check factual relations, cross-lingual expression, and graph-structured evidence alignment.
- [PhD](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Liu_PhD_A_ChatGPT-Prompted_Visual_Hallucination_Evaluation_Dataset_CVPR_2025_paper.html): evaluates visual hallucination in MLLMs. Core idea: use ChatGPT-prompted image-question cases to test whether multimodal models answer with unsupported visual objects, attributes, or relations.
- [ODE](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Tu_ODE_Open-Set_Evaluation_of_Hallucinations_in_Multimodal_Large_Language_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.html): evaluates open-set hallucination in MLLMs. Core idea: make object and attribute existence tests dynamic enough that visual hallucination diagnosis is less tied to a fixed closed-set label space.
- [AbstentionBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09038): evaluates whether models refuse rather than fabricate when facing unanswerable questions. The core idea is to cover 20 data-source categories such as unknown answers, insufficient information, false premises, subjective interpretation, and outdated information, treating "knowing when not to answer" as part of hallucination risk.
- [FACTORY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00109): evaluates long-form factual generation. The core idea is to use human-verified, answerable, unambiguous fact-seeking prompts to expose unreliability on long-tail facts and atomic claims in long answers.
- [SimpleQA Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07968): evaluates correctness and hallucination rates in short factual QA. The core idea is to reduce interpretation noise with a low-ambiguity question set and strict verification protocol.
- [AuthenHallu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10539): evaluates hallucination detection in real LLM-human interactions. The core idea is to annotate hallucinations from real conversations rather than synthetic inducement samples.
- [CAP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22395): evaluates multilingual hallucination detection in scientific text generation. The core idea is to construct 900 scientific questions and more than 7,000 model answers from ACL papers, with annotations for scientific factual errors and language fluency.
- [MMM-Fact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25120): evaluates multimodal, multi-domain fact checking under different retrieval-difficulty levels. Core idea: test whether models can ground factual claims in retrieved multimodal evidence rather than relying on unsupported generation.
- [Vectara Hallucination Evaluation](https://huggingface.co/spaces/vectara/Hallucination-evaluation-leaderboard): evaluates whether generated content in summarization/RAG scenarios is faithful to input facts. The core idea is to connect real document summaries and automatic factuality judges into a public leaderboard protocol, suitable for tracking detectors such as HHEM and FaithJudge.

## 1.4.4 Agent Harness

- [RARR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08726) ([open-source code](https://github.com/anthonywchen/RARR)): retrieval-backed hallucination repair. A typical loop is `claim decomposition -> evidence retrieval -> evidence filtering/alignment -> revision`.
- [SelfCheckGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896) ([open-source code](https://github.com/potsawee/selfcheckgpt)): detects suspicious claims through self-consistency and variant sampling. The core idea is to treat "inconsistency" as a hallucination-risk signal.
- [FacTool](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13528) ([open-source code](https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/factool)): a tool-augmented factuality detection framework. The core idea is to decompose generated content in tasks such as QA, code, math, and scientific literature review into verifiable units, then call external tools and evidence to judge factual errors.
- [Chain-of-Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11495): decomposes complex answers into subclaims to verify one by one. The core idea is to make the verification control flow explicit, reducing the pattern where an answer looks plausible overall but fabricates details.
- [SAFE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18802) ([open-source code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/long-form-factuality)): a search-augmented factuality evaluation harness. The core idea is to split long answers into atomic claims and then judge whether each is supported by retrieved evidence.
- [MiniCheck](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10774) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Liyan06/MiniCheck)): a lightweight grounded factuality verifier. The core idea is to train/evaluate small detectors on LLM-AggreFact and judge answer-reference consistency at lower cost.
- [RefChecker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14486) ([open-source code](https://github.com/amazon-science/RefChecker)): a reference-based fine-grained hallucination checker. The core idea is to split answers into verifiable fragments and align them with reference evidence, producing span/claim-level hallucination judgments.
- [RAGChecker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08067) ([open-source code](https://github.com/amazon-science/RAGChecker)): a diagnostic evaluation framework for RAG systems. The core idea is to locate factuality errors in retrieval and generation through claim-level support, omissions, and noise.
- [SelfCheckAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01812): a multi-agent, zero-resource hallucination detection framework. The core idea is to combine symbolic, specialized-detection, and contextual-consistency agents and use multidimensional consistency signals to judge whether generated content is reliable.
- [REFIND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13622) ([open-source code](https://github.com/oneonlee/REFIND)): retrieval-augmented factuality hallucination detection. The core idea is to use retrieved documents and a context sensitivity ratio to locate hallucinated spans and validate robustness in multilingual settings.
- [FactSelfCheck](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17229): black-box hallucination detection based on fact triples. The core idea is to decompose answers into fine-grained facts and perform multi-round consistency checks.
- [Premise Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06438): a retrieval-augmented logical verification framework for false-premise queries. The core idea is to convert a user question into logical premises before generation and verify them one by one, blocking the hallucination chain that follows a false premise.
- [FaithJudge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04847) ([open-source code](https://github.com/vectara/FaithJudge)): an automatic judge for factual faithfulness. The core idea is to use a reusable evaluator to judge whether generated content is faithful to given evidence, supporting later versions of the Vectara hallucination leaderboard.

## 1.4.5 Skill

- [fact-checker](https://skills.sh/shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps/fact-checker) is suitable for fact checking and evidence tracing.
- [fact-checker](https://skills.sh/daymade/claude-code-skills/fact-checker) leans more toward "report-style verification results."
- [fact-check](https://clawhub.ai/webguhui/fact-check) can turn arbitrary claims into a cross-checking workflow.
- [clarity-gate](https://clawhub.ai/frmoretto/clarity-gate) explicitly flags risks such as "missing uncertainty markers" and "verifiable claims not verified."
- [citation-validator](https://skills.sh/liangdabiao/claude-code-stock-deep-research-agent/citation-validator) is suitable for intercepting cases where a cited source appears to exist but does not support the conclusion.
