# 2.5.2 Survey

- [GUI Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13501): Reviews perception, grounding, planning, action spaces, benchmarks, and failures across interfaces.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Agents for Computer Use: Foundations, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16150): Surveys environments, interaction loops, memory, safety, and evaluation for computer-use agents.
- [Towards Trustworthy GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23434): Uses trustworthiness as the organizing lens for GUI agents, spanning perception, grounding, planning, action safety, and evaluation.
- [A Survey on (M)LLM-Based GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13865): Maps UI grounding, action prediction, model capabilities, and system challenges.
- [A Survey on the Safety and Security Threats of Computer-Using Agents: JARVIS or Ultron?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10924): Reviews risks from GUI action, cross-application execution, privacy exposure, and tool misuse.
- [OS Agents: A Survey on MLLM-based Agents for General Computing Devices Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04482): Focuses on general computing-device operation, observation, action, and evaluation.
