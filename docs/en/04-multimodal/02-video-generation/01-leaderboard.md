# 4.2.1 Leaderboard

- [Towards a GENEA Leaderboard - an Extended, Living Benchmark for Evaluating and Advancing Conversational Motion Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06327): Adds a leaderboard entry for video gen, centered on video-generation, video-editing, or video-model contribution.
- [VBench Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Vchitect/VBench_Leaderboard): The public leaderboard associated with the VBench video-generation benchmark suite.
- [Artificial Analysis Text to Video Leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video): A live platform comparison for text-to-video generation systems.
- [T2V-CompBench Leaderboard](https://github.com/KaiyueSun98/T2V-CompBench#how-to-join-t2v-compbench-leaderboard): The official compositional text-to-video generation leaderboard with public submission instructions.
- [VideoScore Leaderboard](https://tiger-ai-lab.github.io/VideoScore/#results--leaderboard): The official metric leaderboard comparing video-generation evaluators on VideoFeedback, EvalCrafter, GenAI-Bench, and VBench.
- [WorldModelBench Leaderboard](https://worldmodelbench-team.github.io/#leaderboard): The official world-modeling leaderboard for video generation across instruction following, physical adherence, and common sense.
- [VideoPhy-2 Leaderboard](https://videophy2.github.io/#human-leaderboard-on-video-generation-models): The official human leaderboard for action-centric physical commonsense in generated videos.
- [Video-Bench Leaderboard](https://github.com/Video-Bench/Video-Bench#leaderboard): The official human-preference-aligned video-generation ranking across quality, temporal consistency, and text-video alignment dimensions.
- [WorldBench Leaderboard](https://world-bench.github.io/#results): The official physical-world modeling results page for video prediction and text-response evaluation.
