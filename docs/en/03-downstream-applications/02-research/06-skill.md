- [chtc66/academic-skills](https://github.com/chtc66/academic-skills) is a Chinese academic-skill suite for paper deep notes, survey writing, arXiv/Feishu digesting, rebuttal drafting, experiment-log summaries, benchmark extraction, research-gap analysis, and weekly lab updates.

- [voidful/academic-skills](https://github.com/voidful/academic-skills) is a general academic-research skill collection; because no confirmed citing paper was found in the URL-citation pass, treat it as project-only and compare implementation depth before relying on it over larger suites.
- [ARIS research skill library](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/tree/main/skills) provides concrete research-pipeline, literature, idea-discovery, experiment-bridge, paper-writing, citation-audit, research-wiki, and Codex-mirror skills for autonomous research workflows.

- [research-paper-writing](https://skills.sh/master-cai/research-paper-writing-skills/research-paper-writing) ([source repository](https://github.com/Master-cai/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills/tree/main/research-paper-writing)) packages ML/CV/NLP paper-writing guidance for section structure, paragraph flow, claim-evidence alignment, figure/table presentation, and reviewer-facing self-review, making it a compact counterpart to larger paper-writing skill suites.
