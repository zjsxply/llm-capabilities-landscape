# 1.14.3 Bench

- [One Prompt To Rule Them All: LLMs for Opinion Summary Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11683): Adds an evaluation resource or protocol for Writing capabilities.
- [Shaping Human-AI Collaboration: Varied Scaffolding Levels in Co-writing with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11723): Compares co-writing interfaces with different scaffolding levels, measuring how guidance changes collaboration, agency, and writing outcomes.
- [Reading Subtext: Evaluating Large Language Models on Short Story Summarization with Writers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01061): Evaluates short-story summarization with writers of unpublished fiction, finding frequent faithfulness errors and weak agreement between automatic or LLM judges and author ratings.
- [FaaF: Facts as a Function for the evaluation of generated text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03888): Uses function-call-style fact extraction and checking to evaluate factual recall in generated text, especially for RAG outputs, with lower-cost unsupported-fact detection than prompt-only judging.
- [LMStyle Benchmark: Evaluating Text Style Transfer for Chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08943): Provides an automated benchmark for chatbot style transfer, testing whether responses preserve meaning while shifting toward requested styles.
- [Writing with AI Lowers Psychological Ownership, but Longer Prompts Can Help](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03108): Uses controlled writing experiments to measure psychological ownership in AI-assisted writing and shows that longer, more self-directed prompts can partially restore ownership.
- [Evaluating Character Understanding of Large Language Models via Character Profiling from Fictional Works](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12726): Builds a character-profiling evaluation from fictional works, testing whether models infer traits, relationships, and motivations from narrative evidence.
- [SemEval-2024 Task 8: Multidomain, Multimodel and Multilingual Machine-Generated Text Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14183): SemEval-2024 Task 8 evaluates machine-generated-text detection across multiple generators, domains, and languages, with separate settings for binary detection, source attribution, and human-to-machine change-point detection; the shared task makes cross-domain and mixed-authorship robustness directly comparable.
- [Automated Long Answer Grading with RiceChem Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14316): Uses the RiceChem long-answer dataset to evaluate automated grading of extended chemistry responses, focusing on rubric-aligned assessment of student writing.
- [On the Evaluation of Machine-Generated Reports](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00982): Analyzes report-generation evaluation, focusing on whether metrics capture factuality, structure, completeness, and usefulness in machine-generated reports.
- [Evaluating Large Language Models for Structured Science Summarization in the Open Research Knowledge Graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02105): Tests LLMs on structured science summarization for the Open Research Knowledge Graph, where outputs must fit schema-like research contribution slots.
- [Toward Reliable Detection of LLM-Generated Texts: A Comprehensive Evaluation Framework with CUDRT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09056): Introduces CUDRT as a comprehensive evaluation framework for LLM-generated-text detection, stressing reliability under varied domains, models, prompts, and perturbations.
- [Evaluation of Instruction-Following Ability for Large Language Models on Story-Ending Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16356): Provides a dataset, benchmark, metric, or evaluation protocol for measuring Writing capabilities.
- [Suri](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19371) ([code](https://github.com/chtmp223/suri)): What it evaluates: multi-constraint instruction following in long-form generation. Core idea: break topic, format, length, style, and content constraints in long-form tasks into checkable dimensions, specifically testing whether models can maintain many fine-grained requirements across long outputs.
- [FineSurE: Fine-grained Summarization Evaluation using LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00908): Decomposes summary quality into fine-grained error or aspect judgments so LLM evaluators can localize factual, coverage, and coherence problems rather than emit only one overall score.
- [Pron vs Prompt: Can Large Language Models already Challenge a World-Class Fiction Author at Creative Text Writing?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01119): Compares LLM-generated creative texts with work from a world-class fiction author, making human literary preference and attribution part of the writing evaluation.
- [Towards Enhancing Coherence in Extractive Summarization: Dataset and Experiments with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04855): Provides a dataset and LLM experiments for extractive-summary coherence, evaluating sentence selection and ordering rather than only content overlap.
- [LongLaMP: A Benchmark for Personalized Long-form Text Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11016): Benchmarks personalized long-form generation, testing whether systems use user profiles or histories across extended outputs.
- [LongWriter / LongBench-Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07055) ([code](https://github.com/THUDM/LongWriter)): What it evaluates: 10,000-word-level long-text generation and length scaling. Core idea: use LongWrite-Ruler and LongBench-Write to show that "being able to read long context" is not the same as "being able to write long output reliably", with emphasis on length control, structural expansion, and mid-to-late output degradation.
- [LongGenBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02076): What it evaluates: long-form generation by long-context LLMs. Core idea: use longer inputs, longer outputs, and multidimensional quality evaluation to distinguish short summary-like output from true long-form drafting.
- [AI Suggestions Homogenize Writing Toward Western Styles and Diminish Cultural Nuances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11360): Studies how AI suggestions homogenize writing style and cultural nuance.
- [Small Language Models can Outperform Humans in Short Creative Writing: A Study Comparing SLMs with Humans and LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11547): Compares small language models, larger LLMs, and humans on short creative-writing tasks using human preference or quality judgments.
- [HelloBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16191): What it evaluates: LLM long-text generation ability. Core idea: construct multiple prompt types and evaluation protocols around long-output scenarios to observe systematic degradation in long-form coherence, repetition, structural stability, and instruction satisfaction.
- [When Stereotypes GTG: The Impact of Predictive Text Suggestions on Gender Bias in Human-AI Co-Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.20390): Measures how predictive text suggestions affect gender bias in human-AI co-writing, linking suggestion design to biased final text.
- [CS4: Measuring the Creativity of Large Language Models Automatically by Controlling the Number of Story-Writing Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04197): Varies the number and specificity of story-writing constraints to reduce training-corpus retelling and automatically probe creativity, instruction following, and narrative coherence.
- [Mitigating the Impact of Reference Quality on Evaluation of Summarization Systems with Reference-Free Metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10867): Tests reference-free summarization metrics as a way to reduce evaluation instability caused by noisy or low-quality gold summaries.
- [DomainSum: A Hierarchical Benchmark for Fine-Grained Domain Shift in Abstractive Text Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15687): Builds a hierarchical domain-shift benchmark for abstractive summarization, measuring how systems degrade across coarse and fine-grained domain changes.
- [AIDBench: A benchmark for evaluating the authorship identification capability of large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13226): Evaluates whether LLMs can identify authorship from writing samples, testing attribution ability across authors and generated or human text.
- [Do LLMs Agree on the Creativity Evaluation of Alternative Uses?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15560): Tests agreement among LLM judges on Alternative Uses Task creativity ratings, exposing how novelty and usefulness judgments vary across models.
- [Evaluate Summarization in Fine-Granularity: Auto Evaluation with LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19906): Uses LLM-based automatic judging to score summaries at fine granularity, separating local content errors from broad summary-level quality.
- [SumSurvey: An Abstractive Dataset of Scientific Survey Papers for Long Document Summarization](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.findings-acl.574): Provides scientific survey papers as long-document summarization targets, testing whether models condense multi-paper synthesis into faithful abstracts.
- [CCSum: A Large-Scale and High-Quality Dataset for Abstractive News Summarization](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.naacl-long.406): Releases a large abstractive news summarization dataset with quality controls for evaluating coverage, concision, and factuality.
- [ParaRev: Building a dataset for Scientific Paragraph Revision annotated with revision instruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05222): Builds ParaRev, a scientific paragraph revision dataset with revision instructions.
- [LongProc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05414) ([code](https://github.com/princeton-pli/LongProc), [leaderboard](https://princeton-pli.github.io/LongProc/)): What it evaluates: long procedural text generation. Core idea: require models to generate recipes, tutorials, workflows, manuals, and other procedural long texts, evaluating step order, dependency consistency, completeness, and executability rather than only single-paragraph fluency.
- [Agent-as-Judge for Long-Narrative Summaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09993): What it evaluates: factual summarization of long narratives. Core idea: use an agentic judge to inspect long summaries for factual accuracy rather than relying only on lexical overlap.
- [OpenGenAlign: A Preference Dataset and Benchmark for Trustworthy Reward Modeling in Open-Ended, Long-Context Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13264): Evaluates long-form or open-ended generation; core idea: measure reward modeling, preference alignment, or writing quality under realistic prompts.
- [ExPerT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14956): What it evaluates: personalized long-form text generation. Core idea: score whether outputs satisfy user-specific writing preferences while providing explainable evaluation signals.
- [Policies and Evaluation for Online Meeting Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03111): Studies policies and evaluation for online meeting summarization, adding an interaction-derived summarization benchmark line to writing and long-form generation.
- [IssueBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08395): uses millions of realistic prompts to measure issue bias in LLM writing assistance.
- [EssayJudge: A Multi-Granular Benchmark for Assessing Automated Essay Scoring Capabilities of Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11916): Benchmark/evaluation candidate for writing and text generation: Benchmarks multimodal automated essay scoring at multiple granularities.
- [NUTSHELL: A Dataset for Abstract Generation from Scientific Talks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16942): Pairs ACL conference-talk recordings with abstracts for speech-to-abstract generation, evaluating whether systems can produce faithful scientific abstracts from presentations.
- [LongEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19103): Evaluates long-text generation through direct and plan-based paradigms. Core idea: measure length satisfaction, information density, and quality degradation as outputs grow longer.
- [Argument Summarization and its Evaluation in the Era of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00847): Studies argument summarization and LLM-based evaluation with a human benchmark dataset.
- [WritingBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05244) ([code](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench), [leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/WritingBench/WritingBench)): What it evaluates: generative writing capability across 6 core writing domains and 100 subdomains. Core idea: decompose writing tasks into query-relevant evaluation criteria, using prompts, rubrics, and review protocols closer to real needs to measure whether models can write long texts that satisfy purpose, style, structure, and content constraints.
- [LongInOutBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06868): Evaluates long-input and long-output generation. Core idea: diagnose lost-in-the-middle failures when a model must preserve and use important input content across long generated texts.
- [DeFine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07170): Evaluates long-form article generation with decomposed and fine-grained annotations. Core idea: provide hierarchical topic and content annotations so models can be tested on structure, coverage, and depth rather than only final-surface fluency.
- [CoKe: Customizable Fine-Grained Story Evaluation via Chain-of-Keyword Rationalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17136): Evaluates stories with customizable criteria and chain-of-keyword rationales, making judge decisions more inspectable than a single holistic story score.
- [Poor Alignment and Steerability of Large Language Models: Evidence from College Admission Essays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20062): Uses college-admission-essay writing as a steerability test, showing where LLMs fail to follow preference and alignment controls in high-stakes personal writing.
- [StyleRec: A Benchmark Dataset for Prompt Recovery in Writing Style Transformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04373): Benchmarks prompt recovery for writing style transformation, testing whether systems can infer the style-control prompt behind an edited text.
- [StoryReasoning Dataset: Using Chain-of-Thought for Scene Understanding and Grounded Story Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10292): Provides scene-understanding and grounded story-generation data with chain-of-thought rationales, testing whether generated narratives follow visual or scene evidence.
- [WebNovelBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14818): What it evaluates: web-novel-style long narrative writing. Core idea: compare LLM-generated fiction against real web novel distributions, focusing on characters, plot, narrative pacing, chapter continuation, and reader preference rather than only short story fragments.
- [UNCLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16922): What it evaluates: uncertainty expression in long-form generation. Core idea: specifically check whether models use appropriate, calibrated language to express uncertainty in long texts, avoiding turning ambiguous information into overconfident assertions in open-ended writing.
- [P2P](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17104): Evaluates automated paper-to-poster generation. Core idea: turn a research paper into a structured poster and score fine-grained content selection, layout organization, and faithful visual-textual summarization.
- [CreataSet / CrEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19236): Evaluates textual creativity with pairwise comparison across diverse domains. Core idea: use shared contextual instructions and large human/synthetic creative-response pairs to train and test an evaluator aligned with human creativity judgments.
- [PatentMind: A Multi-Aspect Reasoning Graph for Patent Similarity Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19347): Represents patent similarity with a multi-aspect reasoning graph, evaluating whether systems compare inventions beyond surface lexical overlap.
- [APE: A Data-Centric Benchmark for Efficient LLM Adaptation in Text Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19912): Benchmarks or measures writing and summarization, providing a candidate reusable evaluation signal.
- [AdParaphrase v2.0: Generating Attractive Ad Texts Using a Preference-Annotated Paraphrase Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20826): Releases preference-annotated ad paraphrases for evaluating whether advertising text rewrites become more attractive while preserving the source message.
- [Argument Representation Coverage](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23654): What it evaluates: argument preservation in long-document summarization. Core idea: represent source arguments bottom-up and measure whether generated summaries cover salient argument structure.
- [MSumBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00549): What it evaluates: summarization across domains and languages. Core idea: add domain-specific criteria and multilingual coverage so summary quality is not judged by a single-domain English rubric.
- [ExpertLongBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01241) ([leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/launch/ExpertLongBench)): What it evaluates: expert-level long-form generation tasks. Core idea: use structured checklists to review professional long texts, emphasizing task-specific requirements, verifiable subcriteria, and overall long-form quality to reduce instability from relying on a single overall judge score.
- [EssayBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02596): Evaluates multi-genre Chinese essay writing. Core idea: use genre-specific rubrics and hierarchical scoring to assess structure, rhetoric, and critical thinking beyond coarse text-quality metrics.
- [Identifying Reliable Evaluation Metrics for Scientific Text Revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04772): Evaluates scientific text revision, writing quality, or writing metrics.
- [TeXpert](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16990): What it evaluates: multi-level LaTeX code generation. Core idea: test whether models can produce compilable, structured scientific-document artifacts rather than only plain text.
- [Statistical Multicriteria Evaluation of LLM-Generated Text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18082): Benchmark/evaluation candidate for writing and text generation: Studies multicriteria statistical evaluation for LLM-generated text.
- [Arena-Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18841) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THU-KEG/Arena-Write)): What it evaluates: pairwise preference and Elo ranking under real writing prompts. Core idea: put long-form writing outputs into arena-style comparisons, addressing the difficulty of capturing holistic preference with a single rubric critic.
- [LitBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00769): What it evaluates: reliable automated evaluation of creative writing through human-labeled story comparisons. Core idea: provide a standardized preference benchmark and paired dataset for testing LLM judges and reward models on literary generation quality.
- [Targeted Syntactic Evaluation for Grammatical Error Correction](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1026): Probes grammatical error correction with targeted syntactic test cases, separating specific grammar phenomena from aggregate correction scores.
- [AI-generated stories favour stability over change: homogeneity and cultural stereotyping in narratives generated by gpt-4o-mini](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22445): Analyzes gpt-4o-mini narratives for homogeneity, cultural stereotyping, and preference for stable plots over transformative change.
- [PREF: Reference-Free Evaluation of Personalised Text Generation in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10028): Provides a reference-free protocol for personalized text generation, scoring whether outputs satisfy user preferences without requiring a gold target text.
- [Paper-Introduction Writing Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14273): What it evaluates: LLM writing of research-paper introductions. Core idea: make academic introduction drafting an explicit benchmark with pressure on motivation, positioning, structure, and scholarly tone.
- [T2R-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19813): evaluates article-level report generation from real industrial tables. Core idea: use 457 bilingual tables across 19 industry domains and a report-quality evaluation protocol to test whether models can turn structured tabular evidence into coherent, accurate reports.
- [Benchmark of stylistic variation in LLM-generated texts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10179): Measures stylistic variation in LLM-generated text so evaluators can distinguish controllable style diversity from generic fluency.
- [RedNote-Vibe: A Dataset for Capturing Temporal Dynamics of AI-Generated Text in Lifestyle Social Media](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22055): Tracks AI-generated lifestyle posts on RedNote over time, enabling evaluation of temporal drift, platform style, and social-media text authenticity.
- [From Internal Representations to Text Quality: A Geometric Approach to LLM Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25359): Relates internal representation geometry to generated-text quality, offering a diagnostic signal beyond output-only scoring.
- [CML-Bench: A Framework for Evaluating and Enhancing LLM-Powered Movie Scripts Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06231): Evaluates writing and long-form generation. Core idea: use the paper's task, dataset, or protocol around a framework for evaluating and enhancing llm-powered movie scripts generation to make this capability more comparable.
- [WritingPreferenceBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14616): evaluates subjective writing preferences across cultures after controlling objective correctness, factuality, and length. Core idea: use English and Chinese preference pairs across creative genres to test whether reward models and LLM judges capture style, creativity, and emotional resonance rather than only objective errors.
- [Readability Reconsidered: A Cross-Dataset Analysis of Reference-Free Metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15345): Compares reference-free readability metrics across datasets, showing when readability scores transfer and when dataset artifacts dominate.
- [CreativityPrism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20091): Evaluates LLM creativity across divergent thinking, creative writing, and logical reasoning. Core idea: separate quality, novelty, and diversity so creative-writing strength is not conflated with other forms of creative generation.
- [FicSim: A Dataset for Multi-Faceted Semantic Similarity in Long-Form Fiction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20926): Builds a long-form fiction similarity dataset with multiple semantic facets, testing whether metrics capture plot, character, setting, and theme similarity.
- [LongWeave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24345): What it evaluates: realistic, relevant, and verifiable long-form generation. Core idea: make long-form tasks both close to real writing needs and verifiable through citations, facts, and structured constraints, connecting writing quality with verifiability.
- [BookAsSumQA: An Evaluation Framework for Aspect-Based Book Summarization via Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06183): Evaluates book summaries through aspect-based questions, using answerability to reveal missing or unsupported content in long narrative summaries.
- [CreBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13626): What it evaluates: human-aligned creativity from idea to process to product. Core idea: assess creative generation across multiple stages instead of judging only final artifacts.
- [FEANEL: A Benchmark for Fine-Grained Error Analysis in K-12 English Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22883): Provides fine-grained error labels for K-12 English writing, enabling diagnosis of grammar, usage, organization, and revision-support failures.
- [2K-Characters-10K-Stories: A Quality-Gated Stylized Narrative Dataset with Disentangled Control and Sequence Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05557): Releases stylized stories with disentangled control signals and sequence-consistency checks, supporting evaluation of character style and long narrative continuity.
- [FIND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18601): What it evaluates: finding inconsistencies in documents. Core idea: insert expert-written inconsistencies into realistic documents and test whether models can audit them.
- [DramaBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19012) ([leaderboard](https://dramabench.pages.dev/web/leaderboard.html)): What it evaluates: drama script continuation. Core idea: assess long-form narrative continuation across plot, character, style, coherence, and drama-specific constraints.
- [SlideTailor: Personalized Presentation Slide Generation for Scientific Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20292): Evaluates personalized presentation-slide generation from scientific papers, adding a writing benchmark where outputs must follow user presentation preferences.
- [ASAP 2.0](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asw.2025.100954): What it evaluates: source-based writing quality at corpus scale. Core idea: provide a large assessment-oriented dataset for judging whether generated or student writing uses provided sources faithfully and effectively.
- [The AraGenEval Shared Task on Arabic Authorship Style Transfer and AI Generated Text Detection](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.arabicnlp-sharedtasks.1): Defines Arabic shared-task evaluations for authorship style transfer and AI-generated-text detection, adding non-English style and provenance tests.
- [Not too long do read: Evaluating LLM-generated extreme scientific summaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23206): Evaluates extremely short scientific summaries, testing whether LLMs preserve key claims under severe length compression.
- [Artificial intelligence-generated content (AIGC) quality evaluation: a comprehensive indicator system grounded in both users and literature](https://doi.org/10.1108/el-08-2025-0332): Proposes an AIGC quality indicator system derived from user perspectives and literature evidence, giving generated-text assessment a multi-criteria rubric.
- [MDD-TD: A dataset of large language model text source and content authenticity detection](https://doi.org/10.11922/11-6035.csd.2025.0191.zh): Releases a dataset for detecting LLM text source and content authenticity, supporting provenance and human-versus-model text analysis.
- [AI-Generated Versus Human Text: Introducing a New Dataset for Benchmarking and Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1109/tai.2025.3544183): Releases paired AI-generated and human-written text for detector benchmarking and analysis of linguistic differences between sources.
- [SPoRC-VIST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01062): Evaluates visually grounded podcast-style narrative generation from image sequences, pairing 4,000 image-dialogue examples with measures of conversational naturalness, narrative depth, and visual consistency.
- [WRAVAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03268): What it evaluates: writing-assist tasks such as tone and style modification. Core idea: benchmark practical writing assistance where task-specific evaluation data is scarce, especially for smaller language models.
- [RIGOURATE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04350): What it evaluates: scientific claim overstatement against paper evidence. Core idea: retrieve supporting evidence from the paper body and score whether claims are proportionate to the evidence, making evidential restraint measurable in academic writing.
- [SlidesGen-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09487): Evaluates automated slide generation with computational and quantitative metrics. Core idea: compare code-driven and image-centric slide systems through rendered visual outputs so layout, content coverage, and reliability are scored under one protocol.
- [PodBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14903): Evaluates instruction-aware podcast script generation; the core idea is to test long-context, multi-speaker, audio-oriented writing with quantitative constraints and quality judging over 800 structured samples.
- [AI Personalization Paradox: Reading Highlights for Personalized AI-Assisted Writing Increases Engagement but Undermines Autonomy and Ownership](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17846): Evaluates a personalized writing-assistant interface where reading highlights increase engagement but reduce perceived autonomy and psychological ownership.
- [CDAT / Conditional Divergent Association Task](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20546): Evaluates verbal creativity by separating novelty from contextual appropriateness. Core idea: repair weaknesses of the Divergent Association Task by making creativity conditional on a meaningful context, so noisy word choice is not mistaken for creative writing ability.
- [CLFEC: A New Task for Unified Linguistic and Factual Error Correction in paragraph-level Chinese Professional Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23845): Defines unified linguistic and factual error correction for paragraph-level Chinese professional writing, including prompting, retrieval, and agentic correction baselines.
- [PresentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07244) ([leaderboard](https://presentbench.github.io/)): What it evaluates: slide generation with fine-grained rubrics. Core idea: score presentation content, structure, layout, and visual fidelity through a rubric-based protocol rather than only global preference.
- [Why AI-Generated Text Detection Fails: Evidence from Explainable AI Beyond Benchmark Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23146): Diagnoses AI-text detectors with explainable-AI analysis, showing why high benchmark accuracy can hide brittle cues, domain shortcuts, and unreliable decisions.
- [PosterReward / PosterBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29855): Evaluates high-quality graphic-design poster generation and poster reward models. - [POEMetric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03695): What it evaluates: poetry generation quality. Core idea: score form, theme, creativity, lexical diversity, emotion, and imagery so poetry evaluation is more structured than preference-only judging.
- [PaperWritingBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05018): What it evaluates: AI research paper writing. Core idea: organize abstract, introduction, related work, method, experimental narration, and other paper-writing stages into evaluable tasks, checking academic writing structure, argumentation, citation context, and paragraph quality.
- [HoWToBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19071): What it evaluates: human-level writing capability. Core idea: propose the Tree of Writing, decomposing writing into nodes such as intent understanding, material organization, structural planning, local paragraph generation, and global revision for more fine-grained evaluation of the writing process.
- [Beyond Rating: A Comprehensive Evaluation and Benchmark for AI Reviews](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19502): Evaluates and benchmarks AI reviews beyond scalar ratings.
- [LongSumEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25130): What it evaluates: long-document summarization through question answering. Core idea: turn summary assessment into structured questions whose answers reveal missing, unsupported, or weakly refined content.

- [EditPropBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02083): What it evaluates: factual edit propagation in scientific manuscripts. Core idea: test whether a local factual edit is consistently reflected across related claims, tables, and narrative context.
- [DoGMaTiQ: Automated Generation of Question-and-Answer Nuggets for Report Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04458): Generates question-answer nuggets from source material so report evaluations can check whether long outputs cover required facts and answerable content.
- [StoryRMB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04831): Evaluates reward models on human story preferences. - [NARRA-Gym for Evaluating Interactive Narrative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08503): Provides an interactive narrative environment for evaluating agents on story progression, choice handling, and narrative coherence over turns.
- [When Style Similarity Scores Fail: Diagnosing Raw CSD Cosine in Artist-Style Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09030): Diagnoses raw CSD cosine for artist-style evaluation, showing when style-similarity scores mis-rank generated outputs and need calibration.
- [PaperFit-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10341): Evaluates visual typesetting optimization for scientific documents; the core idea is to turn a compilable LaTeX paper into a publication-ready PDF through render-inspect-edit loops over floats, equations, tables, and page layout defects.

- [Evaluation and Optimization of Large Language Models for Cross-Cultural Sentiment Analysis and Text Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/aieta69357.2026.11508902): Evaluates and tunes LLMs for cross-cultural sentiment analysis and text generation, focusing on whether outputs preserve culturally specific sentiment cues.
- [Trusting AI to detect AI?
