# 1.3.2 Survey

- [Instruction Tuning for Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10792): A foundational review of instruction-tuning datasets, methods, evaluation, and limitations.
- [From Prompt Engineering to Prompt Science with Humans in the Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04122): Positions prompt design for research as a reproducible human-in-the-loop process, using codebook-style labeling, deliberation, and multi-phase verification to document prompt generation.
- [A Survey on Data Selection for LLM Instruction Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05123): Organizes instruction-tuning data selection around commonly used datasets, subset-selection taxonomies, evaluation strategies, reported results, and open challenges.
- [A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering in Large Language Models: Techniques and Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07927): Categorizes prompt-engineering techniques by application area, summarizing each method's prompting strategy, models, datasets, strengths, limitations, and open challenges.
- [A Survey on Recent Advances in LLM-Based Multi-turn Dialogue Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18013): Reviews LLM-based open-domain and task-oriented multi-turn dialogue, covering model adaptation, datasets, metrics, and emerging dialogue-system problems.
- [Efficient Prompting Methods for Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01077): Formalizes resource-efficient prompting through automatic prompt engineering for prompt components and prompt compression in continuous and discrete spaces.
- [The Prompt Report: A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering Techniques](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608): Provides a detailed taxonomy of prompting techniques and evaluation considerations.
- [A Complete Survey on LLM-based AI Chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16937): Traces chatbot development from foundational systems to deployed LLM chatbots, then reviews sector applications, training-data risks, misuse of generated knowledge, and reliability challenges.
- [A Survey of Prompt Engineering Methods in Large Language Models for Different NLP Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12994): Surveys 39 prompting methods across 29 NLP tasks, comparing datasets, LLMs used, task-specific performance, and a prompting-method taxonomy.
- [Unleashing the Power of Data Tsunami: A Comprehensive Survey on Data Assessment and Selection for Instruction Tuning of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02085): Reviews data assessment and selection for instruction tuning, grouping methods into quality-, diversity-, and importance-based categories and comparing officially reported results.
- [A Survey of Calibration Process for Black-Box LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12767): Defines black-box LLM calibration as confidence estimation plus calibration, then surveys API-compatible methods, applications, and reliability challenges.
- [A Survey on Multi-Turn Interaction Capabilities of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09959): Surveys multi-turn interaction capabilities, including context tracking, instruction persistence, and conversational adaptation.
- [A Survey of Automatic Prompt Engineering: An Optimization Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11560): Reviews automatic prompt engineering from an optimization perspective, covering search spaces, objectives, algorithms, and evaluation choices for prompt-based instruction control.
- [A Systematic Survey of Automatic Prompt Optimization Techniques](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16923): Reviews automated prompt search, feedback, and optimization for instruction adherence.
- [Call for Rigor in Reporting Quality of Instruction Tuning Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04807): Shows that arbitrary fine-tuning hyperparameter choices can overturn conclusions about instruction-data quality, using LIMA and Alpaca subsets as case studies.
- [Towards Alignment-Centric Paradigm: A Survey of Instruction Tuning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17184): Updates instruction tuning through an alignment-centered view of data, training, and evaluation.
- [A Comprehensive Review of Supervised Fine-Tuning for Large Language Models in Creative Applications and Content Moderation](https://doi.org/10.1109/icict64420.2025.11005111): Narrows supervised fine-tuning coverage to LLM creative applications and content moderation in an IEEE ICICT review paper.
- [The fine art of fine-tuning: A structured review of advanced LLM fine-tuning techniques](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlp.2025.100144): Categorizes advanced fine-tuning into training-method changes, adapters, quantization, parameter selection, mixture-of-experts, and application-based methods, comparing benchmark trade-offs across datasets and base models.
- [How LLMs Follow Instructions: Skillful Coordination, Not a Universal Mechanism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06015): Analyzes instruction following as skillful coordination rather than a single universal mechanism.
- [A Survey on Quality Evaluation of Instruction Fine-tuning Datasets for Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.3724/2096-7004.di.2025.0021): Classifies instruction fine-tuning dataset-quality evaluation into human, statistics-based, model-based, and LLM-based methods, then outlines limitations and future directions.
- [The role of prompt engineering in enhancing LLMs: a systematic review of applications and ethical implications](https://doi.org/10.11591/ijai.v15.i2.pp1071-1086): Reviews prompt-engineering applications and ethical concerns, comparing chain-of-thought prompting and retrieval-augmented generation for reasoning, hallucination reduction, bias mitigation, and high-stakes reliability.
