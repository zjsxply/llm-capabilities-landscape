# 1.11.2 Survey

- [Personal LLM Agents: Insights and Survey about the Capability, Efficiency and Security](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05459): Maps personal LLM agents around capability, efficiency, and security, with tool access, personalization memory, and user-data risk treated as interacting design constraints.
- [When Large Language Models Meet Vector Databases: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01763): Reviews vector databases as the retrieval substrate for LLM systems, covering embedding storage, indexing, similarity search, RAG applications, and deployment challenges.
- [How NeRFs and 3-D Gaussian Splatting Are Reshaping SLAM: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13255): Adjacent systems survey for spatial tool use; it explains how NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting change SLAM maps, reconstruction pipelines, and downstream robot or AR perception stacks.
- [What Are Tools Anyway? A Survey from the Language Model Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15452): Clarifies boundaries among tools, APIs, actions, external resources, and skills.
- [Exploring Autonomous Agents through the Lens of Large Language Models: A Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04442): Reviews LLM-agent architectures through planning, memory, tool use, multi-agent collaboration, and application patterns, giving a broad taxonomy for external execution loops.
- [A Survey on Retrieval-Augmented Text Generation for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10981): Organizes retrieval-augmented generation by retrieval source, retriever-generator coupling, knowledge integration, evaluation, and failure modes in text generation.
- [A Survey on RAG Meeting LLMs: Towards Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06211): Traces RAG from retrieval corpora and chunking through augmentation strategies, generation, evaluation, and deployment limits for retrieval-backed LLM systems.
- [On the Brittle Foundations of ReAct Prompting for Agentic Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13966): Surveys recent methods, risks, and open problems for Tool Use.
- [Tool Learning with Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17935): Reviews tool discovery, selection, invocation, feedback, datasets, and benchmarks.
- [Research Trends for the Interplay between Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08223): Surveys Tool Use methods, task boundaries, and open challenges relevant to the capability taxonomy.
- [Temporal Knowledge Graph Question Answering: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14191): Temporal Knowledge Graph Question Answering: A Survey surveys tool, API, SQL, function-calling, or structured-query use and helps map methods, risks, or evaluation boundaries.
- [LLM With Tools: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18807): Provides a systems view of tool representation, planning, execution feedback, and evaluation.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Evolution, Current Landscape and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12837): Surveys tool-use, API-use, retrieval, or agent-tool orchestration capability, adding a recent map of methods, benchmarks, risks, or open problems.
- [A Survey of Large Language Model-Based Generative AI for Text-to-SQL: Benchmarks, Applications, Use Cases, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05208): Surveys recent methods, risks, and open problems for Tool Use.
- [A Survey on LLM Test-Time Compute via Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10069): Frames search as a test-time compute primitive, tying task structure, model profiling, search algorithms, and agent frameworks together.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Integrating Large Language Models with Knowledge-Based Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13947): Survey candidate for tool use and retrieval-augmented execution: Surveys integration of LLMs with knowledge-based methods.
- [Position: Emergent Machina Sapiens Urge Rethinking Multi-Agent Paradigms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04388): Position paper arguing that increasingly capable AI systems require multi-agent paradigms with explicit coordination, communication, governance, and interaction assumptions.
- [Interpretable Text Embeddings and Text Similarity Explanation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14862): Surveys interpretable text embeddings and text-similarity explanations, useful for retrieval and RAG diagnostics.
- [Graph-Based Re-ranking: Emerging Techniques, Limitations, and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14802): Surveys tool use, retrieval, API use, or structured retrieval methods and challenges, adding a review lens for this capability area.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Industry: An Interview Study on Use Cases, Requirements, Challenges, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14066): Survey candidate for tool use and retrieval-augmented execution: Reports industry requirements and evaluation challenges for RAG systems.
- [Filtered Vector Search: State-of-the-art and Research Challenges](https://doi.org/10.14778/3750601.3750700): Tutorial-style survey of vector search with relational filters, summarizing optimization strategies and recall pitfalls for retrieval backends used by RAG tools.
- [Model Context Protocol (MCP): Landscape, Security Threats, and Future Research Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23278): Reviews the MCP ecosystem and security issues in modern tool middleware.
- [Humanizing LLMs: A Survey of Psychological Measurements with Tools, Datasets, and Human-Agent Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00049): Surveys psychological measurement tools, datasets, and human-agent application settings, useful for tool-use pages because it treats instruments and tasks as evaluation interfaces.
- [A survey of agent interoperability protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02279): Compares MCP, ACP, A2A, and ANP as protocol layers for tool access, agent communication, and cross-system coordination.
- [From Standalone LLMs to Integrated Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04565): Uses compound AI systems as the organizing frame for combining models, retrievers, tools, planners, and evaluators.
- [Survey of LLM Agent Communication with MCP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05364): Reviews MCP-centered agent communication through software design patterns such as Mediator, Observer, Publish-Subscribe, and Broker.
- [Dynamic and Parametric Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06704): Surveys dynamic and parametric retrieval-augmented generation, clarifying retrieval behavior that tool-use agents rely on.
- [Compound AI Systems Optimization: A Survey of Methods, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08234): Surveys optimization methods for multi-component AI workflows.
- [Conversational Agents: A Framework for Evaluation (CAFE) (Dagstuhl Perspectives Workshop 24352)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11112): Dagstuhl framework that structures conversational-agent evaluation around task goals, interaction context, user effects, and system behavior rather than a single dialogue metric.
- [Engineering RAG Systems for Real-World Applications: Design, Development, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20869): Surveys engineering practices for real-world RAG systems across design, development, and evaluation.
- [A Survey of Optimization Modeling Meets LLMs: Progress and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10047): Surveys how LLMs formulate, solve, explain, and interact with optimization models, making mathematical solvers and modeling languages part of the tool-use boundary.
- [From Search to Reasoning: a Five-Level Rag Capability Framework for Enterprise Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21324): Proposes a five-level enterprise RAG ladder from simple search toward reasoning over governed enterprise data, clarifying what retrieval tools must provide at each maturity stage.
- [SoK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06914): Defines trust-authorization mismatch as a core risk boundary for delegated tool use and multi-agent interaction.
- [ReGraphRAG: Reorganizing Fragmented Knowledge Graphs for Multi-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.290): Reorganizes fragmented knowledge graphs for multi-perspective RAG, making graph restructuring itself a retrieval-stage intervention rather than only a generator prompt trick.
- [Review of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation Research](https://doi.org/10.12677/airr.2025.142040): Reviews GraphRAG research across graph construction, graph retrieval, answer generation, and evaluation, complementing text-only RAG surveys.
- [A Survey of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/ictbai68361.2025.00008): Surveys RAG architectures, retrieval components, generation strategies, evaluation practices, and open challenges for LLM systems that depend on external knowledge.
- [Exploring Research and Tools in AI Security: A Systematic Mapping Study](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3567195): Maps recent work and open issues for tool use capability coverage.
- [Text to SQL AI Agentic Workflow Implementations: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/icatc68823.2025.11407744): Systematic review of text-to-SQL agent workflows, covering schema understanding, query planning, execution feedback, and deployment issues for database tools.
- [From Static Templates to Dynamic Runtime Graphs: A Survey of Workflow Optimization for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22386): Surveys agentic computation graphs that optimize tool, retrieval, code, memory, and verification workflows.
- [Agentic Tool Use in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00835): Organizes tool-use methods into prompting, supervised tool learning, and reward-driven tool policy learning, connecting training recipes with evaluation and failure modes.
- [The Evolution of Tool Use in LLM Agents: From Single-Tool Call to Multi-Tool Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22862): Tracks routing, composition, traces, and coordination in multi-tool orchestration.
- [Agentic AI in Engineering and Manufacturing: Industry Perspectives on Utility, Adoption, Challenges, and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09633): Industry-perspective survey of agentic AI in engineering and manufacturing, organized around utility, adoption barriers, workflow integration, and operational challenges.
- [A Systematic Security Analysis of Model Context Protocol](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAIC67076.2026.11395848): Systematizes MCP vulnerabilities, exploit patterns, and mitigations, grounding tool-use security in server, client, and protocol behavior.
- [Emerging materials for next-generation energy storage: A roadmap to solid-state and beyond](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nxener.2026.100588): Off-axis materials roadmap kept only as an adjacent discovery note; it is about solid-state and next-generation energy-storage materials rather than LLM tool use.
- [Personalized Federated Learning for Sovereign Personal AI Agents: A Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/icecco67619.2026.11488769): Reviews personalized federated learning for sovereign personal AI agents, emphasizing user data locality, personalization, and distributed adaptation.
- [Enabling Intelligence in Digital Twins Through Semantic Enrichment and Multi-Agent Systems: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3671689): Systematic review of semantic enrichment and multi-agent systems in digital twins, relevant where agents use structured domain models as external operational tools.
- [Knowledge Graph Interoperability and Reasoning: A Brief Survey of Alignment Techniques and Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1109/icsc67292.2026.00068): Surveys tool use and retrieval, with emphasis on Knowledge Graph Interoperability and Reasoning: A Brief Survey of Alignment Techniques and Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approaches.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Architecture, Ethical Challenges, and Optimization Strategies](https://doi.org/10.1109/icmlas67792.2026.11483907): Surveys tool use and retrieval, with emphasis on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Architecture, Ethical Challenges, and Optimization Strategies.
- [LLM-Based Agents for Tool Learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41019-025-00296-9): Gives an agent-centric map of tool discovery, selection, invocation, feedback use, and tool-learning evaluation.
- [Large Language Models Meet Next-Generation Networking Technologies: A Review](https://doi.org/10.3390/fi16100365): Reviews LLM use with next-generation networking technologies, including network management, optimization, and protocol-facing applications.
- [Knowledge Graph Combined with Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Enhancing LMs Reasoning: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.54097/h21fky45): Surveys knowledge-graph and RAG combinations for reasoning, focusing on graph construction, retrieval, grounding, and answer generation.
- [Rethinking Pseudo Word Learning in Zero-Shot Composed Image Retrieval: From an Object-Aware Perspective](https://doi.org/10.1145/3726302.3730074): Adjacent composed-image-retrieval work; its value here is the object-aware retrieval lens rather than general tool orchestration.
- [Agent-based approaches in studying algorithm-mediated communication: a methodological review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44382-026-00022-7): Methodological review of agent-based approaches for algorithm-mediated communication, adjacent to agent-tool studies through simulation and interaction modeling.
