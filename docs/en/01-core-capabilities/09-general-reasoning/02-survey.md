# 1.9.2 Survey

- [Comprehensive Survey on Prompts Generating via Knowledge-Guided Chain-of-Thought](https://doi.org/10.26599/ijcs.2024.9100038): Focuses on prompt generation where explicit knowledge steers chain-of-thought construction for harder reasoning tasks.
- [Test-time Computing: from System-1 Thinking to System-2 Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02497): Surveys test-time computing from fast System-1 responses to deliberate System-2 reasoning.
- [Towards Large Reasoning Models: A Survey of Reinforced Reasoning with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09686): Reviews rewards, search, verification, and training pipelines for reinforced reasoning.
- [Reasoning Language Models: A Blueprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11223): Positions reasoning language models as a blueprint for training and inference-time reasoning design.
- [A Survey of In-Context Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07978): Surveys in-context reinforcement learning, linking policy adaptation within context to reasoning and decision making.
- [Logical Reasoning in Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09100): Covers formal, symbolic, and rule-governed reasoning tasks and failures.
- [From System 1 to System 2: A Survey of Reasoning Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17419): Surveys the shift from fast generation to deliberate multi-step reasoning.
- [Stop Overthinking: A Survey on Efficient Reasoning for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16419): Reviews adaptive compute and cost-quality trade-offs for efficient reasoning.
- [A Survey of Frontiers in LLM Reasoning: Inference Scaling, Learning to Reason, and Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09037): Connects inference scaling, learning-to-reason, and agentic reasoning systems.
- [Efficient Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10903): Makes compute efficiency the organizing axis for reasoning, spanning compact models, adaptive inference, and cost-quality trade-offs.
- [Perception, Reason, Think, and Plan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04921): Treats multimodal reasoning as an integrated loop of perception, deliberate reasoning, planning, and action-oriented cognition.
- [Reasoning Beyond Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16782): Centers latent chain-of-thought, studying reasoning traces that occur in hidden states rather than fully verbalized rationales.
- [A Survey of Reinforcement Learning for Large Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08827): Reviews RL objectives, reward design, data, infrastructure, and scaling challenges behind large reasoning models.
- [A Survey of Inductive Reasoning for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10182): Reviews inductive reasoning methods, benchmarks, sandbox evaluation, data augmentation, post-training, and test-time scaling for LLMs.
- [Wiring the 'Why'](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08016): Provides a taxonomy and survey of abductive reasoning in LLMs, linking philosophical foundations, task definitions, benchmarks, and model behavior.
- [A short survey on small reasoning models: training, inference, applications, and research directions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-025-50990-0): Surveys small reasoning models, covering training, inference, deployment, and research directions for compact reasoning systems.
