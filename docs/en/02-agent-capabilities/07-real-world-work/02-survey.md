# 2.7.2 Survey

- [Large Language Models for Constructing and Optimizing Machine Learning Workflows: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10478): Connects agents, AutoML, pipeline search, data processing, selection, and evaluation.
- [A Survey on Large Language Model-based Agents for Statistics and Data Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14222): Organizes data cleaning, analysis, visualization, modeling, reporting, and human collaboration.
- [Towards Scientific Intelligence: A Survey of LLM-based Scientific Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24047): Covers literature, experiment planning, analysis, hypotheses, and tools for professional research work.
- [Table Question Answering in the Era of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09671): Surveys LLM-based table QA tasks, methods, benchmarks, and evaluation issues for structured-data work.
- [Empowering Real-World: A Survey on the Technology, Practice, and Evaluation of LLM-driven Industry Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17491): Reviews deployed settings, task practice, evaluation, and operational constraints.
- [A Survey of Data Agents: Emerging Paradigm or Overstated Hype?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23587): Examines durable gains in data access, transformation, analysis, and decision support.
- [What Really Matters for Table LLMs?](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.findings-eacl.195): Meta-evaluates model and data effects for table LLMs, clarifying which factors drive structured-data question-answering performance.
- [Agentic information systems](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-025-00861-0): Frames agentic information systems as autonomous, decision-making systems that can act in unstructured environments and delegate work, linking agents to organizational IS design.
