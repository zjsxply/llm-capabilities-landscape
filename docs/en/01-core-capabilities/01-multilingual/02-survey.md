# 1.1.2 Survey

- [Beyond Neutrality: Mapping Two Decades of Research on Machine Translation Bias (2005–2024)](https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440251392700): Uses bibliometric analysis and close reading of Web of Science and Scopus studies from 2005 to 2024 to map publication phases, bias themes, methods, and equity risks in machine translation.
- [A Survey of Multilingual Neural Machine Translation Based on Sparse Models](https://doi.org/10.26599/tst.2023.9010097): Categorizes sparse-model approaches for massively multilingual NMT, comparing their parameter efficiency, interference reduction, modeling principles, and translation-quality tradeoffs.
- [Natural Language Processing for Dialects of a Language: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05632): Reviews dialect NLP through resource scarcity, dialect identification, normalization, generation, machine translation, and evaluation gaps for regional language varieties.
- [Claim Detection for Automated Fact-checking: A Survey on Monolingual, Multilingual and Cross-Lingual Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11969): Reviews multilingual claim-detection work for automated fact-checking, organizing systems around verifiability, priority, similarity, data sources, and cross-platform misinformation settings.
- [How do Large Language Models Handle Multilingualism?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18815): Proposes and probes the MWork hypothesis that LLMs route multilingual inputs through English-centric intermediate reasoning while preserving language-specific neurons for input and output handling.
- [From Handcrafted Features to LLMs: A Brief Survey for Machine Translation Quality Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14118): Traces machine-translation quality estimation from handcrafted features and traditional machine learning to neural models and LLM-based evaluators.
- [A Survey on Multilingual Large Language Models: Corpora, Alignment, and Bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00929): Surveys multilingual LLM data, alignment, evaluation, and bias, linking benchmark gaps to upstream choices.
- [Multilingual Large Language Model: A Survey of Resources, Taxonomy and Frontiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04925): Maps multilingual resources, task taxonomy, evaluation frontiers, and open challenges.
- [Understanding Cross-Lingual Alignment: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06228): Reviews cross-lingual alignment mechanisms and evaluation settings behind transfer robustness.
- [Culturally Aware and Adapted NLP: A Taxonomy and a Survey of the State of the Art](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03930): Proposes a taxonomy for culturally aware and culturally adapted NLP, covering cultural knowledge representation, data collection, adaptation methods, and evaluation risks.
- [The sociolinguistic foundations of language modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09241): Reframes language models as models of language varieties, connecting corpus design to social bias, domain adaptation, alignment, language change, and scaling.
- [Pitfalls and Outlooks in Using COMET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15366): Analyzes reliability pitfalls in COMET machine-translation evaluation, including metric misuse, interpretation limits, and outlooks for safer MT-quality assessment.
- [Computational Approaches to Arabic-English Code-Switching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13318): Reviews methods and resources for Arabic-English code-switching, a hard multilingual mixed-language setting.
- [A Survey of Large Language Models for Arabic Language and its Dialects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20238): Reviews Arabic LLM architectures, pretraining datasets, monolingual and multilingual coverage, downstream tasks, openness, and dialectal data gaps.
- [The Zeno’s Paradox of ‘Low-Resource’ Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20817): Critiques "low-resource language" as a moving target where data, tooling, and community needs keep raising the bar for adequate language technology.
- [Survey of Cultural Awareness in Language Models: Text and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00860): Surveys cultural awareness in language models across text and multimodal settings.
- [Why We Build Local Large Language Models: An Observational Analysis from 35 Japanese and Multilingual LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14471): Compares 35 Japanese, English, and multilingual LLMs on Japanese and English benchmarks to separate transferable general abilities from Japanese-specific knowledge and translation skills.
- [Overview of the First Workshop on Language Models for Low-Resource Languages (LoResLM 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16365): Summarizes LoResLM 2025 themes around data creation, evaluation, adaptation, and deployment for language models in low-resource communities.
- [A survey of multilingual large language models](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2024.101118): Reviews multilingual LLM data, architectures, alignment, evaluation, bias, and deployment challenges across high-resource and low-resource languages.
- [Recent Highlights in Multilingual and Multimodal Speech Translation](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.iwslt-1.29): Summarizes recent IWSLT directions in multilingual and multimodal speech translation, including speech-to-text, speech-to-speech, and visual-context translation settings.
- [Arabic Text Formality Modification: A Review and Future Research Directions](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3511661): Reviews Arabic formality modification datasets, rule-based and neural approaches, evaluation practices, and open issues for dialectal and Modern Standard Arabic rewriting.
- [Indigenous Languages Spoken in Argentina: A Survey of NLP and Speech Resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09943): Surveys NLP and speech resources for Indigenous languages spoken in Argentina.
- [A Survey of Code-switched Arabic NLP: Progress, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13419): Reviews Arabic code-switching resources, methods, challenges, and research gaps across dialectal and foreign-language mixing.
- [Large Multimodal Models for Low-Resource Languages: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05568): Surveys low-resource multilingual LMM adaptation through data creation, visual enhancement, transfer, and fusion strategies.
- [A Survey of Multilingual Reasoning in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09457): Surveys multilingual reasoning data, methods, and failure modes beyond translation quality.
- [NaijaNLP: A Survey of Nigerian Low-Resource Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19784): Maps NLP resources and methods for Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and other Nigerian low-resource languages.
- [Parallel Corpora for Machine Translation in Low-resource Indic Languages: A Comprehensive Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04797): Reviews parallel-corpus resources and data challenges for low-resource Indic machine translation.
- [Bridging the Linguistic Divide: A Survey on Leveraging Large Language Models for Machine Translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01919): Reviews LLM-based machine translation methods and evaluation for multilingual generation workflows.
- [Automatic Evaluation Metrics for Document-level Translation: Overview, Challenges and Trends](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14804): Reviews reference-based and reference-free document-level MT metrics, including traditional, model-based, and LLM-as-a-judge approaches plus challenges around discourse context, alignment, and interpretability.
- [A Survey of NLP Progress in Sino-Tibetan Low-Resource Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.396/): Reviews ACL Anthology coverage of Sino-Tibetan low-resource languages, showing limited representation despite more venues, techniques, and discussion of the language family over time.
- [Overcoming Data Scarcity in Generative Language Modelling for Low-Resource Languages: A Systematic Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04531): Systematically reviews data scarcity in low-resource generative language modeling, relevant to multilingual coverage.
- [Cross-Linguistic Transfer in Multilingual NLP: The Role of Language Families and Morphology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13908): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for multilingual capability; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
- [Editing Across Languages: A Survey of Multilingual Knowledge Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14393): Organizes multilingual knowledge editing by cross-lingual edit propagation, locality, portability, language imbalance, and evaluation protocols for factual updates.
- [Charting the Landscape of African NLP: Mapping Progress and Shaping the Road Ahead](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21315): Maps African NLP progress across 884 recent papers, language-resource gaps, community-led initiatives, and future directions for underrepresented languages.
- [The Landscape of Arabic Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01340): Surveys Arabic LLM development, resources, evaluation, and gaps for Arabic-centric language capability.
- [Beyond the Sentence: A Survey on Context-Aware Machine Translation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07583): Reviews LLM-based context-aware MT through prompting, fine-tuning, automatic post-editing, and translation-agent work beyond sentence-level baselines.
- [Beyond Weaponization: NLP Security for Medium and Lower-Resourced Languages in Their Own Right](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03473): Surveys multilingual security or capability gaps; core idea: map risks and evaluation needs for medium- and lower-resource languages.
- [Natural Language Processing for Tigrinya: Current State and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17974): Reviews more than 50 Tigrinya NLP studies from 2011 to 2025 across morphology, tagging, NER, MT, QA, speech recognition, and synthesis, highlighting morphology-aware and community-centered directions.
- [Social Bias in Multilingual Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20201): Surveys social-bias measurement and mitigation issues in multilingual language models.
- [Culture is Everywhere: A Call for Intentionally Cultural Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01301): Calls for intentional cultural evaluation, framing culture as a systematic dimension of model assessment rather than an incidental subgroup label.
- [Multilingual Vision-Language Models, A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22123): Surveys 33 multilingual vision-language models and 23 benchmarks, contrasting language-neutral representations with culturally aware evaluation and data design.
- [Beyond Monolingual Assumptions: A Survey of Code-Switched NLP in the Era of Large Language Models across Modalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07037): Surveys code-switched NLP in the LLM era across modalities, mapping tasks, resources, and evaluation issues for mixed-language settings.
- [Evaluating Arabic Large Language Models: A Survey of Benchmarks, Methods, and Gaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13430): Reviews more than 40 Arabic LLM benchmarks across knowledge, NLP tasks, culture and dialects, and target-specific evaluations, contrasting native, translated, and synthetic data collection.
- [Tibetan Language and AI: A Comprehensive Survey of Resources, Methods and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19144): Surveys Tibetan AI resources, NLP and speech tasks, MT, LLM developments, datasets, tools, and bottlenecks such as data sparsity, orthographic variation, and missing unified metrics.
- [Culture Cartography: Mapping the Landscape of Cultural Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27672): Introduces the CultureCartography mixed-initiative methodology and CultureExplorer tool, where LLM uncertainty and human edits surface culture-specific knowledge gaps for culturally grounded systems.
- [Multimodal Neural Machine Translation: A Survey of the State of the Art](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1125/): Surveys MNMT milestones, datasets, and methods for integrating visual or other non-text modalities with textual translation inputs.
- [Explainability and Interpretability of Multilingual Large Language Models: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1033): Surveys explainability and interpretability methods for multilingual LLMs, connecting cross-lingual behavior, bias, and diagnostic tooling.
- [From Challenges to Opportunities](https://doi.org/10.1109/MITP.2025.3569009): Reviews how agentic AI can support low-resource languages, covering data scarcity, cross-lingual transfer, tooling gaps, and deployment challenges for multilingual agents.
- [A Systematic Review of Few-Shot and Zero-Shot Learning for NLP in Low-Resource Languages: Insights and Challenges](https://doi.org/10.1109/IC3INA68387.2025.11325582): Reviews 21 low-resource NLP studies from 2020 to 2025, comparing FSL and ZSL across tasks and highlighting prompt learning, cross-lingual transfer, typological variation, and benchmark bias.
- [Multimodal Methods for Improving Natural Language Processing in Low-Resource Languages:Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRTEECT67512.2025.11448668): Surveys multimodal routes for low-resource NLP applications and challenges where text-only datasets, parsers, and annotated resources are limited.
- [Video-guided Machine Translation: A Survey of Models, Datasets, and Challenges](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.ijcnlp-long.178): Surveys video-guided machine translation models, datasets, and challenges, emphasizing how temporal visual context changes multimodal translation beyond image-guided MT.
- [A Systematic Review on Machine Translation and Transliteration Techniques for Code-Mixed Indo-Aryan Languages](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.wat-1.6): Systematically reviews MT and transliteration techniques for code-mixed Indo-Aryan languages, focusing on regional mixed-script and mixed-language processing.
- [Research challenges and future directions in transformer-based neural machine translation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2025.131062): Reviews transformer-based NMT challenges and future directions, with the multilingual-page fit strongest for MT architecture and evaluation gaps.
- [Incorporating Diverse Perspectives in Cultural Alignment: Survey of Evaluation Benchmarks Through A Three-Dimensional Framework](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.862): Organizes cultural-alignment evaluation benchmarks with a three-dimensional framework for capturing whose perspectives are represented, how culture is operationalized, and what evaluation setting is used.
- [Exploring cultural commonsense in multilingual large language models: A survey](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.is.2025.102649): Reviews cultural-commonsense resources, probing tasks, cross-cultural knowledge gaps, and cultural-bias evaluation for multilingual LLMs.
- [Machine Translation Performance for Low-Resource Languages: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3562918): Applies a PRISMA-style review to 69 low-resource MT studies from 2020 to 2024, analyzing data scarcity, language complexity, NMT performance, and strategies such as active learning, augmentation, multilingual models, and transfer learning.
- [Overview of Research on Low-Resource Language Machine Translation Based on Artificial Intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccea65460.2025.11103314): Reviews AI-based low-resource MT around data augmentation, transfer learning, pretraining, neural architectures, and evaluation challenges under scarce parallel data.
- [Multilingual Relation Extraction: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3604258): Reviews multilingual relation-extraction methods, evaluation datasets, feature choices, target domains, extraction levels, language coverage, and low-resource limitations.
- [Approaches for Summarization of Articles Across Multiple Languages: A Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/icmlas64557.2025.10968896): Reviews approaches for summarizing articles across multiple languages.
- [A decade of gender bias in machine translation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2025.101257): Surveys a decade of gender bias in machine translation, relevant to multilingual generation and bias evaluation.
- [Culturally-Grounded Governance for Multilingual Language Models: Rights, Data Boundaries, and Accountable AI Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00497): A survey or related-work map for multilingual capability. Core idea: Multilingual large language models (MLLMs) are increasingly deployed across cultural, linguistic, and political contexts, yet existing governance frameworks largely assume English-centric data, homogeneous user populations, and abstract notions of fairness.
- [Code Mixologist : A Practitioner's Guide to Building Code-Mixed LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11181): Surveys code-mixed LLM data, modeling, evaluation, and safety issues, turning code-switching research into a practical playbook for building and testing mixed-language systems.
- [Multilingual Hate Speech Detection and Counterspeech Generation: A Comprehensive Survey and Practical Guide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19279): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for multilingual capability; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
- [Arabic Dialect NLP: A Unified Taxonomic, Methodological, and Trend‑Driven Survey](https://doi.org/10.70715/jitcai.2026.v3.i2.051): Organizes Arabic dialect NLP by taxonomy, methods, and research trends, covering dialect resources, tasks, modeling paradigms, and low-resource challenges.
- [Multilingual NLP Models: A Review of Techniques, Tools, and Real-World Use Cases](https://doi.org/10.1109/iitcee67948.2026.11394472): Reviews multilingual NLP techniques and use cases across sentiment analysis, machine translation, and hate-speech detection, with attention to XLM-R, GPT-4, Llama-2, transfer learning, tokenization, bias, and evaluation inconsistency.
- [A Comprehensive Review of English-Tamil Machine Translation from Statistical Models to Neuro-Symbolic Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1109/icict68280.2026.11510898): Reviews English-Tamil MT from rule-based and statistical systems to neural, hybrid, transfer-learning, and neuro-symbolic approaches that address Tamil data scarcity and morphology.
- [Adversarial Robustness in Multilingual and Code-Mixed NLP: Insights From Indian Language Settings](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3691377): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for multilingual capability; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
- [Lost in Translation? Found in Evaluation: A Comprehensive Survey on Sentence-Level Translation Evaluation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3735970): Reviews sentence-level MT evaluation datasets, human assessment practices, automatic metrics, metric-selection decision trees, and cross-language meta-evaluation gaps.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Transformer-Based Machine Translation: Identifying Research Gaps and Solutions for Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3773076): Surveys transformer-based machine translation and identifies research gaps and LLM-era solutions for multilingual generation.
- [Arabic Natural Language Processing (NLP): A Comprehensive Review of Challenges, Techniques, and Emerging Trends](https://doi.org/10.3390/computers14110497): Reviews Arabic NLP challenges in morphology, dialects, syntax, diacritics, ambiguity, and datasets, then surveys tokenization, normalization, NER, POS tagging, sentiment, summarization, QA, MT, and LLM trends.
- [Large Language Model-Based Translation Agents: A Review and Future Perspectives](https://doi.org/10.63313/llcs.9146): Reviews LLM translation agents by workflow roles, planning and revision loops, human interaction, evaluation, and future directions for agentic machine translation.
- [Multilingual question answering systems for knowledge graphs - a survey](https://doi.org/10.3233/sw-243633): Reviews semantic parsing, entity linking, cross-lingual retrieval, query generation, and evaluation settings for multilingual KG question answering.
- [Detecting hate in diversity: a survey of multilingual code-mixed image and video analysis](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-025-01167-w): Surveys datasets, modality fusion, code-mixed language phenomena, and evaluation challenges for multilingual hate detection in images and videos.
- [Human-centered LLMs for Inclusive Language Technology: The Need to Embrace Variation Holistically in NLP](https://doi.org/10.15439/2025f4610): Argues that inclusive language technology should model linguistic variation holistically rather than treating variation as noise to be normalized away.
- [Tri-dimensional perspectives in hate speech detection: a review of multilingual, multimodal and fairness-aware approaches](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42452-025-08047-1): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for multilingual capability; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
