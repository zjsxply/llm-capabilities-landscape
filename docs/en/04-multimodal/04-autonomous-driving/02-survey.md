# 4.4.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Multimodal Large Language Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12320): A foundational review of perception, reasoning, planning, datasets, and benchmarks.
- [A Survey for Foundation Models in Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01105): Reviews foundation models across perception, prediction, planning, simulation, and evaluation.
- [A Survey of World Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11260): Surveys driving world models for prediction, simulation, planning, and evaluation.
- [Knowledge Integration Strategies in Autonomous Vehicle Prediction and Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10477): Surveys knowledge-integration strategies for autonomous-vehicle prediction and planning.
- [Multi-Agent Autonomous Driving Systems with Large Language Models: A Survey of Recent Advances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16804): Reviews communication, coordination, scenario reasoning, and decision support.
- [Trajectory Prediction for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03262): Reviews progress, limitations, and future directions in autonomous-driving trajectory prediction.
- [A Survey of Reinforcement Learning-Based Motion Planning for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23650): Reviews reinforcement-learning motion-planning methods from a driving-task perspective.
- [Foundation Models for Autonomous Driving Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00911): Provides a structured literature roadmap for integrating foundation models into autonomous-driving infrastructure, in-vehicle systems, evaluation, and assurance.
- [Generative AI for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08854): Surveys generative AI across the autonomous-driving stack. Core idea: connect image, LiDAR, trajectory, occupancy, video generation, LLM-guided reasoning, synthetic data, digital twins, evaluation, and safety into one frontier map.
- [A Survey on Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24044): Bridges perception, language-grounded reasoning, and action generation.
- [Foundation Models for Autonomous Driving Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08302): Surveys perception-centered foundation models for autonomous driving across sensing, representation, reasoning, and deployment capabilities.
- [Maps for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12632): Surveys HD, lightweight, and implicit map representations, production workflows, and integration with end-to-end autonomous-driving frameworks.
- [A Review of Learning-Based Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11944): Reviews learning-based autonomous-driving motion planning and frames data-driven optimal control as a path toward safer, adaptive planning.
- [Trends in motion prediction toward deployable and generalizable autonomy](https://doi.org/10.1108/FTROB-05-2025-0079): Reviews motion prediction for deployable and generalizable autonomy, bridging perception and decision-making.
- [Large (Vision) Language Models for Autonomous Vehicles](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2025.3628969): Surveys current trends and future directions for large language and vision-language models in autonomous vehicles.
- [End-to-End Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2025.3635092): Surveys the shift from classic end-to-end driving paradigms to large-model-empowered autonomous-driving systems.
- [A survey of transformer architectures for autonomous driving](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2025.130338): Reviews transformer architectures used across autonomous-driving perception, prediction, planning, and control.
- [Large Language Models for Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC60802.2025.11423242): Reviews technical challenges, datasets, and simulation environments for LLM use in autonomous driving.
- [A survey of decision-making and planning methods for self-driving vehicles](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbot.2025.1451923): Reviews decision-making and planning algorithms for self-driving vehicles, with emphasis on neurorobotics and autonomous-driving deployment challenges.
- [Artificial Intelligence for Modeling and Simulation of Mixed Automated and Human Traffic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12857): Surveys AI methods for modeling and simulating traffic where automated and human-driven vehicles interact.
- [A comprehensive survey of pretrained models for autonomous driving](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2026.104457): Reviews pretrained-model methods and applications across the autonomous-driving stack.
- [Design, features, and development of in-vehicle intelligent assistant](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmcj.2026.102210): Systematically reviews in-vehicle intelligent-assistant design, features, and development trends.
- [Vision-Language Models in Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.56028/aetr.16.1.558.2026): Reviews VLMs for traffic-scene understanding, referential grounding, and interpretable autonomy in autonomous driving.
- [A Survey of Human Intelligence Augmented Artificial Intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42154-025-00361-z): Reviews human-intelligence-augmented AI for autonomous driving, focusing on how human knowledge can support robust open-world driving behavior.
- [A Survey on Future Physical World Generation for Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1145/3769748.3773345): Reviews physical-world generation for autonomous driving, connecting generative simulation with future driving-scene modeling.
- [A Comprehensive Literature Review on Modular Approaches to Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.3390/smartcities8030079): Reviews modular autonomous-driving systems across perception, planning, and control for road and racing scenarios.
