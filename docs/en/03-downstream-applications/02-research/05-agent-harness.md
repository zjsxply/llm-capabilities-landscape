# 3.2.5 Agent Harness

- [MLAgentBench Research Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03302) ([open-source code](https://github.com/snap-stanford/MLAgentBench)) is the baseline research agent used for research-task evaluation in MLAgentBench.
- [CoQuest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06155) ([open-source code](https://github.com/yiren-liu/coquest)) is an LLM-based research-question co-creation system; it compares breadth-first and depth-first interaction designs for helping researchers frame candidate questions while keeping human control over ideation.
- [The AI Scientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06292) ([open-source code](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist)) is the earlier open-source milestone for automated ML research, covering idea generation, code, experiments, visualization, paper writing, and automated review; keep it distinct from AI Scientist v2.
- [PaperQA2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13740) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa)) is a scientific-literature synthesis agent optimized for cited answers and contradiction discovery, providing a strong practical reference for research-literature skills before full end-to-end research pipelines.
- [Scideator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14634) is a human-LLM scientific ideation system grounded in research-paper facet recombination; it extracts and recombines purpose, mechanism, and evaluation facets so researchers can explore and screen candidate ideas with literature context.
- [IdeaSynth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04025) is a research-idea development system that uses literature-grounded feedback to evolve and compose problem, solution, evaluation, and contribution facets, making problem framing and contribution positioning explicit in the ideation loop.
- [BudgetMLAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07464) (open-source code: not released) uses multi-agent collaboration, retrieval reuse, and LLM cascades for low-cost research-task automation on MLAgentBench, subject to the paper.
- [Dolphin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03916) ([project page](https://alpha-innovator.github.io/Dolphin-project-page); open-source code: no stable public repository found) is an early closed-loop auto-research framework that connects feedback-conditioned idea generation, code-template implementation/debugging, result analysis, and the next research round.
- [Agent Laboratory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04227) ([open-source code](https://github.com/SamuelSchmidgall/AgentLaboratory); [project page](https://agentlaboratory.github.io/)) is an end-to-end research-assistant workflow that starts from a human idea and coordinates literature review, experimentation, and report writing with human feedback checkpoints.
- [AIDE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13138) ([open-source code](https://github.com/WecoAI/aideml)) represents the iterative code-experiment line for research/data-science agents and is one of the central open-source ML engineering agents in the official initial MLE-bench evaluation; its design focus is experiment-tree iteration via `propose experiment -> edit/run code -> inspect metric/log -> refine`, rather than one-shot modeling-script generation.
- [AI co-scientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18864) (open-source code: not released): Google Research's Gemini 2.0 multi-agent scientific hypothesis-generation system, centered on an asynchronous hypothesis tournament of `generate -> debate -> evolve`; it reports experimentally validated clues in drug repurposing, target discovery, and bacterial evolution mechanisms.
- [ReviewAgents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08506): A reviewer-agent harness for academic paper reviews. Core idea: use Review-CoT data and structured reviewer-agent reasoning over summary, related work, strengths, weaknesses, and final recommendation to better match human review logic.
- [AgentRxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18102) ([open-source code](https://github.com/SamuelSchmidgall/AgentLaboratory); [project page](https://agentrxiv.github.io/)) adds shared research memory to autonomous research labs by letting agents upload, retrieve, and build on prior agent-generated reports, making it a close external analogue of ARIS research-wiki memory.
- [MC-NEST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19309): A scientific hypothesis-generation harness using Monte Carlo tree search and Nash-equilibrium self-refinement. Core idea: iterate hypothesis proposal, exploration, diversity control, and self-validation so research ideation becomes a search process rather than one-shot brainstorming.
- [AI-Newton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01538): A concept-driven scientific-discovery system for deriving physical laws from multi-experiment data. Core idea: autonomously propose interpretable concepts and progressively generalize laws without prior physical knowledge, making law discovery an agentic research workflow.
- [Multi-Agent LLMs with Interpretable Physics Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01911): Coordinates LLM roles with interpretable reasoning, symbolic manipulation, and human-facing checks for physics research assistance.
- [AI Scientist v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066) ([open-source code](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2)) emphasizes an end-to-end loop from research ideas to experiments and paper drafts.
- [Paper2Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17192) ([open-source code](https://github.com/going-doer/Paper2Code)) is specialized research-agent work for "paper to code implementation."
- [Robin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13400) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Future-House/robin)) is FutureHouse's lab-in-the-loop multi-agent scientific discovery system; it connects literature search, hypothesis generation, experiment design, data analysis, and subsequent hypothesis updates, and completed experimental validation in a dAMD drug-candidate discovery case.
- [R&D-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14738) ([open-source code](https://github.com/microsoft/RD-Agent)) focuses on role-divided multi-agent workflow orchestration for R&D scenarios.
- [R&D-Agent-Quant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15155) ([open-source code](https://github.com/microsoft/RD-Agent)) extends R&D-Agent to quantitative finance; its core workflow decomposes quant research into hypothesis-driven factor mining, code generation, backtesting feedback, and adaptive scheduling for joint factor-model optimization.
- [AI-Researcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18705) ([open-source code](https://github.com/HKUDS/AI-Researcher)) is an autonomous scientific-innovation system paired with Scientist-Bench, covering literature review, hypothesis generation, algorithm implementation, and manuscript preparation through guided and open-ended research workflows.
- [MLR-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19955) ([open-source code](https://github.com/chchenhui/mlrbench)) is the modular scaffold provided by the MLR-Bench paper, covering the four stages of idea, proposal, experiment, and writing.
- [TreeReview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07642): A hierarchical question-tree harness for LLM-based scientific peer review. Core idea: recursively decompose review questions, dynamically expand follow-ups, and aggregate leaf evidence back to full reviews or actionable comments.
- [MLE-STAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15692) ([open-source code](https://github.com/google/adk-samples/tree/main/python/agents/machine-learning-engineering)) is Google ADK's machine-learning engineering agent. Its core workflow is `web/search reference solutions -> generate baseline -> targeted refinement around bottlenecks`, making it an important strong open-source baseline after AIDE on MLE-bench.
- [ML-Master](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16499) ([open-source code](https://github.com/sjtu-sai-agents/ML-Master)) targets AI-for-AI machine-learning research tasks, integrating exploration and reasoning into a multi-role collaboration workflow; it is a representative research agent on MLE-bench/MLE tasks.
- [Agent Reviewers](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/lu25p.html): A multimodal reviewer-agent harness with shared memory for paper review. Core idea: combine reviewer personas, multimodal paper feedback, and a shared historical-paper memory pool so AI review systems can emulate field-aware peer-review processes.
- [STELLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02004) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a self-evolving multi-agent system for biomedical research; its core is an evolving Template Library and dynamic Tool Ocean, allowing tool-creation agents to continuously discover and integrate new bioinformatics tools.
- [AIRA-dojo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02554) ([open-source code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/aira-dojo)) is a "dojo-style research-agent behavior framework" for constructing and evaluating research-agent behaviors.
- [StructSense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03674): A modular agentic framework for structured information extraction from scientific literature. Core idea: combine ontology-guided symbolic knowledge, self-evaluative refinement, and human-in-the-loop validation so extraction quality can be benchmarked across specialized tasks.
- [cmbagent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07257): An open-source planning-and-control multi-agent system for autonomous scientific discovery. Core idea: orchestrate specialist agents for literature and code retrieval, coding, result interpretation, critique, and local execution, exposing a reusable no-human-in-loop scientific workflow beyond single-paper generation.
- [DREAMS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14267): A hierarchical multi-agent research harness for DFT materials simulation. Core idea: coordinate structure generation, convergence testing, HPC scheduling, and error handling through LLM agents and a shared canvas so complex simulation workflows become more autonomous and auditable.
- [Beyond Brainstorming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04575): Studies a structured multi-agent harness for scientific idea generation. Core idea: compare leader-led versus leaderless teams, group size, interdisciplinarity, and seniority so proposal quality can be tied to concrete collaboration design choices.
- [PaperEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05129): An LLM-based paper-evaluation harness with domain-aware retrieval and latent reasoning. Core idea: retrieve concurrent related work, reason over novelty and methodology, and iteratively refine relative quality rankings for academic-paper filtering.
- [SciLink](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06569): An open-source multi-agent workflow for materials characterization with theory in the loop. Core idea: connect experimental observation, novelty assessment, and theoretical simulation so unexpected findings can be routed into follow-up analysis rather than discarded.
- [K-Dense Analyst](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07043) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a hierarchical multi-agent system for bioinformatics analysis. Core idea: connect high-level scientific goals, code execution, and result validation with a planning loop and validated execution loop.
- [RePro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16671) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) emphasizes fine-grained verification and reflective repair, and is representative paper-reproduction-agent work in the PaperBench Code-Dev direction.
- [Automated Generation of Research Workflows from Academic Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12955) ([code](https://github.com/ZH-heng/research_workflow)): A paper-to-workflow mining framework that extracts workflow-descriptive paragraphs, generates process phrases, classifies them into stages, and renders structured research flowcharts for reproducibility analysis.
- [ToolUniverse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23426) ([open-source code](https://github.com/mims-harvard/ToolUniverse)) is the tool ecosystem and MCP/SDK substrate for building AI scientists; it standardizes 1000+ scientific tools, models, databases, and APIs as callable components, and supports generating/optimizing tool interfaces from natural language.
- [AutoLabs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25651): A self-correcting multi-agent lab-planning harness for autonomous chemical experimentation. Core idea: decompose natural-language experimental goals into specialist-agent subtasks, tool-assisted calculations, and hardware-ready protocols with iterative validation.
- [DeepEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06056) ([open-source code](https://github.com/liugangcode/deepevolve)) combines deep research with algorithm evolution, linking external knowledge retrieval, cross-file code editing, debugging, implementation, and benchmark feedback into a scientific algorithm-discovery loop.
- [AutoMLGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08511) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Alpha-Innovator/InternAgent)) provides fine-grained AutoML optimization navigation for coding agents; it reports medal rate and valid-submission metrics on MLE-bench and is a paper-version extension of the InternAgent/MLE engineering line.
- [MOSAIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08804) (open-source code: the paper says it will be released after acceptance) is a multi-agent scientific coding system for SciCode, decomposing, executing, and verifying scientific coding tasks through task-intelligent orchestration, and reporting main-problem/subproblem performance under the official SciCode protocol.
- [ReviewerToo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08867): A modular harness for studying AI-assisted peer review. Core idea: configure specialized reviewer personas and structured evaluation criteria over real conference submissions so AI program-committee workflows can be tested before deployment.
- [CodeEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14150) ([open-source code](https://github.com/inter-co/science-codeevolve)) is an open-source evolutionary coding agent for algorithmic discovery, using islands-based search, LLM orchestration, execution feedback, and task metrics to guide candidate variation and selection.
- [freephdlabor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15624) ([project page](https://freephdlabor.github.io/); [open-source code](https://github.com/ltjed/freephdlabor)) is a personalized research-group harness with dynamic workflows, workspace communication, context compaction, persistent memory, and non-blocking human intervention for sustaining longer research projects.
- [TrustResearcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20844): A transparent multi-agent research-ideation harness. Core idea: expose structured knowledge curation, diversified idea generation, multi-stage idea selection, and expert-panel synthesis as inspectable stages so researchers can audit and steer evidence-grounded ideation.
- [Famou-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26144) ([open-source code](https://github.com/baidubce/FM-Agent)) is a closed-loop coding and experimentation agent for machine-learning engineering tasks; in the MLE-bench context it is closer to a "competition modeling expert harness," emphasizing historical experience, experiment planning, execution feedback, and multi-round model/feature iteration.
- [Generative Reviewer Agents](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-industry.8/): A reviewer-agent harness for scalable peer-review simulation. Core idea: extend LLMs with reviewer personas and memory so agents can provide detailed feedback, predict paper outcomes, and support experiments about reviewer expertise and fairness.
- [InfAL](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.667/): A multi-agent adversarial-learning harness for research ideation. Core idea: have LLM agents challenge and refine generated research ideas at inference time, then use relative ranking to screen novelty and feasibility without additional training.
- [SciCompanion](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1315/): A graph-grounded research-evaluation harness for scientific arguments. Core idea: build dynamic knowledge graphs from recent papers, domain databases, and metadata, then use multi-hop reasoning to generate structured critiques aligned with expert judgments.
- [Kosmos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a long-running AI scientist for data-driven scientific discovery; through a structured world model, it connects literature-search and data-analysis agents and can continuously read papers, write code, generate hypotheses, and output traceable reports over 12-hour-scale runs.
- [ArchPilot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03985) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a multi-agent machine-learning engineering framework for MLE-bench; compared with baselines such as AIDE and ML-Master, it emphasizes agent division of labor, model-solution search, and experiment-feedback-driven architecture improvement.
- [AgenticSciML](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07262) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) targets scientific machine-learning discovery; 10+ specialized agents jointly design SciML architectures, loss functions, and training strategies through structured debate, method memory, and evolutionary search.
- [Autonomous Knowledge Pipeline for AI Research](https://doi.org/10.1109/AAIML67890.2026.11498163): An LLM-agent pipeline for AI-research knowledge transfer. Core idea: automate paper curation, multimodal media generation, and online publishing as a connected research-communication workflow rather than isolated summarization steps.
- DS-GURU ([open-source code](https://github.com/mitdbg/KramaBench); no standalone paper; the data-science agent paired with KRAMABENCH, decomposing data-lake insight tasks into subtasks and generating executable Python data-processing/analysis pipelines)
- [Chain of Unit-Physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01010): A physics-grounded scientific code-synthesis harness. Core idea: encode expert unit-physics tests as constraints for a multi-agent code loop so generated solvers must satisfy first-principles checks during iteration.
- MLEvolve ([open-source code](https://github.com/InternScience/MLEvolve); no arXiv paper) is an evolutionary ML agent with public code on the MLE-bench leaderboard, emphasizing multiple candidate experiments, evolutionary search, and performance-feedback-driven solution iteration.
- [PaperDebugger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02589) ([open-source code](https://github.com/PaperDebugger/paperdebugger)) is an in-editor multi-agent academic writing and revision assistant for Overleaf, combining document-state access, MCP tooling, literature search, reference lookup, structured review, and diff-based revision workflows.
- [Prompt-Free Collaborative Agents for Paper2Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02812) ([open-source code](https://github.com/going-doer/Paper2Code)) proposes a prompt-free collaborative-agent framework for Paper2Code.
- [DeepCode](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07921) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is an open-ended agentic coding system compared with commercial agents and human baselines on PaperBench, representing follow-up work in paper reproduction and long-horizon code generation.
- [TIB AIssistant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16442): A research-life-cycle platform composed of task-specific assistants and scholarly tools. Core idea: store generated assets and export them as RO-Crate bundles so literature, writing, and research-management steps remain transparent and reproducible.
- [DIAGPaper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07611): A multi-agent paper-diagnosis harness for finding valid and specific weaknesses in scientific papers. Core idea: separate criteria-driven critique, rebuttal-aware validation, and weakness prioritization instead of returning unranked generic review comments.
- [SciNets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09727): A graph-constrained literature-synthesis harness that builds query-local concept graphs and searches multi-hop paths to generate mechanistic explanations. Core idea: make reasoning depth, diversity, and grounding stability measurable rather than relying only on unconstrained RAG summaries.
- [ML-Master 2.0 / Cognitive Accumulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10402) ([open-source code](https://github.com/sjtu-sai-agents/ML-Master)) extends ML-Master to ultra-long-horizon machine-learning engineering, emphasizing cross-stage cognitive accumulation, experience reuse, and long-duration experiment progress.
- [Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12542) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) comes from "Rethinking the AI Scientist"; its core is specialized agents such as planner, data analysis, literature search, and novelty detection sharing a persistent world state, moving scientific discovery from offline batch processing to minute-scale interactive loops.
- [RebuttalAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14171) ([open-source code](https://github.com/AutoLab-SAI-SJTU/Paper2Rebuttal); [demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Mqleet/RebuttalAgent)) is the Paper2Rebuttal multi-agent author-response harness, coordinating semantic encoding, issue extraction, literature retrieval, strategy generation, human refinement, rebuttal writing, and rebuttal review.
- InternAgent ([open-source code](https://github.com/InternScience/InternAgent); no arXiv paper) is an open-source agent system for deep research and ML engineering tasks, with public MLE-bench submissions; it can serve as an experiment-execution harness reference beyond AIDE / R&D-Agent.
- [DeepInflation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14288): Combines LLM agents, symbolic regression, retrieval-augmented scientific knowledge, and verification loops to explore and test candidate inflation models.
- [Execution-Grounded Automated AI Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14525) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) binds research-idea generation to real execution feedback. Core idea: have agents test ideas by running experiments rather than only generating plausible research proposals.
- [BibAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16993) is an agentic citation-verification framework for traceable miscitation detection, combining retrieval, reasoning, adaptive evidence aggregation, and downstream citation consensus for paywalled sources.
- [Idea2Story / Idea2Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20833) ([open-source code](https://github.com/AgentAlphaAGI/Idea2Paper); [demo](http://paperbuild.cn)) turns underspecified research ideas into structured scientific story skeletons through an offline paper knowledge graph, pattern retrieval, anchored multi-agent review, and iterative correction, providing the first core module of the broader Idea2Paper pipeline.
- [MARS / MARS+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02660) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a modular reflective search agent for MLE-bench; the paper decomposes ML modeling into candidate solution generation, experiment execution, result reflection, branch search, ensemble/refinement, and related stages, representing a typical test-time search / reflective ML engineering harness.
- [IV Co-Scientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07943): A multi-agent causal-research harness for instrumental-variable discovery. Core idea: coordinate proposal, critique, and refinement agents around treatment-outcome pairs, with evaluation against established and discredited instruments.
- [ARQ / Asking the Right Questions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19069) is a question-generation and decomposition framework that is useful as a research-ideation neighbor: it emphasizes asking intermediate questions that expose missing assumptions before committing to a research plan, but it is not itself a full research-agent benchmark.
- [ArchAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22425) (code: no stable public repository found; an agentic computer-architecture discovery system built around automated design-space search; design keywords: cache-policy design, code generation, simulation feedback, competition-style evaluation)
- [SciDER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01421) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a data-center-style end-to-end scientific research agent. Core idea: start from raw experimental data and automatically complete data processing, analysis, hypothesis formation, and report generation, complementing research agents focused only on papers or competition data.
- [OrchMAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03005) (code: no stable public repository found; a heterogeneous scientific-expert multi-agent orchestration framework; design keywords: two-tier orchestration, dynamic role and workflow adaptation, model routing, revision under divergent intermediate reasoning)
- [EvoScientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08127) ([open-source code](https://github.com/EvoScientist/EvoScientist)) is a self-evolving multi-agent AI scientist; with Researcher Agent, Engineer Agent, and Evolution Manager Agent plus persistent ideation and experimentation memory, it continuously improves research strategy and code-experiment ability from interaction history.
- [AI Co-Scientist for Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22376): A domain research-agent harness for discovering search-ranking models. Core idea: automate idea generation, code implementation, cloud training-job scheduling, and multi-LLM result analysis while keeping experts in the loop for ranking-model research.
- [Karpathy autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) (project-only; no paper found in this pass) is a minimal repo-first autonomous ML-research loop for single-GPU nanochat training. Core idea: encode the research organization in `program.md`, constrain the agent to edit only `train.py`, run fixed five-minute experiments, log `val_bpb`, and use git to keep or revert changes, making metric-grounded overnight experimentation a compact reference point for ARIS-style experiment loops.
- [Bilevel Autoresearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23420) (open-source code: no stable public repository verified in this pass) treats the autoresearch loop itself as an optimization target. Core idea: use an outer LLM loop to generate and inject new search mechanisms into the inner experiment loop at runtime, directly matching ARIS-style `meta-optimize` questions about improving the research harness, not only the model being trained.
- [AIRA_2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26499) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a direct extension of AIRA-dojo. It addresses bottlenecks in throughput, evaluation noise, and fixed operators with asynchronous multi-GPU execution, Hidden Consistent Evaluation, and ReAct operators, and continues reporting long-horizon results on MLE-bench-30.
- [DEFEND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27360) is an automated rebuttal-generation workflow with minimal author guidance; it structures reviewer-comment segmentation, deficiency/error typing, rebuttal actions, and author-in-the-loop reasoning, complementing Paper2Rebuttal-style end-to-end response drafting.
- [CliffSearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01210) ([project page](https://cliffsearch.ai/)) is a structured agentic co-evolution framework for scientific algorithm discovery; its LLM agents evolve theory-and-code or code-only artifacts with correctness, originality, runtime, and benchmark feedback as first-class selection signals.
- [LitPivot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02600): A research-ideation harness for developing well-situated ideas through literature contextualization and critique. Core idea: keep the literature landscape in the loop while agents revise research directions, making novelty and fit part of the workflow rather than an after-the-fact check.
- [Deep Researcher Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05854) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Xiangyue-Zhang/auto-deep-researcher-24x7)) is an execution agent for deep-research-style harnesses, emphasizing multi-round retrieval, evidence compression, and conclusion generation.
- UniScientist ([official article](https://unipat.ai/blog/UniScientist), [open-source code](https://github.com/UniPat-AI/UniScientist), [model](https://huggingface.co/UnipatAI/UniScientist-30B-A3B); paper pending) is UniPat AI's scientific-research intelligence model and agentic inference framework; its core is Active Evidence Integration, Model Abduction, Evolving Polymathic Synthesis, and multi-rollout report aggregation.
- [Agent-Driven Corpus Linguistics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07189): A tool-using research harness for autonomous linguistic discovery. Core idea: let an LLM agent generate hypotheses, query a corpus engine, interpret results, and refine analyses while grounding every finding in verifiable corpus evidence.
- [AlphaLab](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08590) ([project page](https://brendanhogan.github.io/alphalab-paper/); [open-source code](https://github.com/morganstanley/MSML/tree/main/projects/alpha-lab)) is a multi-agent autonomous research harness for quantitative optimization domains, combining Strategist and Worker roles, self-built evaluation, persistent playbook memory, and domain adapters.
- [DeepReviewer 2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09590): A traceable agentic system for auditable scientific peer review. Core idea: build claim-evidence-risk ledgers, run agenda-driven retrieval, and export only reviews that meet traceability and coverage budgets.
- [ResearchCube](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11538) is a research-ideation interface for multi-dimensional trade-off exploration. Core idea: let researchers define bipolar evaluation axes and move ideas through a 3D evaluation space, making problem-framing trade-offs inspectable before committing to a direction.
- [Toward Autonomous Long-Horizon Engineering for ML Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13018) ([open-source code](https://github.com/AweAI-Team/AiScientist)) proposes long-horizon agent mechanisms for ML research engineering, evaluating collaboration/state-management designs such as File-as-Bus on PaperBench and MLE-Bench Lite.
- [AIBuildAI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14455) ([open-source code](https://github.com/aibuildai/AI-Build-AI)) is an agent for automatically building AI models; its implementation focuses on automatically reading data, generating training and inference code, continuously running experiments, and fixing submissions, with automatic modeling results reported on MLE-bench.
- [EvoMaster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17406) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) applies multi-agent collaboration and evolutionary search to scientific problem solving. Core idea: improve performance on difficult scientific reasoning tasks such as FrontierScience through hypothesis generation, candidate evolution, and validation feedback.
- [SHARP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18752): A scientific human-agent reproduction pipeline for turning papers and documentation into executable analysis code. Core idea: decompose reproduction into agent-executed steps with specialized code, testing, and QA subagents while preserving human review checkpoints.
- [ARA / Agent-Native Research Artifacts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24658) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Orchestra-Research/Agent-Native-Research-Artifact)) expands research artifacts from linear papers into executable, auditable agent-native artifacts, reporting improvements in review/reproduction workflows on PaperBench and RE-Bench.
- [SciResearcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01489) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) extends deep research agents to frontier scientific reasoning, emphasizing multi-round evidence integration, task decomposition, and stable gains on frontier scientific reasoning benchmarks.
- [ARIS / Auto-Research-In-Sleep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03042) ([open-source code](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep); [intro](https://wanshuiyin.github.io/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep/ARIS_INTRO.html)) is a skill-based autonomous ML research harness with cross-model adversarial review, literature discovery, experiment bridging, paper writing, research wiki memory, and Codex/Claude/OpenClaw portability.
- [PARNESS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05258) ([open-source code](https://github.com/gtrhythm/PARNESS)) is a paper harness for end-to-end automated research; through dynamic YAML workflows, full-text indexing, code-repository indexing, and cross-run knowledge accumulation, it addresses rigidity in fixed-flow systems such as AI Scientist, AutoSOTA, and DeepResearch.
- [BioResearcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05985) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) is a scenario-guided multi-agent system for translational medicine, evaluated on biomedical benchmarks such as BixBench-Verified-50 for heterogeneous evidence synthesis, multi-omics analysis, and traceable report generation.
- [NanoResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10813) ([open-source code](https://github.com/OpenRaiser/NanoResearch)) is a personalized research-automation harness whose skill bank, long-term memory, and label-free policy learning co-evolve across projects, making process knowledge and research preferences reusable rather than session-local.
- [DataMaster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10906) (related open-source baseline referenced by the paper: [ml-intern](https://github.com/huggingface/ml-intern)) is a data-centered autonomous AI research agent evaluated on MLE-Bench Lite and PostTrainBench, treating data discovery, cleaning, modeling, and iterative optimization as a unified automated research process.
- [GEAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13874) (open-source code: no stable public repository found) uses genetic AutoResearch to maintain multiple research states, treating code, reflection, and performance trajectories as genetic units for population-based search; it is an evolutionary follow-up to the AIDE/AIRA/EvoScientist line.
- [AutoResearchClaw](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20025) ([open-source code](https://github.com/aiming-lab/AutoResearchClaw)) is a multi-agent auto-research pipeline with debate, self-healing execution, verifiable reporting, human-in-the-loop modes, cross-run evolution, and ARC-Bench-style evaluation.
- [Sibyl-AutoResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22343) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Sibyl-Research-Team/AutoResearch-SibylSystem)) argues that autonomous research needs self-evolving trial-and-error harnesses rather than paper generators alone. Core idea: preserve positive and negative trial outcomes, route lessons into planning, validation, claim scoping, scheduling, critique, writing, and harness repair, and expose file-backed traces for auditing trial-to-behavior conversion.
- [Claw AI Lab](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22662) ([open-source code](https://github.com/Claw-AI-Lab/Claw-AI-Lab)) reframes auto-research as an interactive lab infrastructure with role customization, dashboard monitoring, artifact inspection, rollback/resume, and Claw-Code Harness integration.
- Feynman ([open-source code](https://github.com/companion-inc/feynman); [project page](https://feynman.is)) is a repo-first autonomous research harness with researcher, reviewer, verifier, and writer agents plus autoresearch, deep-research, replication, paper-code-audit, paper-writing, and compute skills.
- DeepScientist ([open-source code](https://github.com/ResearAI/DeepScientist); [project page](https://deepscientist.cc/); [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=cZFgsLq8Gs)) is a local-first autonomous research studio with one-repo-per-quest organization, baseline reproduction, dependency-failure tracking, continuous experiments, Findings Memory, Bayesian optimization, and paper-ready outputs.
- Dr. Claw ([open-source code](https://github.com/OpenLAIR/dr-claw); [project page](https://openlair.github.io/dr-claw)) is a full-stack research workspace with Research Lab, Auto Research Hub, multi-agent backend, and more than 100 research skills; it should be treated as a research-workspace harness rather than a benchmark.
- Oh My Paper ([open-source code](https://github.com/LigphiDonk/Oh-my--paper)) is a Claude Code research harness with `/omp:survey`, `/omp:ideate`, `/omp:experiment`, `/omp:write`, `/omp:review`, and delegation commands backed by specialized research agents and skills.
- Hugging Face ml-intern ([open-source code](https://github.com/huggingface/ml-intern); [demo](https://smolagents-ml-intern.hf.space/); no arXiv paper found) is an experimental ML-training intern agent in the Hugging Face ecosystem, useful as a practical reference for data loading, training-loop execution, and ML experiment assistance rather than a paper-backed benchmark.
