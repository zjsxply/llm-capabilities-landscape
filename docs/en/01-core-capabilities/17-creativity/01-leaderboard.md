# 1.17.1 Leaderboard

- [AesBench](https://aesbench.github.io/): The official benchmark page ranks multimodal models on expert image-aesthetics perception across aesthetic perception, empathy, assessment, and interpretation.
- [NoveltyBench](https://novelty-bench.github.io/): The official leaderboard tracks model ability to generate multiple distinct, high-quality answers to open-ended prompts and real user queries.
- [AidanBench](https://aidanbench.com/): The official open-ended novel-idea benchmark page reports creativity, reliability, contextual attention, and instruction-following results; the [AI Stats mirror](https://ai-stats.phaseo.app/benchmarks/aidanbench) provides a readable public snapshot.
- [EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html): A widely used creative-writing leaderboard for subjective quality, style, narrative control, and literary expression.
- [EQ-Bench Longform Creative Writing](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing_longform.html): A widely used longform creative-writing leaderboard that stresses sustained style, pacing, and narrative coherence.
- [LLM Creative Story-Writing Benchmark](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing): A public constrained short-story benchmark and leaderboard that compares models on creative writing prompts with explicit requirements.
- [HumorRank: A Tournament-Based Leaderboard for Evaluating Humor Generation in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19786): Creates a tournament-based HumorRank leaderboard for LLM joke generation, ranking systems by pairwise humor judgments rather than single-prompt examples.
- [Creative AI Index](https://schapiro.ai/creative-ai-index/) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13450)): Public leaderboard for LLM creativity tests and creative-achievement predictors, covering DAT, Conditional DAT, DRAT, creative writing, divergent thinking, and scientific ideation rather than a single story-writing score.
