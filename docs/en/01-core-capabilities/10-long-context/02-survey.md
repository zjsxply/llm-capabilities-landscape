# 1.10.2 Survey

- [The What, Why, and How of Context Length Extension Techniques in Large Language Models - A Detailed Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07872): Organizes why context extension matters, what extrapolation failures arise, how existing extension strategies work, and where evaluation standards for longer contexts remain unsettled.
- [Beyond the Limits: A Survey of Techniques to Extend the Context Length in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02244): A foundation for RoPE, interpolation, recurrence, and retrieval approaches.
- [On the Resurgence of Recurrent Models for Long Sequences - Survey and Research Opportunities in the Transformer Era](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08132): Surveys Long Context methods, task boundaries, and open challenges relevant to the capability taxonomy.
- [Mamba-360: Survey of State Space Models as Transformer Alternative for Long Sequence Modelling: Methods, Applications, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16112): Organizes state-space long-sequence models into gating, structural, and recurrent families, then compares S4/Mamba-style variants across language, vision, video, audio, speech, and time-series benchmarks.
- [Understanding the RoPE Extensions of Long-Context LLMs: An Attention Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13282): Interprets RoPE extension methods through attention-pattern preservation, attention uncertainty, retrieval errors, and continual-pretraining length, using benchmark experiments rather than treating context extension as a black-box recipe.
- [Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs? A Comprehensive Study and Hybrid Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16833): Compares retrieval-augmented generation and long-context LLMs and studies hybrid use.
- [Prompt Compression for Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12388): Classifies prompt compression into hard and soft prompt methods, then compares mechanisms through attention optimization, PEFT, multimodal integration, and synthetic-language perspectives.
- [When Precision Meets Position: BFloat16 Breaks Down RoPE in Long-Context Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13476): Diagnoses how BFloat16 precision breaks RoPE's relative-position behavior at long lengths and introduces AnchorAttention, a plug-and-play attention variant that keeps a shared first-token anchor while reducing long-context training cost.
- [A Survey on Large Language Model Acceleration based on KV Cache Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19442): Reviews memory and serving trade-offs for efficient long-context inference.
- [Thus Spake Long-Context Large Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17129): Surveys long-context LLM progress across architecture, training, infrastructure, evaluation, and million-token context extensions.
- [A Survey on Transformer Context Extension: Approaches and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13299): Separates architectural context extension from downstream task success.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Long Context Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17407): Reviews long-context methods, benchmarks, evaluation pitfalls, and system constraints.
- [Beyond Single-Turn: A Survey on Multi-Turn Interactions with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04717): Surveys multi-turn LLM interaction as a stateful setting, covering dialogue history, tool or memory feedback, user adaptation, evaluation protocols, and failure modes that are hidden in single-turn tests.
- [Real-World Summarization: When Evaluation Reaches Its Limits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11508): Studies hotel-highlight summarization as an input-faithfulness evaluation case, showing span-level human annotation, word-overlap metrics, trainable scorers, and LLM judges can disagree sharply in out-of-domain real deployments.
- [A Survey of Context Engineering for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13334): Surveys context construction, retrieval, memory, tool traces, and prompt-state management.
- [Efficient Attention Mechanisms for Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19595): Surveys efficient attention or context-extension techniques for long-context models.
- [A Survey of Token Compression for Efficient Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20198): Surveys token-compression methods for efficient multimodal large language models.
- [State-space modeling in long sequence processing: a survey on recurrence in the transformer era](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2025.108039): Reviews recurrent and state-space sequence models as Transformer-era long-sequence alternatives, emphasizing how recurrence re-enters modern architectures to trade attention cost for persistent sequence state.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Mamba: Architectures, Challenges, and Opportunities](https://doi.org/10.1109/mc.2025.3571322): Focuses on Mamba and selective state-space architectures, summarizing architectural variants, application domains, efficiency advantages, and open issues for long-sequence modeling.
- [A systematic review of long document summarization methods: Evaluation metrics and approaches](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.131287): Reviews long-document summarization through evaluation design, comparing metrics and summarization approaches under inputs that exceed ordinary context or discourse length.
- [From Static Inference to Dynamic Interaction: A Survey of Streaming Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04592): Surveys streaming LLMs for dynamic inputs, real-time interaction, memory, and incremental inference.
- [Beyond the Parameters: A Technical Survey of Contextual Enrichment in Large Language Models: From In-Context Prompting to Causal Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03174): Frames inference-time context enrichment as a continuum from prompting to RAG, GraphRAG, and CausalRAG, adding a literature-screening protocol, claim-audit framework, and deployment-oriented decision guide.
- [Long-Context Benchmark Volatility](https://doi.org/10.1145/3805621.3807653): analyzes how benchmark volatility distorts long-context evaluation accuracy and proposes mitigation strategies.
- [Selection of low and ultra-low thermal conductivity materials for insulating layers of TBC systems in the context of computational materials science opportunities—a review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10973-025-14226-4): Surveys Long Context methods, task boundaries, evidence sources, and open challenges relevant to this capability area.
