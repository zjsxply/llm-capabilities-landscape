# 1.5.4 Model

- [LLaVA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485): A high-impact visual instruction-tuning method that connects a vision encoder to an LLM and trains on synthetic image-instruction data.
- [Integrating Language Guidance Into Image-Text Matching for Correcting False Negatives](https://doi.org/10.1109/tmm.2023.3261443): Uses language-guided image-text matching to identify false negative pairs during contrastive vision-language training.
- [Improved Zero-Shot Classification by Adapting VLMs with Text Descriptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02460): Adapts zero-shot VLM classifiers by enriching class prompts with descriptive text rather than relying on bare label names.
- [Chain-of-Table: Evolving Tables in the Reasoning Chain for Table Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04398): Represents table reasoning as a sequence of table-evolution operations so an LLM can transform intermediate tables while answering questions.
- [Inducing High Energy-Latency of Large Vision-Language Models with Verbose Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11170): Shows that text-dense verbose images can inflate VLM token processing and energy latency, exposing a model-side robustness and efficiency failure mode.
- [EarthGPT: A Universal Multimodal Large Language Model for Multisensor Image Comprehension in Remote Sensing Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16822): Trains a remote-sensing multimodal LLM to interpret multisensor imagery for scene understanding, grounding, and question answering.
- [RL-VLM-F: Reinforcement Learning from Vision Language Foundation Model Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03681): Uses feedback from a vision-language foundation model as a reward signal for reinforcement learning over visual-language outputs.
- [Task-customized Masked Autoencoder via Mixture of Cluster-conditional Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05382): Customizes masked autoencoding with cluster-conditioned expert modules so visual representations adapt to downstream task structure.
- [Question Aware Vision Transformer for Multimodal Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05472): Conditions a Vision Transformer on the question text so visual features are selected for multimodal reasoning rather than generic image recognition.
- [ViGoR: Improving Visual Grounding of Large Vision Language Models with Fine-Grained Reward Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06118): Trains fine-grained reward models for visual grounding, giving VLMs token- or region-level feedback on localization quality.
- [Mitigating Object Hallucination in Large Vision-Language Models via Image-Grounded Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08680): Reduces VLM object hallucination by steering generation with evidence grounded in the input image.
- [EFUF: Efficient Fine-Grained Unlearning Framework for Mitigating Hallucinations in Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09801): Uses fine-grained unlearning to mitigate hallucinations in multimodal large language models.
- [Vision-Flan: Scaling Human-Labeled Tasks in Visual Instruction Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11690): Scales visual instruction tuning with a broad collection of human-labeled vision-language tasks.
- [Scaffolding Coordinates to Promote Vision-Language Coordination in Large Multi-Modal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12058): Adds coordinate scaffolds to multimodal prompts and training examples so VLMs learn more precise spatial references.
- [Tables as Images? Exploring the Strengths and Limitations of LLMs on Multimodal Representations of Tabular Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12424): Studies when rendering tables as images helps or hurts LLM table understanding compared with textual table encodings.
- [Uncertainty-Aware Evaluation for Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14418): Uses uncertainty estimates to diagnose confidence and reliability in VLM predictions across visual-language tasks.
- [Efficient Temporal Extrapolation of Multimodal Large Language Models with Temporal Grounding Bridge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16050): Adds a temporal grounding bridge so multimodal LLMs can extrapolate visual understanding across time with less retraining.
- [IBD: Alleviating Hallucinations in Large Vision-Language Models via Image-Biased Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18476): Biases decoding toward image-conditioned evidence to suppress hallucinated objects and attributes in VLM responses.
- [Peacock: A Family of Arabic Multimodal Large Language Models and Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01031): Builds Arabic multimodal LLMs and evaluation data for image understanding, visual question answering, and OCR-heavy Arabic use cases.
- [InfiMM-HD: A Leap Forward in High-Resolution Multimodal Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01487): Extends multimodal understanding to high-resolution inputs so the model can preserve fine visual details and dense text.
- [One Prompt Word is Enough to Boost Adversarial Robustness for Pre-Trained Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01849): Improves adversarial robustness of pretrained VLMs by tuning the prompt with a single carefully chosen word.
- [Multi-Modal Instruction Tuned LLMs with Fine-Grained Visual Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02969): Instruction-tunes multimodal LLMs with fine-grained perception data for localized visual understanding.
- [TextMonkey: An OCR-Free Large Multimodal Model for Understanding Document](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04473): Builds an OCR-free document MLLM that reads text-rich pages directly from visual tokens.
- [MoAI: Mixture of All Intelligence for Large Language and Vision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07508): Combines multiple specialized visual experts with an LLM so the model can route among detection, OCR, and reasoning skills.
- [ChartInstruct: Instruction Tuning for Chart Comprehension and Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09028): Creates chart-specific instruction data for tuning VLMs to answer chart comprehension and reasoning questions.
- [The First to Know: How Token Distributions Reveal Hidden Knowledge in Large Vision-Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09037): Analyzes VLM token distributions to reveal when visual-language knowledge appears before final decoding.
- [GiT: Towards Generalist Vision Transformer through Universal Language Interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09394): Frames diverse vision tasks behind a universal language interface for a generalist Vision Transformer.
- [Generative Region-Language Pretraining for Open-Ended Object Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10191): Uses generative region-language pretraining to support open-ended object detection.
- [X-LLaVA: Optimizing Bilingual Large Vision-Language Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11399): Optimizes bilingual alignment between visual features and language responses for Chinese-English VLM use.
- [mPLUG-DocOwl 1.5: Unified Structure Learning for OCR-free Document Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12895): Learns unified document structure representations for OCR-free understanding of layouts, tables, and text-rich pages.
- [Bridge the Modality and Capability Gaps in Vision-Language Model Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13797): Studies how to select VLMs by separating modality coverage from downstream capability fit.
- [Once for Both: Single Stage of Importance and Sparsity Search for Vision Transformer Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15835): Searches ViT importance and sparsity in one stage to compress visual encoders used by multimodal models.
- [Elysium: Exploring Object-level Perception in Videos via MLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16558): Uses multimodal LLMs to probe object-level perception and temporal object understanding in video frames.
- [mChartQA: A universal benchmark for multimodal Chart Question Answer based on Vision-Language Alignment and Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01548): Couples chart question answering with vision-language alignment and reasoning signals for chart-focused VLM training and analysis.
- [Ferret-v2: An Improved Baseline for Referring and Grounding with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07973): Improves referring and grounding in multimodal LLMs through stronger region-level visual representations.
- [Two Effects, One Trigger: On the Modality Gap, Object Bias, and Information Imbalance in Contrastive Vision-Language Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07983): Analyzes how modality gaps, object bias, and information imbalance shape contrastive VLM representations.
- [TextCoT: Zoom-In for Enhanced Multimodal Text-Rich Image Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09797): Uses zoom-in reasoning over text-rich image regions to improve multimodal understanding of documents and screenshots.
- [Groma: Localized Visual Tokenization for Grounding Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13013): Introduces localized visual tokenization so MLLMs can ground responses in specific image regions.
- [AdvLoRA: Adversarial Low-Rank Adaptation of Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13425): Applies adversarial low-rank adaptation to improve VLM robustness without full fine-tuning.
- [Exploring Diverse Methods in Visual Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13565): Compares VQA modeling strategies for mapping visual evidence and question text into answer predictions.
- [Graphic Design with Large Multimodal Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14368): Adapts large multimodal models to reason over graphic-design layouts and generate design-oriented feedback.
- [Cantor: Inspiring Multimodal Chain-of-Thought of MLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16033): Encourages multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning so an MLLM decomposes visual evidence before answering.
- [TinyChart: Efficient Chart Understanding with Program-of-Thoughts Learning and Visual Token Merging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16635): Combines program-of-thought learning with visual token merging for efficient chart understanding.
- [Choose What You Need: Disentangled Representation Learning for Scene Text Recognition, Removal and Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04377): Learns disentangled scene-text representations that can support recognition, text removal, and text editing with shared features.
- [VisionGraph: Leveraging Large Multimodal Models for Graph Theory Problems in Visual Context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04950): Uses multimodal models to parse graph diagrams and solve graph-theory questions from visual context.
- [Boosting Multimodal Large Language Models with Visual Tokens Withdrawal for Rapid Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05803): Speeds MLLM inference by withdrawing low-value visual tokens while retaining answer-relevant image evidence.
- [SpeechGuard: Exploring the Adversarial Robustness of Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08317): Studies adversarial robustness of multimodal LLMs when speech and visual inputs interact.
- [Xmodel-VLM: A Simple Baseline for Multimodal Vision Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09215): Provides a simple VLM baseline for aligning visual encoders with language models on multimodal tasks.
- [Rethinking Overlooked Aspects in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11850): Audits underexplored VLM design choices that affect perception, language alignment, and downstream robustness.
- [No Filter: Cultural and Socioeconomic Diversity in Contrastive Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13777): Examines how data filtering choices affect cultural and socioeconomic representation in contrastive VLMs.
- [Alleviating Hallucinations in Large Vision-Language Models through Hallucination-Induced Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15356): Optimizes VLMs with hallucination-inducing cases so training directly penalizes unsupported visual claims.
- [Meteor: Mamba-based Traversal of Rationale for Large Language and Vision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15574): Uses a Mamba-style rationale traversal module to connect visual evidence with reasoning steps in VLMs.
- [Enhancing Visual-Language Modality Alignment in Large Vision Language Models via Self-Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15973): Iteratively improves visual-language alignment by using the model's own feedback to refine multimodal training signals.
- [Benchmarking and Improving Detail Image Caption](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19092): Builds and uses detailed-caption supervision to make image captioning models capture fine-grained visual attributes.
- [MetaToken: Detecting Hallucination in Image Descriptions by Meta Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19186): Adds a meta-classification token for detecting hallucinated content in generated image descriptions.
- [Adaptive Image Quality Assessment via Teaching Large Multimodal Model to Compare](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19298): Teaches multimodal models to compare images adaptively for image-quality assessment.
- [Matryoshka Query Transformer for Large Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19315): Uses nested query representations to transfer visual evidence from encoders into large language models at multiple granularities.
- [Visual Perception by Large Language Model's Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20339): Investigates how visual perception can emerge from or be encoded within LLM weights.
- [DeCo: Decoupling Token Compression from Semantic Abstraction in Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20985): Separates visual token compression from semantic abstraction to reduce MLLM cost without discarding key evidence.
- [StrucTexTv3: An Efficient Vision-Language Model for Text-rich Image Perception, Comprehension, and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21013): Builds an efficient VLM specialized for perceiving and understanding text-rich images.
- [TabPedia: Towards Comprehensive Visual Table Understanding with Concept Synergy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01326): Uses concept synergy among table structure, cell content, and visual layout for visual table understanding.
- [VisTR: Visualizations as Representations for Time-series Table Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03753): Converts time-series tables into visualizations so VLMs can reason over trends and tabular values.

- [DocKylin: A Large Multimodal Model for Visual Document Understanding with Efficient Visual Slimming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19101): Uses visual slimming to make a document MLLM more efficient on layout, text, and document-question tasks.
- [FlowVQA: Mapping Multimodal Logic in Visual Question Answering with Flowcharts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19237): Targets VQA over flowcharts by modeling the logical structure of nodes, arrows, and multimodal evidence.
- [A Bounding Box is Worth One Token: Interleaving Layout and Text in a Large Language Model for Document Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01976): Encodes each bounding box as a token so an LLM can interleave layout geometry with document text.
- [Crossroads of Continents: Automated Artifact Extraction for Cultural Adaptation with Large Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02067): Introduces DalleStreet, a human-validated 9,935-image country-concept dataset, and CultureAdapt for extracting and editing cultural artifacts in LMM-generated imagery.
- [FineCLIPER: Multi-modal Fine-grained CLIP for Dynamic Facial Expression Recognition with AdaptERs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02157): Off-axis for OCR; adapts CLIP to dynamic facial-expression recognition with frame, mask, landmark, and MLLM-generated facial-change descriptions.
- [Why do LLaVA Vision-Language Models Reply to Images in English?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02333): Diagnoses LLaVA-style multilingual bias, finding that visual inputs push non-English queries toward English responses and that language-backbone choice and attention interventions reduce the effect.
- [Improving Visual Storytelling with Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02586): Off-axis for OCR; instruction-tunes LLM and LVLM components on a visual-story dataset with supervised and reinforcement learning for coherent image-grounded narratives.
- [ChartGemma: Visual Instruction-tuning for Chart Reasoning in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04172): Tunes PaliGemma on chart-image instruction data generated directly from visual trends and low-level chart details, improving chart summarization, QA, and fact-checking.
- [Rethinking Visual Prompting for Multimodal Large Language Models with External Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04681): Embeds masks, OCR, or other specialist-vision outputs into a spatial visual-prompt map so MLLMs receive fine-grained external knowledge without coordinate text prompts.
- [A Single Transformer for Scalable Vision-Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06438): Presents SOLO, a 7B single-transformer LVLM trained from an LLM through ImageNet pretraining, web-scale pretraining, and instruction tuning to avoid separate encoder-connector-LLM scaling limits.
- [Graph-Based Captioning: Enhancing Visual Descriptions by Interconnecting Region Captions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06723): Builds GBC10M, a graph-caption dataset whose entity, composition, and relation nodes give CLIP training structured region-level image descriptions.
- [Pseudo-RIS: Distinctive Pseudo-supervision Generation for Referring Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07412): Off-axis for OCR; generates referring-segmentation pseudo labels by pairing foundation-model masks with distinctive captions and filtering weak referring expressions.
- [IDA-VLM: Towards Movie Understanding via ID-Aware Large Vision-Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07577): Off-axis for OCR; adds ID-reference visual instruction tuning and the MM-ID benchmark for tracking character identity across movie scenes.
- [Quantized Prompt for Efficient Generalization of Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10704): Uses prompt quantization error as lightweight regularization for CLIP adaptation, reducing overfitting and storage cost across downstream datasets.
- [Naturally Occurring Feedback is Common, Extractable and Useful](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10944): Off-axis for OCR; extracts unsolicited user feedback from chat logs and trains preference-aligned language models from the resulting conversation-derived signals.
- [Reflective Instruction Tuning: Mitigating Hallucinations in Large Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11422): Adds REVERIE, a 115K reflective-rationale visual instruction dataset that trains LVLMs to justify correct and confusing answers to reduce hallucination.
- [BEAF: Observing BEfore-AFter Changes to Evaluate Hallucination in Vision-language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13442): Uses before-after image pairs to expose whether VLMs notice visual changes or hallucinate unchanged objects and attributes.
- [Qalam: A Multimodal LLM for Arabic Optical Character and Handwriting Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13559): Builds an Arabic multimodal LLM for printed OCR and handwriting recognition, targeting Arabic script reading rather than generic visual QA.
- [Beyond Augmentation: Empowering Model Robustness under Extreme Capture Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13640): Off-axis for OCR; improves person re-identification robustness under extreme capture conditions with grid-based multi-mode synchronization augmentation.
- [EVLM: An Efficient Vision-Language Model for Visual Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14177): Combines cross-attention image-text interaction, hierarchical ViT features, and MoE modules to reduce MLLM compute for image and video captioning.
- [Token-level Correlation-guided Compression for Efficient Multimodal Document Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14439): Adds a plug-and-play document-token compressor that uses patch-token correlation and CLS-token sampling to keep informative cropped-document tokens.
- [On Pre-training of Multimodal Language Models Customized for Chart Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14506): Introduces CHOPINLLM, using raw chart-data alignment, random textual chart replacement, and extract-then-answer fine-tuning for deeper chart value reading.
- [Harmonizing Visual Text Comprehension and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16364): Builds TextHarmony with Slide-LoRA experts and DetailedTextCaps-100K so one model can both read and generate visual text.
- [Graph-based Unsupervised Disentangled Representation Learning via Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18999): Off-axis for OCR; uses MLLMs to infer weighted semantic-factor graphs for unsupervised disentangled representation learning.
- [LLAVADI: What Matters For Multimodal Large Language Models Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19409): Studies small MLLM distillation and finds that joint token alignment and logit alignment are key for 2.7B students to approach larger teachers.
- [ControlMLLM: Training-Free Visual Prompt Learning for Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21534): Optimizes a test-time latent variable that steers MLLM attention toward boxed, masked, scribbled, or pointed regions for referring descriptions and reasoning.
- [Mitigating Multilingual Hallucination in Large Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00550): Uses cross-lingual hallucination-aware response pairs and direct preference optimization to reduce LVLM hallucination across 13 languages.
- [Alleviating Hallucination in Large Vision-Language Models with Active Retrieval Augmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00555): Proposes ARA, which triggers hierarchical image-aware retrieval only at low-certainty moments and filters retrieval evidence to reduce LVLM hallucination.
- [Advancing Post-OCR Correction: A Comparative Study of Synthetic Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02253): Compares synthetic-data volume, augmentation, and glyph-similarity generation for ByT5-style post-OCR correction across low-resource languages.
- [How Aligned are Human Chart Takeaways and LLM Predictions? A Case Study on Bar Charts with Varying Layouts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06837): Tests whether LLMs reproduce human bar-chart takeaways across layouts and finds weak factual accuracy and poor alignment with human comparison types.
- [ChemVLM: Exploring the Power of Multimodal Large Language Models in Chemistry Area](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07246): Trains a chemistry MLLM on bilingual chemical visual-text data and evaluates chemical OCR, multimodal chemical reasoning, and molecule understanding.
