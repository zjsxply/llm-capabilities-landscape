# 2.4.2 Survey

- [A Comprehensive Survey of Deep Research: Systems, Methodologies, and Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12594): Organizes deep research around decomposition, search, evidence management, synthesis, and evaluation.
- [Deep Research Agents: A Systematic Examination And Roadmap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18096): Defines capability boundaries, systems, benchmarks, and reproducibility issues.
- [A Survey of LLM-based Deep Search Agents: Paradigm, Optimization, Evaluation, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05668): Covers the retrieval-intensive search and evidence stage of research agents.
- [Deep Research: A Survey of Autonomous Research Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12752): Decomposes autonomous deep-research systems into planning, question development, iterative web exploration, and evidence-grounded report generation, then compares optimization methods and benchmarks for these four stages.
- [Exploring the scope of generative AI in literature review development](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-025-00754-2): Uses an epistemological lens to distinguish six literature-review activities: research synthesis, evidence aggregation, critique, theory building, gap identification, and research-agenda development, and assesses where generative AI can complement researchers in each activity.
- [Reinforcement Learning Foundations for Deep Research Systems: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06733): Connects long-horizon search and report quality with supervision and reward design.
- [The future of reviews](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-025-00560-z): Discusses how scholarly review may shift from static narrative articles toward living, evidence-updated syntheses with clearer provenance and machine-assisted literature monitoring.
