# 4.3.2 Survey

- [Recent Advances in Speech Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03751): Surveys representation, understanding, generation, dialogue, and evaluation in speech language models.
- [WavChat: A Survey of Spoken Dialogue Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13577): Reviews speech-to-speech interaction, turn-taking, dialogue management, and evaluation.
- [Audio-Language Models for Audio-Centric Tasks: A Systematic Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15177): Covers audio-centric tasks, datasets, architectures, and evaluation.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Generative AI for Video-to-Music Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12489): Reviews conditioning inputs, conditioning mechanisms, and music-generation frameworks for video-to-music generation.
- [When Large Language Models Meet Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19548): Surveys text-based, latent-representation-based, and audio-token-based approaches for integrating speech with LLMs.
- [On The Landscape of Spoken Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08528): Maps spoken-language-model architectures, tasks, datasets, and open problems.
- [Towards Holistic Evaluation of Large Audio-Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15957): Reviews capability, robustness, safety, and benchmark design for audio-language models.
- [Voice of a Continent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18436): Maps African speech datasets, technologies, benchmarks, and model behavior, highlighting resource gaps for underrepresented languages.
- [ASAudio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10924): Surveys advanced spatial-audio research across representation, generation, and evaluation.
- [Training Strategies for Speech Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/FLLM67465.2025.11391055): Surveys training strategies for speech large language models, complementing broader speech-language-model surveys with a training-focused view.
- [Real-Time Voice](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCECE61355.2025.10940301): Surveys automatic speech recognition and transcription, with emphasis on real-time voice systems.
- [AI-Enabled Text-to-Music Generation](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics14061197): Reviews methods, frameworks, and open directions for text-to-music generation.
- [From Tools to Creators](https://doi.org/10.3390/info16080656): Reviews AI music generation methods, applications, and development trends, complementing speech and audio generation coverage beyond voice-only systems.
