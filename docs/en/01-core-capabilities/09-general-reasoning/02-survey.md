# 1.9.2 Survey

- [Exploring the Reasoning Abilities of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs): A Comprehensive Survey on Emerging Trends in Multimodal Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06805): Organizes multimodal reasoning by task type, modality interaction, benchmark family, and open failure modes beyond text-only inference.
- [In-context Learning with Retrieved Demonstrations for Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11624): Reviews retrieved-demonstration pipelines for in-context learning, covering example selection, ordering, retrieval sources, and evaluation effects.
- [A Survey to Recent Progress Towards Understanding In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02212): Synthesizes mechanistic, Bayesian, optimization, and empirical accounts of why in-context learning emerges in language models.
- [A survey of table reasoning with large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08259): Maps table reasoning around table understanding, question answering, fact verification, program-aided methods, and evaluation gaps.
- [Large Language Models(LLMs) on Tabular Data: Prediction, Generation, and Understanding - A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17944): Surveys tabular prediction, synthetic table generation, and table understanding as complementary reasoning uses of LLMs over structured data.
- [Large Language Models and Causal Inference in Collaboration: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09606): Frames LLMs and causal inference as a bidirectional collaboration spanning causal discovery, effect estimation, counterfactual reasoning, and assumption risks.
- [LLMs as Writing Assistants: Exploring Perspectives on Sense of Ownership and Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00027): Uses writing-assistant studies to isolate how ownership, revision control, and explicit reasoning support shape human use of LLM suggestions.
- [LLM as a Mastermind: A Survey of Strategic Reasoning with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01230): Surveys strategic reasoning through game-theoretic interaction, planning, opponent modeling, negotiation, and multi-agent decision settings.
- [Beyond Accuracy: Evaluating the Reasoning Behavior of Large Language Models - A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01869): Centers evaluation on reasoning behavior rather than final accuracy, including trace quality, robustness, consistency, and process-level diagnostics.
- [Relevant or Random: Can LLMs Truly Perform Analogical Reasoning?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12728): Audits analogical reasoning by separating relational mapping from lexical or retrieval shortcuts in LLM benchmark behavior.
- [Beyond Chain-of-Thought: A Survey of Chain-of-X Paradigms for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15676): Taxonomizes Chain-of-X prompting variants such as thoughts, tools, verification, critique, knowledge, and multimodal intermediate structures.
- [Enhancing Decision-Making in Optimization through LLM-Assisted Inference: A Neural Networks Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07212): Reviews LLM-assisted optimization decision making through neural-network inference, surrogate modeling, and human-guided search workflows.
- [Towards Better Chain-of-Thought: A Reflection on Effectiveness and Faithfulness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18915): Reviews when chain-of-thought improves answers and when explanations become unfaithful rationalizations or evaluation artifacts.
- [Anthropocentric bias in language model evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03859): Critiques human-centered assumptions in language-model evaluation and their effect on interpreting capabilities.
- [Multi-Step Reasoning with Large Language Models, a Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11511): Surveys decomposition, intermediate-state control, verification, and benchmark protocols for multi-step LLM reasoning.
- [Reinforcement Learning for LLM Post-Training: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16216): Reviews post-training RL objectives, preference signals, reward modeling, and alignment trade-offs that shape reasoning behavior.
- [LASP: Surveying the State-of-the-Art in Large Language Model-Assisted AI Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01806): Surveys LLM-assisted planning systems across plan generation, validation, replanning, planner integration, and benchmarked planning domains.
- [Preference Tuning with Human Feedback on Language, Speech, and Vision Tasks: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11564): Organizes human-feedback preference tuning across reward models, comparison data, optimization algorithms, and multimodal task coverage.
- [From Linguistic Giants to Sensory Maestros: A Survey on Cross-Modal Reasoning with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18996): Reviews cross-modal reasoning across perception, alignment, multimodal chain-of-thought, and evaluation for language-grounded sensory inputs.
- [Everything Everywhere All at Once: LLMs can In-Context Learn Multiple Tasks in Superposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05603): Studies in-context learning when several tasks are represented simultaneously, exposing superposition effects in task inference and demonstration use.
- [A Survey of Uncertainty Estimation in LLMs: Theory Meets Practice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15326): Connects uncertainty theory with practical LLM confidence estimation, calibration, selective prediction, and risk-aware deployment.
- [Cognitive Biases in Large Language Models: A Survey and Mitigation Experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00323): Surveys cognitive-bias phenomena in LLM outputs and pairs the taxonomy with mitigation experiments for reasoning reliability.
- [Neural-Symbolic Reasoning over Knowledge Graphs: A Survey from a Query Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10390): Organizes neural-symbolic knowledge-graph reasoning by query form, symbolic constraints, neural scoring, and benchmark coverage.
- [A Research Agenda for Usability and Generalisation in Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16970): Positions RL generalization and usability as survey axes for reproducible environments, evaluation transfer, and deployable decision policies.
- [Large Language Models Meet Graph Neural Networks: A Perspective of Graph Mining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19211): Surveys graph-mining interfaces between LLMs and GNNs, including graph representation, reasoning over graph tasks, and hybrid model designs.
- [Position: Theory of Mind Benchmarks are Broken for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19726): Argues that ToM benchmarks for LLMs need tighter task validity, contamination control, and interaction-aware evaluation boundaries.
- [Comprehensive Survey on Prompts Generating via Knowledge-Guided Chain-of-Thought](https://doi.org/10.26599/ijcs.2024.9100038): Focuses on prompt generation where explicit knowledge steers chain-of-thought construction for harder reasoning tasks.
- [Artificial Intelligence and the Illusion of Understanding: A Systematic Review of Theory of Mind and Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2024.0536): Systematically reviews ToM claims for LLMs and separates social-cognitive evidence from benchmark artifacts and anthropomorphic interpretation.
- [Test-time Computing: from System-1 Thinking to System-2 Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02497): Surveys test-time computing from fast System-1 responses to deliberate System-2 reasoning.
- [Towards Large Reasoning Models: A Survey of Reinforced Reasoning with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09686): Reviews rewards, search, verification, and training pipelines for reinforced reasoning.
- [Reasoning Language Models: A Blueprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11223): Positions reasoning language models as a blueprint for training and inference-time reasoning design.
- [A Survey of Theory of Mind in Large Language Models: Evaluations, Representations, and Safety Risks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06470): Surveys Theory-of-Mind evaluation, representation, and safety risks in large language models.
- [A Survey of In-Context Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07978): Surveys in-context reinforcement learning, linking policy adaptation within context to reasoning and decision making.
- [Logical Reasoning in Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09100): Covers formal, symbolic, and rule-governed reasoning tasks and failures.
- [Evaluating Step-by-step Reasoning Traces: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12289): Surveys evaluation of step-by-step reasoning traces, including process quality, faithfulness, and scoring methods.
- [From Principles to Applications: A Comprehensive Survey of Discrete Tokenizers in Generation, Comprehension, Recommendation, and Information Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12448): Surveys discrete tokenizers across generation, comprehension, recommendation, and retrieval.
- [From System 1 to System 2: A Survey of Reasoning Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17419): Surveys the shift from fast generation to deliberate multi-step reasoning.
- [LLM Post-Training: A Deep Dive into Reasoning Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21321): Provides a deep-dive survey of post-training methods for reasoning large language models.
- [A Survey on Enhancing Causal Reasoning Ability of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09326): Reviews prompting, data augmentation, fine-tuning, tool use, and evaluation strategies for improving causal reasoning in LLMs.
- [Towards Reasoning Era: A Survey of Long Chain-of-Thought for Reasoning Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09567): Surveys long chain-of-thought methods, training recipes, and open issues for reasoning large language models.
- [Multimodal Chain-of-Thought Reasoning: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12605): Reviews multimodal CoT paradigms for image, video, audio, and other multimodal reasoning settings.
- [Stop Overthinking: A Survey on Efficient Reasoning for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16419): Reviews adaptive compute and cost-quality trade-offs for efficient reasoning.
- [Understanding R1-Zero-Like Training: A Critical Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20783): Critically examines R1-Zero-like training and its assumptions for reasoning models.
- [A Survey of Efficient Reasoning for Large Reasoning Models: Language, Multimodality, and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21614): Surveys methods for reducing redundant reasoning traces and improving cost-quality trade-offs in LRMs.
- [Efficient Inference for Large Reasoning Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23077): Reviews token, memory, and latency reduction methods for deliberative reasoning inference.
- [Harnessing the Reasoning Economy: A Survey of Efficient Reasoning for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24377): Surveys efficient reasoning methods that reduce System-2 reasoning cost while preserving accuracy.
- [A Survey of Scaling in Large Language Model Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02181): Surveys how reasoning scales through data, model size, inference, and multi-agent collaboration.
- [Why Reasoning Matters? A Survey of Advancements in Multimodal Reasoning (v1)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03151): Surveys multimodal reasoning advances and explains why reasoning matters beyond text-only LLMs.
- [A Survey of Frontiers in LLM Reasoning: Inference Scaling, Learning to Reason, and Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09037): Connects inference scaling, learning-to-reason, and agentic reasoning systems.
- [Position: Stop Anthropomorphizing Intermediate Tokens as Reasoning/Thinking Traces!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09762): Position paper cautioning against treating intermediate tokens as faithful reasoning traces.
- [Rethinking Theory of Mind Benchmarks for LLMs: Towards A User-Centered Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10839): Reviews limitations in importing human ToM tasks into LLM evaluation and argues for user-centered, interactional benchmark criteria for social reasoning.
- [Efficient Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10903): Makes compute efficiency the organizing axis for reasoning, spanning compact models, adaptive inference, and cost-quality trade-offs.
- [Meta-Thinking in LLMs via Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14520): Surveys meta-thinking capabilities in LLMs through a multi-agent reinforcement-learning lens.
- [Principles and Guidelines for the Use of LLM Judges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19076): Distills judge-model guidance around task framing, rubric design, calibration, bias control, and reliability checks.
- [Reinforced MLLM: A Survey on RL-Based Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21277): Surveys reinforcement-learning methods for improving reasoning in multimodal large language models.
- [Consistency in Language Models: Current Landscape, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00268): Reviews consistency as a reasoning reliability axis across factual, logical, temporal, and self-consistency evaluation settings.
- [100 Days After DeepSeek-R1: A Survey on Replication Studies and More Directions for Reasoning Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00551): Summarizes early DeepSeek-R1 replication studies and open directions for reinforcement learning, distillation, evaluation, and efficient reasoning.
- [Perception, Reason, Think, and Plan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04921): Treats multimodal reasoning as an integrated loop of perception, deliberate reasoning, planning, and action-oriented cognition.
- [Rethinking the Role of Prompting Strategies in LLM Test-Time Scaling: A Perspective of Probability Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10981): Recasts prompting strategies for test-time scaling through probability theory, clarifying when sampling, voting, and search improve reasoning.
- [Visual Thoughts: A Unified Perspective of Understanding Multimodal Chain-of-Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15510): Unifies textual and interleaved multimodal chain-of-thought methods for understanding LVLM reasoning.
- [Reasoning Beyond Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16782): Centers latent chain-of-thought, studying reasoning traces that occur in hidden states rather than fully verbalized rationales.
- [On the Mechanisms of Weak-to-Strong Generalization: A Theoretical Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18346): Uses theoretical mechanisms to explain when weak supervisors can elicit stronger model behavior and where generalization can fail.
- [Using Large Language Models to Tackle Fundamental Challenges in Graph Learning: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18475): Surveys LLM use in graph learning for representation, reasoning, generation, data scarcity, and graph-task generalization.
- [Large Language Models for Planning: A Comprehensive and Systematic Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19683): Systematically reviews LLM planning by task formulation, prompt or tool-based planner coupling, feedback loops, and evaluation domains.
- [It's High Time: A Survey of Temporal Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20243): Surveys temporal question answering through time-aware representations, temporal reasoning operations, datasets, and evaluation protocols.
- [Revisiting Test-Time Scaling: A Survey and a Diversity-Aware Method for Efficient Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04611): Surveys test-time scaling and adds a diversity-aware efficient-reasoning method to manage sample redundancy.
- [Does It Make Sense to Speak of Introspection in Large Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05068): Examines whether introspection is a coherent capability framing for language models.
- [Large Language Models for Combinatorial Optimization: A Systematic Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03637): Survey candidate for general reasoning and optimization: Systematically reviews LLMs for combinatorial optimization.
- [A Survey on Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06203): Surveys reasoning-model methods, inference scaling, or reinforced reasoning.
- [Reasoning in Computer Vision: Taxonomy, Models, Tasks, and Methodologies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10523): Surveys visual-reasoning taxonomies across relational, temporal, causal, symbolic, and commonsense settings.
- [Explain Before You Answer: A Survey on Compositional Visual Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17298): Surveys decomposition, grounding, and multi-step inference methods for visual reasoning.
- [Implicit Reasoning in Large Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02350): Surveys latent and non-verbalized reasoning methods as alternatives to explicit chain-of-thought.
- [A Survey of Reinforcement Learning for Large Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08827): Reviews RL objectives, reward design, data, infrastructure, and scaling challenges behind large reasoning models.
- [Reinforcement Learning Meets Large Language Models: A Survey of Advancements and Applications Across the LLM Lifecycle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16679): Surveys reinforcement-learning methods across the LLM lifecycle.
- [Enhancing Large Language Model Reasoning with Reward Models: An Analytical Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01925): Analyzes how outcome, process, and preference reward models support search, verification, training, and inference-time reasoning.
- [A Survey of Inductive Reasoning for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10182): Reviews inductive reasoning methods, benchmarks, sandbox evaluation, data augmentation, post-training, and test-time scaling for LLMs.
- [Putting on the Thinking Hats: A Survey on Chain of Thought Fine-tuning from the Perspective of Human Reasoning Mechanism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13170): Surveys CoT fine-tuning through supervised and reinforced traces from a human-reasoning perspective.
- [Test-time Scaling of LLMs: A Survey from A Subproblem Structure Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14772): Organizes test-time scaling by subproblem structure, including decomposition, search allocation, aggregation, and compute-efficiency trade-offs.
- [Stabilizing Reinforcement Learning with LLMs: Formulation and Practices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01374): Formulates stabilization practices for reinforcement learning with LLMs, making RL training stability a survey-level axis for reasoning models.
- [Breaking the Reasoning Barrier A Survey on LLM Complex Reasoning through the Lens of Self-Evolution](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.386): Surveys complex LLM reasoning through the lens of self-evolution, organizing how models refine reasoning behavior through iterative data, feedback, and optimization loops.
- [Modeling Reasoning as Markov Decision Processes: A Theoretical Investigation into NLP Transformer Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/icecai66283.2025.11170436): Frames NLP transformer reasoning as Markov decision processes, linking reasoning trajectories to states, actions, rewards, and policy behavior.
- [Neuro-Symbolic AI for Advanced Signal and Image Processing: A Review of Recent Trends and Future Directions](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3598909): Reviews neuro-symbolic reasoning for signal and image processing, with emphasis on symbolic constraints, robustness, and uncertainty in perceptual pipelines.
- [A Survey of Complex Reasoning Enhancement Methods for Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccwamtip68645.2025.11352613): Surveys complex-reasoning enhancement methods for LLMs across prompt engineering, knowledge injection, fine-tuning, verification, and external-tool support.
- [Large Language Model (LLM) based Question and Answering System (QAS): A systematic literature review](https://doi.org/10.1109/ict4da67218.2025.11282707): Systematically reviews LLM-based QA systems by architecture, retrieval use, evaluation evidence, and reasoning limits in answer generation.
- [Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning: Performance, Challenges, and Benchmarks: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.53982/ajeas.2025.0301.05-j): Systematically reviews neuro-symbolic reasoning performance, challenges, and benchmarks.
- [Reinforcement Learning for Prompt Optimization in Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey of Methods, Representations, and Evaluation Challenges](https://doi.org/10.62762/tetai.2025.790504): Surveys reasoning-model methods, inference scaling, or reinforced reasoning.
- [Towards a Mechanistic Understanding of Large Reasoning Models: A Survey of Training, Inference, and Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19928): Surveys training, inference, and failure modes of large reasoning models from a mechanistic angle.
- [Reasoning Capabilities of Large Language Models. Lessons Learned from General Game Playing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19160): Distills lessons on LLM reasoning capability from general game playing.
- [The Reasoning Error About Reasoning: Why Different Types of Reasoning Require Different Representational Structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21736): Position-style analysis arguing that causal, logical, spatial, social, and temporal reasoning require different representational structures.
- [Wiring the 'Why'](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08016): Provides a taxonomy and survey of abductive reasoning in LLMs, linking philosophical foundations, task definitions, benchmarks, and model behavior.
- [Agentic World Modeling: Foundations, Capabilities, Laws, and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22748): Agentic World Modeling separates world-model capability into prediction, simulation, and evolution, then maps those capabilities across physical, digital, social, and scientific environments; the taxonomy connects model-based agents to the laws, feedback, and action loops needed for long-horizon reasoning.
- [Position: Artificial Intelligence Needs Meta Intelligence -- the Case for Metacognitive AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15567): Argues for metacognitive AI as a reasoning layer for monitoring uncertainty, self-assessment, strategy selection, and failure awareness.
- [A Primer in Post-Training Reasoning Data: What We Know About How It Works](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02113): Synthesizes more than 150 public studies and system reports on post-training reasoning data, organizing the field by data objects, usefulness criteria, construction pipelines, and scaling behavior.
- [Test-Time Scaling in Multimodal Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey of Generation and Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08231): Surveys test-time scaling for multimodal foundation models and builds a unified taxonomy of inference-time strategies for multimodal generation and reasoning.
- [Bridging the Agent-World Gap: Text World Models for LLM-based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09032): Reviews text world models for LLM agents as transition models over textual states, covering state representation, learning, planning, and evaluation across web navigation, code editing, tool use, and dialogue.
- [Table intelligence with large language models: A comprehensive survey and unified benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cosrev.2026.100996): Combines a table-intelligence survey with a unified benchmark covering table understanding, reasoning, generation, and task transfer.
- [A short survey on small reasoning models: training, inference, applications, and research directions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-025-50990-0): Surveys small reasoning models, covering training, inference, deployment, and research directions for compact reasoning systems.
- [How to evaluate the cognitive abilities of LLMs](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-02096-z): Gives methodological guidance for testing LLM cognitive abilities without overclaiming human-like reasoning.
- [Federated reasoning LLMs: a survey](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-025-50480-3): Surveys federated reasoning LLMs around distributed data, privacy-preserving training or inference, collaborative reasoning, and deployment constraints.
- [A Comprehensive Review of Neuro-symbolic AI for Robustness, Uncertainty Quantification, and Intervenability](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13369-025-10887-3): Reviews neuro-symbolic AI through robustness, uncertainty quantification, and intervenability as reliability axes for reasoning systems.
- [Large Language Models in Multi Criteria Decision Making: A Systematic Review, Taxonomy, and Future Research Agenda](https://doi.org/10.65069/ara21202614): Systematically reviews LLM use in multi-criteria decision making, proposing a taxonomy and future agenda for preference-sensitive reasoning.
