# 4.4.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Multimodal Large Language Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12320): A foundational review of perception, reasoning, planning, datasets, and benchmarks.
- [A Survey for Foundation Models in Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01105): Reviews foundation models across perception, prediction, planning, simulation, and evaluation.
- [A Survey of World Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11260): Surveys driving world models for prediction, simulation, planning, and evaluation.
- [Multi-Agent Autonomous Driving Systems with Large Language Models: A Survey of Recent Advances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16804): Reviews communication, coordination, scenario reasoning, and decision support.
- [A Survey on Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24044): Bridges perception, language-grounded reasoning, and action generation.
