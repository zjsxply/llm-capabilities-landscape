# 1.5.2 Survey

- [Visual Text Meets Low-level Vision: A Comprehensive Survey on Visual Text Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03082): Surveys visual text processing at the intersection of low-level vision and text-centric perception.
- [Large language model for table processing: a survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05121): Organizes LLM-based table understanding, reasoning, generation, augmentation, and question-answering work, giving OCR-adjacent document intelligence a table-specific survey lens.
- [From Pixels to Insights: A Survey on Automatic Chart Understanding in the Era of Large Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12027): Covers chart-understanding tasks, datasets, methods, and evaluation under foundation models.
- [Exploring the Frontier of Vision-Language Models: A Survey of Current Methodologies and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07214): Reviews VLM architectures, training data, alignment, evaluation, and application trends, providing broad context for image understanding beyond narrow OCR pipelines.
- [The Evolution of Multimodal Model Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17927): Traces how multimodal architectures moved from dual encoders and fusion modules toward unified LLM-centered visual-language systems, which matters for OCR and document VLM backbones.
- [Charting the Landscape of Nefarious Uses of Generative Artificial Intelligence for Online Election Interference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01862): A misuse-risk survey for generated media in elections; keep only as an adjacent visual-forensics lead rather than a core OCR survey.
- [Deep Learning based Visually Rich Document Content Understanding: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01287): Surveys visually rich document understanding across layout analysis, text recognition, information extraction, table/form parsing, and multimodal document reasoning.
- [Surveying the Landscape of Image Captioning Evaluation: A Comprehensive Taxonomy, Trends, and Metrics Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04909): Taxonomizes image-captioning metrics by lexical, semantic, embedding, reference-free, and human-alignment criteria, clarifying why caption quality cannot be reduced to OCR correctness.
- [Masked Image Modeling: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06687): Reviews masked-image pretraining objectives, masking strategies, reconstruction targets, and downstream transfer, useful for understanding visual encoders that later feed OCR and document VLMs.
- [Image Segmentation in Foundation Model Era: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12957): Organizes foundation-model-era segmentation around promptable masks, open-vocabulary segmentation, adaptation, and evaluation, an adjacent visual grounding layer for document and scene-text systems.
- [Advancing Object Detection in Transportation with Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs): A Comprehensive Review and Empirical Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18286): Reviews transportation object detection with MLLMs and adds empirical tests, making it an applied visual-perception survey rather than an OCR-specific one.
- [Recent Advances of Multimodal Continual Learning: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05352): Surveys how multimodal systems retain and update visual-language skills under streaming tasks, a deployment issue for document and OCR agents that face changing layouts or domains.
- [Natural Language Understanding and Inference with MLLM in Visual Question Answering: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17558): Reviews VQA systems through language understanding, inference, and multimodal reasoning, including how questions over images stress text, objects, and visual evidence jointly.
- [Explainable and Interpretable Multimodal Large Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02104): Organizes interpretability methods for MLLMs, including attention, attribution, rationale, and probing tools that help diagnose OCR and document-understanding failures.
- [Toward Visual Grounding: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20206): Surveys phrase grounding, referring expressions, region localization, and open-vocabulary grounding, which are key for connecting extracted text or document answers back to image regions.
- [Large Vision-Language Model Alignment and Misalignment: A Survey Through the Lens of Explainability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01346): Surveys image and vision-language understanding methods, evaluation gaps, or deployment issues.
- [Survey on Question Answering over Visually Rich Documents: Methods, Challenges, and Trends](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02235): Surveys document QA over layout, OCR, structure, and reasoning challenges.
- [Multimodal Large Language Models for Text-rich Image Understanding: A Comprehensive Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16586): Reviews MLLM progress, tasks, and challenges for text-rich image understanding.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Composed Image Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18495): Surveys image and vision-language understanding methods, evaluation gaps, or deployment issues.
- [Keeping Yourself is Important in Downstream Tuning Multimodal Large Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04543): Reviews and benchmarks downstream tuning methods for multimodal large language models.
- [Visual Text Processing: A Comprehensive Review and Unified Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21682): Reviews visual text processing from detection and recognition to reconstruction and manipulation, and introduces VTPBench plus VTPScore for unified evaluation.
- [A Survey on MLLM-based Visually Rich Document Understanding: Methods, Challenges, and Emerging Trends](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09861): Updates document understanding for end-to-end multimodal large language models.
- [Multimodal Referring Segmentation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00265): Surveys language-guided segmentation methods and evaluation for referring visual targets.
- [Document Intelligence in the Era of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13366): Places OCR, layout analysis, information extraction, document QA, and workflow automation inside one LLM-era document-intelligence taxonomy.
- [Scaling Beyond Context: A Survey of Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Document Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15253): Reviews multimodal retrieval for long and multi-page document understanding.
- [Chart Accessibility](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3571626): Reviews chart alt-text generation, accessibility requirements, datasets, and evaluation gaps relevant to chart understanding and image-to-text generation.
- [Systematic Literature Review of Machine Learning Models and Applications for Text Recognition](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3618109): Surveys text-recognition models, application settings, and evaluation issues, providing OCR-side context for document and image understanding.
- [A Review on Vision-Language-Based Approaches: Challenges and Applications](https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2025.060363): Reviews vision-language approaches across representation learning, cross-modal fusion, applications, and challenges such as grounding, robustness, and data bias.
- [Pixels to prose: A comprehensive survey of image captioning techniques with deep learning and generative artificial intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.132385): Surveys image captioning from CNN-RNN systems through transformers and generative AI, emphasizing datasets, metrics, and remaining semantic-grounding gaps.
- [Systematic Literature Review on General-Use Handwritten Document Processing Using OCR and LLM in Mobile Application](https://doi.org/10.1109/icimtech67074.2025.11265362): Systematically reviews handwritten document processing with OCR and LLMs in mobile applications.
- [A comprehensive survey of Vision-Language Models: Pretrained models, fine-tuning, prompt engineering, adapters, and benchmark datasets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2025.103623): Surveys image and vision-language understanding methods, evaluation gaps, or deployment issues.
- [A comparison study on optical character recognition models in mathematical equations and in any language](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rico.2025.100532): Compares OCR models on equation and multilingual recognition, adding survey-style evidence for OCR robustness beyond ordinary printed text.
- [Can VLMs Actually See and Read? A Survey on Modality Collapse in Vision-Language Models](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.1256): Surveys modality collapse in VLMs, clarifying failures in visual recognition and reading despite fluent language outputs.
- [Multimodal Information Fusion for Chart Understanding: A Survey of MLLMs - Evolution, Limitations, and Cognitive Enhancement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10138): Surveys MLLM-based chart understanding through information fusion, limitations, and cognitive-enhancement directions.
- [Position: Evaluation of Visual Processing Should Be Human-Centered, Not Metric-Centered](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00643): Argues that visual-processing evaluation should prioritize human use cases, perceptual quality, and task consequences instead of optimizing isolated automatic metrics.
- [A Survey of OCR Evaluation Methods and Metrics and the Invisibility of Historical Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25761): Anchors OCR-specific metrics and historical-document blind spots.
- [Large Language Models in Document Intelligence: A Comprehensive Survey, Recent Advances, Challenges, and Future Trends](https://doi.org/10.1145/3768156): Surveys document intelligence with LLMs, covering OCR-adjacent extraction, layout understanding, and document question answering.
- [Decoding Technical Diagrams: A Survey of AI Methods for Image Content Extraction and Understanding](https://doi.org/10.3390/info17020165): Focuses on diagram understanding, covering symbol and text extraction, relation parsing, semantic interpretation, and evaluation for technical visual documents.
- [Deep learning–driven image captioning: Progress through transformers and large language models](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0345012): Reviews image captioning progress from convolutional encoders to transformer and LLM-based captioners, with attention to dataset bias, evaluation, and controllability.
- [The evolution and open challenges of text-based visual question answering: a review of research and data trends](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-026-21656-1): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for image understanding and OCR; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
