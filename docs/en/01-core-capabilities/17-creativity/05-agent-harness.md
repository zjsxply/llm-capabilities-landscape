# 1.17.5 Agent Harness

- [Understanding Nonlinear Collaboration between Human and AI Agents: A Co-design Framework for Creative Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07312): This work frames human-AI creative design as nonlinear collaboration, giving co-design systems a workflow lens for shifting initiative, iteration, and agency between humans and AI agents.
- [AI-Augmented Brainwriting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14978): Embeds an LLM in collaborative group ideation. Core idea: support both divergent idea generation and convergent evaluation inside a Brainwriting workflow, then compare the resulting solution space and LLM-based idea ratings with expert and novice evaluators.
- [ByteComposer: a Human-like Melody Composition Method based on Language Model Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17785): Builds an LLM-agent workflow for melody composition. Core idea: decompose composition into agent-controlled musical decisions so generated melodies follow human-like planning rather than a single unconstrained continuation.
- [MIMOSA: Human-AI Co-Creation of Computational Spatial Audio Effects on Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15107): MIMOSA lets users co-create computational spatial-audio effects for videos, coupling visual context with interactive audio-effect generation.
- [Humor Mechanics: Advancing Humor Generation with Multistep Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07280): Turns one-liner joke writing into a multi-step reasoning workflow. Core idea: explicitly plan humor mechanics before generation, then use human-labeled comparisons to show that structured reasoning improves joke quality over zero-shot prompting.
- [Ai.llude: Investigating Rewriting AI-Generated Text to Support Creative Expression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17843): Ai.llude studies 27 AI-assisted writing sessions and shows that deliberately imperfect intermediate suggestions can motivate writers to rewrite AI text and preserve ownership over creative expression.
- [MetaDesigner: Advancing Artistic Typography through AI-Driven, User-Centric, and Multilingual WordArt Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19859): MetaDesigner orchestrates Pipeline, Glyph, and Texture agents with multimodal and user feedback for multilingual WordArt synthesis.
- [Proxona: Supporting Creators' Sensemaking and Ideation with LLM-Powered Audience Personas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10937): Proxona uses LLM-powered audience personas to help creators make sense of likely audience reactions and turn that simulated feedback into new ideation directions.
- [Initial Development and Evaluation of the Creative Artificial Intelligence through Recurring Developments and Determinations (CAIRDD) System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02291): Implements an iterative concept-injection and refinement harness for deliberate creativity. Core idea: use recurring developments and determinations to repeatedly steer LLM outputs toward more creative concepts instead of relying on one-pass generation.
- [PersonaFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12538): Simulates domain-specific experts for research ideation. Core idea: let users customize multiple LLM expert personas, producing more relevant and creative research directions while increasing critical-thinking activity without raising perceived cognitive load.
- [Scideator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14634): Turns scientific ideation into human-guided facet recombination, extracting purposes, mechanisms, and evaluations from papers so researchers can generate and screen ideas through literature-grounded analogies.
- [Let's Think Outside the Box](https://doi.org/10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.01258): Explores leap-of-thought prompting for creative humor generation. Core idea: make the model reason over sparse and non-obvious associations before producing jokes, treating humor as a structured creative-search workflow rather than a one-shot completion.
- [Innovative Thinking, Infinite Humor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10370): Builds the LoL framework for humor generation through structured thought leaps. Core idea: evolve humor-specific instructions, inject external knowledge, and use reinforcement learning over extracted reasoning logic so joke generation benefits from multi-hop creative reasoning.
- [Semantic Navigation for AI-assisted Ideation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03575): Semantic Navigation replaces single prompt-output ideation with LLM-supported movement through problem and solution spaces plus relevance, coherence, and alignment filters.
- [A Random Walk Modeling Framework for Boosting the Creativity of Humans and AI](https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.2024.0982): Models ideation as random walks over concept networks and tests prompt interventions such as constraints, context shifts, and delayed selection to increase creativity in humans and LLMs.
- [LLMs can realize combinatorial creativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14141): Implements combinatorial-creativity theory as an LLM ideation workflow. Core idea: use generalization-level retrieval to find cross-domain concepts, then recombine them through a structured process so creative idea generation is driven by explicit knowledge transfer rather than free-form brainstorming.
- [MDSF: Context-Aware Multi-Dimensional Data Storytelling Framework based on Large language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01014): MDSF uses an LLM-centered, context-aware framework to organize data, audience, visualization, and narrative dimensions into data stories rather than treating storytelling as a one-shot captioning task.
- [Toyteller](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13284): Uses toy-like character-symbol motion as a control interface, mapping motions and text into a shared semantic space for bidirectional story-text and symbol-motion generation.
- ["See What I Imagine, Imagine What I See": Human-AI Co-Creation System for 360° Panoramic Video Generation in VR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15456): Builds a VR co-creation loop for 360-degree panoramic video, letting users iteratively externalize imagined scenes and revise generated immersive footage.
- [Jupybara: Operationalizing a Design Space for Actionable Data Analysis and Storytelling with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16661): Jupybara operationalizes an LLM design space for actionable data analysis and storytelling, connecting analysis steps to narrative and follow-up actions.
- [The Beatbots: A Musician-Informed Multi-Robot Percussion Quartet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00966): Builds a musician-informed multi-robot percussion quartet and evaluates it through a live public performance, treating robotic timing, coordination, and performer feedback as an embodied music-creation harness.
- [Conceptual Metaphor Theory as a Prompting Paradigm for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01901): Uses conceptual metaphor theory as an LLM prompting paradigm, structuring figurative mappings so models can reason and generate through source-target conceptual frames.
- [AI Humor Generation: Cognitive, Social and Creative Skills for Effective Humor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07981): Compares ordinary LLM meme captions with captions generated after adding cognitive, social, creative, and audience-awareness humor skills, showing how explicit skill scaffolds change Gen Z humor ratings.
- [WhatELSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18641): Lets authors sculpt AI-generated interactive narrative spaces through pivot, outline, and variant views, then unfolds the controlled space into executable game events.
- [Brickify: Enabling Expressive Design Intent Specification through Direct Manipulation on Design Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21219): Introduces direct manipulation on design tokens, extracting subjects, styles, and colors from reference images so designers can specify visual intent without fully verbalizing it.
- [Augmenting Online Meetings with Context-Aware Real-time Music Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01354): Introduces Discussion Jockey 2, which reads live meeting transcripts and generates adaptive background music to support relaxation, concentration, and engagement.
- [Diverse AI Personas Can Mitigate the Homogenization Effect in Human-AI Collaborative Ideation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13868): Uses diverse AI personas during collaborative ideation to reduce homogenized suggestions and keep the human-AI idea pool broader.
- [Expanding the Generative AI Design Space through Structured Prompting and Multimodal Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14320): Combines structured prompting and multimodal interfaces for small-business advertising design, reducing friction when novice users cannot verbalize creative goals.
- [A Multi-Agent AI Framework for Immersive Audiobook Production through Spatial Audio and Neural Narration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04885): Coordinates LLM interpretation, neural narration, character-specific voices, temporally aligned sound effects, and diffusion-based spatial audio to turn text narratives into immersive audiobook scenes.
- [Card Sorting Simulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09478): Uses LLMs to simulate card-sorting categorizations across 28 real practitioner studies, providing automated feedback for information-architecture ideation tools.
- [Persona Alchemy: Designing, Evaluating, and Implementing Psychologically-Grounded LLM Agents for Diverse Stakeholder Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18351): Builds psychologically grounded LLM personas for stakeholder representation, giving design teams simulated viewpoints for ideation and critique.
- [Guiding Generative Storytelling with Knowledge Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24803): Adds a KG-assisted storytelling pipeline where users edit knowledge graphs to steer LLM narrative coherence, structure, and control during story generation.
- [Feedstack: Layering Structured Representations Over Unstructured Feedback to Scaffold Human–AI Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03052): Feedstack layers structured representations over free-form feedback conversations so users and AI can externalize intent, navigate design proposals, and revisit unresolved feedback.
- [Exploring the Potential of Metacognitive Support Agents for Human-AI Co-Creation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12879): Adds metacognitive support agents to creative collaboration so users can reflect on goals, assumptions, and iteration choices instead of receiving only generative suggestions.
- [LLM-enhanced Interactions in Human-Robot Collaborative Drawing with Older Adults](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18711): Studies a Drawing with Robots course where LLM-enhanced robots offer drawing suggestions and participants act as curators, teachers, or coaches, exposing role design for embodied co-creative drawing.
- [Do Conversational Interfaces Limit Creativity? Exploring Visual Graph Systems for Creative Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08260): Builds a node-based visual graph interface for chaining generative models in creative writing, replacing linear chat with reusable and shareable exploration templates.
- [Human-AI Co-Creation: A Framework for Collaborative Design in Intelligent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17774): Frames collaborative design as an iterative AI-supported proposal, critique, and revision loop for intelligent systems rather than a one-shot generation task.
- [IdeaBlocks: Expressing and Reusing Divergent Intents for Graphic Design Exploration using Generative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22163): Modularizes open-ended graphic-design intents into reusable Exploration Blocks, enabling branching, chaining, and context-adaptive reuse during generative design exploration.
- [Explainability-in-Action: Enabling Expressive Manipulation and Tacit Understanding by Bending Diffusion Models in ComfyUI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07183): Treats diffusion models in ComfyUI as manipulable creative materials, exposing internal structure for expressive control and tacit understanding instead of using XAI only to explain final outputs.
