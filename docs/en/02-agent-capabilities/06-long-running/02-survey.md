# 2.6.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432): A foundational review of planning, memory, tool use, and action-feedback loops.
- [Large Language Model based Multi-Agents: A Survey of Progress and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01680): Reviews collaboration, communication, task decomposition, and evaluation challenges.
- [Understanding the planning of LLM agents: A survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02716): Covers decomposition, search, reflection, external tools, and replanning.
- [Levels of AI Agents: from Rules to Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06643): Adds a survey entry for Long-Running, because it taxonomizes AI agents from rule-based systems to large language models.
- [AgentReview: Exploring Peer Review Dynamics with LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12708): Maps recent work and open issues for long running capability coverage.
- [A Survey on Complex Tasks for Goal-Directed Interactive Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18538): A Survey on Complex Tasks for Goal-Directed Interactive Agents surveys long-horizon, self-improving, or persistent task execution and helps map methods, risks, or evaluation boundaries.
- [Generalist Virtual Agents: A Survey on Autonomous Agents Across Digital Platforms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10943): Maps recent work and open issues for long running capability coverage.
- [Self-Directing AI: The Road to Fully Autonomous AI Agents](https://doi.org/10.1109/mc.2024.3507513): Surveys the road toward fully autonomous self-directing AI agents.
- [A Roadmap to Guide the Integration of LLMs in Hierarchical Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08068): Maps how LLMs can be integrated into hierarchical planning, including decomposition and execution-control challenges.
- [Evaluating LLM-based Agents for Multi-Turn Conversations: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22458): Surveys how LLM-based agents are evaluated in multi-turn conversational settings.
- [PlanGenLLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.958/): A modern survey of LLM planning capabilities, useful for separating planning, replanning, and execution-control issues in long-running agents.
- [A Survey on Agent Workflow - Status and Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01186): Surveys how long-running workflows are structured, optimized, and monitored.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Self-Evolving AI Agents: A New Paradigm Bridging Foundation Models and Lifelong Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07407): Connects accumulated experience, self-improvement, and lifelong adaptation.
- [A Practitioner's Guide to Multi-turn Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01132): Organizes practical choices for training multi-turn agents with reinforcement learning, including trajectory structure, rewards, and stability concerns.
- [Beyond Pipelines: A Survey of the Paradigm Shift toward Model-Native Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16720): Surveys the shift from pipeline-style orchestration toward model-native agentic AI, clarifying where agent behavior moves into model training and inference.
- [Agentic AI: A Paradigm Shift in Autonomous Decision-Making and Intelligent Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/rcsm67767.2025.11507473): Surveys, reviews, or taxonomizes long-running agents methods, risks, datasets, or evaluation practices.
- [Agentic AI in the Enterprise: Autonomy, Ethics, and Architecture](https://doi.org/10.1109/ictbig68706.2025.11323732): Surveys, reviews, or taxonomizes long-running agents methods, risks, datasets, or evaluation practices.
- [Mind the Workflow: Unmasking the Super Agents of Today](https://doi.org/10.1109/aimlsystems67835.2025.11331213): Surveys, reviews, or taxonomizes long-running agents methods, risks, datasets, or evaluation practices.
- [Externalization in LLM Agents: A Unified Review of Memory, Skills, Protocols and Harness Engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08224): Reviews externalization in LLM agents across memory, skills, protocols, and harness engineering.
- [Rethinking Agentic Reinforcement Learning In Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27859): A survey or position-style entry on agentic reinforcement learning for long-horizon language-model agents.
- [A Survey of Task Planning with Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.34133/icomputing.0124): Reviews LLM task-planning methods, complementing long-running-agent surveys on decomposition, replanning, and execution control.
- [Conceptual Foundations of LLM-Powered Agents: From Language Processing to Autonomous Reasoning](https://doi.org/10.54691/atmxvb43): Surveys, reviews, or taxonomizes long-running agents methods, risks, datasets, or evaluation practices.
- [From Model Scaling to System Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26112): Frames agent capability progress as harness scaling, surveying context governance, trustworthy memory, dynamic skill routing, orchestration, governance, and the CheetahClaws reference harness.
