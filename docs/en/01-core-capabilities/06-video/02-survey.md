# 1.6.2 Survey

- [Video Understanding with Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17432): Organizes Vid-LLMs into analyzer, embedder, and hybrid LLM couplings, with tasks, datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation methods.
- [Advancing Video Anomaly Detection: A Concise Review and a New Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04857): Links VAD model progress to dataset gaps and introduces MSAD, a 14-scenario multi-view surveillance anomaly dataset.
- [The Revolution of Multimodal Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12451): Broad visual-MLLM review covering architectures, alignment, training, visual grounding, image generation/editing, and visual understanding tasks.
- [Networking Systems for Video Anomaly Detection: A Tutorial and Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10347): Frames VAD as deployable NSVAD, covering learning frameworks, edge and IoVT scenarios, shared resources, and system challenges.
- [Efficient multimodal large language models: a survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10739): Catalogs efficient MLLM structures and strategies, representative model timelines, applications, and edge-computing constraints.
- [Video anomaly detection in 10 years: a survey and outlook](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19387): Reviews deep VAD across weakly supervised, self-supervised, and unsupervised paradigms, including dataset/model coupling and VLM feature extractors.
- [A Comprehensive Review of Multimodal Large Language Models: Performance and Challenges Across Different Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01319): Compares MLLM applications across text, vision, audio, video, and physiological sequences while summarizing task focus and open shortcomings.
- [Deep Learning for Video Anomaly Detection: A Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05383): Builds a five-way VAD taxonomy spanning semi-, weakly, fully, unsupervised, and open-set settings, plus large-model VAD, datasets, code, and metrics.
- [From Seconds to Hours: Reviewing MultiModal Large Language Models on Comprehensive Long Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18938): Contrasts image, short-video, and long-video MM-LLMs, emphasizing long-term temporal dependencies, design choices, training methods, and length-varied benchmarks.
- [Human Action Anticipation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14045): Unifies action anticipation, activity forecasting, intent and goal prediction, datasets, metrics, and comparisons across eleven anticipation datasets.
- [Privacy-Preserving Video Anomaly Detection: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14565): Defines P2VAD and taxonomizes data, feature, and system-level privacy approaches for VAD, including privacy leakage and appearance-bias concerns.
- [Video Quality Assessment: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04508): Reviews VQA algorithms and psychometric video-quality databases for UGC, from natural-statistics models to deep and LMM-based predictors.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Action Quality Assessment: Method and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11149): Proposes a modality-driven AQA taxonomy and a unified benchmark for video-based AQA methods with standardized evaluation protocols.
- [Video anomaly detection: A systematic review of issues and prospects](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2024.127726): Neurocomputing systematic review on VAD issues and prospects, retained as a compact map of surveillance anomaly-detection problem framing.
- [Video Question Answering: A survey of the state-of-the-art](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jvcir.2024.104320): JVCIR state-of-the-art survey on video question answering, a task that links temporal visual evidence to natural-language questions.
- [A Decade of Action Quality Assessment: Largest Systematic Survey of Trends, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02817): Uses PRISMA over 200 AQA papers, covering definitions, frameworks, metrics, datasets, trends, challenges, and future directions.
- [A Survey on Video Analytics in Cloud-Edge-Terminal Collaborative Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06581): Reviews CETC video analytics architectures, resource management, task offloading, edge/cloud processing, and LLM/multimodal opportunities.
- [SAM2 for Image and Video Segmentation Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12781): Analyzes SAM and SAM2 foundations, static-image and video segmentation applications, domain adaptation, and limitations across 200-plus papers.
- [VideoLLM Benchmarks and Evaluation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03829): Maps VideoLLM benchmark characteristics, closed/open/specialized evaluation protocols, performance trends, and gaps in temporal and spatiotemporal testing.
- [A Challenge to Build Neuro-Symbolic Video Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13851): Surveys video understanding; useful for mapping methods, risks, and evaluation gaps in video understanding.
- [Leveraging Auxiliary Information in Text-to-Video Retrieval: A Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23952): Reviews 81 T2V retrieval papers using auxiliary objects, temporal/spatial context, speech, and rephrased captions to bridge video-text semantic gaps.
- [Video Scene Parsing Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13552): Reviews semantic, instance, panoptic, tracking, and open-vocabulary video parsing, with datasets and metrics for temporal consistency.
- [LLMs for Crash Detection in Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02074): Reviews LLM/VLM crash-detection fusion strategies, datasets, architectures, benchmarks, and deployment challenges.
- [The Evolution of Video Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21649): Unifies DNN- and LLM-based VAD, tracking how MLLMs change annotation, modalities, architectures, and task objectives.
- [Segment Anything for Video Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22792): Structures SAM/SAM2 video object segmentation and tracking around past memory, current-frame features, and future motion prediction.
- [A Survey on Video Temporal Grounding with Multimodal Large Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10922): Organizes VTG-MLLMs by functional roles, training paradigms, and video feature processing, with benchmarks and evaluation protocols.
- [A Survey on Video Anomaly Detection via Deep Learning: Human, Vehicle, and Environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14203): Organizes VAD by supervision level, online/active/continual adaptation, and human, vehicle, and environment scenarios.
- [Looking Beyond the Obvious: A Survey on Abstract Concept Recognition for Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20765): Reviews tasks and datasets for abstract concept recognition in videos and revisits high-level semantic reasoning under foundation models.
- [Video Understanding by Design: How Datasets Shape Architectures and Insights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09151): Explains how motion complexity, temporal span, hierarchy, and multimodal richness in datasets drive video model architecture choices.
- [Rethinking Visual Intelligence: Insights from Video Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24448): Surveys video pretraining or video-understanding capability.
- [Traffic Anomaly VLM Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC60802.2025.11423492): Reviews prompt-learning, end-to-end fine-tuning, and feature-adapter VLM methods for video-based traffic anomaly detection, plus datasets and metrics.
- [Abnormal Behavior Detection in Video Surveillance Survey](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.113438): Engineering Applications of AI survey on optimized-ML abnormal-behavior detection in intelligence-information video surveillance systems.
- [Bridging Multimodal and Video Summarization: A Unified Survey](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.newsum-main.11): ACL workshop survey aligning multimodal and video summarization, including evidence selection, temporal coverage, and summary-quality evaluation.
- [Multimodal Video Moment Retrieval: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcnn64981.2025.11227753): IJCNN survey of multimodal video moment retrieval, connecting language, visual, and other modality cues for temporal grounding.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Video Summarization: Challenges and Advances](https://doi.org/10.1109/tcsvt.2025.3596006): TCSVT survey on video summarization challenges and advances, complementing LLM-summary entries with broader methods and evaluations.
- [Remote Sensing Video Tracking: Current Status, Challenges, and Future](https://doi.org/10.1109/jstars.2025.3573572): JSTARS review of remote-sensing video tracking status, challenges, and future directions for aerial and satellite video streams.
- [Real-Time Deep Anomaly Detection: An Overview of Benchmark Datasets and Performance Metrics](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trpro.2025.03.091): Overview of real-time deep anomaly-detection benchmark datasets and performance metrics for surveillance-style VAD evaluation practice.
- [A survey on video emotion recognition: Segmentation, classification, and explainable AI techniques](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.image.2025.117442): Reviews video emotion recognition through segmentation, classification, and explainable-AI techniques.
- [Multi-modal Video Summarization: A Comprehensive Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/icrm66809.2025.11349049): ICRM survey of multimodal video summarization, separating multi-signal summary construction from single-stream video skimming.
- [Advances in Computer Vision for Public Safety: AI-Powered Video Surveillance](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccca66364.2025.11325229): IEEE ICCCA survey lead on AI-powered video surveillance for public-safety monitoring and incident detection.
- [Video Understanding: Through A Temporal Lens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00683): Candidate survey and taxonomy entry point for video understanding; it directly targets this page's capability scope and belongs in the `Survey` track.
- [Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17840): Structures video understanding into low-level geometry, high-level semantics, and unified video models for scalable spatiotemporal reasoning.
- [Distorted or Fabricated? A Survey on Hallucination in Video LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12944): Taxonomizes Vid-LLM hallucinations as dynamic distortion and content fabrication, then reviews evaluation, mitigation, and root causes.
- [Is Video Anomaly Detection Misframed?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12725): Position-style study argues VAD should refocus on single-scene, spatially aware, explainable normality instead of weakly supervised multi-scene semantic labels.
- [Edge-IoT Video Anomaly Detection Survey](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iswa.2026.200635): Edge/IoT survey of VAD input modalities and real-time application constraints.
- [Spiking Neural Networks for Video Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2026.108844): Reviews spiking-neural-network models and architectures for video analysis, emphasizing event-style temporal processing.
- [Lightweight Video Anomaly Detection for Industrial Contexts: A Multi-Axis Taxonomy of Modeling, Efficiency, and Operational Robustness](https://doi.org/10.1109/estream70144.2026.11511513): Multi-axis taxonomy for lightweight industrial VAD, spanning modeling, efficiency, and operational robustness.
- [Language-Guided Video Object Segmentation Survey](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44336-025-00018-9): Tracks language-guided VOS from referring queries to reasoning-oriented segmentation, with datasets and reasoning-aware evaluation.
- [Transformers for Video Anomaly Detection](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-025-11218-1): Reviews transformer and Vision Transformer methods for VAD, including advantages, limitations, and technical challenges.
- [Generative Artificial Intelligence and the Evolving Challenge of Deepfake Detection: A Systematic Analysis](https://doi.org/10.3390/jsan14010017): Reviews deepfake generation and detection, including GAN/diffusion methods, forensic and hybrid detectors, interpretability, real-time use, and regulation.
- [A review of deep learning based multimodal forgery detection for video and audio](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42452-025-07629-3): Reviews audio-visual forgery detection fusion strategies, public datasets, benchmarks, cross-domain generalization, and lightweight deployment needs.
- [Video understanding with image, audio, and text](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3049519): SPIE proceedings metadata identifies a multimodal video-understanding article combining image, audio, and text signals.
- [Video Summarization Using Deep Learning and Optimization Approaches: A Systematic Review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3798047): Systematically classifies two decades of video summarization methods, including deep learning, optimization, single/multi-view settings, datasets, and evaluation.
- [Computational and Deep Learning Approaches for Video Analysis: An Engineering Perspective on Methods, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11831-026-10523-9): Springer abstract frames video analysis as digital forensics, covering video-forgery detection pipelines, spatiotemporal detectors, and XAI forensic engineering.
