# 2.8.3 Bench

- [NIFTY Financial News Headlines Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09747): Packages financial news headlines for market-forecasting experiments, with NIFTY-LM for supervised fine-tuning and NIFTY-RL for reward-model or RLHF-style learning from market movement signals.
- [LTSM-Bundle: A Toolbox and Benchmark on Large Language Models for Time Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14045): Benchmarks design choices for large time-series models, comparing prompting, tokenization, training paradigm, base model selection, and data diversity before composing a stronger zero- and few-shot forecasting bundle.
- [MIRAI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01231): Evaluates agentic forecasting for international events. Core idea: let agents access historical structured event databases and news text, call domain APIs through code interfaces, and evaluate source integration, tool use, and cross-time reasoning; an early representative of the live future-prediction line.
- [Temporal Event Forecasting Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11638): Builds MidEast-TE-mini with graph and text signals for temporal event forecasting, then compares raw-text, structured, fine-tuned, and retrieval-augmented LLM setups to expose popularity bias and long-tail event failures.
- [Stock Market Trend Prediction Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12408): Compares TCN, N-BEATS, TFT, N-HiTS, TiDE, and xLSTM-TS on S&P 500 and EWZ daily/hourly close-price direction prediction, showing that wavelet denoising changes the apparent strength of deep time-series forecasters.
- [ForecastBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) ([Leaderboard](http://www.forecastbench.org/)): Evaluates dynamically generated, periodically updated real future-event forecasting. Core idea: include only questions unresolved at submission time, compare experts, crowds, and LLMs together, and specifically stress-test probabilistic forecasting under minimized data leakage.
- [Context is Key (CiK)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18959) ([project page](https://servicenow.github.io/context-is-key-forecasting/)): Evaluates time-series forecasting with necessary textual context. Core idea: bind numerical history with natural-language constraints/background knowledge so models must understand text to forecast, testing "forecast after reading context" rather than only curve extrapolation.
- [WORLDREP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14042): Builds a text-based international-event prediction dataset with LLM-generated labels validated by political-science experts, plus released automation for collecting, labeling, and benchmarking event-forecasting examples.
- [FOReCAst](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19676) ([open-source code](https://github.com/MoyYuan/FOReCAst), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MoyYuan/FOReCAst)): Evaluates probabilistic judgment and confidence calibration on real forecasting tasks across domains. Core idea: combine Boolean, timeframe, and quantity question types while evaluating both prediction accuracy and confidence, addressing evaluations that look only at final answers and ignore calibration.
- [MTBench: A Multimodal Time Series Benchmark for Temporal Reasoning and Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16858): Evaluates multimodal time-series temporal reasoning and question answering. Core idea: connect time-series signals with language questions so temporal reasoning is tested beyond scalar forecasting error.
- [PROPHET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01509): Evaluates future event prediction with causal interventions. Core idea: split event prediction into inferable future states and post-intervention likelihood estimation, adding an interpretable future-prediction evaluation line before FutureX/Prophet Arena.
- [Can LLM-based Financial Investing Strategies Outperform the Market in Long Run?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07078): Introduces FINSABER, a long-horizon backtesting framework for LLM-based investing strategies across broader symbols and time spans.
- [Outcome-based RL Forecasting Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17989): Evaluates future-event probability prediction and calibration on prediction-market questions. Core idea: combine recent prediction-market questions, news clues, and delayed real outcomes, using outcome reward to train/evaluate smaller models and showing that future prediction can form an RLVR-style loop.
- [ExAnte: A Benchmark for Ex-Ante Inference in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19533): Large language models (LLMs) face significant challenges in ex-ante reasoning, where analysis, inference, or predictions must be made without access to information from future events.
- [WXImpactBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20249): Evaluates disruptive-weather impact understanding from regional-newspaper corpora, using a four-stage dataset pipeline and two tasks: multi-label impact classification and ranking-based question answering.
- [Predicting Empirical AI Research Outcomes with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00794): Evaluates forecasting empirical AI research outcomes. Core idea: make future prediction measurable with task-specific data, protocols, metrics, or diagnostic environments rather than generic aggregate scores.
- [Prompt Engineering Large Language Models' Forecasting Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01578): What it evaluates: a forecasting-capability study centered on prompt engineering choices.
- [FinAR-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07315): Decomposes financial-statement fundamental analysis into key-information extraction, financial-indicator calculation, and logical reasoning, so report-generation failures can be localized rather than scored as one opaque finance answer.
- [NSW-EPNews: A News-Augmented Benchmark for Electricity Price Forecasting with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11050): Benchmarks or measures future prediction and forecasting, providing a candidate reusable evaluation signal.
- [Bench to the Future (BTF)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21558): Evaluates reproducible pastcasting for forecasting agents. Core idea: pair historical forecasting questions with known outcomes with frozen web corpora, letting agents research, assign probabilities, and leave reasoning traces offline as if forecasting in real time, avoiding waits for future resolution and live-web drift.
- [Evaluating LLMs on Real-World Forecasting Against Expert Forecasters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04562): Evaluates LLM probability forecasts on real Metaculus forecasting questions. Core idea: compare frontier models and expert forecasters on the same real forecasting questions using Brier score and calibration, complementing evaluations that only rank models against one another.
- [Frozen Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13942): Evaluates frozen vision backbones by training latent diffusion forecasters in each model's representation space, then scoring full future trajectories across four tasks from pixel prediction to object motion.
- [ConDiFi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18368): Evaluates divergent and convergent reasoning in financial scenarios, combining macro-financial future-generation prompts with adversarial multi-hop questions to test whether models can produce plausible, decision-relevant forecasts under uncertainty.
- [Forecast Stability for Demand Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10063): turns forecast stability into an explicit evaluation signal for demand-planning time-series models, complementing accuracy-only forecasting benchmarks.
- [FutureX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11987) ([project page and leaderboard](https://futurex-ai.github.io/)): Evaluates live agents for future-event prediction. Core idea: use real-time updating, automatic question collection, and answer collection to reduce data contamination, requiring agents to dynamically gather information, weigh uncertainty, and continuously adapt to new trends, close to analyst/strategic-research work.
- [Dunkelflaute Risk Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24788): Adapts generative downscaling of CMIP6 climate simulations to estimate future low-wind and low-solar episodes in Germany under SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5 scenarios, making renewable-grid risk a concrete long-horizon forecasting case.
- [Information Leakage in Time-Series Foundation-Model Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13654): Analyzes known and unknown information leakage in time-series foundation-model evaluation, strengthening forecasting benchmark reliability.
- [OpenEstimate: Evaluating LLMs on Reasoning Under Uncertainty with Real-World Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15096): A benchmark for reasoning under uncertainty with real-world data. Core idea: evaluate calibrated estimation and uncertainty reasoning beyond deterministic QA.
- [Prophet Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17638) ([project page](https://www.prophetarena.co/)): Evaluates real-time forecasting intelligence for LLM-as-a-Prophet. Core idea: continuously collect live forecasting tasks and separately analyze event recall, source understanding, near-resolution information aggregation, and probability calibration, helping identify which part of the forecasting pipeline fails.
- [Extreme Precipitation Spatial Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25045): Extends high-resolution assessment with threshold-weighted scoring rules, showing that AI weather and high-resolution NWP model rankings change with neighborhood size and extreme-event thresholds.
- [LiveTradeBench: Seeking Real-World Alpha with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03628): Evaluates live trading and decision-making under market uncertainty. Core idea: make this capability comparable through a defined task set, protocol, or scoring interface.
- [Lead-Lag Forecasting Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03877): Formalizes lead-lag forecasting for social platforms, releasing arXiv access-to-citation and GitHub activity-to-fork datasets with long horizons and statistical tests for delayed outcome predictability.
- [Future Is Unevenly Distributed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18394): Evaluates LLM forecasting on real-world social, political, and economic events after model cutoffs, analyzing how domain structure, question framing, and added news context affect accuracy and calibration.
- [HOTD-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18929): Evaluates open-future task discovery from real-world videos, using more than 2,000 annotated scenes and a simulation protocol to test whether LMMs can identify helpful tasks under dynamic human intentions.
- [CryptoBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00417) ([project page and leaderboard](https://cryptobench.space/)): A dynamic expert-curated benchmark for cryptocurrency agents, combining specialized information retrieval, adversarial source synthesis, and prediction-oriented analysis.
- [KalshiBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16030) (dataset: [2084Collective/kalshibench-v2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/2084Collective/kalshibench-v2)): Evaluates epistemic calibration on prediction-market questions. Core idea: use resolved Kalshi events that occurred after training cutoffs to check whether model self-reported confidence matches actual correctness.
- [TS-Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20761): Evaluates live time-series forecasting through pre-registered predictions made before future observations are known. Core idea: reduce retrospective tuning by turning forecasting evaluation into a time-stamped arena for models and methods.
- [FinDeepForecast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05039) ([OpenFinArena leaderboard](https://openfinarena.com/fin-deep-forecast)): Evaluates deep research agents in financial forecasting scenarios. Core idea: put live multi-agent research, financial event prediction, and traceable evidence chains into one evaluation, connecting ForecastBench-style dynamic prediction with Deep Research Bench-style evidence gathering.
- [LakeBeD-US](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-17-3141-2025): Harmonizes high- and low-frequency observations from 21 U.S. lakes and includes a computer-science benchmark for multivariate water-quality time-series prediction across temperature and dissolved oxygen targets.
- [Metrics Matters for ENSO Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosres.2025.108545): Reproduces a deep CNN ENSO forecaster with documented data generation and implementation, then shows how error-based and skill-based metrics can change judgments of climate forecast quality.
- [PriceSeer: Evaluating Large Language Models in Real-Time Stock Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06088): A bench candidate for future prediction, with the title/abstract indicating a benchmark-style task, dataset, diagnostic protocol, or evaluation suite.
- [What If TSF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08509): Reframes time-series forecasting as scenario-guided multimodal forecasting, testing how textual what-if assumptions change forecasts.
- [FutureX-Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12259) ([FutureX leaderboard](https://futurex-ai.github.io/)): Evaluates future prediction in high-value vertical domains including finance, retail, public health, natural disasters, and search. Core idea: inherit FutureX's live, contamination-free pipeline and push forecasting tasks into capital-intensive or safety-critical industries, testing whether general agents have enough domain grounding.
- [TruthTensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13545): Evaluates prediction-market reasoning under drift and holistic human-imitation behavior.
- [FutureOmni](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13836): Evaluates future prediction in multimodal context. Core idea: put text, images, video, and structured clues into the same future-event judgment task, checking whether agents can form probabilistic forecasts from cross-modal evidence.
- [Fin-RATE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07294): Mirrors financial analyst work over SEC filings through three pathways: individual disclosure reasoning, cross-entity comparison, and longitudinal tracking, with both ground-truth-context and retrieval-augmented evaluations.
- [Signature-Kernel Forecasting Metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10182): Proposes signature-kernel metrics for robust probabilistic and tail-event forecasting, adding a calibration-oriented evaluation tool.
- [Energy-Load Time-Series Foundation-Model Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10848): Benchmarks zero-shot time-series foundation models for energy-load forecasting on consumer hardware.
- [TemporalBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13272) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Melady/TemporalBench); [Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Melady/TemporalBench_Leaderboard)): Evaluates context- and event-driven time-series forecasting for LLM agents. Core idea: split tasks into historical-structure explanation, context-free prediction, contextual reasoning, and event-conditioned prediction, diagnosing whether models truly use external events rather than only fitting curves.
- [SenTSR-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19455): Benchmarks time-series reasoning with injected knowledge, separating pattern recognition from reasoning over supplied domain insights.
- [Transportation TSFM Benchmark Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24238): Benchmarks time-series foundation models as baselines for transportation forecasting, extending forecasting evaluation beyond generic event questions.
- [Impermanent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08707) ([open-source code](https://github.com/TimeCopilot/impermanent); [Dashboard](https://impermanent.timecopilot.dev/)): Evaluates live temporal generalization for time-series forecasting. Core idea: score continuously on changing data streams such as GitHub activity, testing whether models maintain forecasting stability under open-world temporal drift.
- [YC-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01212): What it evaluates: state memory in long-term planning and consistent execution. Core idea: make agents repeatedly use historical goals, resource states, and intermediate decisions in ongoing business-management-style tasks, observing the real benefits of context truncation, scratchpads, and memory strategies.
- [TimeSeek](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04220): Evaluates temporal reliability for agentic forecasters. Core idea: replay forecasts at multiple points in a question's lifecycle, comparing no-retrieval, web-retrieval, and different models on the quality of probability updates as resolution approaches.
- [TFRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05364): Evaluates reasoning ability in forecasting systems. Core idea: decompose forecasting into a diagnosable reasoning benchmark focused on information selection, temporal causality, constraint interpretation, and forecast consistency rather than only final error.
- [Market-Bench: Benchmarking Large Language Models on Economic and Trade Competition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05523): Benchmarks LLMs on economic and trade competition. Core idea: evaluate strategic economic decisions and trade competition rather than static finance QA only.
- [Prediction Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07355) ([live arena](https://www.predictionarena.ai/)): Evaluates live model competition for prediction-market/future-event tasks. Core idea: compare model forecasts with continuously updated questions and competition rules, uniformly recording probabilities, rationales, and calibration before and after resolution.
- [TempusBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11529): provides an evaluation framework for time-series forecasting, adding a direct benchmark entry for forecasting-model reliability.
- [PolyBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14199): Evaluates LLM forecasting and trading ability based on live prediction-market data. Core idea: connect probability judgment, trading returns, and calibration in markets such as Polymarket, assessing whether agents can turn forecasts into actionable strategies.
- [QuantSightBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15859) ([project page and leaderboard](https://quantsightbench.com/); [open-source code](https://github.com/aisa-group/quantsightbench)): Evaluates interval prediction and uncertainty calibration for continuous quantities. Core idea: require models to provide prediction intervals instead of point or binary answers, specifically exposing systematic overconfidence at extreme magnitudes.
- [CT Open](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16742): Evaluates live clinical-trial outcome prediction through an open-access platform. Core idea: make trial-outcome forecasting auditable before results are released, giving future-prediction systems a domain-specific, time-sensitive benchmark.
- [Time Series Augmented Generation for Financial Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19633): What it evaluates: financial time-series reasoning by LLM agents; core idea: isolate query parsing and computational orchestration rather than only final forecast accuracy.
- [Energy-Arena: A Dynamic Benchmark for Operational Energy Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24705): Evaluates forecasting and future prediction with a defined task, protocol, or dataset.
- [Bench to the Future 2 (BTF-2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26106) ([FutureSearch Evaluations](https://evals.futuresearch.ai/)): Evaluates strategic reasoning and research-judgment decomposition for forecasting agents. Core idea: use 1,417 pastcasting questions, a frozen 15-million-document research corpus, and full reasoning traces to distinguish whether agents fail at information research, probabilistic judgment, or black-swan/incentive modeling.
- [FutureWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26733): Evaluates live agentic RL environments for future prediction. Core idea: connect prediction, outcome resolution, and parameter updates into a loop, using delayed real outcomes to backfill rewards and replay trajectories, studying online improvement through "predict-resolve-learn."
- [KellyBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27865) ([results leaderboard](https://www.gr.inc/releases/introducing-kellybench)): Evaluates long-horizon sequential decision making in sports-betting markets. Core idea: place agents in a season-long simulation with odds, lineups, statistics, bankroll constraints, and delayed outcomes, testing whether forecasts can be converted into calibrated actions and long-term returns.
- [Foresight Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00420) ([arena](https://foresightarena.xyz/)): Evaluates AI forecasting agents on live prediction markets through an on-chain commit-reveal protocol, using Brier Score and Alpha Score to separate honest probability reporting and predictive edge over market consensus.
- [OracleProto](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03762): Evaluates reproducible pastcasting for LLM-native forecasting. Core idea: construct repeatable forecasting protocols with knowledge cutoffs and temporal masking, specifically diagnosing hindsight leakage and retrieval-time contamination.
- [FAME: Forecasting Academic Impact via Continuous-Time Manifold Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07208): Uses academic-impact forecasting as a proxy for evaluating research-idea judgment; the core idea is to compare forecasts against later citation or impact signals.
- [Teaching Large Language Models When Not to Know: Learning Temporal Critique for Ex-Ante Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14636): Adds a benchmark or evaluation suite for future prediction, centered on Teaching Large Language Models When Not to Know: Learning Temporal Critique for Ex-Ante Reasoning.
- [FutureSim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15188): Evaluates adaptive agents by replaying real-world news and event resolutions in chronological order. Core idea: let agents forecast January-March 2026 events beyond their knowledge cutoff while receiving time-ordered news, testing long-horizon adaptation, memory, search, and calibrated uncertainty under a realistic simulated timeline.
- [Metaculus FutureEval](https://www.metaculus.com/futureeval/) ([methodology](https://www.metaculus.com/futureeval/methodology/)): Evaluates the continuous future-event forecasting leaderboard released by Metaculus in 2026-02. Core idea: continuously feed open Metaculus forecasting questions to mainstream AI models and compare them with the Metaculus community/professional forecasters on calibration, coverage, and real forecasting performance over model release times; this line can be further traced through BTF-2's references to the Metaculus AI Benchmarking Series.
- [Echo](https://echo.unipat.ai/) ([official article](https://unipat.ai/blog/Echo)): Evaluates a dynamic future-prediction leaderboard and training system across finance, politics, crypto, sports, esports, and other domains. Core idea: combine multi-point alignment Elo, Train-on-Future, and AI-native prediction APIs into a full forecasting loop, addressing misaligned prediction timestamps and delayed training signals across models.
