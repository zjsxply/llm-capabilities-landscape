# 2.9.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Trustworthy LLM Agents: Threats and Countermeasures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09648): Organizes threats and defenses across planning, tools, memory, collaboration, and deployment.
- [Mind the Agent: A Comprehensive Survey on Large Language Model-Based Agent Safety](https://openreview.net/forum?id=DHe0UXipKU): Distinguishes agent safety through tool execution, memory poisoning, interaction, and multi-agent failures.
- [A Survey on the Safety and Security Threats of Computer-Using Agents: JARVIS or Ultron?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10924): Focuses on action-taking risks across graphical environments and applications.
- [A Survey on Autonomy-Induced Security Risks in Large Model-Based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23844): Explains risks introduced by planning, delegation, persistence, tools, and feedback.
- [The Attack and Defense Landscape of Agentic AI: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11088): Maps attack surfaces and mitigations in tool-using workflow systems.
