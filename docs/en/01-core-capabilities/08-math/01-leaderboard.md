# 1.8.1 Leaderboard

- [MATH / Papers with Code Leaderboard](https://paperswithcode.com/sota/problem-solving-on-math) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03874), [code](https://github.com/hendrycks/math)): A classic public ranking surface for competition-math problem solving, useful for historical continuity with MATH and MATH-500 style informal mathematical reasoning.
- [MathVista Leaderboard](https://mathvista.github.io/#leaderboard) ([code](https://github.com/lupantech/MathVista)): The official visual-math leaderboard for MathVista testmini and private-test evaluation, useful for tracking multimodal mathematical reasoning over charts, geometry, word problems, and scientific figures.
- [OlympiadBench Leaderboard](https://github.com/OpenBMB/OlympiadBench#leaderboard) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hothan/OlympiadBench)): The official repository leaderboard for bilingual multimodal olympiad-style mathematics and physics, with separate full-benchmark and text-only results.
- [MATH-Vision Leaderboards](https://mathllm.github.io/mathvision/) ([code](https://github.com/mathllm/MATH-V)): The official MATH-Vision page includes main, open-source, and wild leaderboards for competition-derived visual mathematics problems.
- [MathVerse Leaderboard](https://mathverse-cuhk.github.io/#leaderboard) ([code](https://github.com/ZrrSkywalker/MathVerse)): The official MathVerse leaderboard compares multimodal models across text-dominant, vision-dominant, and vision-only variants to separate diagram understanding from textual shortcut use.
- [Omni-MATH Results](https://omni-math.github.io/) ([code](https://github.com/KbsdJames/Omni-MATH)): The official Omni-MATH page publishes model scores by mathematical subdomain and difficulty level, plus rule-based evaluation results for olympiad-level math.
- [LiveBench Mathematics](https://livebench.ai/) ([code](https://github.com/LiveBench/LiveBench)): The mathematics sub-leaderboard in LiveBench updates its questions over time, making it suitable for comparing mathematical capability within a broader general-model leaderboard.
- [FrontierMath / Epoch Benchmarking Hub](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath): An expert-level mathematics leaderboard maintained by Epoch AI, including private sets, public sample questions, and a log viewer; especially suitable for observing difficult mathematical solving settings with executable Python and formal answer functions.
- [U-MATH Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/toloka/u-math-leaderboard): Toloka's public U-MATH and mu-MATH leaderboard compares university-level text and visual mathematics solving, plus math-solution judging.
- [MathArena](https://matharena.ai/?view=problem) ([model page](https://matharena.ai/models?page=1&q=); [output data](https://huggingface.co/MathArena)): One of the most practical continuous competitive-math leaderboards today, publishing problem-level scores, raw outputs, and model-level aggregate results for tasks such as AIME, HMMT, USAMO, IMO, Project Euler, and Apex.
- [G-Pass@k / LiveMathBench Leaderboard](https://open-compass.github.io/GPassK/) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/opencompass/LiveMathBench)): The official OpenCompass G-Pass@k leaderboard reports live and contamination-aware mathematical evaluation across LiveMathBench-Hard, MATH500, AIME 2024, and AIME 2025.
- [MathCanvas-Bench Leaderboard](https://mathcanvas.github.io/#leaderboard) ([code](https://github.com/wangjy020524/MathCanvas)): The official MathCanvas leaderboard tracks multimodal models on interleaved visual-and-textual mathematical solution generation.
- [IMO-Bench Leaderboard](https://imobench.github.io/#leaderboard) ([code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/superhuman/tree/main/imobench)): The official Google DeepMind leaderboard for IMO-ProofBench, covering proof-generation performance in the IMO task family.
- [AMO-Bench Leaderboard](https://amo-bench.github.io/#Leaderboard) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GAIR/AMO-Bench)): The official leaderboard for original high-school competition mathematics problems at least at IMO difficulty.
- [PolyMATH Leaderboard](https://polymathbenchmark.github.io/#leaderboard): The official multimodal mathematical-reasoning leaderboard for PolyMATH, covering image-heavy and text-heavy problem variants.
- [LiveMathematicianBench Leaderboard](https://livemathematicianbench.github.io/) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/LiveMathematicianBench)): A live, regularly updated research-level mathematics leaderboard built from recent arXiv theorem-comprehension questions.
- [MathNet Results](https://mathnet.mit.edu/#results) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/shaden/MathNet), [code](https://github.com/shadenx/MathNet)): The official MathNet page publishes results for olympiad problem solving, math-aware retrieval, and retrieval-augmented problem solving over a 30K-problem multilingual corpus.
