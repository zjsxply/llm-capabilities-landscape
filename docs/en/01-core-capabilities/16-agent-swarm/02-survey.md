# 1.16.2 Survey

- [Agent AI: Surveying the Horizons of Multimodal Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03568): Defines Agent AI as grounded multimodal agents that perceive visual, language, and environmental signals and act in physical or virtual worlds, with next-embodied-action prediction, external knowledge, multisensory input, and human feedback as the organizing lens.
- [MARG: Multi-Agent Review Generation for Scientific Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04259): Studies a multi-agent review-generation workflow where specialized LLM reviewers discuss full papers across experiment, clarity, and impact subtasks, using generic-comment rate and good-comment counts as the evaluation question.
- [LLM Multi-Agent Systems: Challenges and Open Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03578): Organizes LLM multi-agent open problems around task allocation, iterative debate, layered context, and memory management, then connects those coordination issues to blockchain and distributed-system applications.
- [Graph Neural Network Meets Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: Fundamentals, Applications, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04898): Surveys how graph neural networks support multi-agent reinforcement learning, covering fundamentals, application patterns, and open coordination problems.
- [Advancing Social Intelligence in AI Agents: Technical Challenges and Open Questions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11023): Frames Social-AI as agents that sense, infer, learn from, and respond to other agents' affect, behavior, and cognition, tying the research boundary across NLP, robotics, HCI, vision, speech, and multimodal learning.
- [From Persona to Personalization: A Survey on Role-Playing Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18231): Surveys role-playing language agents from persona design to personalization.
- [LLM-based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: Current and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11106): Reviews how LLM-based reinforcement learning extends from single agents to cooperative MARL, emphasizing communication, shared-goal coordination, and human-in-the-loop or human-on-the-loop settings.
- [Position: Foundation Agents as the Paradigm Shift for Decision Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17009): Positions foundation agents as decision-making systems that combine perception, memory, and reasoning, with a roadmap from large interactive data collection to self-supervised pretraining, adaptation, and knowledge or value alignment.
- [AI Agents Under Threat: A Survey of Key Security Challenges and Future Pathways](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02630): Groups AI-agent security gaps into unpredictable multi-step user inputs, complex internal executions, variable operating environments, and interaction with untrusted external entities.
- [Self-assessment, Exhibition, and Recognition: a Review of Personality in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17624): Organizes LLM personality research into self-assessment, exhibited personality, and personality recognition, clarifying how persona-like behavior should be measured before it is used in social-agent populations.
- [The Oscars of AI Theater: A Survey on Role-Playing with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11484): Builds a role-playing language-model taxonomy over data, model and alignment methods, agent architecture, and evaluation, with risk points around dynamic profiles and high-level persona consistency.
- [Scaffolded Language Models with Language Supervision for Mixed-Autonomy: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16392): Treats prompts, tools, and scaffold code as non-parametric variables in multi-step language-model systems, reviewing how language supervision and feedback optimize these mixed-autonomy scaffolds.
- [From Individual to Society: A Survey on Social Simulation Driven by Large Language Model-based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03563): Classifies LLM-agent social simulation into individual, scenario, and society simulation, using cost, scale, and ethics of human-participant studies as the boundary for when agent societies are useful.
- [A Survey on Multi-Generative Agent System: Recent Advances and New Frontiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17481): Defines LLM-based multi-agent systems and organizes applications into complex-task solving, scenario simulation, and generative-agent evaluation, then separates current challenges from future research directions.
- [Thinking with Many Minds: Using Large Language Models for Multi-Perspective Problem-Solving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02348): Proposes synthetic deliberation, where multiple perspective-specific LLM agents explore viewpoints in parallel and a synthesis step preserves cognitive flexibility for complex problem solving.
- [Multi-Agent Collaboration Mechanisms: A Survey of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06322): Surveys collaboration mechanisms in LLM-based multi-agent systems.
- [Optimization and Learning in Open Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16847): Surveys optimization and learning problems in open multi-agent systems.
- [When One LLM Drools, Multi-LLM Collaboration Rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04506): Argues for multi-LLM collaboration in complex and subjective tasks, organizing collaboration by information-exchange levels from API and text to logits and weights.
- [Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate -- We Must Rethink Evaluation and Embrace Model Heterogeneity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08788): Positions multi-agent debate research around stronger evaluation practice and model heterogeneity.
- [Position: Stop Acting Like Language Model Agents Are Normal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10420): Argues that language-model agents should not be analyzed as ordinary agents, sharpening the conceptual boundary for multi-agent and agentic-system surveys.
- [A Roadmap Towards Improving Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning With Causal Discovery And Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17803): Uses cooperative MARL experiments with causal augmentation to identify where causal discovery and inference may improve policy efficacy, convergence, generalization, safety, and interpretability.
- [LLMs Working in Harmony: A Survey on the Technological Aspects of Building Effective LLM-Based Multi Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01963): Surveys technological aspects of building effective LLM-based multi-agent systems, covering coordination, communication, and system design.
- [Adaptive Human-Agent Teaming: A Review of Empirical Studies from the Process Dynamics Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10918): Reviews human-agent teaming through the T4 process-dynamics lens of team formation, task and role development, team development, and team improvement, with metrics for roles, shared mental models, and backup behaviors.
- [A Trustworthy Multi-LLM Network: Challenges,Solutions, and A Use Case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03196): Frames trustworthy multi-LLM networks with challenges, solutions, and an applied use case.
- [Beyond Frameworks: Unpacking Collaboration Strategies in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12467): Decomposes LLM multi-agent collaboration into governance, participation control, interaction dynamics, and dialogue-history management, evaluating accuracy and efficiency on evidence-integration and evidence-synthesis tasks.
- [Multi-Party Conversational Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18845): Surveys agents that participate in multi-party conversations, covering coordination, turn-taking, roles, and interaction evaluation.
- [Literature Review Of Multi-Agent Debate For Problem-Solving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00066): Reviews multi-agent debate as a problem-solving pattern, covering debate roles, interaction protocols, and evaluation concerns.
- [Agentic AI and Multiagentic: Are We Reinventing the Wheel?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01463): Critiques the new agentic and multiagentic terminology by grounding it in classic intelligent-agent and multi-agent-system theory, including autonomy, reactivity, proactivity, social capability, and BDI-style architectures.
- [From Conversation to Orchestration: HCI Challenges and Opportunities in Interactive Multi-Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20091): Examines multi-agentic systems such as AutoGen and OpenAI Swarm from an HCI perspective, surfacing user-facing challenges in orchestration, conflict resolution, transparency, and control.
- [Position: LLM Social Simulations Are a Promising Research Method](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/anthis25a.html): Positions LLM social simulation as a research method by reviewing empirical comparisons with human subjects and naming five challenges, including context-rich prompting, social-science fine-tuning data, and iterative evaluation.
- [GenAI-based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning towards Distributed Agent Intelligence: A Generative-RL Agent Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09495): Surveys generative reinforcement-learning perspectives on multi-agent and distributed agent intelligence.
- [Adaptability in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: A Framework and Unified Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10142): Introduces adaptability as a MARL review lens for changing agent populations, task goals, and execution conditions, making reliability under shifting multi-agent configurations the evaluation focus.
- [A survey of multi-agent geosimulation methodologies: from ABM to LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23694): Reviews geosimulation from agent-based modeling to LLM components, formalizing agent activities such as perception, memory, planning, and action as the architecture for spatial multi-agent simulation platforms.
- [Toward Goal-Oriented Communication in Multi-Agent Systems: An overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07720): Surveys task-relevant communication under resource constraints, bridging information theory, communication theory, and machine learning for coordination in swarm robotics, edge intelligence, and distributed control.
- [Taxonomy of Hierarchical Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12683): Surveys design patterns, coordination mechanisms, and industrial applications for hierarchical multi-agent systems. Core idea: organize multi-agent architectures by hierarchy, delegation, and coordination choices so reusable swarm and subagent harness patterns can be compared across domains.
- [Can Lessons From Human Teams Be Applied to Multi-Agent Systems? The Role of Structure, Diversity, and Interaction Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07488): Applies human team-science variables of structure, diversity, and interaction dynamics to LLM-agent teams, comparing flat and hierarchical teams across commonsense and social-reasoning tasks.
- [Challenges in Credit Assignment for Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning in Open Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27659): Analyzes credit-assignment challenges for multi-agent reinforcement learning in open agent systems.
- [Distributed Multi-Agent AI Systems: Scalability, Challenges, and Applications](https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcsw63273.2025.00083): Surveys distributed multi-agent AI systems, covering scalability, communication, coordination, and deployment challenges for agent swarms beyond a single centralized runner.
- [Agentic AI in Action: A Review of Architectures, Communication, and Coordination in Intelligent Multi-Agent Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/nkcon66957.2025.11345772): This review covers agentic AI architectures, communication, and coordination, adding a compact multi-agent-systems survey for the agent-swarm page.
- [A Survey on the Feedback Mechanism of LLM-based AI Agents](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2025/1175): Categorizes feedback for LLM agents into internal, external, multi-agent, and human feedback, alongside evaluation protocols and benchmarks for iterative agent strategy optimization.
- [SurveyPilot: an Agentic Framework for Automated Human Opinion Collection from Social Media](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.221): Presents a finite-state orchestrated agent framework for traceable social-media opinion collection, with bias mitigation via diversity search and evaluation against authentic survey results.
- [Revisão Sistemática da Literatura integrando Sistemas Multiagente e Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.5753/wesaac.2025.37538): Provides a systematic literature review that identifies, selects, evaluates, and synthesizes evidence at the intersection of multi-agent systems and LLMs for real-world problem domains.
- [Towards fluid human-agent collaboration: From dynamic collaboration patterns to models of theory of mind reasoning](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2025.1532693): Defines fluid collaboration as on-the-fly changes in partner tasks and resource use, connecting dynamic Theory-of-Mind reasoning to the Cooperative Cuisine human-agent environment.
- [Multi-Perspective Explanations for Multi-Agent Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/acsos-c66519.2025.00037): Focuses the survey boundary on explanations that account for multiple stakeholder or agent perspectives in self-organizing multi-agent systems, rather than only single-agent rationale generation.
- [Architecture of decentralized multi-agent systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/colcaci67437.2025.11230899): Reviews decentralized multi-agent architecture choices, using distribution of control and coordination responsibilities as the main contrast with centralized swarm runners.
- [Reinforcement Learning Models for Multi-Agent Environment – An Extensive Study](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccds64403.2025.11209219): Reviews reinforcement-learning models for multi-agent environments.
- [A Comparative Survey of Trust-Building Methods in Multi-Agent AI Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/cogmi67134.2025.00063): Surveys trust-building methods in multi-agent AI systems.
- [Role-Playing Agents Driven by Large Language Models: Current Status, Challenges, and Future Trends](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10122): Traces role-playing agents from rule templates through style imitation to cognitive simulation, highlighting psychological-scale character modeling, memory-augmented prompting, and motivation-situation decision control.
- [Social Catalysts, Not Moral Agents: The Illusion of Alignment in LLM Societies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02598): Uses Public Goods Game simulations with altruistic anchoring agents to distinguish local cooperation gains from genuine norm internalization, exposing strategic compliance and transfer failures in LLM societies.
- [Towards Adaptive, Scalable, and Robust Coordination of LLM Agents: A Dynamic Ad-Hoc Networking Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08009): Frames LLM-agent coordination as adaptive dynamic ad-hoc networking.
- [The Five Ws of Multi-Agent Communication: Who Talks to Whom, When, What, and Why - A Survey from MARL to Emergent Language and LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11583): Surveys who communicates with whom, when, what, and why across MARL, emergent language, and LLM-based agents.
- [Developing AI Agents with Simulated Data: Why, what, and how?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15816): Gives a tutorial framework for simulation-based synthetic data, explaining when digital twins can generate diverse training data and what data-volume, data-quality, and design-analysis constraints remain.
- [Cognitive Models and AI Algorithms Provide Templates for Designing Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22523): Treats cognitive-model and classical-AI algorithms as reusable templates for modular language agents, clarifying how roles and composition patterns can be transferred into swarm design.
- [The Future of AI is Many, Not One](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29075): Argues from complex systems, organizational behavior, and philosophy of science that epistemically diverse AI teams can widen solution search and delay premature consensus better than a single superintelligent model.
- [Differentiable Mixture-of-Agents Incentivizes Swarm Intelligence of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15706): Introduces DMoA as a self-evolving multi-agent framework with differentiable context-aware routing, contrasting adaptive sparse activation with fixed communication topologies.
- [Orchestration and Verification of Agentic AI Systems: A Survey of Multi-Agent Collaboration and Safety](https://doi.org/10.59324/ejaset.2026.4%282%29.16): Surveys orchestration and verification in agentic AI systems, emphasizing how multi-agent collaboration must be paired with monitoring, safety checks, and trace-level validation.
- [AI agents: opportunity, hype, and the way through](https://doi.org/10.20517/aiagent.2026.07): Positions current AI-agent systems by separating durable opportunities from overclaiming.
- [Agent-Based Hybrid AI Models and Technologies: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3661027): Systematically reviews agent-based hybrid AI by separating neural, symbolic, and agent coordination ingredients, with hybrid reasoning technologies as the object scope rather than a single swarm framework.
- [Modeling and Operation of Underwater Intelligent Communication Systems: A Survey of Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning-Based Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2026.3689477): Narrows MARL survey coverage to underwater intelligent communication, where distributed agents must coordinate modeling and operation under communication and environment constraints.
- [A Vision for Autonomous Data Agent Collaboration: From Query-by-Integration to Query-by-Collaboration](https://vldb.org/cidrdb/2026/a-vision-for-autonomous-data-agent-collaboration-from-query-by-integration-to-query-by-collaboration.html): Positions data management around autonomous data agents that collaborate to answer queries, shifting the design lens from schema integration to query-by-collaboration among agents.
- [Bridging manual heuristics and automated shaping: Elevating exploration via intrinsic-driven dual-agent collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2026.131283): Studies a dual-agent reinforcement-learning collaboration in which intrinsic exploration signals and automated shaping replace manual heuristics, with exploration efficiency as the evaluation boundary.
- [A Trustworthy Agentic Multi-LLM Network: Challenges, Solutions, and a Use Case](https://doi.org/10.1109/mwc.2026.3666842): Summarizes trustworthy agentic multi-LLM networks and their deployment challenges.
- [A Systematic Literature Review of Agentic AI: Definitions, Architectures, and Challenges](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3668138): Systematizes agentic AI terminology, architectures, and challenges through a formal literature-review protocol, useful for separating agent autonomy, orchestration, and deployment concerns.
- [A Survey on Large Language Model-Based Social Agents in Game-Theoretic Scenarios](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Q13xLqyQh2): Surveys social agents built on large language models through game-theoretic scenarios, organizing strategic interaction, coordination, and social-behavior evaluation for multi-agent systems.
- [Beyond Pretend-Reality Dualism: Frame Analysis of LLM-powered Role Play with Social Agents](https://doi.org/10.1145/3687272.3690894): Uses frame analysis to study LLM-powered role play with social agents, clarifying how pretend and reality frames shape social-agent interaction design.
- [Large Language Model Applied in Multi-agent SystemA Survey](https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/109/20241330): Reviews how LLMs affect cooperation, reasoning, and adaptation in multi-agent systems, centering the question of how to integrate LLM capabilities into MAS agents effectively.
- [A survey on LLM-based multi-agent systems: workflow, infrastructure, and challenges](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44336-024-00009-2): Structures LLM-based multi-agent systems around profile, perception, self-action, mutual interaction, and evolution, then surveys infrastructure, benchmarks, and safety challenges along that workflow.
- [Conversational Agents: An Exploration into Chatbot Evolution, Architecture, and Important Techniques](https://doi.org/10.55549/epstem.1518795): Reviews conversational agents from rule-based chatbots to machine-learning and NLP architectures, using evolution, system architecture, and dialogue techniques as the taxonomy.
- [Assessing Interaction Quality in Human-AI Dialogue: An Integrative Review and Multi-Layer Framework for Conversational Agents](https://doi.org/10.3390/make8020028): Synthesizes 125 empirical studies into pragmatic, social-affective, and ethical-trust layers for evaluating user-perceived interaction quality in conversational agents.
- [Agentic AI systems in the age of generative models: architectures, cloud scalability, and real-world applications](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11458-6): Reviews generative-model-era agentic systems through architecture, cloud scalability, and application deployment, making infrastructure and real-world operation the organizing frame.
