# 1.6.5 Skill

- [ffmpeg](https://skills.sh/digitalsamba/claude-code-video-toolkit/ffmpeg) is suitable for clipping, frame extraction, and transcoding.
- [whisper](https://skills.sh/davila7/claude-code-templates/whisper) is suitable for speech transcription from video.
- [faster-whisper](https://skills.sh/theplasmak/faster-whisper/faster-whisper) is suitable for low-latency speech transcription.
- [ffmpeg-analyse-video](https://skills.sh/fabriqaai/ffmpeg-analyse-video-skill/ffmpeg-analyse-video) is suitable for structured analysis of video content.
- [video-understand](https://github.com/heygen-com/skills/tree/master/skills/video-understand) is a local `ffmpeg + whisper` video-understanding workflow skill; it is closer to a reusable video-processing pipeline than to something named after a specific benchmark.
