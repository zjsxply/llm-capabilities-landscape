# 2.4.3 Bench

- [SciFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974): Given a scientific claim, retrieve paper abstracts and judge whether to support or refute; it is more like claim verification, but is critical for evidence recall and position judgment in subsequent evidence-grounded QA.
- [QASPER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03011): Answer questions based on the full text of NLP papers and annotate supporting evidence; it advances scientific QA from abstracts to full-text papers, but the domain and document collection are still relatively controlled.
- [OARelatedWork](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01930): Provides a large-scale related-work generation dataset from open-access papers, pairing related-work sections with full texts of cited papers so systems must select and synthesize real sources.
- [ResearchArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10291) ([open-source code](https://github.com/cxcscmu/ResearchArena)): Separates academic-survey work into literature discovery, relevance and impact selection, and hierarchical knowledge organization, using a locally reconstructed environment of 12 million full-text papers and 7,900 surveys to compare LLM agents with keyword retrieval.
- [RelevAI-Reviewer: A Benchmark on AI Reviewers for Survey Paper Relevance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10294):RelevAI-Reviewer turns survey-paper relevance into a four-way candidate-selection task with 25,164 prompt-and-paper instances. A BERT end-to-end classifier outperforms the conventional ML baselines, providing a concrete benchmark and public challenge for automated peer review.
- [SciER: An Entity and Relation Extraction Dataset for Datasets, Methods, and Tasks in Scientific Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21155):Releases a full-text scientific-document extraction benchmark with over 24,000 entities, 12,000 relations, and an out-of-distribution test set.
- [OpenScholar / ScholarQABench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14199) ([open-source code](https://github.com/AkariAsai/OpenScholar); [demo](https://open-scholar.allen.ai/)): Evaluates large-scale scientific literature search and citation-backed synthesis by pairing expert-written multi-domain questions with long-form reference answers and citation-accuracy checks.
- [ResearchRubrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02077) ([code](https://github.com/scaleapi/researchrubrics)): Evaluates the quality of research-oriented long-form outputs; core idea: use rubrics to make dimensions such as coverage, evidence, citations, reproducibility, structure, and writing quality explicit and comparable.
- [Highlighting Case Studies in LLM Literature Review of Interdisciplinary System Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16515): Evaluates evidence extraction over four systematic-review case studies by checking quotation fidelity, expert-judged answers to research questions, and embedding similarity; the reported quote accuracy exceeds 95 percent while question-answer accuracy is about 83 percent.
- [arXiv2Table](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10284): Evaluates literature-review table generation from paper pools with realistic user demands, retrieval distractors, and utilization-oriented scoring rather than oracle schemas.
- [BrowseComp-ZH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19314) ([code](https://github.com/PALIN2018/BrowseComp-ZH); [Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/PALIN2018/BrowseComp-ZH); [Deep Research SOTA on BrowseComp-ZH](https://www.wizwand.com/sota/deep-research-on-browsecomp-zh-score)): Evaluates difficult web browsing and multi-hop retrieval reasoning in the Chinese internet environment; core idea: explicitly benchmark platform fragmentation, cross-page retrieval, and information synthesis in the Chinese web ecosystem, and it is often used as a SOTA comparison for deep-search/deep-research agents.
- [Research Impact Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14838): Evaluates time-aware summaries of a paper's scientific impact using temporal citation-intent analysis to capture confirmation, critique, and the evolution of influence.
- [MIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00249): Evaluates retrieval of prior methods that can inspire a scientific problem, using a methodology-inspiration dataset and Methodology Adjacency Graph to score conceptual usefulness.
- [Deep Research Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06287) ([Leaderboard](https://drb.futuresearch.ai/)): Evaluates reproducible web-research agents; core idea: use 89 multi-step web research tasks and a frozen `RetroSearch` web corpus to avoid live-web drift while auditing tool use, forgetting, and hallucination in long trajectories.
- [ScIRGen: Synthesize Realistic and Large-Scale RAG Dataset for Scientific Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11117): Synthesizes realistic large-scale RAG datasets for scientific research information needs.
- [Deep Research Arena](https://www.deepresearcharena.com/): What it evaluates: public deep research battles and leaderboard-style comparisons. Core idea: compare productized deep research systems, open-source harnesses, and commercial agents in a continuous arena entry point, complementing paper benchmarks where coverage of deployed systems is limited.
- [DeepResearch Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763) ([homepage](https://deepresearch-bench.github.io/); [code](https://github.com/Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench)): Evaluates deep research agents' long-chain retrieval, evidence organization, and report-generation capability across 22 domains; core idea: combine `RACE` and `FACT` protocols to measure report quality and citation credibility respectively.
- [Characterizing Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04183): Evaluates broad conceptual exploration in deep research; core idea: distinguish deep research from ordinary long-form QA in the formal definition, emphasizing high fan-out, cross-concept retrieval, and reasoning-intensive information exploration.
- [BrowseComp-VL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05748): A supplementary sub-benchmark for multimodal deep research; core idea: include visual web evidence in deep research trajectory synthesis and evaluation.
- [BrowseComp-Plus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06600) ([code](https://github.com/texttron/BrowseComp-Plus)): Evaluates deep research agents over a fixed corpus. Core idea: derive a controllable document collection, human-verified supporting documents, and hard negatives from BrowseComp so researchers can separate retrieval quality, citation accuracy, and agent context engineering.
- [Expert Preference-based Evaluation of Automated Related Work Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07955): GREP was used to simulate multiple rounds of fine-grained evaluation of expert preferences, and it was found that it is difficult for the general LLM judge to stably judge whether related work satisfies verification constraints and domain writing standards.
- [ReportBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15804) ([code](https://github.com/ByteDance-BandAI/ReportBench)): Evaluates the quality of academic survey reports generated by deep research agents; core idea: reverse-engineer tasks from expert surveys and separately check cited-paper relevance, citation support, and correctness of uncited facts.
- [SurveyGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17647): Build a quality-aware dataset using more than 4,200 human reviews and 242,143 citations to evaluate citation quality and critical analysis at different levels of engagement from fully automated to human-guided writing.
- [DeepScholar-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20033) ([code](https://github.com/guestrin-lab/deepscholar)): Evaluates generative academic research synthesis; core idea: extract tasks from recent high-quality arXiv papers, requiring systems to retrieve, synthesize, and cite related work while automatically measuring knowledge synthesis, retrieval quality, and verifiability.
- [DeepTRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04499): Evaluates evidence-tracing reliability in deep research and generative search systems; core idea: decompose answers, sources, and citations into statement-level support matrices to audit whether conclusions are actually supported by cited evidence.
- [AirQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16952): Evaluates multimodal and multi-task question answering over 13,956 AI-research papers and provides ExTrActor-generated tool-interaction trajectories.
- [DRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00172) ([code](https://github.com/ServiceNow/drbench), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ServiceNow/drbench)): Evaluates enterprise deep research. Core idea: require agents to retrieve evidence across public webpages, private files, emails, chats, and productivity tools, then generate cited reports; explicitly distinct from Dr. Bench.
- [Dr. Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02190): Evaluates deep research agents across dimensions from short answers to full reports; core idea: cover task decomposition, cross-source retrieval, multi-stage reasoning, information integration, and structured output simultaneously, avoiding final-answer-only scoring.
- [PaperArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10909) ([project page](https://paperarena-ai.github.io/); [open-source code](https://github.com/Melmaphother/PaperArena)): Evaluates tool-assisted multi-paper reasoning that integrates evidence from multiple formats through parsing, retrieval, and computation tools.
- [LiveResearchBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14240) ([homepage](https://livedeepresearch.github.io/); [code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/LiveResearchBench)): Evaluates user-centered deep research in real dynamic web environments; core idea: use live, user-centric, multi-faceted tasks and `DeepEval` multi-protocol evaluation to explicitly test real-time retrieval, synthesis analysis, and citation-link quality.
- [DeepWideSearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20168): Evaluates the simultaneous satisfaction of "deep multi-hop reasoning" and "wide-area information collection" in agentic information seeking; core idea: require agents to reason over multi-hop retrieval paths among many candidate pieces of information, exposing failure modes such as insufficient reflection, insufficient retrieval, and context overflow.
- [MuISQA: Multi-Intent Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Scientific Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16283): Develops multi-intent RAG for scientific question answering.
- [DEER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17776): Evaluates expert-level report generation by deep research agents; core idea: use expert report tasks to jointly evaluate report quality, domain expertise, and report-wide claim verification, reducing bias from scoring only style or local citations.
- [DeepSynth-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03540): Evaluates post-retrieval information consolidation in deep survey writing. Core idea: provide oracle contexts and checklist-style objective scoring so synthesis quality can be separated from retrieval quality.
- [Over-Searching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05503): Evaluates the opposite failure mode of premature stopping in search-augmented agents. Core idea: measure unnecessary search, noisy evidence uptake, and the cost-quality tradeoff through search-efficiency metrics.
- [IDRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06676): Evaluates interactive deep research agents; core idea: unfold the research process into multi-turn retrieval, plan updates, and evidence synthesis, emphasizing process quality rather than only the final draft.
- [Video Deep Research Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06943): What it evaluates: video deep research on the open web. Core idea: require agents to watch, retrieve, and reason over video evidence, evaluating the closed loop of long-video understanding, web search, and cross-modal evidence integration.
- [DeepResearch Bench II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08536) ([code](https://github.com/imlrz/DeepResearch-Bench-II)): What it evaluates: rubric diagnosis for deep research reports. Core idea: decompose expert reports into verifiable items and directly inspect information retrieval, analysis, and presentation.
- [DeepResearchEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09688): Evaluates automated task construction and agentic evaluation for deep research. Core idea: generate persona-driven tasks and actively fact-check reports even when citations are missing, weak, or unreliable.
- [DR-Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10504): Evaluates deep research agents through dynamic trend-grounded tasks. Core idea: build information trees from recent topics and adaptively escalate task difficulty, distinct from the public Deep Research Arena battle website.
- [MMDeepResearch-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12346): Evaluates multimodal deep research agents. Core idea: require image-text evidence collection, long-form report synthesis, citation alignment, and visual-integrity checks in one benchmark.
- [TaxoBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12369): Evaluates whether deep research agents can retrieve and organize a field. Core idea: compare retrieved papers and taxonomy trees against expert taxonomies rather than only judging report prose.
- [Mr Dre](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13217): Evaluates multi-turn report revision for deep research agents. Core idea: measure whether agents can incorporate user feedback while preserving citation support and avoiding regression in already-correct sections.
- [DeepSearchQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20975): Evaluates difficult multi-step information-seeking for deep research agents. Core idea: use many-field exhaustive-answer tasks to test whether agents search broadly enough, manage long evidence chains, and avoid stopping after a partial answer.
- [ScholarGym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21654): What it evaluates: the information-gathering stage of deep research. Core idea: separately benchmark the "find materials before researching" front half, directly testing whether agents can locate, filter, and organize high-value sources.
- [Deep Research Hallucination Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22984): What it evaluates: hallucination across complete deep research trajectories. Core idea: audit not only factual errors in the final report, but also how hallucinations arise and propagate along search, reading, notes, and citation chains.
- [Wiki Live Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01590): What it evaluates: expert-level Wikipedia-entry-style deep research. Core idea: use dynamic encyclopedia-entry needs to force broad retrieval, evidence synthesis, and citable writing, reducing static QA leakage.
- [Vision-DeepResearch Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02185) ([code](https://github.com/Osilly/Vision-DeepResearch)): What it evaluates: deep research capability with mixed visual and text search. Core idea: place visual web evidence, image retrieval, and text evidence into the same deep research task to diagnose whether multimodal search truly improves report quality.
- [When Is Enough Not Enough?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07549): What it evaluates: premature stopping and false sense of completion in search agents. Core idea: construct tasks where the answer appears to have been found but key evidence is still missing, evaluating when agents continue searching and when they stop.
- [DRACO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11685): Evaluates deep research accuracy, completeness, and objectivity on cross-domain user-style research questions. Core idea: grade final reports with expert-written rubrics that separately score factuality, breadth and depth, citation support, and presentation.
- [DREAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18940): Evaluates deep research with agentic metrics. Core idea: make the evaluator itself tool-using and adaptive so temporal validity, factual correctness, and task-specific evidence can be checked beyond static rubrics.
- [DEEPSYNTH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21143) ([code](https://github.com/agentdeepsynthesis/deepsynth-bench)): Evaluates deep cross-source information synthesis; core idea: use 120 real time-consuming tasks across 7 domains, requiring agents to collect official data sources, form hypotheses, conduct structured reasoning, and produce verifiable insights.
- [TRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21230): What it evaluates: trajectory-level comprehensive evaluation of deep research agents. Core idea: use trajectory-aware metrics to jointly assess correctness, evidence quality, efficiency, and reasoning robustness, avoiding the high-score illusion caused by final scores alone.
- [DeepConsult](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21658): Evaluates long-chain research and report generation for "consulting-style questions"; core idea: constrain open-ended research into scoreable long-form output and process norms.
- [LitBench: A Graph-Centric Large Language Model Benchmarking Tool For Literature Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00051): Benchmarks domain-specific literature tasks through automatically curated literature graphs. Core idea: build node- and edge-level training and evaluation datasets from user-selected scholarly subgraphs so literature agents can be tested on connecting domain terminology, references, and related-work structure rather than only generic text generation.
- [Super Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00582): What it evaluates: extremely broad and deep research on complex questions. Core idea: isolate problems requiring large-scale evidence collection, long-term planning, and heterogeneous-source synthesis, covering the intersection of Deep Research and Wide Search.
- [DeepFact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05912): Evaluates factuality in deep research reports. Core idea: co-evolve benchmark items and auditing agents so claim-level labels remain revisable and evidence-backed rather than fixed once.
- [MyScholarQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16120): Evaluates personalized deep research with real users. Core idea: test whether agents infer individual research preferences and information needs instead of optimizing only task-generic report quality.
- [TRQA (Total Recall QA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18516): Evaluates fact retrieval and synthesis by deep research systems under long evidence chains; core idea: make "whether retrieval is complete and citations are accurate" explicit through high-recall requirements.
- [SciArena](https://sciarena.allen.ai/SciArena_An_Open_Evaluation_Platform_for_Foundation_Models_in_Scientific_Literature_Tasks.pdf) ([platform](https://sciarena.allen.ai/)): An open platform for evaluating scientific-literature understanding, synthesis, citation-backed answers, and user preferences.
- [MiroEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28407): What it evaluates: process and results of deep research systems. Core idea: evaluate final reports, fact verification, and process auditing together.
- [Reference Hallucination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03173): Evaluates fabricated, invalid, and stale references in commercial LLMs and deep research agents. Core idea: separate broken links from invented citations and provide tooling for large-scale URL-validity audits.
- [Does ChatGPT Ignore Article Retractions and Other Reliability Concerns?](https://doi.org/10.1002/leap.2018): Tests ChatGPT 4o-mini on 217 retracted or otherwise concerning papers across 6,510 quality reports, showing that literature-review assistance can omit retraction and error signals even when it assigns high quality.
- [EpiBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05557): Evaluates episodic multimodal research workflows over scientific papers. Core idea: require agents to proactively search papers across turns, align evidence from figures and tables, maintain accumulated evidence in memory, and answer objective cross-paper comparison questions with process-level diagnostics.
- [Towards Knowledgeable Deep Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07720): What it evaluates: frameworks and evaluation for knowledge-enhanced deep research. Core idea: put external knowledge organization, retrieval, and report generation into one protocol, distinguishing "can search" from "can form knowledge structures".
- [PaperScope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11307): Evaluates agentic deep research across massive scientific papers. Core idea: use a knowledge graph of more than 2,000 AI papers and multi-modal evidence from text, tables, and figures to test retrieval, reasoning, summarization, and problem solving across coherent multi-paper sets.
- [DR3-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14683) ([code](https://github.com/NJU-LINK/DR3-Eval)): What it evaluates: realistic, multimodal, reproducible deep research evaluation. Core idea: use user files, a static sandbox, and fine-grained rubrics.
- [AutoResearchBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25256) ([project page](https://cheryou.github.io/autoresearchbench.github.io/); [open-source code](https://github.com/CherYou/AutoResearchBench)): Uses 1,000 expert-curated questions to evaluate precise target-paper discovery and broad collection of paper sets satisfying specified conditions.
- [Bloom-Eval: A Hierarchical Evaluation Benchmark for Automatic Survey Generation Based on Bloom's Taxonomy](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1315): Bloom-Eval supplies a six-level, cross-disciplinary benchmark for automatic scholarly survey generation, using deterministic criteria and abstract-ability evaluation.
- [Cited but Not Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06635): Evaluates source attribution in deep research reports. Core idea: parse Markdown citations reproducibly, then score link validity, relevance, and factual support at report scale.
- [ViDR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13034): What it evaluates: alignment between multimodal deep research reports and visual evidence. Core idea: require report conclusions to point back to specific visual sources, reducing multimodal hallucination where a page is cited but visual evidence does not support the claim.
- [REFLECT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19196): Evaluates LLM judges of evidence-based deep-research agents through controlled perturbations to evidence, claims, and review conditions.
- [DeepScholar-Bench](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BkYlCIfaBB): Use the live research synthesis task assessment related work to generate scores based on knowledge synthesis, retrieval quality and verifiability. It is suitable as an evaluation protocol for "document positioning supporting argumentation" in writing discussions.
- [PEQQS](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746252.3761653): Builds a benchmark for extractive quantity-focused QA from scientific literature, requiring systems to retrieve numerical evidence and return structured quantities rather than broad prose answers.
- [RWGBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24894): Input the title, abstract, optional introduction and large-scale candidate literature library of the target paper, and output a related work section that can select, place, organize and frame citations.
- [Phantom References](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00738): Audits hallucinated citations that survive peer review at top-tier conferences, treating references as a concrete verification surface for measuring how unsupported scientific claims enter archival papers.
- [Generating Multiple Choice Questions from Scientific Literature via Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/ickg63256.2024.00035): Turns scientific papers into multiple-choice questions, making paper comprehension, distractor generation, and question-quality checking an explicit educational-literature workflow.
- [AISE-Bench: A Full-Cycle Curated Benchmark for Information Seeking on Academic Knowledge Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20498): Evaluates academic knowledge-graph agents with 1,133 realistic requests, validated API trajectories and parameters, source-grounded answers, and separate measures for planning, execution, reference grounding, and answer quality.
- [SciExplore: Evaluating Autonomous Agents from Scientific Navigation to Information Integration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20926): SciExplore evaluates scientific agents on expert-curated database navigation, ambiguous literature retrieval, reference completion, and cross-source evidence synthesis across disciplines.
