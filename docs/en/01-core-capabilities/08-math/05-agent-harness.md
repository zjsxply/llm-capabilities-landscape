- [Draft, Sketch, and Prove](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12283) ([code](https://github.com/albertqjiang/draft_sketch_prove)): A two-stage formal proving harness that first generates proof sketches, then calls provers/hammers to complete formal proofs.

- [TheoremGraph: Bridging Formal and Informal Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25363): TheoremGraph joins 11.7M informal theorem-like environments and 18.3M candidate dependencies with LeanGraph's 388,105 declarations and 11.3M typed edges, then links the two worlds semantically and exposes datasets, extractors, an API, and MCP retrieval; graph expansion reaches 0.775 Recall@10 without an LM reranker.
- [LAMP: Lean-based Agentic framework with MCP and Proof Repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28841): LAMP supplies Planner, Builder, and Verifier agents with an MCP-connected ontology for Combinatorics on Words instead of fine-tuning the prover; after adding 93 Lean declarations in eight modules, it proves 96.7% of 90 CoW theorems, and removing the tool grounding or role separation costs about 12 points.
- [Beyond the Library: An Agentic Framework for Autoformalizing Research Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31134): Theo's orchestrator lets general coding LLMs extend Lean definitions outside Mathlib and validate the extensions with auxiliary lemmas before formalizing the target theorem; it machine-checks samples from PutnamBench and seven research papers, with two developments needing no axioms beyond Lean's kernel.
- [MechMath Agent Team: LLM Driven Agents for Mathematical Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04394): MMAT separates mathematical research into Control, Execution, and Augmentation planes and closes a loop among a knowledge-base manager, a natural-language prover, and a formal-language prover; in a two-month deployment it reports 11 solved open problems across five mathematical areas.
- [ReasFlow: Assisting Reasoning-Centric Scientific Discovery in Applied Mathematics via a Knowledge-Based Multi-Agent System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14178): ReasFlow casts the human as principal investigator and the agent as a graduate-student collaborator, adding an internal logical-verification loop plus retrieval-driven self-improvement for procedural heuristics; it unifies synthesis, algorithm design, theorem proving, experiments, and writing, producing five complete theory-heavy papers.
- [Improving mathematical proving skills through interactive theorem proving](https://doi.org/10.5937/jesmac2402037s): Discusses interactive theorem proving as a practice environment for mathematical proof skills; its contribution appears educational and may be off-axis for LLM agent harnesses.
