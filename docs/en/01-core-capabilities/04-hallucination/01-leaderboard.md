# 1.4.1 Leaderboard

- [The FACTS Leaderboard: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Large Language Model Factuality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10791): formalizes the FACTS Leaderboard suite across multimodal, parametric, search, and grounding factuality settings, with public and private splits for ongoing submissions.
- [Hallucinations Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hallucinations-leaderboard/leaderboard): an early open hallucination leaderboard attempt. Its value is aggregating signals such as TruthfulQA, HaluEval, and FEVER into one comparison entry point.
- [LLM-AggreFact Leaderboard](https://llm-aggrefact.github.io/): a leaderboard for grounded factuality verifiers. Its value is unifying multi-source factual-consistency data into comparable evaluation sets and connecting to runnable detectors such as MiniCheck.
- [Vectara Hallucination Evaluation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/vectara/Hallucination-evaluation-leaderboard): a public hallucination leaderboard for summarization/RAG scenarios. Its value is continuously comparing whether answers are supported by input facts and providing leads on automatic evaluators such as HHEM and FaithJudge.
- [FACTS Grounding Leaderboard](https://www.kaggle.com/facts-leaderboard): the Kaggle leaderboard for Google FACTS Grounding. Its value is giving a concrete public ranking entry for long-form grounded factuality before the broader FACTS suite.
- [FACTS Leaderboard on Kaggle Benchmarks](https://www.kaggle.com/benchmarks/google/facts): the public Kaggle entry for the expanded FACTS factuality suite. Its value is separating multimodal, parametric, search, and grounding factuality tracks under one submission surface.
- [SimpleQA Verified Leaderboard](https://www.kaggle.com/benchmarks/deepmind/simpleqa-verified): the Kaggle benchmark page for DeepMind's verified short factuality evaluation. Its value is tracking hallucination and correctness on low-ambiguity, objectively checked factoid QA.
- [AVeriTeC Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/fever/AVeriTeC): the FEVER-hosted public leaderboard for automated textual-claim verification. Its value is comparing systems that retrieve evidence and verify complex real-world claims.
- [SciHal25 Kaggle Leaderboard](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/hallucination-detection-scientific-content-2025/leaderboard): the public competition leaderboard for hallucination detection in scientific content. Its value is tracking multilingual scientific hallucination detectors under the shared-task protocol.
- [FACTS entries on Google DeepMind Eval Suite](https://deepmind.google/research/evals/): public entry points for FACTS factuality evaluation. Its value is organizing grounded, search, parametric, and multimodal factuality settings into comparable submissions and review tracks.
