# 1.18.3 Bench

- [CharacterEval: A Chinese Benchmark for Role-Playing Conversational Agent Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01275): evaluates role-playing conversational agents on 1,785 Chinese multi-turn dialogues with 77 fictional characters, using character profiles and human-controlled extraction to score personality consistency, conversation quality, and role fidelity.
- [How predictable is language model benchmark performance?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04757): analyzes BIG-Bench and BIG-Bench Hard scaling trends across 11 model architectures and five orders of magnitude of compute, separating predictable aggregate benchmark scores from noisier individual-task forecasts.
- [I am a Strange Dataset: Metalinguistic Tests for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05300): hand-crafts generation and verification tests for metalinguistic self-reference, checking whether LLMs can reason about words and positions inside the very sentence being evaluated.
- [Mind Your Format: Towards Consistent Evaluation of In-Context Learning Improvements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06766): stress-tests in-context learning claims across 21 models and four classification datasets by varying prompt templates, showing that formatting choices can erase apparent method gains.
- [A Comparative Study on Annotation Quality of Crowdsourcing and LLm Via Label Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09760): reuses existing crowdsourcing datasets to compare aggregated labels from human workers and LLM annotators, focusing on label aggregation quality rather than single-annotator averages.
- [When Large Language Models Meet Evolutionary Algorithms: Potential Enhancements and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10510): surveys LLM and evolutionary-algorithm intersections, mapping token generation to evolutionary operators and flagging evaluation gaps for evolutionary fine-tuning, prompt search, and LLM-assisted optimization.
- [Mementos: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Multimodal Large Language Model Reasoning over Image Sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10529): tests sequential image reasoning on 4,761 image sequences of varying lengths, using GPT-4-assisted scoring to diagnose hallucinated objects and behaviors across nine MLLMs.
- [Benchmarking Large Multimodal Models against Common Corruptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11943): measures LVLM robustness under common image corruptions, perturbing standard vision-language tasks to quantify how noise, blur, weather, and digital artifacts degrade perception and reasoning.
- [DsDm: Model-Aware Dataset Selection with Datamodels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12926): frames dataset selection as target-task performance optimization, comparing selected pretraining subsets against random and quality-filtered data on diverse held-out LM benchmarks.
- [AgentBoard: An Analytical Evaluation Board of Multi-turn LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13178) ([leaderboard](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/agentboard/)): evaluates multi-turn agents in partially observable environments with a fine-grained progress-rate metric, exposing where agents fail before final task success or failure.
- [Investigate-Consolidate-Exploit: A General Strategy for Inter-Task Agent Self-Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13996): evaluates inter-task self-evolution by having agents investigate prior tasks, consolidate reusable experience, and exploit it on later tasks, making transfer across task episodes the measured signal.
- [PROXYQA: An Alternative Framework for Evaluating Long-Form Text Generation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15042): evaluates long-form report and article generation through human-authored meta-questions and proxy questions with known answers, using answer coverage instead of surface-overlap metrics.
- [MT-Eval: A Multi-Turn Capabilities Evaluation Benchmark for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16745): tests multi-turn dialogue abilities with capability-specific conversation tasks, measuring whether models preserve instructions, context, and reasoning across turns rather than only one-shot answers.
- [Synthetic Dialogue Dataset Generation using LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17461): studies LLM-agent pipelines for generating task-oriented synthetic dialogues, evaluating whether agent-generated conversations can replace costly hand-authored dialogue data.
- [Enhancing Multimodal Large Language Models with Vision Detection Models: An Empirical Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17981): empirically tests detector-augmented MLLMs by injecting vision-detection outputs into multimodal prompts and measuring gains and failure modes on visual recognition and reasoning tasks.
- [Dolma: an Open Corpus of Three Trillion Tokens for Language Model Pretraining Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00159): releases a 3T-token open pretraining corpus with documented mixture construction, enabling controlled comparison of data curation choices in language-model training research.
- [The Landscape and Challenges of HPC Research and LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02018): reviews HPC tasks where LLMs could assist code, workflow, and scientific-computing work, identifying benchmark gaps for evaluating models in high-performance-computing settings.
- [MULTI: multimodal understanding leaderboard with text and images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03173) ([leaderboard](https://opendfm.github.io/MULTI-Benchmark/)): Evaluates multimodal models on Chinese examination-derived image-text understanding, reasoning, and knowledge tasks, with an official leaderboard and detailed model results.
- [When geoscience meets generative AI and large language models: Foundations, trends, and future challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03349): surveys generative-AI uses in geoscience prediction, simulation, and decision support, useful here mainly as a domain-specific evaluation-gap map rather than a standalone benchmark.
- [UniTSyn: A Large-Scale Dataset Capable of Enhancing the Prowess of Large Language Models for Program Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03396): builds 2.7M focal-method and unit-test pairs with Language Server Protocol linking, targeting LLM evaluation and training for unit-test synthesis.
- [MLLM-as-a-Judge: Assessing Multimodal LLM-as-a-Judge with Vision-Language Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04788): evaluates MLLMs as multimodal judges through scoring, pair-comparison, and batch-ranking tasks, measuring agreement with human preferences and judge bias.
- [Towards Principled Assessment of Tabular Data Synthesis Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06806): assesses eight tabular-data synthesizer families on 12 real-world datasets with fidelity, privacy, and downstream-utility metrics, including diffusion and LLM-based synthesizers.
- [Properties and Challenges of LLM-Generated Explanations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10532): analyzes explanation outputs from multi-domain instruction-tuned LLMs, checking selectivity, illustrative content, subjectivity, and misleadingness as evaluation dimensions for self-rationalization.
- [RENOVI: A Benchmark Towards Remediating Norm Violations in Socio-Cultural Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11178): provides 9,258 multi-turn socio-cultural dialogues with norm annotations and staged remediation tasks, mixing 512 human-authored and 8,746 ChatGPT-generated conversations.
- [FinBen: A Holistic Financial Benchmark for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12659) ([Open FinLLM Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/TheFinAI/Open-FinLLM-Leaderboard)): evaluates LLMs across financial knowledge, reasoning, forecasting, risk, and decision tasks, with an Open FinLLM leaderboard for domain-specialized model comparison.
- [A Touch, Vision, and Language Dataset for Multimodal Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13232): releases 44K in-the-wild vision-touch pairs with human and GPT-4V language labels, evaluating touch-vision-language alignment through open-vocabulary classification and text generation.
- [PIRB: A Comprehensive Benchmark of Polish Dense and Hybrid Text Retrieval Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13350): evaluates Polish information retrieval across 41 tasks, including 10 new datasets, and compares more than 20 sparse, dense, and hybrid retrievers.
- [KorNAT: LLM Alignment Benchmark for Korean Social Values and Common Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13605): measures Korean national alignment with 4K social-value multiple-choice questions from a 6,174-person survey and 6K common-knowledge questions from textbooks and GED materials.
- [Copilot Evaluation Harness: Evaluating LLM-Guided Software Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14261): provides IDE-interaction data and tools for LLM-guided programming, combining static and execution-based success metrics across programming scenarios and languages.
- [MIKE: A New Benchmark for Fine-grained Multimodal Entity Knowledge Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14835): evaluates fine-grained multimodal entity editing with vanilla name answering, entity-level captioning, complex-scenario recognition, and multi-step editing tasks.
- [DyVal 2: Dynamic Evaluation of Large Language Models by Meta Probing Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14865): uses meta probing agents to transform benchmark questions into psychometrics-inspired probes for language understanding, problem solving, and domain knowledge.
- [tinyBenchmarks: evaluating LLMs with fewer examples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14992): constructs small curated subsets of Open LLM Leaderboard, MMLU, HELM, and AlpacaEval 2.0, showing that about 100 MMLU examples can estimate full-benchmark performance.
- [KIEval: A Knowledge-grounded Interactive Evaluation Framework for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15043): turns benchmark questions into multi-round knowledge-grounded dialogues with an LLM interactor to test whether answers reflect applied understanding rather than memorized contamination.
- [PCA-Bench: Evaluating Multimodal Large Language Models in Perception-Cognition-Action Chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15527): tests MLLMs on autonomous-driving, domestic-robotics, and open-world-game decisions that require perception, cognition, and action planning, with PCA-Eval localizing perception, knowledge, and reasoning errors.
- [GAOKAO-MM: A Chinese Human-Level Benchmark for Multimodal Models Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15745): converts Chinese college-entrance-exam material into eight-subject multimodal questions over diagrams, function graphs, maps, and photos, setting human-level perception, knowledge, and reasoning demands.
- [Towards Fair Graph Anomaly Detection: Problem, Benchmark Datasets, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15988): defines FairGAD and releases Reddit and Twitter graph datasets with anomaly labels and political-leaning sensitive attributes, evaluating nine anomaly detectors and five fairness methods.
- [Researchy Questions: A Dataset of Multi-Perspective, Decompositional Questions for LLM Web Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17896): curates roughly 100K non-factoid search-log questions that require decomposition and multi-perspective web research, using user-effort signals and GPT-4 difficulty checks.
- [Meta-Task Prompting Elicits Embeddings from Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18458): evaluates MetaEOL, an unsupervised embedding protocol that averages one-word-limited meta-task prompts, on STS and downstream sentence-embedding benchmarks without fine-tuning.
- [Private Benchmarking to Prevent Contamination and Improve Comparative Evaluation of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00393): proposes private-test evaluation protocols and the TRUCE system, comparing confidential-computing and cryptographic setups for keeping benchmark data hidden from model owners.
- [Predictions from language models for multiple-choice tasks are not robust under variation of scoring methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00998): compares free-generation, probability, Likert-style, and embedding-similarity scoring for multiple-choice LLM predictions, showing that reported item answers vary strongly with scoring method.
- [Revisiting Dynamic Evaluation: Online Adaptation for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01518): empirically studies test-time online fine-tuning as dynamic evaluation, measuring sample efficiency, distribution-shift sensitivity, and gradient-update overhead.
- [HaluEval-Wild: Evaluating Hallucinations of Language Models in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04307): evaluates hallucination in real user interactions by filtering challenging ShareGPT queries, grouping five hallucination types, and using GPT-4 plus retrieval-augmented references.
- [StableToolBench: Towards Stable Large-Scale Benchmarking on Tool Learning of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07714): redesigns ToolBench evaluation around stable tool servers and reproducible scoring, reducing instability from changing APIs when testing LLM tool-use trajectories.
- [A Comprehensive Study of Multimodal Large Language Models for Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10854): tests MLLMs on full-reference and no-reference image-quality assessment with nine prompt systems spanning psychophysical procedures and NLP prompting strategies.
- [RouterBench: A Benchmark for Multi-LLM Routing System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12031): supplies more than 405K inference outcomes from representative LLMs to compare routing strategies under performance and cost-aware serving objectives.
- [VL-ICL Bench: The Devil in the Details of Multimodal In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13164): broadens multimodal in-context learning evaluation beyond few-shot VQA and captioning, covering image-text input-output tasks and challenge types that expose demonstration-order and example-format sensitivity.
- [Hierarchical NeuroSymbolic Approach for Comprehensive and Explainable Action Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13798): evaluates action-quality assessment for diving by extracting interpretable symbols from video, applying expert rules, and comparing objective reports against end-to-end neural scoring.
- [Visual CoT: Advancing Multi-Modal Language Models with a Comprehensive Dataset and Benchmark for Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16999): Evaluates visual CoT: Advancing Multi-Modal Language Models with a Comprehensive Dataset and Benchmark for Chain-of-Thought Reasoning, adding a benchmark or evaluation-protocol candidate for Other Core Capabilities / Bench.
- [Verbing Weirds Language (Models): Evaluation of English Zero-Derivation in Five LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17856): tests five LLMs on English zero-derivation in a natural-language-inference setup, measuring whether models generalize when nouns, verbs, and adjectives appear in non-prototypical syntactic roles.
- [ChroniclingAmericaQA: A Large-scale Question Answering Dataset based on Historical American Newspaper Pages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17859): builds a QA benchmark from historical American newspaper pages, stressing retrieval and reading over noisy OCR, old typography, and temporally grounded news context.
- [The Invalsi Benchmarks: measuring the Linguistic and Mathematical understanding of Large Language Models in Italian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18697): introduces Italian-native Invalsi MATE, Invalsi ITA, and Olimpiadi MATE exams to measure language and mathematical understanding against school-system and math-olympiad materials.
- [PointCloud-Text Matching: Benchmark Datasets and a Baseline](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19386): defines instance-level point-cloud and text retrieval with the SceneDepict-3D2T dataset and RoMa baseline, targeting noisy sparse 3D scenes and ambiguous textual descriptions.
- [MANGO: A Benchmark for Evaluating Mapping and Navigation Abilities of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19913): evaluates mapping and navigation from natural-language spatial descriptions, testing whether LLMs can build and use implicit maps rather than answer isolated spatial facts.
- [Unsolvable Problem Detection: Robust Understanding Evaluation for Large Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.20331): introduces MM-UPD Bench for absent answers, incompatible answer sets, and image-question mismatches, testing whether LMMs abstain on unsolvable MCQA cases.
- [Evalverse: Unified and Accessible Library for Large Language Model Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00943): unifies LLM evaluation requests, reporting, and communication-platform integration so non-expert users can run model assessments through a single library workflow.
- [Measuring Social Norms of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02491): creates 12,383 K-12-inspired questions over 402 social-norm skills, comparing LLM social understanding with elementary-student performance and multi-agent improvement strategies.
- [Evaluating LLMs at Detecting Errors in LLM Responses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03602): measures whether models can identify errors in other LLM outputs, turning response validation into the target task rather than using LLMs only as graders.
- [Capabilities of Large Language Models in Control Engineering: A Benchmark Study on GPT-4, Claude 3 Opus, and Gemini 1.0 Ultra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03647): introduces ControlBench for undergraduate classical-control problems and uses expert grading to score GPT-4, Claude 3 Opus, and Gemini 1.0 Ultra on accuracy, reasoning, and explanations.
- [PRobELM: Plausibility Ranking Evaluation for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03818): evaluates language models by ranking the plausibility of candidate statements, focusing on graded commonsense and linguistic acceptability rather than exact-answer classification.
- [A Comparison of Methods for Evaluating Generative IR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04044): compares offline evaluation methods for generative information retrieval, including human assessment, LLM judging, and adapted IR metrics for systems that generate new text instead of ranked documents.
- [FreeEval: A Modular Framework for Trustworthy and Efficient Evaluation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06003): provides modular abstractions for models, data, and metrics, with contamination detection, human-evaluation hooks, distributed inference, and caching for large-scale LLM evaluation.
- [Exploring the True Potential: Evaluating the Black-box Optimization Capability of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06290): compares LLMs on discrete and continuous black-box optimization tasks, contrasting numerical optimization weaknesses with stronger performance in broader non-numerical optimization settings.
- [Frontier AI Ethics: Anticipating and Evaluating the Societal Impacts of Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06750): frames evaluation questions for tool-using language-model agents by analyzing likely five-to-ten-year societal impacts rather than offering a scored dataset.
- [UltraEval: A Lightweight Platform for Flexible and Comprehensive Evaluation for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07584): reimplements model, data, and metric components as composable evaluation modules, supporting task, prompt, benchmark, and metric combinations through a unified HTTP service.
- [Sample-Efficient Human Evaluation of Large Language Models via Maximum Discrepancy Competition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08008): selects informative instructions by maximum-discrepancy competition, uses three-alternative forced-choice human judgments, and aggregates model rankings with Elo scores.
- [Dataset Reset Policy Optimization for RLHF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08495): evaluates dataset-reset policy optimization on TL;DR summarization and Anthropic Helpful-Harmless preference tasks, measuring RLHF gains from resetting online rollouts to offline preference states.
- [An empirical evaluation of pre-trained large language models for repairing declarative formal specifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11050): benchmarks LLM repair of Alloy declarative specifications across six single-agent and dual-agent settings with feedback and auto-prompting variants.
- [Character is Destiny: Can Large Language Models Simulate Persona-Driven Decisions in Role-Playing?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12138): introduces LIFECHOICE with 1,462 character decision points from 388 books, evaluating whether role-playing LLMs predict persona-driven choices from preceding stories.
- [Who Validates the Validators? Aligning LLM-Assisted Evaluation of LLM Outputs with Human Preferences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272): presents EvalGen, where human grades over sampled LLM outputs select and refine generated evaluator prompts or Python assertion functions.
- [A User-Centric Multi-Intent Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13940): builds 1,846 real-world user scenarios from 712 participants across 23 countries, categorizes six user intents, and benchmarks 10 LLM services with GPT-4-as-judge plus human-preference checks.
- [How Well Can LLMs Echo Us? Evaluating AI Chatbots' Role-Play Ability with ECHO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13957): uses an acquaintance-based Turing-test protocol to evaluate ordinary-person role play, reporting a 48.3% leading deception success rate for OpenAI GPTs.
- [Insights into Alignment: Evaluating DPO and its Variants Across Multiple Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14723): evaluates DPO variants with and without supervised fine-tuning across 13 benchmarks covering dialogue, reasoning, math, QA, truthfulness, MT-Bench, BIG-Bench, and Open LLM Leaderboard tasks.
- [Return of EM: Entity-driven Answer Set Expansion for QA Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15650): replaces LLM-based QA grading with soft EM and entity-driven answer-set expansion, comparing interpretability and reliability against traditional and LLM-as-judge metrics.
- [MMT-Bench: A Comprehensive Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating Large Vision-Language Models Towards Multitask AGI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16006) ([leaderboard](https://mmt-bench.github.io/)): evaluates LVLMs on 31,325 multiple-choice visual questions covering 32 meta-tasks and 162 subtasks across recognition, localization, reasoning, planning, driving, and embodied-navigation scenarios.
- [Examining the robustness of LLM evaluation to the distributional assumptions of benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16966): studies how benchmark conclusions change under different assumptions about task distributions, making sampling and aggregation choices the object of evaluation.
- [Replacing Judges with Juries: Evaluating LLM Generations with a Panel of Diverse Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18796): replaces a single LLM judge with a panel of diverse judge models, measuring whether jury aggregation yields more reliable free-form generation evaluation.
- [Benchmarking Benchmark Leakage in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18824): detects benchmark leakage with perplexity and n-gram accuracy signals, applying the pipeline to 31 LLMs on mathematical-reasoning benchmarks to flag possible test-set exposure.
- [CLIP-Mamba: CLIP Pretrained Mamba Models with OOD and Hessian Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19394): evaluates CLIP-pretrained Mamba vision-language models on 26 zero-shot classification and 16 OOD datasets, adding Hessian analysis to diagnose robustness and parameter efficiency.
- [Evaluating LLMs for Hardware Design and Test](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02326): benchmarks conversational LLMs on eight Verilog design and verification tasks, including generated test code and a SkyWater 130nm tapeout check of hardware artifacts.
- [LLMs Can Patch Up Missing Relevance Judgments in Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04727): simulates holes in TREC Deep Learning relevance judgments and tests whether LLM-assigned fine-grained labels can reduce bias from unjudged retrieval results.
- [LLMC: Benchmarking Large Language Model Quantization with a Versatile Compression Toolkit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06001): standardizes LLM quantization comparisons through a plug-and-play toolkit covering many algorithms, model families, hardware settings, and integer or floating-point compression schemes.
- [EDA Corpus: A Large Language Model Dataset for Enhanced Interaction with OpenROAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06676): releases more than 1,000 OpenROAD EDA question-answer and code-script pairs, enabling LLM training and evaluation for chip-design tool interaction.
- [Sakuga-42M Dataset: Scaling Up Cartoon Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07425): releases 42 million cartoon animation frames for sketch, coloring, interpolation, and cartoon video modeling research, separating hand-drawn animation distribution shift from natural-video benchmarks.
- [Is the Pope Catholic? Yes, the Pope is Catholic. Generative Evaluation of Intent Resolution in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08760): evaluates intent resolution with generative prompts that test whether LLMs recover user intent behind obvious, rhetorical, and context-dependent questions.
- [Hummer: Towards Limited Competitive Preference Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11647): constructs a limited competitive preference dataset where model outputs are compared under constrained preference evidence, supporting evaluation of preference modeling beyond abundant pairwise labels.
- [CLAMBER: A Benchmark of Identifying and Clarifying Ambiguous Information Needs in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12063): provides roughly 12K ambiguity-identification and clarification-question examples organized by an information-need taxonomy, exposing LLM overconfidence on underspecified queries.
- [MathBench: Evaluating the Theory and Application Proficiency of LLMs with a Hierarchical Mathematics Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12209): organizes mathematics problems hierarchically to separate theoretical knowledge from application proficiency, helping diagnose where LLM math performance breaks down.
- [DnA-Eval: Enhancing Large Language Model Evaluation through Decomposition and Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15329): decomposes LLM-as-judge evaluation into pedagogical stages and aggregates sub-decisions, improving interpretability and reported agreement with human labels.
- [Benchmark Underestimates the Readiness of Multi-lingual Dialogue Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17840): evaluates in-context multilingual task-oriented dialogue on X-RiSAWOZ across Chinese, English, French, Korean, Hindi, and code-mixed Hindi-English, showing turn-level DST can be competitive despite standard benchmark scores.
- [Evaluating the External and Parametric Knowledge Fusion of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19010): probes how LLMs combine retrieved external context with parametric knowledge, measuring conflicts, complementarity, and answer reliability under knowledge-fusion settings.
- [Auto-Arena: Automating LLM Evaluations with Agent Peer Battles and Committee Discussions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20267) ([leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Auto-Arena/Leaderboard), [project page](https://auto-arena.github.io/blog/)): automates arena-style LLM evaluation through agent peer battles and committee discussions, producing leaderboard scores without relying on one static judge prompt.
- [Luna: An Evaluation Foundation Model to Catch Language Model Hallucinations with High Accuracy and Low Cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00975): fine-tunes a 440M DeBERTa-large detector for RAG hallucination evaluation, targeting high-accuracy, low-latency, and low-cost grounding checks against retrieved context.
- [TSpec-LLM: An Open-source Dataset for LLM Understanding of 3GPP Specifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01768): curates 3GPP Release 8 to 19 specification data and technical QA probes, evaluating baseline LLMs and RAG on telecom-standard understanding.
- [FusionBench: A Unified Library and Comprehensive Benchmark for Deep Model Fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03280): standardizes deep-model fusion evaluation across multiple tasks, datasets, model scales, and fusion settings so parameter and prediction merging methods can be compared reproducibly.
- [Evaluating the World Model Implicit in a Generative Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03689): proposes probes for whether a generative model encodes usable world dynamics, evaluating latent knowledge through interventions rather than only output realism.
- [ValueBench: Towards Comprehensively Evaluating Value Orientations and Understanding of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04214): draws on 44 psychometric inventories and 453 value dimensions to test LLM value orientations and open-ended value understanding in realistic human-AI interaction settings.
- [Stratified Prediction-Powered Inference for Hybrid Language Model Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04291): introduces StratPPI, combining limited human labels with larger automatic-label pools under stratified sampling to compute valid confidence intervals for LLM evaluation estimates.
- [GenAI Arena: An Open Evaluation Platform for Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04485): provides a user-voting platform for text-to-image, text-to-video, and image-editing models, using crowd preference feedback where automatic metrics such as FID or CLIP are weak proxies.
- [NATURAL PLAN: Benchmarking LLMs on Natural Language Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04520): tests trip planning, meeting planning, and calendar scheduling with tool outputs supplied as context, reporting steep solve-rate drops as itineraries reach ten cities and ablations for self-correction, few-shot transfer, and long-context planning.
- [WildBench: Benchmarking LLMs with Challenging Tasks from Real Users in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04770) ([leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/allenai/WildBench)): samples 1,024 hard tasks from more than one million user-chatbot logs and scores outputs with checklist-guided WB-Reward and WB-Score metrics, including length-bias mitigation and correlations to Chatbot Arena Elo.
- [FedLLM-Bench: Realistic Benchmarks for Federated Learning of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04845): evaluates federated instruction tuning and preference alignment across four realistic client datasets, eight training methods, and six metrics, with client counts from 38 to 747 and diversity axes for language, quality, length, embeddings, and preferences.
- [The BiGGen Bench: A Principled Benchmark for Fine-grained Evaluation of Language Models with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05761): evaluates 103 language models on 77 generation tasks spanning nine capabilities, using instance-specific criteria and five evaluator LMs to reduce coarse helpfulness-style scoring.
- [Can I understand what I create? Self-Knowledge Evaluation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06140): asks models to answer questions generated by the same model across diverse tasks, using self-generated probes to expose gaps between fluent generation and self-knowledge of the created content.
- [LLM Dataset Inference: Did you train on my dataset?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06443): shifts contamination testing from single-sequence membership inference to dataset-level statistical tests, showing that same-distribution nonmembers break many MIAs while aggregated signals can detect Pile subset usage.
- [MixEval: Deriving Wisdom of the Crowd from LLM Benchmark Mixtures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06565) ([leaderboard](https://mixeval.github.io/)): builds MixEval and MixEval-Hard by matching web-mined user queries to existing benchmark items, aiming for fast, cheap, updatable evaluation with reported 0.96 ranking correlation to Chatbot Arena.
- [A Synthetic Dataset for Personal Attribute Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07217): generates SynthPAI through Reddit-style LLM agents seeded with synthetic personal profiles, yielding more than 7,800 manually labeled comments for privacy-preserving personal-attribute inference evaluation.
- [A Multi-Armed Bandit Approach to Online Selection and Evaluation of Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07451): frames generative-model comparison as sample-efficient best-arm identification, deriving FD-UCB and IS-UCB algorithms for online evaluation with Fréchet Distance and Inception Score.
- [scores: A Python package for verifying and evaluating models and predictions with xarray](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07817): provides more than 50 scientifically reviewed forecast-verification metrics, statistical tests, reliability tools, and preprocessing utilities for labeled multidimensional data through xarray and Dask.
- [OLMES: A Standard for Language Model Evaluations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08446): specifies a fully documented open standard for reproducible LM evaluation, covering prompt formatting, in-context examples, probability normalization, and task formulations for cloze and original multiple-choice settings.
- [Can I Introduce My Boyfriend to My Grandmother? Evaluating Large Language Models Capabilities on Iranian Social Norm Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09123): introduces the Iranian Social Norms dataset with 1,699 Farsi norms, demographic and scope annotations, and English translations, testing six LLMs under geographic and linguistic prompt variants.
