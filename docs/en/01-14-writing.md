# 1.14 Writing and Long-Form Generation

> Parent section: 1. Foundational Capabilities

Note: Writing overlaps with DeepResearch, but the evaluation target is different.
Writing tasks mainly ask whether a model can produce high-quality text under constraints on topic, materials, style, structure, length, and audience.
The main failure modes are drifting off topic, losing structural control, inconsistent style, redundancy, incoherent narration or argumentation, and failure to satisfy format or length constraints.
DeepResearch mainly evaluates open-ended information acquisition, source filtering, evidence support, citation consistency, and multi-source synthesis; writing is only its final expression stage.
This section therefore remains an independent foundational capability.
Work that "first searches for evidence and then writes a report" should primarily be placed under [2.4 Deep Research](02-04-deep-research.md); this section only cross-lists representative work that directly contributes to writing quality, long-form structure, or drafting workflows.

## 1.14.1 Leaderboard

- [EQ-Bench Creative Writing](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html): A community-maintained creative writing leaderboard focused on subjective text quality, style, and narrative performance; useful for complementing rubric-driven evaluations such as WritingBench on literary and creative text.
- [LLM Stats Best AI for Writing](https://llm-stats.com/leaderboards/best-ai-for-writing): A third-party continuously updated aggregation of model performance, speed, and pricing for writing scenarios; useful for a quick view of product-oriented rankings of general models in writing, but should be cited as an aggregate leaderboard rather than a single-paper benchmark.
- [WritingBench](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench): The official repository maintains model evaluation scripts, leaderboard pointers, and task definitions, making it useful for tracking model performance on real writing needs.
- [Arena-Write](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THU-KEG/Arena-Write): A pairwise/Elo writing evaluation set released with LongWriter-Zero, useful for observing preference rankings of ultra-long-output models on real writing prompts.

## 1.14.2 Survey

- [Evaluation of Text Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14799): A foundational reference for metrics and protocols behind modern writing evaluation.
- [Text Generation: A Systematic Literature Review of Tasks, Evaluation, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15604): Reviews text-generation tasks, evaluation practice, and open challenges across writing genres.
- [SurveyX: Academic Survey Automation via Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14776): Covers paper collection, organization, drafting, and evaluation for academic survey automation.
- [SurveyForge: On the Outline Heuristics, Memory-Driven Generation, and Multi-dimensional Evaluation for Automated Survey Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04629): Organizes automated survey writing around outline, memory, and evaluation.
- [Attribution, Citation, and Quotation: A Survey of Evidence-based Text Generation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15396): Surveys traceable evidence-grounded generation for academic and report writing.

## 1.14.3 Bench

- [Suri](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19371) ([code](https://github.com/chtmp223/suri)): What it evaluates: multi-constraint instruction following in long-form generation. Core idea: break topic, format, length, style, and content constraints in long-form tasks into checkable dimensions, specifically testing whether models can maintain many fine-grained requirements across long outputs.
- [LongWriter / LongBench-Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07055) ([code](https://github.com/THUDM/LongWriter)): What it evaluates: 10,000-word-level long-text generation and length scaling. Core idea: use LongWrite-Ruler and LongBench-Write to show that "being able to read long context" is not the same as "being able to write long output reliably", with emphasis on length control, structural expansion, and mid-to-late output degradation.
- [EQ-Bench Creative Writing](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html): What it evaluates: subjective quality in creative writing and long stylized text. Core idea: use open writing prompts and a community leaderboard to continuously compare models on literary quality, expression, narrative pacing, and style control, making it a product-oriented reference beyond academic benchmarks.
- [LongGenBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02076): What it evaluates: long-form generation by long-context LLMs. Core idea: use longer inputs, longer outputs, and multidimensional quality evaluation to distinguish short summary-like output from true long-form drafting.
- [HelloBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16191): What it evaluates: LLM long-text generation ability. Core idea: construct multiple prompt types and evaluation protocols around long-output scenarios to observe systematic degradation in long-form coherence, repetition, structural stability, and instruction satisfaction.
- [LongProc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05414) ([code](https://github.com/princeton-pli/LongProc)): What it evaluates: long procedural text generation. Core idea: require models to generate recipes, tutorials, workflows, manuals, and other procedural long texts, evaluating step order, dependency consistency, completeness, and executability rather than only single-paragraph fluency.
- [WritingBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05244) ([code](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench)): What it evaluates: generative writing capability across 6 core writing domains and 100 subdomains. Core idea: decompose writing tasks into query-relevant evaluation criteria, using prompts, rubrics, and review protocols closer to real needs to measure whether models can write long texts that satisfy purpose, style, structure, and content constraints.
- [WebNovelBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14818): What it evaluates: web-novel-style long narrative writing. Core idea: compare LLM-generated fiction against real web novel distributions, focusing on characters, plot, narrative pacing, chapter continuation, and reader preference rather than only short story fragments.
- [UNCLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16922): What it evaluates: uncertainty expression in long-form generation. Core idea: specifically check whether models use appropriate, calibrated language to express uncertainty in long texts, avoiding turning ambiguous information into overconfident assertions in open-ended writing.
- [P2P](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17104): Evaluates automated paper-to-poster generation. Core idea: turn a research paper into a structured poster and score fine-grained content selection, layout organization, and faithful visual-textual summarization.
- [ExpertLongBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01241): What it evaluates: expert-level long-form generation tasks. Core idea: use structured checklists to review professional long texts, emphasizing task-specific requirements, verifiable subcriteria, and overall long-form quality to reduce instability from relying on a single overall judge score.
- [Arena-Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18841) ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THU-KEG/Arena-Write)): What it evaluates: pairwise preference and Elo ranking under real writing prompts. Core idea: put long-form writing outputs into arena-style comparisons, addressing the difficulty of capturing holistic preference with a single rubric critic.
- [ASAP 2.0](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asw.2025.100954): What it evaluates: source-based writing quality at corpus scale. Core idea: provide a large assessment-oriented dataset for judging whether generated or student writing uses provided sources faithfully and effectively.
- [LitBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00769): What it evaluates: reliable automated evaluation of creative writing through human-labeled story comparisons. Core idea: provide a standardized preference benchmark and paired dataset for testing LLM judges and reward models on literary generation quality.
- [CreativityPrism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20091): Evaluates LLM creativity across divergent thinking, creative writing, and logical reasoning. Core idea: separate quality, novelty, and diversity so creative-writing strength is not conflated with other forms of creative generation.
- [LongWeave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24345): What it evaluates: realistic, relevant, and verifiable long-form generation. Core idea: make long-form tasks both close to real writing needs and verifiable through citations, facts, and structured constraints, connecting writing quality with verifiability.
- [SlidesGen-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09487): Evaluates automated slide generation with computational and quantitative metrics. Core idea: compare code-driven and image-centric slide systems through rendered visual outputs so layout, content coverage, and reliability are scored under one protocol.
- [CDAT / Conditional Divergent Association Task](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20546): Evaluates verbal creativity by separating novelty from contextual appropriateness. Core idea: repair weaknesses of the Divergent Association Task by making creativity conditional on a meaningful context, so noisy word choice is not mistaken for creative writing ability.
- [PaperWritingBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05018): What it evaluates: AI research paper writing. Core idea: organize abstract, introduction, related work, method, experimental narration, and other paper-writing stages into evaluable tasks, checking academic writing structure, argumentation, citation context, and paragraph quality.
- [HoWToBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19071): What it evaluates: human-level writing capability. Core idea: propose the Tree of Writing, decomposing writing into nodes such as intent understanding, material organization, structural planning, local paragraph generation, and global revision for more fine-grained evaluation of the writing process.

## 1.14.4 Agent Harness

- [Self-Refine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651) ([code](https://github.com/madaan/self-refine)): A general `generate -> feedback -> revise` loop for writing revision; useful for turning one-shot writing into a reviewable, iterative long-form generation process.
- [Weaver](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17268) (code: no stable public repository found): A creative-writing foundation model and writing-process reference; although it is more model-oriented, it systematically handles setting, plot, and narrative style, making it an important baseline for later creative-writing agent evaluation.
- [STORM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14207) ([code](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm)): A `retrieval -> multi-perspective questioning -> outline -> draft` harness for long encyclopedic writing; closer to report or survey generation than single-turn composition.
- [LongWriter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07055) ([code](https://github.com/THUDM/LongWriter)): A framework for long-text generation and extended writing; the core idea is to split ultra-long output into controlled expansion and segmented generation, making it a useful engineering reference for long-output stability.
- [Heterogeneous Recursive Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08275) (code: no stable public repository found): An adaptive planning harness for long-form writing; core idea: instead of generating a one-shot outline, recursively decompose writing units at different granularities and adjust planning depth during generation as the content requires.
- [BookWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14538): Builds interactive agent societies from novels for creative story generation. Core idea: use character agents, social simulation, and story-world state to extend narrative generation beyond single-pass drafting.
- [Writing-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00103) ([code](https://github.com/damoonsh/writing-zero)): Converts non-verifiable writing tasks into an optimizable reward process; core idea: use procedural feedback and checkable constraints to narrow the gap between subjective writing quality and trainable signals.
- [StoryWriter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16445) (code: no stable public repository found): A multi-agent framework for long story generation; core idea: assign character setting, plot planning, chapter writing, and consistency checking to different agents to reduce character drift and plot breaks in long stories.
- [LongWriter-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18841) (code: no stable public repository found; [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THU-KEG/Arena-Write)): An RL framework for ultra-long writing; core idea: use `think before writing`, length/quality rewards, and Arena-Write evaluation so that models maintain structure and coherence in 10,000-word-level output.
- [ACE-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04903) (code: no stable public repository found): A representative follow-up in the WritingBench citation network; core idea: automatically decompose writing instructions into fine-grained constraints and use constraint satisfaction as the RL reward for long-form generation, making it a training-oriented writing system rather than a purely interactive agent.
- [Auto-Slides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11062): An interactive multi-agent harness for research presentation creation and customization. Core idea: split slide generation, editing, and user-guided refinement across agent roles so presentation writing becomes an iterative artifact workflow rather than one-shot text generation.
- [MUSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03028): a multi-agent harness for unconstrained story envisioning. Core idea: use closed-loop cognitive orchestration so planning, generation, and revision roles can coordinate open-ended narrative development.
- [HiFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04996) (code: no stable public repository found): A hierarchical feedback optimization framework for constrained long-form generation; core idea: provide feedback at global structure, paragraph quality, and local constraint levels to iteratively repair missing constraints and structural imbalance in long texts.
- [PaperOrchestra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05018) (code: no stable public repository found): A multi-agent framework for automated AI research paper writing; core idea: decompose paper writing into topic/context analysis, section drafting, cross-review, and overall revision, corresponding to the academic writing tasks in PaperWritingBench.
