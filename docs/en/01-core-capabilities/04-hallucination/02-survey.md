# 1.4.2 Survey

- [Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03629): A foundational taxonomy of hallucination definitions, causes, detection, datasets, and mitigation.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Hallucination Mitigation Techniques in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01313): Organizes retrieval, verification, decoding, prompting, and training-side mitigation.
- [Factuality of Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02420): Frames hallucination within broader factuality evaluation and improvement methods.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Hallucination in Large Language, Image, Video and Audio Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09589): Maps hallucination across language, image, video, and audio foundation models.
- [Comparing Uncertainty Measurement and Mitigation Methods for Large Language Models: A Systematic Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18346): Treats uncertainty estimation as an operational signal for detecting, calibrating, and mitigating hallucination.
- [Detection and Mitigation of Hallucination in Large Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12886): Uses a mechanistic view of reasoning-model failures, linking hallucination to intermediate reasoning dynamics rather than only final outputs.
- [Loki's Dance of Illusions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02870): Builds a full hallucination map across causes, manifestations, detection, and mitigation for LLM deployments.
- [A Survey of Multimodal Hallucination Evaluation and Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19024): Focuses on benchmark and detector design for multimodal hallucinations.
- [Mitigating Hallucination in Large Language Models (LLMs)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24476): Organizes mitigation around application settings where RAG, reasoning workflows, and agentic systems create different reliability controls.
- [Hallucinations of large multimodal models: Problem and countermeasures](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2025.102970): Surveys hallucination problems and countermeasures in large multimodal models, extending the page beyond text-only hallucination.
- [A Systematic Literature Review of Hallucinations in Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3601206): Synthesizes hallucination definitions, causes, detection approaches, mitigation methods, and evaluation gaps for LLM factuality research.
- [Attribution Techniques for Mitigating Hallucinated Information in RAG Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19927): Centers attribution as the link between generated claims and retrieved evidence, making RAG hallucination mitigation auditable.
- [SoK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07379): Distinguishes agentic RAG architectures, evaluation protocols, and research gaps from ordinary retrieval-augmented generation.
- [A Survey of Hallucination in Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.12677/airr.2026.151016): Provides a compact overview of hallucination sources, detection methods, evaluation, and mitigation for LLMs.
- [Hallucination Detection in Large Language Model Generated Texts](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECTE69292.2026.11429437): Separates internal model-signal detectors from external evidence-based checks for generated-text hallucination.
- [A comprehensive study on factual consistency in LLMs for data-to-text generation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12046-025-02860-5): Reviews factual consistency in data-to-text generation, a long-running hallucination setting with checkable source fidelity.
- [Multi-Layered Framework for LLM Hallucination Mitigation in High-Stakes Applications: A Tutorial](https://doi.org/10.3390/computers14080332): Presents hallucination mitigation as defense in depth for high-stakes applications, spanning model, retrieval, verification, and deployment controls.
- [Survey on Factuality in Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3742420): Reframes hallucination under factuality, connecting factuality evaluation, benchmarks, correction, and reliability risks.
