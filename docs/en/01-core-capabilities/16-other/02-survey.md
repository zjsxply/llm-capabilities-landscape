# 1.16.2 Survey

- [A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432): A foundational review of architectures, planning, memory, tools, multi-agent interaction, and evaluation.
- [LLM-based Human Simulations Have Not Yet Been Reliable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08579): Reviews LLM human-simulation work across social, economic, policy, and psychological settings and proposes reliability requirements for credible simulation.
- [Harnessing Multiple Large Language Models: A Survey on LLM Ensemble](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18036): Surveys ensembles of multiple LLMs, a cross-cutting model-composition pattern for capability aggregation.
- [Thinking Machines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10814): Organizes LLM reasoning strategies as a taxonomy of prompting, search, verification, and self-improvement patterns, making it useful for comparing cross-cutting reasoning systems.
- [Large Language Model Agent: A Survey on Methodology, Applications and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21460): A broad recent review for agent work that does not fit a narrower capability page.
- [Agentic Large Language Models, a survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23037): Frames agentic LLMs around autonomy, planning, memory, tool use, and environment interaction rather than treating them as ordinary chat models.
- [A Survey of AI Agent Protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16736): Surveys communication protocols for LLM agents. Core idea: classify context-oriented and inter-agent protocols across general-purpose and domain-specific settings, then compare security, scalability, latency, and interoperability tradeoffs.
- [Moving From Monolithic To Microservices Architecture for Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07838): Reviews microservice-style MAS design, including architectural principles, ACL, MCP, A2A, and scalability trade-offs.
- [AI Agents vs. Agentic AI: A Conceptual Taxonomy, Applications and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10468): Provides a conceptual taxonomy and broad application and challenge map.
- [Disambiguation in Conversational Question Answering in the Era of LLMs and Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12543): Surveys ambiguity forms, LLM-based disambiguation methods, datasets, and open problems for conversational and agentic QA systems.
- [A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21046): Taxonomizes what parts of an agent can evolve, when evolution is triggered, and how self-improvement loops are evaluated on the path toward more autonomous systems.
- [Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21504): Reviews task design, metrics, reproducibility, environment interaction, and leaderboard-style evaluation.
- [A Survey on Agent Workflow - Status and Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01186): Surveys general-purpose agent workflow design and future directions.
- [Taxonomy of Hierarchical Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12683): Surveys design patterns, coordination mechanisms, and industrial applications for hierarchical multi-agent systems. Core idea: organize multi-agent architectures by hierarchy, delegation, and coordination choices so reusable harness patterns can be compared across domains.
- [Generalizability of Large Language Model-Based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16330): Uses generalization across tasks, domains, environments, tools, and agent scaffolds as the central lens for judging whether LLM agents transfer beyond narrow benchmarks.
- [What Challenges Do Developers Face in AI Agent Systems? An Empirical Study on Stack Overflow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25423): Analyzes AI-agent system developer challenges from Stack Overflow evidence.
- [Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25445): Consolidates agentic AI architectures, applications, and open problems into a system-level map spanning perception, planning, tool use, memory, coordination, and governance.
- [A Design-Driven Taxonomy of AI Agentic Patterns](https://doi.org/10.1109/conecct65861.2025.11306519): Proposes a design-driven taxonomy of agentic AI patterns, useful for classifying cross-cutting agent architectures.
- [Distributed Multi-Agent AI Systems: Scalability, Challenges, and Applications](https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcsw63273.2025.00083): Surveys distributed multi-agent AI systems, covering scalability and coordination issues beyond a single capability page.
- [Generative Agents in Agent-Based Modeling: Overview, Validation, and Emerging Challenges](https://doi.org/10.1109/tai.2025.3566362): Reviews generative agents in agent-based modeling, including validation and simulation challenges.
- [A Survey of Post-Training Scaling in Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.140): Treats post-training as its own scaling regime, connecting data construction, supervised tuning, preference optimization, reinforcement learning, and reward modeling.
- [Data Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04261): Proposes maturity levels for data agents and uses them to compare systems that automate data access, analysis, transformation, and decision support.
- [When Agents Handle Secrets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03213): Centers confidential computing as the missing systems layer for agents that must manipulate secrets across tools, memory, delegation, and multi-party workflows.
- [The hands behind the agents: Understanding practitioner challenges with agentic frameworks](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2026.108183): Empirically studies practitioner challenges with agentic frameworks, grounding cross-cutting agent-system design issues.
- [Orchestration and Verification of Agentic AI Systems: A Survey of Multi-Agent Collaboration and Safety](https://doi.org/10.59324/ejaset.2026.4%282%29.16): Surveys orchestration and verification in agentic AI systems, emphasizing multi-agent collaboration and safety.
- [Generalist multimodal AI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2026.132933): Reviews architectures, challenges, and opportunities for generalist multimodal AI, providing a cross-cutting entry across image, video, audio, and text capabilities.
- [Agentic AI systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2026.134049): Surveys multi-agent architectures, cognitive foundations, interaction, explainability, security, and evaluation for agentic AI systems.
- [Reasoning based on symbolic and parametric knowledge bases](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2026.104880): Compares reasoning over explicit symbolic knowledge bases with reasoning stored in model parameters, clarifying where hybrid symbolic-parametric systems are useful.
- [Sensemaking in Multi-Agent LLM Interfaces: How Users Interpret Transparency and Trustworthiness Cues](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791157): Studies how users interpret transparency and trust cues in multi-agent LLM interfaces.
- [Agentic Design Patterns](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-01402-3): Tutorializes reusable design patterns for agentic systems, providing a pattern-oriented complement to broad agent surveys.
- [Scenario, Role, and Persona](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706599.3719762): Reviews design strategies for socially intelligent AI agents, clarifying how scenarios, roles, and personas shape interactive agent behavior.
- [A systematic survey of LLM-based text-to-SQL](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.3773): Connects text-to-SQL methodology with security vulnerabilities and deployment challenges, making database querying a capability and safety problem rather than only a parsing task.
- [Fundamental Capabilities and Applications of Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3735632): Maps core LLM capability primitives to major application families, showing how cross-cutting abilities compose beyond a single task axis.
- [Multimodal Agent AI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11390-025-4802-8): Surveys multimodal agents as systems that connect perception, reasoning, memory, planning, and action across digital and physical environments.
- [The Rise of Agentic AI](https://doi.org/10.3390/fi17090404): Clarifies definitions, frameworks, architectures, evaluation metrics, and challenges that distinguish agentic AI from ordinary LLM-based applications.
- [The rise and potential of large language model based agents](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11432-024-4222-0): Traces how LLM agents combine planning, memory, tools, and multi-agent interaction to move from text generation toward autonomous task execution.
