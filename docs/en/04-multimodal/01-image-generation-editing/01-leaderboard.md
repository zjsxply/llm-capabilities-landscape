# 4.1.1 Leaderboard

- [Artificial Analysis Text to Image Leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/text-to-image): A public arena-style comparison of text-to-image systems based on blind human preferences.
- [Arena Image Edit Leaderboard](https://arena.ai/en/leaderboard/image-edit): A public arena leaderboard focused on instruction-based image editing systems.
- [GenAI-Bench Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/GenAI-Bench): The official GenAI-Bench ranking for text-to-visual generation evaluators and model outputs.
- [DreamBench++ Leaderboard](https://dreambenchplus.github.io/#leaderboard): The official personalized image-generation ranking for concept preservation and prompt following.
- [MJ-Bench Leaderboard](https://mj-bench.github.io/#leaderboard): The official leaderboard for multimodal judges on text-to-image preference evaluation across alignment, safety, image quality, and bias.
- [AI-GenBench Leaderboard](https://mi-biolab.github.io/aigenbench-website/): The official ongoing benchmark leaderboard for AI-generated image detection.
- [ForensicHub FIDL Leaderboard](https://scu-zjz.github.io/ForensicHub-doc/rank/fidl_rank.html): The official fake-image detection and localization ranking across deepfake, manipulated-image, AIGC, and document-tampering domains.
- [UniGenBench++ Leaderboards](https://github.com/CodeGoat24/UniGenBench): The official UniGenBench++ repository linking maintained English, Chinese, and long-prompt Hugging Face leaderboards.
- [GIR-Bench Leaderboard](https://hkust-longgroup.github.io/GIR-Bench/): The official reasoning-centric image-generation leaderboard for unified multimodal models.
- [GIE-Bench Leaderboard](https://sueqian6.github.io/GIE-Bench-web/#leaderboard): The official grounded image-editing leaderboard for functional correctness and content preservation.
- [BizGenEval Leaderboard](https://microsoft.github.io/BizGenEval/): The official commercial visual content generation leaderboard for layout, branding, instruction following, and content quality.
