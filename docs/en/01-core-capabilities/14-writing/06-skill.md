# 1.14.6 Skill

- [academic-paper](https://skills.sh/imbad0202/academic-research-skills/academic-paper) is suitable for paper writing, structured paragraph organization, and academic expression.
- [avoid-ai-writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing) is a portable writing skill that audits AI-style prose patterns, applies a two-pass rewrite or detect-only mode, uses a large replacement/pattern catalog, and supports voice profiles so academic drafts can be cleaned without flattening their intended tone.
- [writing-plans](https://skills.sh/obra/superpowers/writing-plans) is suitable for decomposing long-form writing into topic selection, structure, paragraph goals, and revision plans.
- [writing-skills](https://skills.sh/obra/superpowers/writing-skills) provides general-purpose writing routines for paragraph-level revision, style control, and draft cleanup.
- [story-architecture](https://skills.sh/haowjy/creative-writing-skills/story-architecture) is suitable for story structure, character relationships, and chapter-level narrative planning.
- [cw-prose-writing](https://skills.sh/haowjy/creative-writing-skills/cw-prose-writing) is suitable for sentence and paragraph style, voice, and descriptive quality control in creative writing.
