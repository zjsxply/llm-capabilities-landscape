# 4.2.2 Survey

- [Bibliometric analysis and review of AI-based video generation: research dynamics and application trends (2020–2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10791-025-09628-9)：以文献计量方式综述 AI 视频生成的研究动态和应用趋势。
- [A Survey on Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10647)：基于扩散的视频生成与时序建模的基础综述。
- [Deep Learning-Based Image and Video Inpainting: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03395)：《Deep Learning-Based Image and Video Inpainting: A Survey》梳理Video Generation的方法、分类、证据或实践边界。
- [A Survey on Future Frame Synthesis: Bridging Deterministic and Generative Approaches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14718)：综述视频生成方向的相关工作，可作为父级复核时的体系化 Survey 候选。
- [Detecting Multimedia Generated by Large AI Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00045)：Detecting Multimedia Generated by Large AI Models 补充视频生成或生成视频综述，覆盖控制、编辑、运动合成、世界建模或真实性检测。
- [Sora: A Review on Background, Technology, Limitations, and Opportunities of Large Vision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17177)：综述 Sora 式大视觉与视频模型，覆盖技术背景、局限与机会。
- [Sora as a World Model? A Complete Survey on Text-to-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05131)：回顾文本到视频架构、数据、评测与 world model 含义。
- [A Survey on Long Video Generation: Challenges, Methods, and Prospects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16407)：覆盖时序连贯、叙事连续、身份一致性与评测。
- [Video Diffusion Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03150)：Video Diffusion Models 补充视频生成或生成视频综述，覆盖控制、编辑、运动合成、世界建模或真实性检测。
- [Is Sora a World Simulator? A Comprehensive Survey on General World Models and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03520)：Is Sora a World Simulator 补充视频生成或生成视频综述，覆盖控制、编辑、运动合成、世界建模或真实性检测。
- [From Sora What We Can See: A Survey of Text-to-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10674)：From Sora What We Can See 补充视频生成或生成视频综述，覆盖控制、编辑、运动合成、世界建模或真实性检测。
- [A Survey on 3D Human Avatar Modeling - From Reconstruction to Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04253)：梳理视频生成的方法、证据与开放问题。
- [Diffusion Model-Based Video Editing: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07111)：围绕Video Generation梳理《Diffusion Model-Based Video Editing: A Survey》涉及的方法、分类或实践。
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Human Video Generation: Challenges, Methods, and Insights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08428)：围绕Video Generation梳理《A Comprehensive Survey on Human Video Generation: Challenges, Methods, and Insights》涉及的方法、分类或实践。
- [Sora and V-JEPA Have Not Learned The Complete Real World Model - A Philosophical Analysis of Video AIs Through the Theory of Productive Imagination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10311)：补充视频生成的 Survey 条目，重点是《Sora and V-JEPA Have Not Learned The Complete Real World Model - A Philosophical Analysis of Video AIs Through the Theory of Productive Imagination》。
- [A Review of Human Emotion Synthesis Based on Generative Technology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07116)：围绕 Video Generation 能力补充《A Review of Human Emotion Synthesis Based on Generative Technology》这一路线，将其作为 Survey 条目记录。
- [AIGC-Based Image and Video Generation Method: A Review](https://doi.org/10.3724/sp.j.1089.2024-00281)：综述视频或视听生成能力，为父级复核提供近期综述线索。
- [A Survey on Human Motion Generation Tasks: Consistency, Diversity, and Customization](https://doi.org/10.1109/ceii65291.2024.00059)：综述 A Survey on Human Motion Generation Tasks: Consistency, Diversity, and Customization，为该能力方向补充可由父级复核的综述线索。
- [Generative AI for Cel-Animation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06250)：综述 cel animation 中故事板、layout、关键帧、中间帧和上色的生成式方法。
- [From Image to Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07001)：实证分析 diffusion representations 如何从 image generation 迁移到 video generation。
- [A Survey: Spatiotemporal Consistency in Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17863)：综述视频生成中的时空一致性问题与方法。
- [A Survey on Human Interaction Motion Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12763)：综述人体交互动作生成中的表示、建模与评测。
- [Advances in 4D Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14501)：综述连接动态场景、视频与世界模型生成的 4D 生成方法。
- [Exploring the Evolution of Physics Cognition in Video Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21765)：回顾生成视频中的动态、物理推理与合理性评测。
- [Generative AI for Film Creation: A Survey of Recent Advances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08296)：综述视频、电影、动画、4D 或生成式世界模型方法。
- [Survey of Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16081)：综述视频扩散模型的基础、实现和应用，为当前文生视频与视频编辑模型族补充新的综述入口。
- [Generative AI for Character Animation: A Comprehensive Survey of Techniques, Applications, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19056)：综述角色动作、表情、手势和动画流程的生成方法，覆盖艺术、游戏与虚拟智能体场景。
- [A Survey of Interactive Generative Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21853)：综述交互式生成视频的方法、控制接口与评估挑战。
- [AceVFI: A Comprehensive Survey of Advances in Video Frame Interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01061)：综述视频帧插值的发展。
- [From 2D to 3D Cognition: A Brief Survey of General World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20134)：面向视频、三维与世界模型生成的综述或相关工作脉络。核心思路是围绕“From 2D to 3D Cognition: A Brief Survey of General World Models”组织可复用线索，便于比较相关模型、评测或智能体工作流。
- [Advancing Talking Head Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02900)：综述说话人头像生成的方法、数据集、评测指标、损失函数，以及姿态、多语种和时序一致性挑战。
- [Controllable Video Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16869)：综述文本、图像、layout、运动、轨迹、镜头与多模态控制。
- [Human Motion Video Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03883)：综述人类运动视频生成的方法、数据与评测，是可控视频生成中的重要子问题。
- [Bridging Text and Video Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04999)：综述视频生成的方法谱系、评测维度与开放问题，为父级审阅补充近期综述线索。
- [Image-to-Video Transfer Learning based on Image-Language Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10671)：建议父级复核的综述候选；按题名与贡献重新路由，而不是沿用旧目标路径。
- [A Survey of Body and Face Motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09005)：综述身体与面部运动生成的数据集、指标、表示方法和双人头像交互难点，补充 talking-head 与可控人像视频生成的综述线索。
- [Understanding Deepfake Detection: Advances, Limitations, and Security Concerns](https://doi.org/10.1109/iconat66879.2025.11362704)：综述 deepfake detection 的进展、局限和安全问题，在已有生成媒体取证综述之外补充一条 deepfake 检测回顾线索。
- [Context-aware Human Motion Generation: A Comprehensive Survey](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.daai.2025.100007)：围绕 Video Generation 能力补充《Context-aware Human Motion Generation: A Comprehensive Survey》，作为 Survey 条目候选。
- [A systematic survey on video frame interpolation: Advances, challenges, and future directions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2025.130660)：补充视频生成的 Survey 条目，重点是《A systematic survey on video frame interpolation: Advances, challenges, and future directions》。
- [Diffusion model in modern detection: Advancing Deepfake techniques](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2025.113922)：综述视频生成、编辑、头像、说话人视频或生成视频真实性能力，梳理近期方法、分类框架、评测边界或开放挑战。
- [Rethinking Video Generation: Overcoming the Limits of Pretrained Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/icnc64010.2025.10993780)：可作为“视频生成 / 综述”候选条目；梳理该方向的方法谱系、评测边界、风险或开放问题。
- [Deep Learning for Video Summarization: Systematic Review, Challenges and Opportunities](https://doi.org/10.1109/jas.2025.125864)：综述视频摘要的深度学习方法。
- [Deepfake Detection in the Age of Synthetic Media: A Systematic Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/netcrypt65877.2025.11102592)：综述合成媒体时代的深伪检测。
- [Video Generation Models as World Models: Efficient Paradigms, Architectures and Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28489)：作为视频生成模型的综述或立场工作候选，聚焦“Efficient Paradigms, Architectures and Algorithms”。
- [VFace Face-Swapping Survey and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00883)：综述高保真 face swapping，并提出视频换脸评测 benchmark。
- [Detecting Deception, Not Deepfakes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09007)：主张媒体取证需要超越 artifact-only deepfake detection，引入社会理论视角。
- [Deep video inpainting and video inpainting detection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jisa.2026.104446)：综述深度视频修复及其检测方法，为视频编辑、恢复和合成视频伪迹分析提供聚焦脉络。
- [From architecture to evaluation: A comprehensive review of video generation techniques](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vrih.2026.03.002)：围绕 Video Generation 能力补充《From architecture to evaluation: A comprehensive review of video generation techniques》，作为 Survey 条目候选。
- [Video diffusion generation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11331-6)：提供近期视频扩散生成方法与开放问题的综合综述。
- [Deepfake Detection Review](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.2139/ssrn.5030191)：综述 deepfake 检测问题与方法，补充生成媒体真实性背景。
- [Artificial Intelligence in Multimedia Content Generation: A Review of Audio and Video Synthesis Techniques](https://doi.org/10.1002/jsid.2111)：综述音频和视频合成技术，为视频生成综述补充更宽的多媒体生成参照。
- [Deepfake Media Forensics: Status and Future Challenges](https://doi.org/10.3390/jimaging11030073)：综述 deepfake media forensics 的现状与未来挑战，将生成式媒体进展与真实性评估连接起来。
- [Combating Digital Deception: A Taxonomical and Systematic Review of Deepfake Detection Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1002/widm.70087)：系统综述 deepfake detection 方法，并按 taxonomy、adversarial robustness 与检测规避挑战梳理生成媒体取证。
- [Text-to-video generators: a comprehensive survey](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-025-01314-3)：梳理video generation, editing, or generated-video authenticity相关方法与挑战，补充该能力方向的综述线索。
- [From Pixels to Motion: A Systematic Analysis of Translation-Based Video Synthesis Techniques](https://doi.org/10.3390/info16110990)：梳理video generation, editing, or generated-video authenticity相关方法与挑战，补充该能力方向的综述线索。
- [DeepFake detection in images and videos: a survey on models, datasets, and evaluation metrics](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-026-21365-9)：综述视频生成、编辑与生成视频真实性，为该方向提供可复用的分类或综合视角。
- [A Review on Background, Technology, Comparison, and Future Tendency of Video Generation](https://doi.org/10.54097/18313836)：补充视频生成的 Survey 条目，重点是《A Review on Background, Technology, Comparison, and Future Tendency of Video Generation》。
- [Combatting Deepfake Misinformation on Social Media: A Scoping Review and Research Agenda](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol56/iss1/22/)：围绕 Video Generation 能力补充《Combatting Deepfake Misinformation on Social Media: A Scoping Review and Research Agenda》，作为 Survey 条目候选。
- [A Critical Survey on Video Steganography: Trends, Pitfalls, and Recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1145/3801971)：围绕 Video Generation 能力补充《A Critical Survey on Video Steganography: Trends, Pitfalls, and Recommendations》，作为 Survey 条目候选。
- [Comparative Analysis of Video Frame Interpolation from Optical Flow to Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.62051/qxv9y811)：梳理视频生成的方法、风险、数据集或评测实践。
- [P‐3.5: A Review on Spatiotemporal Consistency in Artificial Intelligence Text‐to‐Video Generation: Current Status, Challenges and Future Developments](https://doi.org/10.1002/sdtp.18955)：综述视频生成与编辑相关研究，可作为该方向近期相关工作的入口。
