# 1.14.1 Leaderboard

- [EQ-Bench Creative Writing](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html): A community-maintained creative writing leaderboard focused on subjective text quality, style, and narrative performance; useful for complementing rubric-driven evaluations such as WritingBench on literary and creative text.
- [LLM Stats Best AI for Writing](https://llm-stats.com/leaderboards/best-ai-for-writing): A third-party continuously updated aggregation of model performance, speed, and pricing for writing scenarios; useful for a quick view of product-oriented rankings of general models in writing, but should be cited as an aggregate leaderboard rather than a single-paper benchmark.
- [WritingBench](https://huggingface.co/spaces/WritingBench/WritingBench): The official Hugging Face Space ranks models on WritingBench's writing domains and rubric-driven criteria; the [official repository](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench) maintains the tasks and evaluation code.
- [Arena-Write](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THU-KEG/Arena-Write): A pairwise/Elo writing evaluation set released with LongWriter-Zero, useful for observing preference rankings of ultra-long-output models on real writing prompts.
- [LongProc](https://princeton-pli.github.io/LongProc/): The official project page reports model results for long procedural generation, covering recipes, tutorials, workflows, and manuals where step ordering and dependency consistency matter.
- [ExpertLongBench](https://huggingface.co/spaces/launch/ExpertLongBench): The official Hugging Face Space provides a leaderboard for expert-level long-form generation tasks scored with structured checklists and task-specific criteria.
- [PresentBench](https://presentbench.github.io/): The official leaderboard ranks models on fine-grained presentation generation rubrics, including content, structure, layout, and visual fidelity.
- [DramaBench](https://dramabench.pages.dev/web/leaderboard.html): The official leaderboard tracks drama-script continuation quality across plot, character, style, coherence, and drama-specific constraints.
