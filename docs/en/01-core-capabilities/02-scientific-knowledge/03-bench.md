- [GPQA Diamond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12022) (dataset: [Idavidrein/gpqa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Idavidrein/gpqa)): evaluates graduate-level scientific QA, in multiple-choice form and with a "Google-proof" emphasis. The core idea is to have experts construct difficult questions and weaken direct retrieval hits, making success depend more on knowledge understanding and reasoning than snippet matching.
- [Bioinfo-Bench](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.10.18.563023): Provides a benchmark framework for evaluating LLM bioinformatics skills, routing bioinformatics capability measurement to Scientific Knowledge rather than the general Research Bench.

- [Structure-based out-of-distribution (OOD) materials property prediction: a benchmark study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08032): [Structure-based out-of-distribution (OOD) materials property prediction: a benchmark study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08032): Benchmarks structure-based OOD materials property prediction.
