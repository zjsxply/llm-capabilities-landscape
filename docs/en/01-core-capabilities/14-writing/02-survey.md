# 1.14.2 Survey

- [Evaluation of Text Generation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14799): A foundational reference for metrics and protocols behind modern writing evaluation.
- [A Survey on Neural Data-to-Text Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/tkde.2023.3304385): Reviews neural data-to-text generation across input representations, model architectures, datasets, evaluation metrics, factuality, and controllability issues.
- [Leveraging Large Language Models for NLG Evaluation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07103): Organizes LLM-as-evaluator work for NLG around evaluation dimensions, benchmark use, human correlation, prompt design, bias, and reliability challenges.
- [Leveraging Foundation Models for Crafting Narrative Visualization: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14010): Maps how foundation models support narrative-visualization workflows, including data preparation, narrative structuring, text generation, visual design, and interaction.
- [LLM-based NLG Evaluation: Current Status and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01383): Summarizes LLM-based NLG evaluation practice, focusing on evaluator prompts, rating dimensions, human agreement, bias, robustness, and reproducibility challenges.
- [Large Language Models for Data Annotation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13446): Reviews LLM annotation pipelines, covering prompt-only and human-in-the-loop labeling, quality control, cost trade-offs, bias, privacy, and label reliability.
- [A Survey of AI-generated Text Forensic Systems: Detection, Attribution, and Characterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01152): Surveys or systematizes writing and generated-text analysis, adding taxonomy, trend, or risk-boundary coverage for this capability page.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Process-Oriented Automatic Text Summarization with Exploration of LLM-Based Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02901): Organizes automatic summarization by process stages such as content selection, planning, generation, revision, and LLM-based evaluation.
- [A Design Space for Intelligent and Interactive Writing Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14117): Maps the design space of intelligent and interactive writing assistants.
- [A Survey on Personalized Content Synthesis with Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05538): Reviews personalization signals, adaptation methods, datasets, evaluation, privacy, and bias issues in diffusion-based content synthesis.
- [A Survey on Recent Advances in Conversational Data Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13003): Taxonomizes conversational data generation by collection, simulation, augmentation, filtering, and evaluation, with open issues around diversity, privacy, bias, and contamination.
- [Text Generation: A Systematic Literature Review of Tasks, Evaluation, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15604): Reviews text-generation tasks, evaluation practice, and open challenges across writing genres.
- [Recent Trends in Personalized Dialogue Generation: A Review of Datasets, Methodologies, and Evaluations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17974): Reviews personalized-dialogue datasets, persona modeling methods, adaptation strategies, and evaluation protocols for consistency, relevance, and user-specific response quality.
- [A Systematic Survey of Text Summarization: From Statistical Methods to Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11289): Traces summarization from statistical and neural methods to LLM systems, emphasizing datasets, automatic metrics, factuality evaluation, and long-document challenges.
- [The Quest for the Right Mediator: A History, Survey, and Theoretical Grounding of Causal Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01416): Provides historical and theoretical grounding for causal interpretability, centered on mediator choice, intervention design, and explanation validity.
- [What Makes a Good Story and How Can We Measure It? A Comprehensive Survey of Story Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14622): Organizes story-evaluation criteria across plot, character, coherence, novelty, style, and emotional impact, comparing human rubrics with automatic and LLM-based judges.
- [Abstractive text summarization: State of the art, challenges, and improvements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02413): Reviews abstractive summarization systems, common datasets and metrics, factual inconsistency, redundancy, coverage, and improvement strategies.
- [The Imperative of Conversation Analysis in the Era of LLMs: A Survey of Tasks, Techniques, and Trends](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14195): Surveys Writing methods, task boundaries, and open challenges relevant to the capability taxonomy.
- [From Pen to Prompt: How Creative Writers Integrate AI into their Writing Practice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03137): Studies how creative writers integrate AI into writing practice, informing human-AI writing workflows.
- [Explainability Meets Text Summarization: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.inlg-main.49): Classifies explainable summarization by what is explained, how explanations are generated, and how explanation faithfulness and usefulness are evaluated.
- [A Comprehensive Survey on Legal Summarization: Challenges and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17830): Reviews challenges, datasets, methods, and future directions for legal text summarization.
- [LLM-Assisted Writing Adoption Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09747): measures large-scale adoption of LLM-assisted writing across society.
- [Making the Write Connections: Linking Writing Support Tools with Writer Needs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13320): Maps creative writers' needs against 115 writing-support tools and 67 research papers, exposing gaps in pre-writing and visualization support.
- [SurveyX: Academic Survey Automation via Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14776): Covers paper collection, organization, drafting, and evaluation for academic survey automation.
- [Understanding Screenwriters' Practices, Attitudes, and Future Expectations in Human-AI Co-Creation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16153): Reports screenwriter interviews or survey evidence on current AI use, authorship concerns, collaboration expectations, and where co-creative tools fit into scriptwriting practice.
- [A Review of LLM-Assisted Ideation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00946): Reviews LLM-assisted individual and group ideation, covering generation, refinement, evaluation, interaction design, and creative-domain coverage.
- [SurveyForge: On the Outline Heuristics, Memory-Driven Generation, and Multi-dimensional Evaluation for Automated Survey Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04629): Organizes automated survey writing around outline, memory, and evaluation.
- [Beyond Productivity: Rethinking the Impact of Creativity Support Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01601): Reframes creativity-support evaluation beyond output volume, emphasizing process quality, agency, reflection, ownership, and long-term creative development.
- [Generative AI and Creativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17241): Meta-analyzes evidence on GenAI creativity, human-AI creative augmentation, and reduced idea diversity across creative-writing and ideation tasks.
- [Creativity in LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21116): Surveys how multi-agent LLM systems generate, evaluate, and coordinate creative outputs.
- [Reviewriter: AI-Generated Instructions For Peer Review Writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04423): Surveys and organizes writing methods, evidence, and open problems.
- [Attribution, Citation, and Quotation: A Survey of Evidence-based Text Generation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15396): Surveys traceable evidence-grounded generation for academic and report writing.
- [A comprehensive survey on automatic text summarization with exploration of LLM-based methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.131928): Surveys automatic summarization with LLM-based methods, relevant to controlled writing and long-form condensation.
- [Transformer-based large language foundation models for text generation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104477): Reviews LLM text-generation work across languages and application domains, complementing writing-capability surveys with a foundation-model perspective.
- [A Survey on LLMs for Story Generation](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.750): Reviews LLM story-generation methods, datasets, controllability techniques, evaluation criteria, and open challenges around long-range plot and character consistency.
- [A Survey on Integration of Empathy Cues Into Empathetic Text Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3597304): Surveys empathy cues for empathetic text generation.
- [From Use to Oversight: How Mental Models Influence User Behavior and Output in AI Writing Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05166): Studies how users' mental models of AI writing assistants affect prompting, oversight behavior, trust, and final writing output.
- [Writing in the AI era: Rethinking writing, research and teaching](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jslw.2026.101302): Reconsiders second-language writing research and pedagogy under generative AI, including authorship, assessment, feedback, and classroom practice.
- [Human-AI Collaboration in Writing: A Multidimensional Framework for Creative and Intellectual Authorship](https://doi.org/10.47852/bonviewijce52024908): Frames human-AI writing collaboration across creative and intellectual authorship dimensions.
- [Plotania: Exploring Transparency Trade-offs in AI Co-Writing Through Virtual Readers and Transparent Attribution](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790926): Examines co-writing transparency through virtual-reader feedback and attribution displays, measuring trade-offs between support, disclosure, and writer control.
- [Perceptions and integration of generative artificial intelligence in creative practices and industries: a scoping review and conceptual model](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02667-2): Synthesizes how creative workers perceive and integrate GenAI, producing a conceptual model of adoption, concerns, and workflow change across creative industries.
- [Survey on AI-Generated Plagiarism Detection: The Impact of Large Language Models on Academic Integrity](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-024-09576-x): Reviews AI-generated plagiarism detection methods, academic-integrity risks, detector limitations, and institutional response strategies.
- [The Impact of AI Language Models on Scientific Writing and Scientific Peer Reviews: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3677389.3702508): Systematically reviews evidence on AI language models in scientific writing and peer review, including drafting support, review quality, disclosure, and integrity concerns.
- [A Survey on LLM-Generated Text Detection: Necessity, Methods, and Future Directions](https://doi.org/10.1162/coli_a_00549): Surveys the necessity, methods, and future directions of LLM-generated text detection.
- [A Systematic Literature Review on Multimodal Text Summarization](https://doi.org/10.1145/3763245): Reviews multimodal summarization datasets, modality fusion methods, evaluation metrics, and challenges in aligning textual summaries with visual or audio evidence.
- [The Role of AI in Academic Writing: Impacts on Writing Skills, Critical Thinking, and Integrity in Higher Education](https://doi.org/10.3390/soc15090247): Reviews how AI affects academic-writing skills, critical thinking, and integrity in higher education, with attention to assessment and teaching practice.
- [Enhancing the Robustness of AI-Generated Text Detectors: A Survey](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/13/2145):The survey frames AI-generated-text detector robustness around three failure regimes: adversarial text perturbations, out-of-distribution inputs, and mixed human-AI writing. It consolidates the associated methods, datasets, and evaluation metrics and supplements the taxonomy with detector experiments, exposing unresolved generalization gaps beyond standard in-distribution tests.
