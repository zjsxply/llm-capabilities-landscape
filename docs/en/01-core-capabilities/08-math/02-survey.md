# 1.8.2 Survey

- [Top-down Automated Theorem Proving (Notes for Sir Timothy)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02540): The paper argues that automated theorem proving should model the domain concepts and imperfect proof styles used in published mathematics, rather than only composing axioms and valid primitive inferences from the bottom up.
- [Large Language Models for Mathematical Reasoning: Progresses and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00157): Reviews LLM mathematical reasoning across prompting, tool use, verification, benchmarks, and remaining reliability challenges.
- [A Survey on Deep Learning for Theorem Proving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09939): Covers formal systems, proof search, datasets, and theorem-proving evaluation.
- [System 2 thinking in OpenAI's o1-preview model: Near-perfect performance on a mathematics exam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07114): Analyzes o1-preview on a formal mathematics examination and frames test-time deliberation as a distinct System-2-style reasoning regime.
- [A Survey of Mathematical Reasoning in the Era of Multimodal Large Language Model: Benchmark, Method and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11936): Reviews diagram-, chart-, and image-grounded mathematical reasoning.
- [Formal Mathematical Reasoning: A New Frontier in AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16075): Surveys proof-assistant-backed mathematical reasoning, covering formalization, theorem proving, verifier feedback, and evaluation bottlenecks.
- [Mathematics and Machine Creativity: A Survey on Bridging Mathematics with AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16543): This survey frames LLM and reinforcement-learning systems as high-throughput pattern generators whose exploratory creativity can complement, rather than replace, the deductive rigor of mathematical research, and maps the resulting AI-mathematics interface.
- [A Survey on Feedback-based Multi-step Reasoning for Large Language Models on Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14333): Surveys feedback-based multi-step reasoning methods for mathematical problem solving with LLMs.
- [A Survey on Mathematical Reasoning and Optimization with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17726): Surveys mathematical reasoning, optimization, benchmarks, and tool-augmented methods.
- [Plane Geometry Problem Solving with Multi-modal Reasoning: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14340): Surveys multimodal plane-geometry solving across diagram understanding, symbolic geometry reasoning, datasets, and solver pipelines.
- [Autoformalization in the Era of Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23486): Explains the bridge from natural-language mathematics to checker-verifiable formal proof.
- [Towards Geometry Problem Solving in the Large Model Era: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02690): Surveys geometry problem solving in the large-model era across tasks, methods, and evaluation.
- [A Survey on Large Language Models for Mathematical Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08446): A recent overview of LLM mathematical reasoning methods, datasets, and open problems.
- [Large Language Models Don't Make Sense of Word Problems. A Scoping Review from a Mathematics Education Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24006): Reviews mathematical word-problem studies and argues that current LLMs often solve surface forms without modeling real-world context.
- [Foundation of Intelligence: Review of Math Word Problems from Human Cognition Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21999): Surveys math word-problem solving through five cognition-inspired abilities and compares neural and LLM solvers on common benchmarks.
- [AI for Mathematics: Progress, Challenges, and Prospects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13209): The review separates AI for Mathematics into problem-specific models and general-purpose systems for reasoning, retrieval, and exploration, while treating mathematical rigor both as an application constraint and as a testbed for stronger general reasoning.
- [A Survey of Multimodal Mathematical Reasoning: From Perception, Alignment to Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08291): Surveys multimodal mathematical reasoning from visual perception and alignment to deliberate reasoning.
- [OpenCLAW-P2P v7.0-P2PCLAW: Resilient Multi-Layer Persistence, Live Reference Verification, and Production-Scale Evaluation of Decentralized AI Peer Review v7.0 -- Mathematical Corrections&Ecosystem Developments Edition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19792): Describes a decentralized peer-review and reference-verification system with mathematical-correction workflows; its Math-page fit is uncertain because the artifact is mainly review infrastructure.
- [Mathematical Reasoning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19723): Surveys about 120 mathematical-reasoning papers across datasets, architectures, training strategies, tool integration, verifier-guided reasoning, and evaluation protocols, making it a broad map of model and benchmark design rather than a single method.
- [Artificial Intelligence for Mathematical Reasoning: An Integrated Survey of Language Models, Neuro-symbolic Systems, and Verified Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08728): Maps mathematical reasoning from rule-based word-problem and geometry solvers through neural expression generation, LLM prompting, multi-agent systems, neuro-symbolic theorem proving, and verified discovery workflows.
- [Solving Mathematical Problems using Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.3724/2096-7004.di.2025.0064): Organizes LLM math solving as a pipeline of prompting, decomposition, verification, tool use, and answer synthesis.
- [Machine-Assisted Proof](https://doi.org/10.1090/noti3041): Reviews machine-assisted proof as a mathematical reasoning workflow spanning formal tools and AI assistance.
- [Towards robust automated math problem solving: a survey of statistical and deep learning approaches](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12065-024-00957-0): Reviews automated math-problem solving from statistical solvers through neural and deep-learning methods, emphasizing robustness and generalization.
- [Mathematical Modeling Techniques in Virtual Reality Technologies: An Integrated Review of Physical Simulation, Spatial Analysis, and Interface Implementation](https://doi.org/10.3390/sym18020255): Reviews mathematical modeling used inside virtual-reality systems for physical simulation, spatial analysis, and interface implementation; it is domain infrastructure rather than an LLM math-reasoning survey.
- [Towards the Automatic Mathematician](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-79876-5_2): This extended abstract surveys neural prediction of fine-grained proof steps, premise selection, and useful conjectures, and uses those capabilities to outline the N2Formal vision of an automatic mathematician and the remaining obstacles.
- [AI-driven research in pure mathematics and theoretical physics](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-024-00740-1): This Perspective organizes AI-assisted discovery in pure mathematics and theoretical science into top-down, bottom-up, and meta-mathematical approaches, compares their progress and limitations, and argues that human expertise and AI systems will become an integral research partnership.
