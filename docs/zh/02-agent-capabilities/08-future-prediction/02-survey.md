# 2.8.2 Survey

说明：专门针对 AI 未来事件预测的综述仍然较少；最接近且较成熟的文献集中在事件预测与时间序列预测。


- [A Survey on Event Prediction Methods from a Systems Perspective: Bringing Together Disparate Research Areas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04018)：统一事件预测方法、时序建模与应用需求。
- [Large Language Models for Time Series: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01801)：总结基于大语言模型预测的编码、对齐、提示与适配方法。
- [Empowering Time Series Analysis with Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03182)：《Empowering Time Series Analysis with Large Language Models: A Survey》梳理Future Prediction的方法、分类、证据或实践边界。
- [Large Language Models for Forecasting and Anomaly Detection: A Systematic Literature Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10350)：直接聚焦 forecasting 的覆盖、局限与未来方向。
- [Generative AI for Visualization: State of the Art and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18144)：综述预测或未来事件判断能力，补充近期方法、基准、风险或开放问题脉络。
- [Why Has Predicting Downstream Capabilities of Frontier AI Models with Scale Remained Elusive?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04391)：综述与梳理 Future Prediction 相关的方法、基准和应用边界，可作为该能力方向的 Survey 条目。
- [A Review of Multimodal Explainable Artificial Intelligence: Past, Present and Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14056)：综述预测或未来事件判断能力，补充近期方法、基准、风险或开放问题脉络。
- [A Survey on Large Language Models for Communication, Network, and Service Management: Application Insights, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19823)：综述预测或未来事件判断能力，补充近期方法、基准、风险或开放问题脉络。
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Deep Learning for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting: A Channel Strategy Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10721)：从通道策略角度综述深度学习预测方法。
- [Forecasting Frontier Language Model Agent Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15850)：比较前沿语言模型智能体能力预测方法，适合作为未来预测方向的综述线索。
- [Unsupervised Time-Series Signal Analysis with Autoencoders and Vision Transformers: A Review of Architectures and Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16972)：梳理、分类或定位该能力方向，适合作为本页的综述线索。
- [Can LLM Improve for Expert Forecast Combination? Evidence from the European Central Bank Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23154)：综述预测与未来判断，为该方向提供可复用的分类或综合视角。
- [Foundation Models for Clean Energy Forecasting: A Comprehensive Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23147)：Foundation Models for Clean Energy Forecasting: A Comprehensive Review 梳理未来预测方向的方法、风险或评测边界。
- [Future Progress in Artificial Intelligence: A Survey of Expert Opinion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11681)：面向未来预测的综述或相关工作脉络。核心思路是围绕“Future Progress in Artificial Intelligence: A Survey of Expert Opinion”组织可复用线索，便于比较相关模型、评测或智能体工作流。
- [FinPos: A Position-Aware Trading Agent System for Real Financial Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27251)：综述未来预测的方法谱系、任务边界、证据来源与开放挑战。
- [Long-Term Time Series Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCI.2025.3631772)：综述长期时间序列预测的优势、缺陷与评估陷阱，为 future-prediction 模型提供背景。
- [Traffic prediction in time-series, spatial-temporal, and OD data: A systematic survey](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtte.2025.03.001)：梳理、分类或定位该能力方向，适合作为本页的综述线索。
- [Agentic Digital Twins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18799)：为 agentic system 中用于理解可能未来的 digital-twin 能力建立分类。
- [How do large language models bring disruptive change to time series forecasting? A survey and framework](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/tjmaxx/v13y2026i1p17-42.html)：为预测数据场景与工作流提供实践框架。
- [Leveraging Large Language Models for time series forecasting: A systematic literature review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.115938)：回顾纯 forecasting 场景中的架构、tokenization、prompting、基准与评测。
- [Deep Time Series Forecasting Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://doi.org/10.3390/math12101504)：《Deep Time Series Forecasting Models: A Comprehensive Survey》梳理Future Prediction的方法、分类、证据或实践边界。
