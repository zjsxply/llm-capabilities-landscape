# 4.4.1 Leaderboard

- [NAVSIM Public Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/AGC2025/e2e-driving-navhard): A public end-to-end driving-planning leaderboard for NAVSIM scenarios.
- [CARLA Autonomous Driving Leaderboard](https://leaderboard.carla.org/): A public closed-loop benchmark leaderboard for autonomous driving agents in simulation.
- [nuPlan Planning Challenge Leaderboard](https://eval.ai/web/challenges/challenge-page/1856/leaderboard/4360): The official EvalAI leaderboard for closed-loop planning on the nuPlan benchmark.
- [Bench2Drive Leaderboard](https://eval.ai/web/challenges/challenge-page/2098/leaderboard/4942): The official EvalAI leaderboard for multi-ability closed-loop end-to-end driving evaluation.
- [Waymo Open Dataset Challenges](https://waymo.com/open/challenges): Official active and historical leaderboards for Waymo perception, motion, simulation, and end-to-end driving challenges.
- [nuScenes Detection Leaderboard](https://www.nuscenes.org/object-detection?externalData=all&mapData=all&modalities=Camera): Official nuScenes 3D object-detection leaderboard with camera, lidar, and open tracks.
- [OpenAD 3D Object Detection Leaderboard](https://eval.ai/web/challenges/challenge-page/2418/leaderboard): Official EvalAI leaderboard for open-world 3D object detection in autonomous driving.
