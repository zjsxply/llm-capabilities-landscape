# 4.1.3 Bench

- [Steganography With Generated Images: Leveraging Volatility to Enhance Security](https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2023.3341427): Evaluates whether the randomness of generated images can hide steganographic payloads more securely, stressing recoverability and detectability under generated-image variation.
- [Eyes Wide Shut? Exploring the Visual Shortcomings of Multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06209): Probes visual-perception failures in multimodal LLMs, making it relevant for judging whether LMM-based image-generation evaluators actually notice spatial, counting, and fine-detail errors.
- [Benchmarking the Robustness of Image Watermarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08573): Tests image watermark schemes after common edits, degradations, and removal attempts, measuring whether provenance signals survive outside clean watermarked samples.
- [Organic or Diffused: Can We Distinguish Human Art from AI-generated Images?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03214): Compares human artworks with AI-generated images to evaluate whether people or detectors rely on stable visual cues when separating human art from diffusion outputs.
- [The Male CEO and the Female Assistant: Evaluation and Mitigation of Gender Biases in Text-To-Image Generation of Dual Subjects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11089): Audits dual-subject text-to-image prompts for gender stereotypes such as occupation and assistant roles, then tests mitigation strategies for reducing biased subject assignment.
- [WildFake: A Large-scale Challenging Dataset for AI-Generated Images Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11843): Releases a large in-the-wild AI-generated image detection dataset, stressing detector generalization across diverse generators, prompts, and natural-image domains.
- [UnlearnCanvas: A Stylized Image Dataset to Benchmark Machine Unlearning for Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11846): Provides stylized image concepts for diffusion-model unlearning, checking whether target styles are removed while unrelated generation quality and prompt following are preserved.
- [Towards Open-ended Visual Quality Comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16641): Evaluates open-ended pairwise visual quality comparison, asking models to justify relative quality judgments rather than only output a scalar aesthetic or IQA score.
- [Training Unbiased Diffusion Models From Biased Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01189): Measures how biased training data propagates into diffusion outputs and whether debiasing recipes reduce attribute skew without collapsing image quality.
- [Discriminative Probing and Tuning for Text-to-Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04321): Uses discriminative probes and tuning to evaluate whether text-to-image outputs encode prompt-relevant visual concepts, turning generation assessment into feature-level tests of missed objects, attributes, and relations.
- [Data-Independent Operator: A Training-Free Artifact Representation Extractor for Generalizable Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06803): Evaluates a training-free artifact representation for deepfake detection, emphasizing cross-generator generalization without relying on dataset-specific learned cues.
- [A Dataset and Benchmark for Copyright Infringement Unlearning from Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12052): Defines copyright-infringement unlearning for text-to-image diffusion models with copyrighted-style prompts, retained utility checks, and tests for whether removed visual concepts reappear.
- [Can ChatGPT Detect DeepFakes? A Study of Using Multimodal Large Language Models for Media Forensics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14077): Tests whether general multimodal LLMs can act as media-forensics judges for deepfakes, exposing where conversational visual reasoning helps or fails against specialized detectors.
- [FlashEval: Towards Fast and Accurate Evaluation of Text-to-Image Diffusion Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16379): Proposes a faster text-to-image evaluation protocol that samples compact prompt sets while preserving agreement with slower human or metric-based model rankings.
- [Fake or JPEG? Revealing Common Biases in Generated Image Detection Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17608): Reveals dataset biases in generated-image detection, separating synthetic-image cues from JPEG and preprocessing artifacts.
- [DiffusionFace: Towards a Comprehensive Dataset for Diffusion-Based Face Forgery Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18471): Builds a diffusion-based face-forgery dataset for detection and forensic analysis, covering generated facial manipulations rather than only GAN-era deepfakes.
- [Bringing Textual Prompt to AI-Generated Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18714): Incorporates the source prompt into AI-generated image quality assessment so evaluators judge both perceptual quality and prompt-image consistency.
- [AIGCOIQA2024: Perceptual Quality Assessment of AI Generated Omnidirectional Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01024): Benchmarks perceptual quality assessment for AI-generated omnidirectional images.
- [Evaluating Text-to-Visual Generation with Image-to-Text Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01291): Evaluates text-to-visual generation through image-to-text generation, adding an automatic evaluation route for visual generation.
- [Diffusion Deepfake](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01579): Focuses deepfake evaluation on diffusion-generated visual media, extending detector stress tests beyond GAN-style artifacts.
- [Bi‐LORA: A Vision‐Language Approach for Synthetic Image Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01959): Tests synthetic-image detection with lightweight vision-language LoRA adaptation, emphasizing whether compact adapters transfer across generated-image sources.
- [MULAN: A Multi Layer Annotated Dataset for Controllable Text-to-Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02790): Provides multi-layer annotations for controllable text-to-image generation, enabling evaluation of object, attribute, relation, and layout control rather than only prompt-image similarity.
- [Who Evaluates the Evaluations? Objectively Scoring Text-to-Image Prompt Coherence Metrics with T2IScoreScore (TS2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04251): Introduces a meta-evaluation score for text-to-image prompt-coherence metrics, testing whether automatic metrics agree with objective prompt-image alignment signals.
- [Mixture of Low-rank Experts for Transferable AI-Generated Image Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04883): Evaluates transferable AI-generated image detection across generator and dataset shifts, using low-rank expert adaptation as a stress test for cross-source robustness.
- [NTIRE 2024 Challenge on Image Super-Resolution (×4): Methods and Results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09790) ([challenge](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/17553)): Reports the NTIRE ×4 super-resolution challenge, comparing methods under a shared restoration test set and perceptual or fidelity metrics.
- [The Ninth NTIRE 2024 Efficient Super-Resolution Challenge Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10343) ([challenge](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/17547)): Reports an efficient super-resolution challenge where methods must trade off perceptual or fidelity restoration quality against runtime and resource use under a shared track protocol.
- [FakeBench: Probing Explainable Fake Image Detection via Large Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13306): Tests LMMs on fake-image detection with explanation requirements, measuring not only real-versus-fake accuracy but whether the model localizes and describes forensic evidence.
- [SVGEditBench: A Benchmark Dataset for Quantitative Assessment of LLM's SVG Editing Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13710): Quantifies LLM SVG-editing capability with instruction-driven vector edits and rendered-output checks, separating code validity from visual edit correctness.
- [Adaptive Mixed-Scale Feature Fusion Network for Blind AI-Generated Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15163): Evaluates blind quality assessment for AI-generated images with mixed-scale visual artifacts, targeting perceptual defects that reference-free IQA models must rank without a ground-truth image.
- [NTIRE 2024 Quality Assessment of AI-Generated Content Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16687) ([challenge](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/17627)): Reports a challenge for AI-generated content quality assessment, benchmarking models that predict human-perceived quality of synthetic images.
- [Revisiting Text-to-Image Evaluation with Gecko: On Metrics, Prompts, and Human Ratings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16820): Re-examines text-to-image evaluation with curated prompts, human ratings, and metric comparisons, showing where automatic scores diverge from human judgments.
- [Large Multi-modality Model Assisted AI-Generated Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17762): Uses large multimodality models to assess AI-generated image quality.
- [Probing Unlearned Diffusion Models: A Transferable Adversarial Attack Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19382): Evaluates diffusion-model unlearning under transferable adversarial prompts, checking whether erased concepts can be recovered across models and attack settings.
- [SEED-Data-Edit Technical Report: A Hybrid Dataset for Instructional Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04007): Releases a hybrid instruction-editing dataset for training and evaluating image editors on whether they execute natural-language edits while preserving non-target content.
- [Towards Geographic Inclusion in the Evaluation of Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04457): Evaluates geographic inclusion in text-to-image model outputs.
- [Quality Assessment for AI Generated Images With Instruction Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07346): Studies instruction-tuned quality assessment for AI-generated images, using language-guided judgments to score artifacts, aesthetics, and prompt alignment.
- [Semantic Contextualization of Face Forgery: A New Definition, Dataset, and Detection Method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08487): Defines semantic-context face forgery, pairing images with contextual manipulation labels so detectors are evaluated on meaning-level forgery cues as well as pixels.
- [FAIntbench: A Holistic and Precise Benchmark for Bias Evaluation in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17814): Benchmarks bias in text-to-image models with fine-grained prompts and demographic attributes, measuring stereotyped, missing, or imbalanced depictions across groups.
- [Is Synthetic Data all We Need? Benchmarking the Robustness of Models Trained with Synthetic Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20469): Tests classifiers trained on synthetic images under distribution shift, measuring when generated training data improves robustness and when synthetic artifacts create brittle shortcuts.
- [A-Bench: Are LMMs Masters at Evaluating AI-generated Images?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03070): Benchmarks LMMs as judges of AI-generated images, comparing their quality and alignment ratings against human preferences and exposing judge failures on synthetic-image artifacts.
- [Evaluating Durability: Benchmark Insights into Multimodal Watermarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03728): Benchmarks multimodal watermark durability under common transformations and attacks.
- [Semantic Similarity Score for Measuring Visual Similarity at Semantic Level](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03865): Defines a semantic-level visual similarity score, targeting cases where pixel or CLIP-style distances miss whether two generated images preserve the same objects and scene meaning.
- [Image and Video Quality Assessment using Prompt-Guided Latent Diffusion Models for Cross-Dataset Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04654): Uses prompt-guided latent diffusion features for no-reference image and video quality assessment, emphasizing cross-dataset generalization rather than tuning to one IQA corpus.
- [Evaluating and Mitigating IP Infringement in Visual Generative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04662): Measures visual generative models for copyrighted-style or protected-content imitation, then tests mitigation strategies that reduce infringement risk while preserving generation utility.
- [PQPP: A Joint Benchmark for Text-to-Image Prompt and Query Performance Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04746): Defines prompt and query performance prediction for text-to-image systems, asking models to forecast which prompts or retrieval queries will yield high-quality aligned images.
- [Commonsense-T2I Challenge: Can Text-to-Image Generation Models Understand Commonsense?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07546): Uses adversarial prompt pairs with fine-grained commonsense labels to test whether text-to-image models generate real-world-consistent states, such as lit versus unlit objects under different conditions.
- [MMFakeBench: A Mixed-Source Multimodal Misinformation Detection Benchmark for LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08772): Builds a mixed-source misinformation benchmark where LVLMs must detect manipulated or synthetic multimodal claims across AI-generated images, captions, and real-world misinformation contexts.
- [A Large-scale Universal Evaluation Benchmark For Face Forgery Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09181): Builds a broad face-forgery detection benchmark across manipulation sources and datasets, testing whether detectors generalize beyond a single deepfake corpus.
- [Decoupling Forgery Semantics for Generalizable Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09739): Separates semantic forgery cues from source-specific artifacts to evaluate whether deepfake detectors generalize when manipulation type, generator, or dataset distribution changes.
- [BiVLC: Extending Vision-Language Compositionality Evaluation with Text-to-Image Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09952): Extends compositionality evaluation with text-to-image retrieval, testing whether visual-language systems match generated or candidate images to prompts with correct attribute and relation binding.
- [Exploring the Impact of AI-generated Image Tools on Professional and Non-professional Users in the Art and Design Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10640): Studies professional and non-professional art and design users of AI image tools, using user-centered evaluation to surface workflow impact, quality perception, and adoption barriers.
- [PhyBench: A Physical Commonsense Benchmark for Evaluating Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11802): Tests whether text-to-image models obey physical commonsense, using prompts that require plausible gravity, support, reflection, occlusion, and object-interaction behavior.
- [DF40: Toward Next-Generation Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13495): Builds a next-generation deepfake-detection benchmark collection.
- [GenAI-Bench: Evaluating and Improving Compositional Text-to-Visual Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13743) ([leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/GenAI-Bench)): Evaluates compositional text-to-visual generation with prompts that require correct object counts, attributes, relations, and layout, then uses the benchmark to diagnose and improve compositional alignment.
- [EnTruth: Enhancing the Traceability of Unauthorized Dataset Usage in Text-to-image Diffusion Models with Minimal and Robust Alterations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13933): Evaluates dataset-use traceability for text-to-image diffusion models by applying minimal robust alterations to training images and testing whether unauthorized use can later be detected.
- [Evaluating Numerical Reasoning in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14774): Tests text-to-image models on numeric prompts, measuring whether generated images satisfy counts, quantities, and simple arithmetic constraints rather than only object presence.
- [GIM: A Million-scale Benchmark for Generative Image Manipulation Detection and Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16531): Releases a million-scale benchmark for detecting and localizing generative image manipulations, requiring both image-level forgery decisions and spatial masks for edited regions.
- [EVALALIGN: Supervised Fine-Tuning Multimodal LLMs with Human-Aligned Data for Evaluating Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16562): Trains multimodal LLM judges on human-aligned text-to-image evaluation data, then evaluates whether the judge better matches human preferences on prompt alignment and visual quality.
- [Vision-Language Consistency Guided Multi-Modal Prompt Learning for Blind AI Generated Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16641): Assesses blind quality of AI-generated images by explicitly modeling vision-language consistency, so scores reflect prompt faithfulness as well as perceptual artifacts.
- [DreamBench++: A Human-Aligned Benchmark for Personalized Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16855) ([leaderboard](https://dreambenchplus.github.io/#leaderboard)): Extends personalized generation evaluation with human-aligned prompts, subjects, and preference judgments, testing identity preservation, prompt following, and visual fidelity for custom concepts.
- [A Sanity Check for AI-generated Image Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19435): Stress-tests AI-generated image detection, improving evaluation reliability for synthetic media.
- [Analyzing Quality, Bias, and Performance in Text-to-Image Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00138): Audits text-to-image models along output quality, demographic or social bias, and runtime or model-performance tradeoffs under shared prompt sets.
- [MJ-Bench: Is Your Multimodal Reward Model Really a Good Judge for Text-to-Image Generation?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04842) ([leaderboard](https://mj-bench.github.io/#leaderboard)): Evaluates multimodal reward models as judges for text-to-image generation, checking whether reward scores agree with human pairwise preferences across prompt alignment, aesthetics, and common failure modes.
- [Beyond Aesthetics: Cultural Competence in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06863): Evaluates cultural competence in text-to-image outputs, probing whether generators represent long-tail cultural entities, practices, and contexts rather than defaulting to dominant visual stereotypes.
- [TGIF: Text-Guided Inpainting Forgery Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11566): Provides text-guided inpainting forgeries with localized edits, testing whether detectors can find semantically plausible generated regions instead of relying on whole-image authenticity cues.
- [MemBench: Memorized Image Trigger Prompt Dataset for Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17095): Provides trigger prompts for diffusion-model memorization tests, measuring whether near-training images can be reproduced and how memorization varies by prompt and model.
- [G²Face: High-Fidelity Reversible Face Anonymization via Generative and Geometric Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09458): Evaluates reversible face anonymization, balancing privacy protection, identity recovery for authorized users, and visual fidelity after generative and geometric transformations.
- [DiffZOO: A Purely Query-Based Black-Box Attack for Red-teaming Text-to-Image Generative Model via Zeroth Order Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11071): Red-teams text-to-image systems with a query-only black-box attack, using zeroth-order optimization to find prompts that bypass safety filters or elicit unsafe generations.
- [I2EBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Instruction-based Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14180): Benchmarks instruction-based image editing with diverse edit operations, judging whether models apply the requested change while preserving source-image content outside the target edit.
- [ConceptMix: A Compositional Image Generation Benchmark with Controllable Difficulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14339): Varies compositional prompt difficulty in text-to-image generation, testing whether models bind multiple concepts, attributes, and relations as prompts become harder.
- [Learning to Discover Forgery Cues for Face Forgery Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01030): Evaluates face-forgery detection with discovered forensic cues, focusing on whether cue discovery improves localization and cross-manipulation generalization.
- [Unlearning or Concealment? A Critical Analysis and Evaluation Metrics for Unlearning in Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05668): Introduces metrics that distinguish genuine diffusion-model unlearning from concealment, testing target suppression, prompt sensitivity, and retained generation quality.
- [Detect Fake with Fake: Leveraging Synthetic Data-driven Representation for Synthetic Image Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08884): Tests whether synthetic training data can improve synthetic-image detection, using generated examples to learn representations that transfer to unseen generators.
- [ABHINAW: A method for Automatic Evaluation of Typography within AI-Generated Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11874): Automatically evaluates typography in AI-generated images, targeting legibility, spelling, placement, and style consistency for rendered text that general image-quality scores often miss.
- [JourneyBench: A Challenging One-Stop Vision-Language Understanding Benchmark of Generated Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12953): Uses generated images as the visual substrate for a VLM understanding benchmark, testing whether models can answer fine-grained questions about synthetic scenes and artifacts.
- [NTIRE 2024 Challenge on Stereo Image Super-Resolution: Methods and Results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16947) ([track 1](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/17245); [track 2](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/17246)): Reports stereo super-resolution challenge results, evaluating whether methods exploit binocular cues while improving both left-right consistency and high-resolution detail.
- [Subjective and Objective Quality Evaluation of Super-Resolution Enhanced Broadcast Images on a Novel SR-IQA Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17451): Introduces an SR-IQA dataset for broadcast-image super-resolution, pairing subjective human scores with objective metrics to assess perceptual restoration quality.
- [Dog-IQA: Standard-guided Zero-shot MLLM for Mix-grained Image Quality Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02505): Uses standards-guided zero-shot MLLM judging for mix-grained image quality assessment, covering local defects, global quality, and explanation-style quality reasoning.
- [Holistic Unlearning Benchmark: A Multi-Faceted Evaluation for Text-to-Image Diffusion Model Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05664): Evaluates diffusion-model unlearning across target erasure, residual prompt leakage, retained generation quality, and collateral damage to neighboring concepts.
- [ForgeryGPT: Multimodal Large Language Model For Explainable Image Forgery Detection and Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10238): Evaluates explainable forgery detection and localization with an MLLM, requiring systems to identify manipulated regions and produce natural-language forensic rationales.
- [Evaluating Semantic Variation in Text-to-Image Synthesis: A Causal Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10291): Uses a causal lens to measure semantic variation in text-to-image synthesis, testing whether prompt factors systematically control generated objects, attributes, and relations.
- [Aligned Datasets Improve Detection of Latent Diffusion-Generated Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11835): Shows that aligning real and generated image distributions reduces detector shortcuts, testing latent-diffusion detection under fairer train-test data construction.
- [NaturalBench: Evaluating Vision-Language Models on Natural Adversarial Samples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14669): Tests VLMs on naturally occurring adversarial images and question pairs, useful for checking whether image-generation judges remain reliable on subtle visual confounders.

- [Diffusion Beats Autoregressive: An Evaluation of Compositional Generation in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22775): Compares diffusion and autoregressive text-to-image models on compositional generation, focusing on attribute binding, object relations, and multi-entity prompt fidelity.
- [InvisMark: Invisible and Robust Watermarking for AI-generated Image Provenance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07795): Evaluates invisible watermarking for AI-generated image provenance, stressing watermark detectability and robustness after common image edits and transformations.
- [MEMO-Bench: A Multiple Benchmark for Text-to-Image and Multimodal Large Language Models on Human Emotion Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11235): Evaluates whether text-to-image and multimodal models represent and recognize human emotions, including nuanced facial expressions, body cues, and prompt-specified affect.
- [From Words to Structured Visuals: A Benchmark and Framework for Text-to-Diagram Generation and Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11916): Benchmarks text-to-diagram generation and editing with structured visual constraints, evaluating whether systems preserve nodes, links, labels, and layout semantics.

- [OpenING: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Judging Open-ended Interleaved Image-Text Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18499): Judges open-ended interleaved image-text generation, evaluating whether systems produce coherent multimodal documents with aligned images, captions, and narrative context.
- [HumanEdit: A High-Quality Human-Rewarded Dataset for Instruction-based Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04280): Provides human-rewarded instruction-editing data, emphasizing edit success, visual quality, and preservation preferences collected from human comparisons.
